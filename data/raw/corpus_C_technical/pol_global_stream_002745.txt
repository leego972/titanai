# Sterry R. Waterman

University of Vermont. Vermont Law School maintains a scholarship and lecture series in Waterman's name, and the school's Waterman Hall is named for him.

Family

In 1932, Waterman married Frances Knight (1906–1975). They were the parents of two sons, Robert and Thomas.
