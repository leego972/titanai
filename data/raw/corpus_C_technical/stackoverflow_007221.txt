Question: <p>What would be the easiest way to move the mouse around (and possibly click) using python on OS X?  </p>

<p>This is just for rapid prototyping, it doesn't have to be elegant.</p>

<p>Thanks!</p>

Answer: <p>Your best bet is to use the <a href="https://pypi.python.org/pypi/autopy/0.51" rel="nofollow">AutoPy package</a>. It's extremely simple to use, and cross-platform to boot.</p>

<p>To move the cursor to position (200,200):</p>

<pre><code>import autopy
autopy.mouse.move(200,200)
</code></pre>
