Question: <p>Is there something like <a href="http://effbot.org/zone/python-getattr.htm">Python's getattr()</a> in C#? I would like to create a window by reading a list which contains the names of controls to put on the window.</p>

Answer: <p>Yes, you can do this...</p>

<pre><code>typeof(YourObjectType).GetProperty("PropertyName").GetValue(instanceObjectToGetPropFrom, null);
</code></pre>
