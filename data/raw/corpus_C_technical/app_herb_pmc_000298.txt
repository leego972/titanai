Due to its potential pharmaceutical values, more studies about the biological activities of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> have been conducted recently. Meanwhile, the adverse reaction of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> is not a negligible problem in clinic. In this article, we reviewed a series of articles and summarized the recent studies of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> in the areas of phytochemistry and pharmacology. More than 100 constituents have been isolated and identified from <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic>, including steroids, cardiac glycosides, terpenoids, and fatty acid compounds. The crude extracts of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> and its active compounds exhibit various biological activities, such as cardiotonic effect, anticancer action, and anti-inflammatory effect. This paper aims to provide an overall review on the bioactive ingredients, pharmacological effect, and toxicity of this plant.
