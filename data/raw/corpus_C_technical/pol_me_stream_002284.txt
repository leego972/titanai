# Matthew Knights

g the 2012 season Knights was coach of the Geelong VFL Football Club. He coached the team to the 2012 VFL premiership. In 2015, he was promoted to midfields coach.

On September 13, 2021, Knights left Geelong, and was hired by the West Coast Eagles as midfields coach.
