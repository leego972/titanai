# Don Cowie (footballer)

Personal life 
Cowie is a member of a footballing family: his wife, Shelley, previously played football for Glasgow City F.C. and the Scotland national team, but gave up in order to start a family. Her twin sister, Suzanne Grant, played for Celtic Ladies until 2015 and the Scotland national team until 2014. Suzanne is married to former Dundee United and Hamilton Academical player David Winters. His parents are originally from Helmsdale, in Sutherland.
