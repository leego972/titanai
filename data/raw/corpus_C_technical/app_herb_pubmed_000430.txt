Genomics-Driven Natural Product Discovery in Actinomycetes.

Niu G(1).

Author information:
(1)Biotechnology Research Center, Southwest University, Chongqing 400716, China; 
Academy of Agricultural Sciences, Southwest University, Chongqing 400716, China. 
Electronic address: niu062376@swu.edu.cn.

The prevalence of antimicrobial-resistant pathogens has highlighted the urgent 
need for new drugs. Actinomycetes have been the most prominent sources of 
natural products for drug discovery and development. Advances in genomics have 
inspired several emerging strategies to reinvigorate the field of natural 
product discovery, especially in actinomycete-derived natural products.
