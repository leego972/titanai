Question: <p>I want to use the macports version of python instead of the one that comes with Leopard.</p>

Answer: <p>Instead of uninstalling the built-in Python, install the MacPorts version and then modify your <code>$PATH</code> to have the MacPorts version first.</p>

<p>For example, if MacPorts installs <code>/usr/local/bin/python</code>, then modify your <code>.bashrc</code> to include <code>PATH=/usr/local/bin:$PATH</code> at the end.</p>
