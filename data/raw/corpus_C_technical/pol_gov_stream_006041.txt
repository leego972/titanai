# John Solomon Cartwright

Ontario provincial archives, consisting of documents from 1799 to 1913.  The documents were generated by John Solomon Cartwright, his father Richard Cartwright, his brother Reverend Robert David Cartwright, Robert's wife Harriet (Dobbs) Cartwright and their son, Sir Richard Cartwright.
