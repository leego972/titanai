Question: <p>How can I retrieve the page title of a webpage (title html tag) using Python?</p>

Answer: <p>I'll always use <a href="http://lxml.de/">lxml</a> for such tasks. You could use <a href="http://www.crummy.com/software/BeautifulSoup/">beautifulsoup</a> as well.</p>

<pre><code>import lxml.html
t = lxml.html.parse(url)
print t.find(".//title").text
</code></pre>
