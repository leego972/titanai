# André Bikey

ribed by the Middlesbrough Gazette as an "absolute monster, a physically imposing hulk with a massive turning circle who did a solid if sometimes erratic job at Boro but was always worth at least one heart-stopping moment of madness per game". He celebrated his goals with a hat-trick of somersaults.

Personal life
In April 2007, it was reported that Bikey had a Portuguese wife, and that he was a fan of British food and comedy, particularly Mr. Bean.
