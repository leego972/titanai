Question: <p>Is there a framework equivalent to Guice (<a href="http://code.google.com/p/google-guice">http://code.google.com/p/google-guice</a>) for Python?</p>

Answer: <p>pinject (<a href="https://github.com/google/pinject">https://github.com/google/pinject</a>) is a newer alternative. It seems to be maintained by Google and follows a similar pattern to Guice (<a href="https://code.google.com/p/google-guice/">https://code.google.com/p/google-guice/</a>), it's Java counterpart.</p>
