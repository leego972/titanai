# VL Myrsky

s 
Three Myrsky fuselages and several parts have survived. The Finnish Aviation Museum is currently (2023) restoring one of them (MY-14) into a complete museum aircraft. The project was on display at Finnish Air Force 100th anniversary air show at Tikkakoski, Jyväskylä 16 to 17 June 2018.
