Answer: <p>Given your factorGenerator function, here is a divisorGen that should work:</p>

<pre><code>def divisorGen(n):
    factors = list(factorGenerator(n))
    nfactors = len(factors)
    f = [0] * nfactors
    while True:
        yield reduce(lambda x, y: x*y, [factors[x][0]**f[x] for x in range(nfactors)], 1)
        i = 0
        while True:
            f[i] += 1
            if f[i] &lt;= factors[i][1]:
                break
            f[i] = 0
            i += 1
            if i &gt;= nfactors:
                return
</code></pre>

<p>The overall efficiency of this algorithm will depend entirely on the efficiency of the factorGenerator.</p>
