A value close to 0 indicates that a species was mentioned by only a few informants and is therefore of limited local recognition or specialized use. In contrast, values approaching 1 indicate that a species was cited by a large proportion of informants, reflecting widespread knowledge, frequent use, and high cultural relevance within the community. Species with high RFC values were interpreted as locally important MPs and considered priority taxa for further ethnopharmacological investigation and conservation attention.</p><p id="Par49">The Cultural Value Index (CVI) was calculated to quantify the overall cultural importance of each medicinal plant species within the study community [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR30">30</xref>]. The index integrates three key dimensions of ethnobotanical relevance: the frequency with which a species is cited by informants, the diversity of medicinal uses attributed to it, and the number of distinct use categories in which it is employed. By combining these elements, CVI provides a holistic measure of how deeply a plant is embedded in local traditional knowledge and everyday healthcare practices.</p><p id="Par50">The CVI for each species was calculated using the following formula:<disp-formula id="Equb"><tex-math id="d33e607">\documentclass[12pt]{minimal}
