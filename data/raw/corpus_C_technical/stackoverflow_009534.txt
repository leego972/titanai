Question: <p>As the title suggests. I want to be able to change the label of a single field in the admin application. I'm aware of the Form.field attribute, but how do I get my Model or ModelAdmin to pass along that information?</p>

Answer: <p>If your field is a property (a method) then you should use short_description:</p>

<pre><code>class Person(models.Model):
    ...

    def address_report(self, instance):
        ...
    # short_description functions like a model field's verbose_name
    address_report.short_description = "Address"
</code></pre>
