Question: <p>I've googled around for this, but I still have trouble relating to what Django defines as "apps". </p>

<p>Should I create a new app for each piece of functionality in a site, even though it uses models from the main project? </p>

<p>Do you guys have good rule of thumb of when to split off a new app, and when to keep functionality together with the "main project" or other apps?</p>

Answer: <p>The rule I follow is it should be a new app if I want to reuse the functionality in a different project.</p>

<p>If it needs deep understanding of the models in your project, it's probably more cohesive to stick it with the models.</p>
