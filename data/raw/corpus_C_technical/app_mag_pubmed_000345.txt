[Proton magnetic resonance spectroscopy ((1)H-MRS)].

Igarashi H(1), Takeda M, Natsumeda M, Fujii Y.

Author information:
(1)Center for Integrated Human Brain Science, Brain Research Institute, 
University of Niigata.

Proton magnetic resonance spectroscopy(1H-MRS)is a non-invasive method for 
evaluating brain function and metabolism. 1H-MRS can quantify 
low-molecular-weight metabolites in a living brain; it shows their spectra 
without tracer administration. In this paper, we introduce 1H-MRS and MRS for 
imaging the distribution of metabolites. The applications of 1H-MRS imaging for 
several neurological disorders will be outlined.
