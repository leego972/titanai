Question: <p>I've gotten to grips with the basics of Python and I've got a small holiday which I want to use some of to learn a little more Python. The problem is that I have no idea what to learn or where to start. I'm primarily web development but in this case I don't know how much difference it will make.</p>

Answer: <p>I honestly loved the book <a href="http://rads.stackoverflow.com/amzn/click/0596009259" rel="nofollow">Programming Python.</a> It has a large assortment of small projects, most of which can be completed in an evening at a leisurely pace.  They get you acquainted with most of the standard library and will likely hold your interest.  Most importantly these small projects are actually useful in a "day to day" sense.  The book pretty much only assumes you know and understand the bare essentials of Python as a language, rather than knowledge of it's huge API library.</p>

<p>I think you'll find it'll be well worth working through.</p>
