Title: Nanomechanical Characterization of Micellar Surfactant Films via Atomic Force Microscopy at a Graphite Surface

Abstract:
. The addition of 0.2 mM SDS to a 10 mM DAH solution was found to double the measured breakthrough force of the film. Images taken of the surface showed a phase change from cylindrical hemimicelles to a planar film that may have produced the observed differences. The pH of the bulk solution was varied for both 10 mM SDS and DAH films and was found to have little effect on the breakthrough force.
