An evidence-based systematic review of active hexose correlated compound (AHCC) 
by the Natural Standard Research Collaboration.

Ulbricht C(1), Brigham A, Bryan JK, Catapang M, Chowdary D, Costa D, Culwell S, 
D'Auria D, Giese N, Iovin R, Isaac R, Juturu V, Liu A, Mintzer M, Rusie E, 
Shaffer M, Windsor RC.

Author information:
(1)Natural Standard Research Collaboration, Somerville, Massachusetts, USA. 
ulbricht@naturalstandard.com

An evidence-based systematic review of active hexose correlated compound (AHCC) 
by the Natural Standard Research Collaboration consolidates the safety and 
efficacy data available in the scientific literature using a validated, 
reproducible grading rationale. This article includes written and statistical 
analysis of clinical trials, plus a compilation of expert opinion, folkloric 
precedent, history, pharmacology, kinetics/dynamics, interactions, adverse 
effects, toxicology, and dosing.
