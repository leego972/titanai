Introduction</title><p>Gout is a metabolic disease with excessive accumulation of uric acid in the joints and surrounding tissues due to a purine metabolism disorder in the kidneys. Its symptoms include fever and redness, swelling, pain, and limited activity of the joints.<sup>[<xref rid="R1" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>,<xref rid="R2" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>]</sup> In the early stages of arthritis, there is recurrence of localized symptoms, including paroxysmal pain, fever, and swelling, accompanied by systemic symptoms, such as fever, chills, and headache. In chronic joint deformity, gout nodules develop and kidney disease or uric acid stones are induced.<sup>[<xref rid="R3" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>]</sup> According to a report by the World Health Organization, the prevalence of gout is 3.9% in the world population, and its incidence is increasing every year<sup>[<xref rid="R4" ref-type="bibr">4</xref>]</sup>; it is not limited to older people and tends to develop early in the young population.<sup>[<xref rid="R5" ref-type="bibr">5</xref>]</sup></p><p>In Korea, the National Health Insurance Service treatment data from 2016 to 2020 indicate that the number of patients treated for gout steadily increased from 372,898 in 2016 to 466,766 in 2020. Medical expenses have increased from approximately 152.1 billion won in 2016 to 212.1 billion won in 2020. Of the patients, 92.2% were men, 22.7% were in their 50s, and patients in their 20s showed a rapid increase by 61.7%. Gout is strongly associated with metabolic syndrome and arthritis.<sup>[<xref rid="R3" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>,<xref rid="R6" ref-type="bibr">6</xref>]</sup> It can be life-threatening due to disease complications, side effects of various drugs, and accompanying diseases,<sup>[<xref rid="R7" ref-type="bibr">7</xref>,<xref rid="R8" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>]</sup> and causes a more significant economic burden than other diseases.<sup>[<xref rid="R9" ref-type="bibr">9</xref>]</sup></p><p>Since gout treatment is accompanied by severe pain during an acute attack, its treatment is focused on administering anti-inflammatory drugs and pain relief in the acute phase. Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs, colchicine, and glucocorticoids are the primary treatments used in western medicine (WM).<sup>[<xref rid="R10" ref-type="bibr">10</xref>]</sup> These drugs are effective for short-term pain relief, but long-term use can cause side effects, such as gastrointestinal reactions, liver or kidney dysfunction, and rashes.<sup>[<xref rid="R11" ref-type="bibr">11</xref>,<xref rid="R12" ref-type="bibr">12</xref>]</sup> In addition, medications can be discontinued due to side effects, which significantly restrict the treatment process; therefore, a treatment that can replace them is needed.<sup>[<xref rid="R13" ref-type="bibr">13</xref>]</sup></p><p>Gout corresponds to a category of arthralgia syndrome in traditional medicine, such as in Korean medicine (KM) and traditional Chinese medicine (TCM).
