Title: Analyzing China's Cybersecurity Posture Under Cyber Deterrence Theory: Reshaping Indo-Pacific Security Landscape (2020-2024)

Abstract:
This study examines the changing cyber strategy of China in the Indo-Pacific between 2020 and 2024 through the theory of cyber deterrence as the major analytical framework. Since China is declaring itself a digital power, its strategy of cybersecurity, based on deterrence by denial, punishment, and delegitimization, portrays a transformation of reactive defense into proactive dominance. This change can be seen in some prominent cyber activities like Volt Typhoon, and enactment of cyber regulation like the Cybersecurity Law (2017) and Network Data Security Regulation (2024). This paper aims to examine how China employs cyber capabilities and legal systems to keep away the U.S. influence, and promotes its cyber sovereignty model to regional partners, including Indonesia, Vietnam, and Pakistan, via initiatives, such as the Digital Silk Road. Using the cyber deterrence theory, the paper has illuminated the two-pronged approach by China; its offensive capacity that is enhanced by the AI, quantum computing, and 5G dominance, and the defensive systems that are executed through stringent digital policies. In important findings, it is seen that the cyber posture of China is not only causing a shift in threat perception in the region but is also complicating U.S. led counter measures via AUKUS, the Quad and domestic legislation such as the Secure Equipment Act (2021). These U.S. responses are, however, hampered by disjointed regional interests, asymmetries in technology and lack of multilateral coordination. The paper adds to the academic discussion the application of the deterrence theory to a non-Western authoritarian cyber model and the necessarily imminent construction of coherent and resilient cybersecurity frameworks. It advice the Indo-Pacific stakeholders to focus on strategic cyber diplomacy and collective governance to maintain a digital stability in a competitive geopolitical environment.
