Description:
Bupropion is an aromatic ketone that is propiophenone carrying a tert-butylamino group at position 2 and a chloro substituent at position 3 on the phenyl ring. It has a role as an antidepressant, a xenobiotic and an environmental contaminant. It is a secondary amino compound, an aromatic ketone and a member of monochlorobenzenes.
