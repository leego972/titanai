Question: <p>How can I detect, or be notified, when windows is logging out in python?</p>

<p>Edit:
Martin v. LÃ¶wis' answer is good, and works for a full logout but it does not work for a 'fast user switching' event like pressing win+L which is what I really need it for.
<br /><br />Edit: im not using a gui this is running as a service</p>

Answer: <p>In a console application, you can use win32api.SetConsoleCtrlHandler and look for CTRL_LOGOFF_EVENT. In a GUI application, you need a window open and wait for the WM_QUERYENDSESSION message. How precisely that works (and if it works at all) depends on your GUI library.</p>
