# Nikos Gatsos

publications in Greek and books consisting of or including a substantial number of English translations of Gatsos' writings.  The Greek text of Amorgos, Gatsos' most famous work, is not given an individual entry since it went through a number of editions in Greek, most of which are now out of print.
