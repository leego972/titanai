# Alan Tanner

Alan Tanner (born October 16, 1975) is a music video and short film director living in Los Angeles, California. Originally from Orange Park, Florida,  he directed the "weekly movies" for the Saddle Creek Records website. Tanner also played guitar for Saddle Creek recording artist Maria Taylor.
