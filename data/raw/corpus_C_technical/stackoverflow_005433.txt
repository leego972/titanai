Question: <p>Can you add new statements (like <code>print</code>, <code>raise</code>, <code>with</code>) to Python's syntax?</p>

<p>Say, to allow..</p>

<pre><code>mystatement "Something"
</code></pre>

<p>Or,</p>

<pre><code>new_if True:
    print "example"
</code></pre>

<p>Not so much if you <em>should</em>, but rather if it's possible (short of modifying the python interpreters code)</p>

Answer: <p>Not without modifying the interpreter.  I know a lot of languages in the past several years have been described as "extensible", but not in the way you're describing.  You extend Python by adding functions and classes.</p>
