Although the phytochemical composition and pharmacological properties of <italic toggle="yes">N</italic>. <italic toggle="yes">sativa</italic> seeds have been widely explored, Dinagaran, Sridhar and Eganathan [<xref rid="pone.0272457.ref010" ref-type="bibr">10</xref>] reported that the complete chemical profile of <italic toggle="yes">N</italic>. <italic toggle="yes">sativa</italic> seeds oil (NSSO) and/or extracts is not yet determined. In this view, it is necessary to re-evaluate the therapeutic properties, such as the promising antimicrobial effects, of herbal extracts and/or oils due to the escalating search of natural products that confer desirable antibacterial or antimicrobial properties against antibiotic resistance [<xref rid="pone.0272457.ref011" ref-type="bibr">11</xref>] as well as to provide the up-to-date knowledge on their compositional profile and ascertain their medicinal properties.</p><p>On another hand, the basis of many applications such as raw and processed food preservation, pharmaceuticals, and natural therapies is formed by the antimicrobial activities of extracts derived from medicinal plants [<xref rid="pone.0272457.ref002" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0272457.ref010" ref-type="bibr">10</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0272457.ref012" ref-type="bibr">12</xref>–<xref rid="pone.0272457.ref014" ref-type="bibr">14</xref>]. The seeds powder was used for the preparation of different medicinally active extracts and oil.</p><p>The oil was extracted from 25.0 g of <italic toggle="yes">N</italic>. <italic toggle="yes">sativa</italic> seeds powder in a Soxhlet apparatus using 250 mL of two different solvents, namely absolute ethanol (99.9% v/v) and hexane (Skylabs’ chemicals) at room temperature for 4 hours and 2 hours, respectively [<xref rid="pone.0272457.ref009" ref-type="bibr">9</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0272457.ref010" ref-type="bibr">10</xref>]. The hexane oil was concentrated with the rotary evaporator by evaporating the extractants at 40–50°C under reduced pressure whereas the ethanol oil extract was separated to the crude extract by decantation with a separatory funnel.
