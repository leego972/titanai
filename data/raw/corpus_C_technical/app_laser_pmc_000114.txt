Power to detect calculations confirmed that a sample size of 11 patients was required for establishing 90% statistical power to reject the null hypothesis that FLT–PDT provides no benefit over PDT alone.</p></sec></sec><sec id="lsm23178-sec-0130"><title>RESULTS</title><sec id="lsm23178-sec-0140"><title>Patient Population</title><p>The study was conducted between October 2015 and August 2016. A total of 14 adult female patients were screened, met the entry criteria, and were enrolled in the study. Of those, 11 patients aged 53–72 years completed all three treatments and all three, monthly follow‐up visits. Three patients left the study because of withdrawing consent (not related to adverse events).
