Question: <p>I want to open a TCP client socket in Python. Do I have to go through all the low-level BSD create-socket-handle / connect-socket stuff or is there a simpler one-line way?</p>

Answer: <p>For developing portable network programs of any sort in Python, <a href="http://twistedmatrix.com" rel="nofollow">Twisted</a> is quite useful. One of its benefits is providing a convenient layer above low-level socket APIs.</p>
