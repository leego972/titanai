The iPSC-ECs were thawed and matured until confluent in EC-SFM full medium.<sup><xref rid="cit31" ref-type="bibr">31</xref></sup> Both cells were washed in DPBS, detached from the surface using TrypLE 1× at 37 °C with 5% CO<sub>2</sub>, and resuspended in endothelial growth medium 2 (EGM-2, PromoCell GmbH, Germany) to reach a concentration of 10 million cells per mL and 2 million cells per mL for the iPSC-ECs and iPSC-VSMCs, respectively. Both cell suspensions were then mixed 1 : 1 to obtain a 5 : 1 ratio of iPSC-ECs to iPSC-VSMCs, respectively.</p><p>In the meantime, fibrinogen (Sigma-Aldrich, Germany) and thrombin (Sigma-Aldrich, Germany) were thawed on ice and fibrinogen was diluted in DPBS to a working concentration of 10 mg mL<sup>−1</sup>. The thrombin was added to the cell suspension to obtain a thrombin concentration of 5 units per mL. Fibrinogen was then added in equal amounts to the cell–thrombin mixture to obtain a final concentration of 5 mg mL<sup>−1</sup> fibrinogen and 2.5 units per mL thrombin. This mixture was then resuspended quickly, injected into the inlets of the microfluidic chip (<xref rid="fig1" ref-type="fig">Fig. 1A</xref>), and immediately placed in the incubator set at 37 °C with 5% CO<sub>2</sub> for 25 minutes to ensure fast hydrogel crosslinking. These steps were repeated until all microfluidic chips were seeded.</p><p>After incubation, EGM-2 medium supplied with 2.5 μg mL<sup>−1</sup> aprotinin (Sigma-Aldrich, Germany) and 50 ng mL<sup>−1</sup> VEGF was added to the reservoirs and microfluidic chips were placed on a rocker set at 10° and a 60 minutes interval (MIMETAS, The Netherlands). Medium was refreshed daily for 2 or 3 consecutive days for comparison between OCT and confocal microscopy, and the experiment with the treatments, respectively.</p></sec><sec><title>Treatments</title><p>After day 2 of culture, the medium in the microfluidic chips was changed to: i) control medium consisting of EGM-2 medium supplied with 2.5 μg mL<sup>−1</sup> aprotinin; ii) high glucose medium with inflammatory cytokines to induce vessel regression, consisting of EGM-2 medium supplied with 2.5 μg mL<sup>−1</sup> aprotinin, 30 mM <sc>d</sc>-glucose (Sigma-Aldrich, Germany), 1 ng mL<sup>−1</sup> TNF-α (300-01A, PeproTech, USA) and 1 ng mL<sup>−1</sup> IL-6 (A42540, Invitrogen, USA); and iii) VEGF-medium to induce vessel growth, consisting of EGM-2 medium supplied with 2.5 μg mL<sup>−1</sup> aprotinin and 50 ng mL<sup>−1</sup> VEGF. Each medium was refreshed daily until day 5 of culture.</p></sec><sec><title>Perfusability assay</title><p>At day 3 of culture, microfluidic chips with the control medium were measured for perfusability using 0.1 mg mL<sup>−1</sup> dextran fluorescein 40 000 MW (FITC-dextran 40 kDa, D1844, Invitrogen, USA) in EGM-2 medium supplied with 2.5 μg mL<sup>−1</sup> aprotinin.
