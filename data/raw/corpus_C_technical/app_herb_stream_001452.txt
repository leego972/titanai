# Blacas — Overview

France (the mantle azure lined with ermine and fringed with gold). Motto: PRO DEO, PRO REGE. Crest: An oak sinople issuant from a French Baron's coronet proper. War-cry: VAILLANCE. According to Frédéric Mistral, the family crest is canting because blacas also means "white oak" in Provençal References Hubert Cuny, Nicole Dreneau, Le Gotha français : état présent des familles ducales et princières (depuis 1940), Paris 1989.
