Question: <p>I have always used tabs for indentation when I do Python programming. But then I came across a question here on SO where someone pointed out that most Python programmers use spaces instead of tabs to minimize editor-to-editor mistakes.</p>

<p>How does that make a difference? Are there other reasons why one would use spaces instead of tabs for Python? Or is it simply not true?</p>

<p>Should I switch my editor to insert spaces instead of tabs right away or keep on going like I used to?</p>

Answer: <p>The only inconvenience I experience with using spaces instead of tabs is that you cannot easily remove an indentation level, you have to remove 4 spaces instead of just one tab.</p>
