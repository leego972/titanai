Lamiaceae and Myrtaceae are the most common plant families with each four species, Asparagaceae with three species followed by Verbenaceae with two species. All other species belong to different families. On species level, <italic toggle="yes">Aloe vera</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Aloe saponaria</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">Salvia rosmarinus</italic> were reported most often by the participants (37%, 23% and 23%, respectively). Followed by <italic toggle="yes">Mentha piperita</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Ruta chalepensis</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">Lippia graveolens</italic> (each used by 17% of the respondents). The rest of the medicinal plant species were used by less than 11% of the participants. Over a third of all medicinal species (<italic toggle="yes">n</italic> = 12) were found in at least two gardens. <italic toggle="yes">Aloe vera</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Aloe saponaria</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">Salvia rosmarinus</italic> are among the more frequently cultivated medicinal species in the private gardens (Fig. <xref rid="Fig2" ref-type="fig">2</xref>). From the total number of medicinal species encountered, several of these species have been reported in San José before: <italic toggle="yes">Mentha piperita</italic> var. <italic toggle="yes">citrata</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">M</italic>. <italic toggle="yes">spicata</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Salvia rosmarinus, Aloe vera</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Ruta graveolens</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Cymbopogon citratus, Lippia alba, L. graveolens, Justicia pectoralis, Salvia rosmarinus, Mentha piperita, Eucalytus spp., Zingiber officinale, Ruta chalepensis, Tanacetum parthenium</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">Tradescantia zebrina</italic> [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR48">48</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR49">49</xref>].<fig id="Fig2" position="float" orientation="portrait"><label>Fig. 2</label><caption><p>Species of medicinal plants found in at least two private urban gardens of Heredia</p></caption><graphic xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink" id="MO2" position="float" orientation="portrait" xlink:href="13002_2022_505_Fig2_HTML.jpg"/></fig></p><p id="Par19">In the area with the lowest socioeconomic status (BB), 86% of all garden owners reported at least one medicinal plant, in the mixed area (HU) 40% of all garden owners reported at least on medicinal species and in the residential area with a higher socioeconomic status (MA) only 33%.
