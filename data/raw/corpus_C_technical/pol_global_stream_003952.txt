# Syro-Malabar Catholic Eparchy of Kalyan

1 July 2006. On 20 November 2011, the Cathedral church of the Eparchy (St Thomas Cathedral Church) was consecrated and blessed in Kalyan West. On 2 October 2012, the Animation and Renewal Centre (ARC) was blessed in Panvel. In November 2013, the Eparchy of Kalyan celebrated its silver jubilee year.

Parishes under the Eparchy of Kalyan
There are, at present, 101 parishes under the Eparchy of Kalyan. The Cathedral Church of the Eparchy is in Kalyan West (St Thomas Cathedral Church). There are 14 foranes in the Eparchy of Kalyan: Andheri, Antop Hill, Borivli, Kalina, Kalyan, Malad, Nasik, Navi Mumbai, Panvel, Powai, Pune, South-Mumbai, Thane and Vasai.

Bishops of the Eparchy of the Kalyan 

 Mar Paul Chittilapilly (1988-1997)
 Mar Thomas Elavanal (1997-Present)

Saints connected with the Eparchy
 St. Bartholomew the Apostle is considered to have done missionary activity in the Kalyan region.
