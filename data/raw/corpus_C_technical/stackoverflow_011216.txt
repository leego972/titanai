Question: <p>When I call <code>socket.getsockname()</code> on a socket object, it returns a tuple of my machine's internal IP and the port. However, I would like to retrieve my external IP. What's the cheapest, most efficient manner of doing this?</p>

Answer: <p>Using the address suggested in the source of <a href="http://whatismyip.com" rel="nofollow">http://whatismyip.com</a></p>

<pre><code>import urllib
def get_my_ip_address():
    whatismyip = 'http://www.whatismyip.com/automation/n09230945.asp'
    return urllib.urlopen(whatismyip).readlines()[0]
</code></pre>
