Title: Natural Oral Care Products for the Treatment of Stomatitis

Abstract:
Oral stomatitis is the painful inflammation of the oral mucosa which presents as ulceration clinically. Oral stomatitis is of many types based on the occurrence of size, shape, and location. Since, time immemorial, home remedies have a major role in the management of inflammation. But the invasion of the western system of medicine and suppression of the loco regional indigenous Indian system for the management of ailments has obscured the ancient knowledge. Indian medicine is the oldest system for disease management in the inscriptions and writing in the form of Charaka Samhita. In this context, the limited knowledge of the present generation is looking for evidence-based medicine because of the influence of Western medicine. Since the advent of Ayurveda and the disadvantages associated with allopathic medicines, herbal medicine, and home remedies are getting keen attention globally. In this regard, many of the indigenous Indian systems of medical practitioners and learners are getting into the research to provide evidence for the public and others for better utilization and to believe in the management of ailments. Hence, the present chapter reviews the available evidence on natural herbs in recurrent aphthous stomatitis management.
