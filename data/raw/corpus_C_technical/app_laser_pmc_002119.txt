LLLT (660–904 nm) and LED (460–850 nm) modulated proteins involved in proliferation, collagen synthesis, migration, VEGF formation, and extracellular matrix regeneration [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0027" ref-type="bibr">27</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0029" ref-type="bibr">29</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0034" ref-type="bibr">34</xref>]. One study reported increased DNA, total protein, hydroxyproline, and hyaluronic acid levels with LLLT and LED in burn models compared with controls [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0027" ref-type="bibr">27</xref>]. Pulsed LLLT regulated 727 differentially expressed proteins linked to cell signaling and neuronal function, while LED modulated 822 proteins, particularly in metabolic and inflammatory pathways [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0024" ref-type="bibr">24</xref>]. LED was associated with increased mitofusin‐2 (MFN2) and decreased mitochondrial fission protein 1 (FIS1), favoring mitochondrial balance, whereas LLLT increased both proteins, suggesting mitochondrial stress [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0028" ref-type="bibr">28</xref>].</p></sec></sec><sec id="cbf70161-sec-0180"><label>3.4</label><title>Expression of Inflammatory Activity Markers</title><p>Four studies analyzed inflammatory markers [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0026" ref-type="bibr">26</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0028" ref-type="bibr">28</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0029" ref-type="bibr">29</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0036" ref-type="bibr">36</xref>]. LLLT and LED reduced TNF‐α, IL‐1β, IL‐6, COX‐2 levels, increased ERK1/2 phosphorylation and produced the highest nitrite levels [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0026" ref-type="bibr">26</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0029" ref-type="bibr">29</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0036" ref-type="bibr">36</xref>]. LED produced greater COX‐2 reduction than LLLT [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0028" ref-type="bibr">28</xref>].</p></sec><sec id="cbf70161-sec-0190"><label>3.5</label><title>Determinants of Oxidative Stress</title><p>Three studies evaluated oxidative stress markers [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0030" ref-type="bibr">30</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0036" ref-type="bibr">36</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0037" ref-type="bibr">37</xref>]. LLLT 904 nm and LED 632 nm increased oxidative stress, whereas LLLT 660 nm and LED 850 nm exerted antioxidant effects, reducing glutathione peroxidase activity and lowering nitrite and carbonyl levels [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0030" ref-type="bibr">30</xref>]. LLLT and LED at low doses stimulated nitrite production, leukocyte activity and reactive oxygen species generation, whereas higher doses inhibited these processes [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0036" ref-type="bibr">36</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0037" ref-type="bibr">37</xref>]. Both LED and LLLT reduced lipid peroxidation from day 4 onward compared with controls [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0037" ref-type="bibr">37</xref>].</p></sec><sec id="cbf70161-sec-0200"><label>3.6</label><title>Quality Assessment of the Studies</title><p>Four studies used an in vitro methodology, three of which were classified as having a moderate risk of bias [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0021" ref-type="bibr">21</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0022" ref-type="bibr">22</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0024" ref-type="bibr">24</xref>] and one as low risk [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0023" ref-type="bibr">23</xref>]. The criteria that received the most negative evaluations were related to reporting of cell passage number, missed wells, cell handling between establishment and measurement, and cell characterization.
