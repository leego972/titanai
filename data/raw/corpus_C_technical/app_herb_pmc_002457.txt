infections and antibiotic resistance have become a major threat in most parts of the world. Modern medical treatments including organ transplantations, chemotherapy, and surgeries might become risky without the use of effective antibiotics. Recently, the exploration of plants for therapeutic purposes gained popularity due to several reasons, such as, easy access without prescription, low-cost, natural origin, and possibility of reducing the need for synthetic drugs that may have severe side effects [<xref rid="pone.0272457.ref001" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>–<xref rid="pone.0272457.ref003" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>]. Medicinal plants have always been the critical natural factories of phytochemicals such as flavonoids, tannins, phenols, steroids, alkaloids and terpenoids which are responsible for their biological activities [<xref rid="pone.0272457.ref003" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>]. Plant products that are generated from fruits, flowers, seeds, roots, leaves, and barks are part of phytomedicines and many bioactive constituents of plants have been detected and further characterized by using different standard analytical methods [<xref rid="pone.0272457.ref003" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0272457.ref004" ref-type="bibr">4</xref>].</p><p><italic toggle="yes">Nigella sativa</italic> is one of the medicinal plant species that gained popularity for a wide range of medicinal applications due to its seeds, generally known as black seeds, which are rich in phytoconstituents. Series of studies reported that thymoquinone is the major constituent of <italic toggle="yes">N</italic>. <italic toggle="yes">sativa</italic> seeds and accounts for most of the seeds’ pharmacological properties [<xref rid="pone.0272457.ref005" ref-type="bibr">5</xref>–<xref rid="pone.0272457.ref008" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>]. Literature also indicates that extracts and/or oil of <italic toggle="yes">N</italic>. <italic toggle="yes">sativa</italic> seeds contain many other constituents such as proteins, carbohydrates, vitamins, dietary minerals (such as Fe and Zn), crude fiber, alkaloids, saponins, steroid, terpenoids, p-cymene, limonene, and fatty acids [<xref rid="pone.0272457.ref003" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0272457.ref007" ref-type="bibr">7</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0272457.ref009" ref-type="bibr">9</xref>].</p><p>The reported pharmacological properties that are imparted by <italic toggle="yes">N</italic>. <italic toggle="yes">sativa</italic> seeds due to their chemical composition include but not limited to, analgesic, appetizer, antidiabetic, antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, radical scavenger, and antimicrobial properties [<xref rid="pone.0272457.ref003" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0272457.ref004" ref-type="bibr">4</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0272457.ref009" ref-type="bibr">9</xref>].
