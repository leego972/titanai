Question: <p>For example, I'm trying to convert 2008-09-26T01:51:42.000Z to 09/26/2008. What's the simplest way of accomplishing this?</p>

Answer: <p>The easiest way is to use <a href="http://labix.org/python-dateutil">dateutil</a>.parser.parse() to parse the date string into a timezone aware datetime object, then use strftime() to get the format you want.</p>

<pre><code>import datetime, dateutil.parser

d = dateutil.parser.parse('2008-09-26T01:51:42.000Z')
print d.strftime('%m/%d/%Y') #==&gt; '09/26/2008'
</code></pre>
