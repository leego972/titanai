Description:
Aminoacetone is a propanone consisting of acetone having an amino group at the 1-position. It has a role as a mouse metabolite and an Escherichia coli metabolite. It is a member of propanones and a methyl ketone. It is functionally related to an acetone. It is a conjugate base of an ammonioacetone.
