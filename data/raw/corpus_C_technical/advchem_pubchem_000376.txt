Description:
2-(hydroxymethyl)-4-(1-hydroxy-2-{[6-(4-phenylbutoxy)hexyl]amino}ethyl)phenol is a phenol having a hydroxymethyl group at C-2 and a 1-hydroxy-2-{[6-(4-phenylbutoxy)hexyl]amino}ethyl group at C-4; derivative of phenylethanolamine. It is a primary alcohol, an ether, a member of phenols, a secondary alcohol and a secondary amino compound. It is functionally related to a phenylethanolamine.
