Cuisine
Given the strong presence of Italians of Ligurian ancestry in Peru, Peruvian cuisine has been influenced by Italian cuisine, especially Ligurian. Examples of Ligurian dishes commonly eaten in Peru are spaghetti with pesto sauce, , and focaccia.
