Films
Democracy After Death

Democracy After Death: The Tragedy of Uncle Nuamthong Praiwan directed by, the exile, Neti Wichiansaen (Pruay Santihead) in 2016. It was a combination of animation, performances and news footage. The director tried to examined the period of Thai politics defined by the military coup of 2006 and 2014. Neti was accused of violating Article 112 in late 2010.
