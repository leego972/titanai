Question: <p>Say I have the classic 4-byte signed integer, and I want something like
<p> print hex(-1)
<p> to give me something like
<p>>> 0xffffffff
<p> In reality, the above gives me -0x1. I'm dawdling about in some lower level language, and python commandline is quick n easy. 
<p> So.. is there a way to do it?</p>

Answer: <p>"0x{:04x}".format((int(my_num) &amp; 0xFFFF), '04x') , where my_num is the required number</p>
