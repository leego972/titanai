Answer: <p>Yes, it makes quite a bit of sense to use timsort outside of CPython, in specific, or Python, in general.</p>

<p>There is currently an <a href="http://bugs.sun.com/bugdatabase/view%5Fbug.do?bug%5Fid=6804124">effort underway</a> to replace Java's "modified merge sort" with timsort, and the initial results are quite positive.</p>
