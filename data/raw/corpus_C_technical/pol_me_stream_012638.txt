# Iraq War troop surge of 2007

d this question "As you may know, the US. sent 20,000 combat troops to Iraq in 2007 in what was called a surge. Based on what you have read or heard, do you think that surge of U.S. troops to Iraq was a success or a failure?"  60% responded as success, 33% failure and 7% depends or unsure.
