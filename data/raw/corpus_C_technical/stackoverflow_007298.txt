Question: <p>Is there any IDE (like VS) with drag and drop support for building python GUI, connecting to dbs etc?
Eventhough I am an emacs guy, I find it much easier to create GUI with VS.</p>

Answer: <p>For GUI only, I find VisualWx (<a href="http://visualwx.altervista.org/" rel="nofollow">http://visualwx.altervista.org/</a>) to be very good for designing wxPython apps under Windows.</p>

<p>For GUI + database, dabo (<a href="http://dabodev.com/" rel="nofollow">http://dabodev.com/</a>) is probably a good answer.</p>
