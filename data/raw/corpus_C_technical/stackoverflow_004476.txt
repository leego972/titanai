Question: <p>In PHP I can name my array indicies so that I may have something like:</p>

<pre><code>$shows = Array(0 =&gt; Array('id' =&gt; 1, 'name' =&gt; 'Sesaeme Street'), 
               1 =&gt; Array('id' =&gt; 2, 'name' =&gt; 'Dora The Explorer'));
</code></pre>

<p>Is this possible in Python?</p>

Answer: <p>Python has lists and dicts as 2 separate data structures. PHP mixes both into one. You should use dicts in this case. </p>
