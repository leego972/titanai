# Qeshm County

Qeshm County () is in Hormozgan province, Iran. Its capital is the city of Qeshm.

At the 2006 census, the county's population was 103,881 in 22,642 households. The following census in 2011 counted 117,774 people in 28,798 households. At the 2016 census, the county's population was 148,993 in 40,506 households. Qeshm is a free trade zone and has good potential for business.

In May 2017, Larak Rural District was transferred from Shahab District to the Central District. In November 2017, Dulab Rural District and Salakh Rural District were separated from Shahab District in the establishment of Hara District.  In March 2019, Hormuz District was transferred to Bandar Abbas County.

After the 2016 census, the villages of Laft and Ramkan in the Central District were elevated to the status of cities.
