# KHNK

KHNK (95.9 FM,  (Outlaw Country) is a commercial radio station in Columbia Falls, Montana, broadcasting to the Kalispell-Flathead Valley, Montana, area. KHNK airs a country music format heavy on 80s and 90s plus country music recurrents.  It changed to country when KKMT moved.  Before then 95.9's format played variations of adult and hot adult contemporary music.

It is owned by Bee Broadcasting, Inc.  All Bee Broadcasting stations are based at 2431 Highway 2 East, Kalispell.
