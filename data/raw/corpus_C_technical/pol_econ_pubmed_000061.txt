Child labor and health: problems and perspectives.

Fassa AG(1), Facchini LA, Dall'agnol MM, Christiani DC.

Author information:
(1)Departamento de Medicina Social, Universidad Federal de Pelotas, Caixa Postal 
464, Pelotas, RS Brazil. fassa@ufpel.tche.br

Child labor remains a widespread problem. Although it can have positive effects, 
in some situations it has negative effects on health and development of the 
children. Although mainly a problem in developing countries, it is also possible 
to find child workers, some working in hazardous activities, in developed 
countries. The authors describe the child labor profiles in developed and 
developing countries, the principal occupations of children, and their 
concomitant hazards. They summarize the epidemiologic evidence for a greater 
impact of some occupational exposures on the health of children as compared with 
adults, and the theoretical concerns about the impact of child labor on health, 
and suggest policies that can be used to combat harmful child labor.
