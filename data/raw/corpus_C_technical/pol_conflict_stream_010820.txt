# Raid on Combahee Ferry

The site can be viewed from the boat landing parking lot on the Beaufort side of the river. The surrounding area is under private ownership.

In popular culture
The Combahee River Raid forms the basis for the 2019 novel The Tubman Command, by historian Elizabeth Cobbs. The raid was also a major plot point of "The General", the penultimate episode of the second season of Timeless in 2018. The raid was also depicted briefly in the epilogue of the 2019 movie Harriet. It was covered in a segment on Drunk History in 2015.
