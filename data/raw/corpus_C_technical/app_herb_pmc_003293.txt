2021</meta-value></custom-meta></custom-meta-group></article-meta></front><body><sec id="Sec1"><title>Background</title><p id="Par9">Many people around the world rely on traditional healthcare systems involving medicinal plants, which are often sold at open-air markets [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR1">1</xref>–<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR5">5</xref>]. This is also the case in Brazil, where a great variety of medicinal plants are still traded at open-air markets [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR6">6</xref>–<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR8">8</xref>]. These markets are hosted and visited by Brazilians and tourists from various cultural backgrounds. This cultural diversity is reflected in the number of available plant species and their uses [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR9">9</xref>]. It is easy to establish which medicinal plants are popular in the region through market surveys, as markets give an overview of local demand [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR10">10</xref>].</p><p id="Par10">There has been an increasing number of ethnobotanical market surveys in the past decades [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR11">11</xref>]. Although there are a number of studies that compare different markets to each other in terms of availability of plant species [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR12">12</xref>–<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR14">14</xref>], few studies have repeated ethnobotanical surveys in markets that were already monitored decades before to examine changes in species composition [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR15">15</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR16">16</xref>]. Likewise, few market surveys have included an analysis of vernacular names of medicinal species being sold [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR12">12</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR17">17</xref>]. The availability of plant products at markets changes over time [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR16">16</xref>], indicating the importance of periodically conducting market surveys for a more complete picture of plant availability and to assess possible impacts of social and environmental factors [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR15">15</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR18">18</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR19">19</xref>].
