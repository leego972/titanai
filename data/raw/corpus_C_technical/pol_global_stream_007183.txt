# Nichols Farms Historic District

were a part of the original district proposed by the Trumbull Historic District Study Committee for Nichols Farms in May 1976. The Ephraim Hawley House, Zachariah Curtiss House and barn, the summer home of American folklorist Will Geer, and a home of helicopter inventor Igor Sikorsky were excluded.

A subsequent Historic Building Survey, completed in 2010, has recommended these properties be added to the existing historic district.
