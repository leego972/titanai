Question: <p>What is the best way of creating an alphabetically sorted list in Python?</p>

Answer: <p>The proper way to sort strings is:</p>

<pre><code>import locale
locale.setlocale(locale.LC_ALL, 'en_US.UTF-8') # vary depending on your lang/locale
assert sorted((u'Ab', u'ad', u'aa'), cmp=locale.strcoll) == [u'aa', u'Ab', u'ad']

# Without using locale.strcoll you get:
assert sorted((u'Ab', u'ad', u'aa')) == [u'Ab', u'aa', u'ad']
</code></pre>

<p>The previous example of <code>mylist.sort(key=lambda x: x.lower())</code> will work fine for ASCII-only contexts.</p>
