Researchers employ various extraction methods, including maceration, percolation, and hydrodistillation, to isolate these compounds from <italic toggle="yes">Chamaenerion</italic> species. Non-polar solvents like hexane and methyl tert-butyl ether are highly effective for extracting lipophilic compounds, whereas hydrodistillation is ideal for volatile aromatics [<xref rid="B55-molecules-30-01186" ref-type="bibr">55</xref>,<xref rid="B56-molecules-30-01186" ref-type="bibr">56</xref>]. These methodological advancements have ensured the accurate characterization of the complex chemical profiles of <italic toggle="yes">Chamaenerion</italic> species.</p><p>The therapeutic potential of volatile and lipophilic compounds in <italic toggle="yes">Chamaenerion</italic> species is substantial. Alkanes like nonacosane and tetracosane enhance the skin barrier function, making them valuable in dermatological applications [<xref rid="B57-molecules-30-01186" ref-type="bibr">57</xref>]. Meanwhile, sesquiterpenes, including α- and β-caryophyllenes, exhibit potent anti-inflammatory and anticancer activities, with α-caryophyllene constituting a significant portion of the total volatiles [<xref rid="B58-molecules-30-01186" ref-type="bibr">58</xref>].
