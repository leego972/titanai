Answer: <p>Jack M.'s example is nearly correct.</p>

<p>It needs to be a url function, not a tuple, if you want to use named urls.</p>

<pre><code>url(r'^no_monkeys/$', 'views.noMonkeys', {}, "no-monkeys"),
</code></pre>
