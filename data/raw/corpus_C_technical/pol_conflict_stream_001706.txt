# 14th Indiana Infantry Regiment

Indiana Volunteers on the Gettysburg battlefield in 1885. Made of Indiana limestone, the  memorial is located on East Cemetery Hill, adjacent to a statue of Major General Winfield S. Hancock. The monument marks the location where Isaac Morris, the regiment’s color-bearer, was killed on July 2, 1863.
