Title: A Model-Based Thruster Leakage Monitor for the Cassini Spacecraft

Abstract:
The Cassini spacecraft was launched on October 15, 1997.  It uses thrusters to perform many spacecraft control functions: to detumble the spacecraft, to maintain three-axis attitude control, to perform small trajectory correction burns, to desaturate the reaction wheels, and others.
