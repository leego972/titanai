Question: <p>Basically, something similar to System.Xml.XmlWriter - A streaming XML Writer that doesn't incur much of a memory overhead. So that rules out xml.dom and xml.dom.minidom. Suggestions?</p>

Answer: <p><strong>xml.etree.cElementTree</strong>, included in the default distribution of CPython since 2.5. Lightning fast for both reading and writing XML.</p>
