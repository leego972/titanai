Introduction</title><p>
<italic toggle="yes">Dendrobium officinale</italic> Kimura et Migo, belonging to the <italic toggle="yes">Dendrobium</italic> of <italic toggle="yes">Orchidaceae</italic> genus, is widely used as a medicinal and functional food product [<xref rid="B1" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>]. It originated from Nanling Mountains and Yungui Plateau in China, and its cultivation migrated northward or eastward subsequently [<xref rid="B2" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>]. <italic toggle="yes">Dendrobium officinale</italic> was originally used as a tonic herbal medicine to treat stomach disorders and promote the secretion of body fluid in Chinese medicine [<xref rid="B1" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>]. It also has a long history as a food ingredient in Yunnan and Zhejiang Province in China, and the main ways of consumption are making instant food, soup, dishes, juices, tea, and wine. In particular, the dried stem of <italic toggle="yes">Dendrobium officinale</italic> (<italic toggle="yes">Dendrobii officinalis</italic>) has been documented in Chinese Pharmacopoeia for medicinal usage and is officially listed in “Medicine and Drug Homology,” which indicates that <italic toggle="yes">Dendrobium officinale</italic> might be feasible for long-term consumption with high safety [<xref rid="B3" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>].</p><p>Increasing pharmacological studies have found that <italic toggle="yes">Dendrobium officinale</italic> has a high nutritional and medicinal value, such as antioxidant, immune-regulatory, anti-inflammatory, anticancer, antidiabetic, and hepatoprotective activities [<xref rid="B4" ref-type="bibr">4</xref>, <xref rid="B5" ref-type="bibr">5</xref>]. These health benefits are mainly attributed to its abundant bioactive compounds, such as flavonoids, bibenzyls, polysaccharides, and alkaloids [<xref rid="B6" ref-type="bibr">6</xref>]. As a natural plant product, <italic toggle="yes">Dendrobium officinale</italic> poses little toxicity and side effects to human health, and it could combine with other herbal medicines in Chinese medicine decoction for the treatment of diseases. Since the present chemical drugs and therapy could cause some side effects in patients, it is essential to develop natural-derived drugs and adjuvant supplements with fewer side effects for patients.
