Covering: up to the end of 2022Microorganisms are exceptional sources of a wide 
array of unique natural products and play a significant role in drug discovery. 
During the golden era, several life-saving antibiotics and anticancer agents 
were isolated from microbes; moreover, they are still widely used. However, 
difficulties in the isolation methods and repeated discoveries of the same 
molecules have caused a setback in the past. Artificial intelligence (AI) has 
had a profound impact on various research fields, and its application allows the 
effective performance of data analyses and predictions. With the advances in 
omics, it is possible to obtain a wealth of information for the identification, 
isolation, and target prediction of secondary metabolites. In this review, we 
discuss drug discovery based on natural products from microorganisms with the 
help of AI and machine learning.
