Today
The battlefield site is preserved and administered by West Virginia as Droop Mountain Battlefield State Park, and is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. The park was dedicated in 1928, and is West Virginia's oldest state park. Unknown Confederate dead from the battle are buried in the Confederate Cemetery at Lewisburg, which is also listed on the National Register of Historic Places.
