Question: <p>How do I check whether a file exists, without using the <a href="https://docs.python.org/3.6/reference/compound_stmts.html#try"><code>try</code></a> statement?</p>

Answer: <p>Prefer the try statement. It's considered better style and avoids race conditions.</p>

<p>Don't take my word for it. There's plenty of support for this theory. Here's a couple:</p>
