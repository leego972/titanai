Other OCTA indicators of long-term growth of type 1 CNV lesions include the presence of a network of tiny capillaries at the border of the CNV lesion, as well as a lack of mature dilated feeder vessels.<sup><xref rid="bibr68-2515841419840249" ref-type="bibr">68</xref></sup></p><p>The ability of OCTA to analyze lesions three-dimensionally may be important in the study of neovascular AMD as well. Recent work by Nesper and colleagues<sup><xref rid="bibr69-2515841419840249" ref-type="bibr">69</xref></sup> demonstrated the novel use of 3D volume-rendered PR-OCTA in the characterization of complex CNV lesions. After examining parameters such as the number of CNV flow layers, they found that increasing complexity of the lesions was associated with poor treatment response to antiangiogenic agents. Further investigation using OCTA could potentially lead to the development of accurate predictors of treatment response in CNV and could validate the use of similar 3D approaches in the study of other diseases.</p><p>Prior to the availability of OCTA, imaging of subclinical NV (defined as asymptomatic CNV prior to the development of exudation) in dry AMD using ICGA was limited by cost, invasiveness, and an overall limited understanding of the significance of these lesions. More recently, de Oliveira Dias and colleagues<sup><xref rid="bibr70-2515841419840249" ref-type="bibr">70</xref></sup> found that 21.1% of patients with subclinical CNV found on baseline OCTA imaging demonstrated exudation within 1 year, compared to 3.6% of those eyes without subclinical NV at baseline. In general, eyes with subclinical CNV had an approximately 15 times greater risk of exudation than those eyes without NV. RVO.<sup><xref rid="bibr75-2515841419840249" ref-type="bibr">75</xref></sup> Other parameters such as FAZ area have been found to be enlarged in RVO (as well as in fellow unaffected eyes) compared to healthy controls on OCTA,<sup><xref rid="bibr76-2515841419840249" ref-type="bibr">76</xref></sup> and changes in these markers have been demonstrated to be associated with worsening visual acuity.<sup><xref rid="bibr77-2515841419840249" ref-type="bibr">77</xref>,<xref rid="bibr78-2515841419840249" ref-type="bibr">78</xref></sup> Derivation of the aforementioned parameters requires
