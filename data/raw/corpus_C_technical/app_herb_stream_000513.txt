Other students of French's included Gail M. Kelly, May Ebihara, Katherine Verdery, Robert A. Brightman, and Robert E. Moore. In 1988 French received the American Anthropological Association's prestigious Distinguished Service Award, his discipline's highest honor. Death
French died February 12, 1994, in Portland, Oregon. Selected works
