# Liozna

Liozna (; ; ; ; ) is an urban-type settlement in Vitebsk Region, Belarus. It serves as the administrative center of Liozna District. It is located close to the border with Russia by the Vitebsk-Smolensk railroad branch and highway, on the Moshna River.  As of 2023, it has a population of 6,631.

History 
The first known record of the Liozno shtetl (small town with a high Jewish population) is dated 1654.

During World War II, Liozna was under German occupation from 17 July 1941 until 8 October 1943.
