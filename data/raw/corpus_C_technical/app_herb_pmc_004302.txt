# PMC: adaptogen stress response HPA axis — Overview

Stimulating Activity of Adaptogens in Animal Model Systems</title><p>A typical pharmacological assessment of adaptogens includes the evaluation of stimulating, tonic and stress-protective activities in model animal systems, which were subjected to various stress conditions [<xref rid="B112-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">112</xref>,<xref rid="B113-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">113</xref>].</p><sec><title>3.1. Anti-Stress Effect of Adaptogens</title><p>The stress protective effect of adaptogens has been demonstrated on simple organisms and on isolated cells [<xref rid="B17-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">17</xref>,<xref rid="B18-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">18</xref>,<xref rid="B114-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">114</xref>,<xref rid="B115-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">115</xref>]. Thus, there may be an association with regulation and homeostasis of the neuro-endocrine-immune complex. In addition, there may also be a connection with more evolutionary older congential mechanisms of regulation in cellular homeostasis and the adaptive/defense response to external stressors. We hipothezise that this type of defence system is apparently common for all cells and living organisms and probably includes heat shock proteins among the number of key mediators of innate nonspecific resistance to stressors.</p><p>It has been shown that the same mechanism can be found in stress tolerance and lifespan extension, which makes them parallel phenomenon Therefore, it is not surprising that adaptogens prolong the lifespan of the nematode <italic toggle="yes">Caenorhabditis</italic>
