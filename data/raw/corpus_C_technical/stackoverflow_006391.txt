Question: <p>What's the difference between the list methods <code>append()</code> and <code>extend()</code>?</p>

Answer: <p>And in this context it can also be good to remember that strings are also iterable.</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; a = [1, 2]
&gt;&gt;&gt; a
[1, 2]
&gt;&gt;&gt; a.extend('hey')
&gt;&gt;&gt; a
[1, 2, 'h', 'e', 'y']
</code></pre>
