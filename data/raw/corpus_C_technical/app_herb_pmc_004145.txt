The different layers of the skin, the epidermis and dermis, are separated by the basement membrane (BM), a complex mixture of ECMs proteins.<xref rid="cit0094" ref-type="bibr">94</xref> ECMs surround and interact closely with dermal cells such as fibroblasts, immune cells, and vascular cells, providing physical support for these cells. It also allows cells to sense the changing conditions in their local environment, facilitating communication between cells and promoting cellular adaptation to this changing microenvironment.<xref rid="cit0095" ref-type="bibr">95</xref> Nonetheless, changes in ECMs composition can directly contribute to or induce pathological conditions, which is a hallmark of the aging process.<xref rid="cit0094" ref-type="bibr">94</xref> The ECMs is further involved in transmitting important signals to recruit inflammatory cells in response to danger signals.
