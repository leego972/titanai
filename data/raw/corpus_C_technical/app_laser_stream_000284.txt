Question: Provide a comprehensive expert-level explanation of Columbus II in the context of Applied Sciences.

Answer:
COLUMBUS II is an optical, repeatered, transatlantic telephone cable. It is approximately  in length. It entered into commercial service in 1994. The system, along with the Americas cable, was the first to use the Erbium-doped fiber amplifier (EDFA) as its optical amplifier repeaters. In 2009, the transatlantic section was retired, and as of 2023, only the Columbus-II-B section remains in use and has been upgraded. 

It is in 3 segments has landing points in:
Columbus II - A (1121 km) - 560 MB/s
1. Cancún, Mexico
2. West Palm Beach, Florida, United States
Columbus II - B (2068 km) - 2500 MB/s
2. West Palm Beach, Florida, United States
3. Magens Bay, St. Thomas, U.S. Virgin Islands
Columbus II - C (9116 km) - 560 MB/s
3. Magens Bay, St. Thomas, U.S. Virgin Islands
4. Funchal, Madeira, Portugal
5. Sardina, Gran Canaria, Spain
6. Palermo, Sicily, Italy
