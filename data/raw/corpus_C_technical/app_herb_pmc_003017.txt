All tested positive for flavonoids, and all except <italic toggle="yes">Breynia oblongifolia</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">Coleus amoenus</italic> tested positive for saponins. Only <italic toggle="yes">Cajanus reticulatus</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">Exocarpos latifolius</italic> tested positive for alkaloids, and none tested positive for anthraquinones (Table <xref rid="Tab2" ref-type="table">2</xref>). Of the five plants, <italic toggle="yes">Dodonaea lanceolata var. subsessilifolia</italic> showed positive results for the widest range of phytochemical classes (Table <xref rid="Tab2" ref-type="table">2</xref>). Prior to this study, little was known of the phytochemistry of these five medicinal plant species.
