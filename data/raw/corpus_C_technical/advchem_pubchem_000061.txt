Description:
4-aminobutanal is an -aminoaldehyde that is butanal in which one of the hydrogens of the terminal methyl group has been replaced by an amino group. It has a role as a mouse metabolite and an Escherichia coli metabolite. It is an aminobutanal and an omega-aminoaldehyde. It is a conjugate base of a 4-ammoniobutanal.
