# David Richards, Baron Richards of Herstmonceux

worth in the County of Hampshire. He was chosen to carry the Sword of Spiritual Justice at the 2023 Coronation.

Personal life
In 1978 Richards married Caroline Reyne (née Bond). Lady Richards is a trustee of charities Plant for Peace and The Afghan Appeal. They have two daughters, Joanna and Pippa.

Richards is a keen student of military history and a qualified offshore yachtsman. He is Admiral of the British Kiel Yacht Club and Royal Artillery Yacht Club.
