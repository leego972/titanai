Answer: <p>Golden rule of data - don't aggregate too early - it is much easier to glue fields together than separate them. Most people also have a middle name which should be an optional field. Some people have a plethora of middle names. Some people only have <a href="https://stilgherrian.com/category/only-one-name/" rel="nofollow">one name</a>, one word. Some cultures commonly have a dictionary of middle names, paying homage to the family tree back to the Golgafrincham Ark landing.</p>

<p>You don't need a code solution here - you need a business rule.</p>
