# Xiao Zhengde

posing Xiao Zhengde back to his title of Prince of Linhe, although conferring him a highly honorary office of Dasima (大司馬).  Xiao Zhengde became depressed, and when he subsequently met Emperor Wu, he wept bitterly.  Emperor Wu responded, "You weep and weep, but is it not too late for you to regret?"

Xiao Zhengde resented Hou for reneging on the agreement, and he subsequently sent letters to his cousin Xiao Fan (蕭範) the Prince of Poyang, requesting that Xiao Fan come to Jiankang's relief.  The letters were intercepted by Hou's forces, and in summer 549, Hou strangled Xiao Zhengde to death.
