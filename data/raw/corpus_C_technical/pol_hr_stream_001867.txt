2010 re-election
Patricia Todd was unopposed for re-election in 2010, with no Democrat or Republican running against her. She was re-elected to a second four-year term on November 2, 2010.

2014 re-election
Todd faced two opponents in the Democratic primary in June 2014, defeating them with 64% of the vote. She did not face a Republican opponent in November and was re-elected to a third term.
