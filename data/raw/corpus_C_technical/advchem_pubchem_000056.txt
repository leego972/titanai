Description:
3-phenylpropionic acid is a monocarboxylic acid that is propionic acid substituted at position 3 by a phenyl group. It has a role as an antifungal agent, a plant metabolite and a human metabolite. It is a member of benzenes and a monocarboxylic acid. It is functionally related to a propionic acid. It is a conjugate acid of a 3-phenylpropionate.
