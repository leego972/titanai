Question: <p>What's the best Python idiom for this C construct?</p>

<pre><code>while ((x = next()) != END) {
    ....
}
</code></pre>

<p>I don't have the ability to recode next().</p>

<p>update:  and the answer from seems to be:</p>

<pre><code>for x in iter(next, END):
    ....
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>It depends a bit what you want to do. To match your example as far as possible, I would make next a generator and iterate over it:</p>

<pre><code>def next():
   for num in range(10):
      yield num

for x in next():
   print x
</code></pre>
