Question: <p>For example, I'm trying to convert 2008-09-26T01:51:42.000Z to 09/26/2008. What's the simplest way of accomplishing this?</p>

Answer: <p>I know this is really old question, but you can do it with python datetime.strptime()</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; from datetime import datetime
&gt;&gt;&gt; date_format = "%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%S.%fZ" 
&gt;&gt;&gt; datetime.strptime('2008-09-26T01:51:42.000Z', date_format)
datetime.datetime(2008, 9, 26, 1, 51, 42)
</code></pre>
