Question: <p>Is it possible to retrieve items from a Python dictionary in the order that they were inserted?</p>

Answer: <p>Or use any of the implementations for the <a href="http://www.python.org/dev/peps/pep-0372/" rel="nofollow">PEP-372</a> described <a href="http://www.python.org/dev/peps/pep-0372/#reference-implementation" rel="nofollow">here</a>, like the <a href="http://www.voidspace.org.uk/python/odict.html" rel="nofollow">odict module</a> from the <a href="http://www.voidspace.org.uk/python/pythonutils.html" rel="nofollow">pythonutils</a>.</p>

<p>I successfully used the pocoo.org implementation, it is as easy as replacing your</p>

<pre><code>my_dict={}
my_dict["foo"]="bar"
</code></pre>

<p>with</p>

<pre><code>my_dict=odict.odict()
my_dict["foo"]="bar"
</code></pre>

<p>and require just <a href="http://dev.pocoo.org/hg/sandbox/raw-file/tip/odict.py" rel="nofollow">this file</a></p>
