Question: <p>What's the difference between the list methods <code>append()</code> and <code>extend()</code>?</p>

Answer: <p>The following two snippets are semantically equivalent:</p>

<pre><code> for item in iterator:
     a_list.append(item)
</code></pre>

<p>and</p>

<pre><code>a_list.extend(iterator)
</code></pre>

<p>The latter may be faster as the loop is implemented in C.</p>
