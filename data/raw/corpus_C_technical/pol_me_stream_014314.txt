# Tomasz Wieszczycki

Tomasz Witold Wieszczycki (born 21 December 1971) is a Polish former professional footballer who played as a midfielder.

Career

National team
Wieszczycki appeared in 11 matches for the Poland national team, scoring three goals.

Honours

 Ekstraklasa: 1997–98

Polonia Warsaw
 Ekstraklasa: 1999–2000
 Polish Cup: 2000–01
 Polish League Cup: 1999–2000
 Polish Super Cup: 2000

Poland Olympic
 Summer Olympic Games runner-up: 1992
