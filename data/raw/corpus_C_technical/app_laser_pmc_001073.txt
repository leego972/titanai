This method is useful in diagnosing diseases of specific organs like the eye, where a direct biopsy cannot be conducted. Since its inception, significant advancements have been made in its technology. Apart from its initial application in ophthalmology for retinal imaging, substantial technological innovations in OCT brought by the research community have enabled its utilization beyond its original scope and allowed its application in many new clinical areas. This review presents a summary of the clinical applications of OCT in the field of medicine (ophthalmology, cardiology, otology, and dermatology) and dentistry (tissue imaging, detection of caries, analysis of dental polymer composite restorations, imaging of root canals, and diagnosis of oral cancer). BY</meta-value></custom-meta></custom-meta-group></article-meta></front><body><sec sec-type="intro" id="sec1"><title>Introduction</title><p>Optical coherence tomography (OCT) is a non-invasive diagnostic method that can be utilized to acquire high-resolution three-dimensional (3D) images of a biological structure. <sup><xref rid="ref1" ref-type="bibr">1</xref></sup> OCT has quickly gained popularity among clinicians due to several advantages that include delivering quality images with quick imaging speed. <sup><xref rid="ref2" ref-type="bibr">2</xref></sup> This technique has several clinical applications that include (but are not limited to) detection of diabetic macular edema,
<sup><xref rid="ref3" ref-type="bibr">3</xref></sup> epithelial mapping for refractive surgery,
<sup><xref rid="ref4" ref-type="bibr">4</xref></sup> diagnosing and managing patients with neuro-ophthalmic conditions,
<sup><xref rid="ref5" ref-type="bibr">5</xref></sup> imaging of retinal microcirculation to detect hemodynamic disturbances,
<sup><xref rid="ref6" ref-type="bibr">6</xref></sup> detection of age associated changes in teeth,
<sup><xref rid="ref7" ref-type="bibr">7</xref></sup> diagnosing dental caries
<sup><xref rid="ref8" ref-type="bibr">8</xref></sup> and tooth wear,
<sup><xref rid="ref9" ref-type="bibr">9</xref></sup> and post-treatment assessments in dentistry.
