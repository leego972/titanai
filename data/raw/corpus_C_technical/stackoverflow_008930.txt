Answer: <p>I havent tried 3.0 yet. But there is now a bigger distinction between a sequence of bytes and strings. The latter hold unicode codepoints while the former don't hold unicode but only <code>encoded</code> unicode strings. Hashes operate on sequences of bytes. So you will have to encode your (unicode) strings first bevore feeding them to a hash.</p>
