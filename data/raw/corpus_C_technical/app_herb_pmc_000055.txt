Also, standardized extracts of <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> Jack containing a high concentration of quassinoids (20% eurycomanone and 4% of 13α,21-dihydroeurycomanone) may have potential anti-estrogenic effects [<xref rid="B86-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">86</xref>].</p><p>The quassinoid-containing <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> extract affects male infertility by suppressing α-2-HS glycoprotein expression, which indirectly increases the testosterone levels and insulin sensitivity. They indicated that serum α-2-HS glycoprotein was reduced in rats treated with standardized <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> extract, which will provide rational for further investigation in animal models of infertility with diabetes [<xref rid="B113-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">113</xref>].</p><p>A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel group study was conducted to investigate the aphrodisiac clinical evidence of <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> extract in men. The total twelve weeks’ study in 109-men between 30- and 55- years of age, divided in a group of 300 mg of water extract of <italic toggle="yes">E. longifoli</italic>a (Physta<sup>®</sup>)-treated and placebo. The <italic toggle="yes">E. longifoli</italic>a group showed higher scores in the overall erectile-function-domain (IIEF, <italic toggle="yes">p</italic> &lt; 0.001), the sexual libido (14% by week 12), Seminal Fluid Analysis (SFA)-with sperm motility at 44.4%, and semen-volume at 18.2% after treatment [<xref rid="B114-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">114</xref>].</p><p>Chan <italic toggle="yes">et al.</italic>, statistically analyzed the spermatozoa count, morphology, motility, plasma testosterone level and Leydig cell count of the animals by ANOVA. Their results showed that the sperm counts of rats given the standardized methanol extract alone at doses of 50, 100 and 200 mg/kg were increased by 78.9%, 94.3% and 99.2%, respectively, when compared with that of control (<italic toggle="yes">p</italic> &lt; 0.01) [<xref rid="B115-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">115</xref>].</p><p>Ang and Ngai showed that the fractions of <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> Jack (0.5 g/kg) decreased the hesitation time. Furthermore, they caused a transient increase in the percentage of the male rats responding to the right choice; more than 50% of the male rats scored “right choice”; using the electrical copulation cage [<xref rid="B116-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">116</xref>].</p><p><italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> has been shown to elevate serum testosterone and increased muscle strength in humans. Chen <italic toggle="yes">et al.</italic>, investigated the effects of standardized water extract of <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> (Physta<sup>®</sup>) at a dose of 400 mg/day for 6 weeks on testosterone: epitestosterone (T:E) ratio, liver and renal functions in male recreational athletes found no significant difference between the results of supplementation results and placebo [<xref rid="B117-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">117</xref>].</p><p>Study on the sexual qualities of middle-aged male rats after dosing with 0.5 g/kg of various fractions of <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic>, showed that it enhanced the sexual qualities of the middle-aged male rats by decreasing their hesitation time as compared to controls [<xref rid="B118-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">118</xref>].</p><p>A randomized, double-blind, study with placebo-controlled was conducted for proprietary freeze-dried water extract of <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> (Physta<sup>®</sup>) effects on sexual performance and well-being in men. For this study, men aged 40–65 years were screened for 12-week. Results showed the significant improvements in scores for the sexual intercourse attempt diary, erection hardness scale, sexual health inventory of men, and aging male symptom scale (<italic toggle="yes">p</italic> &lt; 0.05 for all), concluded that Physta<sup>®</sup> was well tolerated and more effective than placebo in enhancing sexual performance in healthy volunteers [<xref rid="B59-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">59</xref>].</p><p><italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> extract acts as a potential agent to increase spermatogenesis and sperm counts, and for reversing the effects of estrogen in rats, after fourteen consecutive days of treatment [<xref rid="B119-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">119</xref>].</p><p>In other study, Ang <italic toggle="yes">et al.</italic>, showed that <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> produced a dose-dependent, recurrent and significant increase in the episodes of penile reflexes as evidenced by increases in quick flips, long flips and erections of the treated male rats during 30 min observation period [<xref rid="B17-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">17</xref>].</p><p>According to Tambi and Imran’s investigations, 350 patients were given 200 mg of the <italic toggle="yes">E.
