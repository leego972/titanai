The authors included 13 RCTs (962 participants). The primary outcome measure in all included trials was the improvement of the neurological deficit after treatment. <italic toggle="yes">Eleutherococcus</italic> was found to significantly increase the number of participants with improvement in neurological impairment. However, because the risk of bias in all of the included trials was high, the authors concluded that much larger trials of greater methodological quality are required.<xref rid="med21743-bib-0375" ref-type="ref">
