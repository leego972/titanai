# Secretary for Security (Macau)

The Secretary for Security (; ) is the government department responsible for public safety and security in Macau. The role replaced the former post of Secretary for Public Security. It is responsible for managing the Macau public security and police agencies.

Subordinate entities 
The following entities are controlled under the MSF:
 
 Public Security Police Force
 Immigration Department of the Macao Special Administrative Region is a sub unit under Public Security Police Force
 
 Public Security Forces Affairs Bureau
 Correctional Services Bureau
 Fire Services Bureau
 Academy for Public Security Forces

Macau Customs Service

Macau Customs Service is a government agency responsible for the protection against smuggling; revenue collection, detect and deter drug trafficking; protection of intellectual property rights; the protection of consumer interests; and the protection and facilitation of legitimate trade and maintaining trading integrity in Macau.

Customs Service is most visible at the points of entry into Macau:

Land entry points
 Posto Fronteiriço das Portas do Cerco
 Lotus Bridge
 Hengqin Port Inspection Area

Air entry points
 Macau International Airport

Marine entry points
 Taipa Ferry Terminal
 Inner Harbour Ferry Terminal
 Outer Harbour Ferry Terminal
