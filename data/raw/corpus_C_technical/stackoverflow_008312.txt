Question: <p>Is there a way to ensure all created subprocess are dead at exit time of a Python program? By subprocess I mean those created with subprocess.Popen().</p>

<p>If not, should I iterate over all of the issuing kills and then kills -9? anything cleaner?</p>

Answer: <p>I needed a small variation of this problem (cleaning up subprocesses, but without exiting the Python program itself), and since it's not mentioned here among the other answers:</p>

<pre><code>p=subprocess.Popen(your_command, preexec_fn=os.setsid)
os.killpg(os.getpgid(p.pid), 15)
</code></pre>

<p><code>setsid</code> will run the program in a new session, thus assigning a new process group to it and its children. calling <code>os.killpg</code> on it thus won't bring down your own python process also.</p>
