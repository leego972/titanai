Photobiomodulation Therapy</title><p>The first therapeutic application (day 0), according to group allocation, was performed. Application of light therapy was repeated every second day without anesthesia in a custom made container. Dressing changes were performed every four days, during which fresh hydrogel was applied.</p><p>PBM was performed with red (629 nm), green (540 nm) and blue (470 nm) light. Irradiation time was 6 min (through the transparent wound cover membrane), and all therapy LEDs used a pulse frequency of 2.5 Hz, a duty cycle of 50%, and were normalized to 40 mW/cm<sup>2</sup>. These parameters were chosen based on the positive results of previous in vitro and in vivo studies [<xref rid="B34-ijms-24-05895" ref-type="bibr">34</xref>,<xref rid="B35-ijms-24-05895" ref-type="bibr">35</xref>,<xref rid="B36-ijms-24-05895" ref-type="bibr">36</xref>]. The mouse was placed in a transparent, cylindrical enclosure of a diameter commensurate with the size of light source with surrounding ventilation slots for cooling, to ensure the animal remains within consistent light exposure throughout the therapy while maintaining some mobility (<xref rid="ijms-24-05895-f011" ref-type="fig">Figure 11</xref>). The vertical source distance to the mouse’s dorsum was kept at 10 cm. The overall dose of each treatment was 14.4 J/cm<sup>2</sup>.</p></sec><sec id="sec4dot3-ijms-24-05895"><title>4.3. Wound Assessment</title><p>Wound size was assessed every fourth day from day 0 through to day 28 during wound dressing changes, using a stereoscopic lens (LifeViz micro, Quantificare, Biot, France) and a Canon Rebel XSi camera (Canon, Ota City, Tokyo, Japan) for 3D wound measurement (circumference, surface, average depth). Stereoscopic wound analysis parameters were evaluated using DermaPix Pro 2.28.5. Skin erythema was captured by using a smartphone (iPhone 6 Plus, Apple Inc., Cupertino, CA, USA) with installed CE medical device software Scarletred<sup>®</sup> Vision plus a Scarletred<sup>®</sup> Skin patch, which resulted in auto white balanced and color corrected skin images over treatment time. The images were consecutively uploaded and analyzed within the Scarletred<sup>®</sup> web platform (SCARLETRED Holding GmbH, Vienna, Austria) by using the standard erythema value (SEV *) algorithm [<xref rid="B38-ijms-24-05895" ref-type="bibr">38</xref>,<xref rid="B39-ijms-24-05895" ref-type="bibr">39</xref>,<xref rid="B40-ijms-24-05895" ref-type="bibr">40</xref>].</p><p>Additionally, the animals were scanned via laser Doppler imaging (Moor, UK) under isoflurane inhalation anesthesia on the day of surgery (day 0) and on day 28 to assess superficial tissue perfusion.
