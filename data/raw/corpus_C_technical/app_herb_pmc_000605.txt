Lignanoids and Coumarins</title><p>In recent years, some studies found that <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> contain lignanoids and coumarins, moreover, 21 lignanoids and four coumarins have been discovered in this plant and are displayed in <xref ref-type="fig" rid="molecules-24-00359-f005">Figure 5</xref> and <xref ref-type="fig" rid="molecules-24-00359-f006">Figure 6</xref>. In 2017, xanthiumnolic B (<bold>100</bold>) was found from the fruits of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> and its anti-inflammatory activity has been demonstrated [<xref rid="B40-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">40</xref>]. Later, 14 lignanoids were also isolated from the fruits of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic>, including (-)-1-<italic toggle="yes">O</italic>-β<sc>-d-</sc>glucopyranosyl-2-{2-methoxy-4-[1-(E)-propen-3-ol]phenoxyl} -propane-3-ol (<bold>101</bold>), leptolepisol D (<bold>102</bold>), dihydrodehydrodiconiferyl alcohol (<bold>103</bold>), chushizisin E (<bold>104</bold>), (-)-(2R)-1-<italic toggle="yes">O</italic>-β<sc>-d-</sc>glucopyranosyl-2-{2-methoxy-4-[(Eformylvinyl]phenoxyl}propane-3-ol (<bold>105</bold>), (-)-7<italic toggle="yes">R</italic>,8<italic toggle="yes">S</italic>-dehydrodiconiferyl alcohol (<bold>106</bold>), (-)-simulanol (<bold>107</bold>), 2-(4-hydroxy-3-methoxyphenyl)-3-(2-hydroxy-5-methoxyphenyl)-3-oxo-1-propanol (<bold>108</bold>), diospyrosin (<bold>109</bold>), dehydrodiconiferyl alcohol (<bold>110</bold>), balanophonin A (<bold>111</bold>), threo-dihydroxydehydrodiconiferyl alcohol (<bold>112</bold>), 1-(4-hydroxy-3-methoxy)-phenyl-2-[4-(1,2,3-trihydroxypropyl)-2-methoxy]-phenoxy-1,3-propandiol (<bold>113</bold>), 7<italic toggle="yes">R</italic>,8<italic toggle="yes">S</italic>-dihydrodehydrodiconiferyl alcohol 4-<italic toggle="yes">O</italic>-β<sc>-d-</sc>glucopyranoside (<bold>114</bold>) [<xref rid="B48-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">48</xref>]. Furthermore, syringaresinol (<bold>115</bold>) [<xref rid="B39-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">39</xref>], fructusol A (<bold>116</bold>) [<xref rid="B42-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">42</xref>], balanophonin (<bold>117</bold>) [<xref rid="B24-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">24</xref>], 4-oxopinoresinol (<bold>118</bold>) [<xref rid="B28-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">28</xref>], pinoresinol (<bold>119</bold>) [<xref rid="B24-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">24</xref>] were identified from the plant.</p><p>In 2011, Kan et al. isolated four coumarins from the roots of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> for the first time, including scopoletin (<bold>120</bold>), Jatrocin B (<bold>121</bold>), cleomiscosin A (<bold>122</bold>), cleomiscosin C (<bold>123</bold>) [<xref rid="B39-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">39</xref>].</p></sec><sec id="sec4dot4-molecules-24-00359"><title>4.4. Steroids</title><p>A few studies have been conducted investigating the steroids in <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic>. In 2010, β-sitostenone (<bold>124</bold>), β-sitosterol (<bold>125</bold>), daucosterol (<bold>126</bold>), stigmast-4-en-β-ol-3-one (<bold>127)</bold>, and 5α,8α-epidioxy-22E-ergosta-6,22-dien-3β-ol (128) were isolated from <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> [<xref rid="B39-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">39</xref>]. Furthermore, Chen et al. found 6β-hydroxy-stigmast-4,22-dien-3-one (<bold>129</bold>), 6β-hydroxy-stigmast-4-en-3-one (<bold>130</bold>), 3-oxo-Δ4,5-sitostenone (<bold>131</bold>), β-daucosterol (<bold>132</bold>), β-stigmastero (<bold>133</bold>) and 7-ketositosterol (<bold>134</bold>) from the roots of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> [<xref rid="B28-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">28</xref>].
