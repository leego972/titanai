# Fikret Kızılok

Şevval Sam ("Gönül"), and Sibel Sezai ("Ben Gidersem"). The TV series Bu Kalp Seni Unutur mu? was named after one of his songs. Kızılok was also an innovative instrumentalist and album arranger, the first to incorporate sitar and Indian tabla within Turkish pop music.
