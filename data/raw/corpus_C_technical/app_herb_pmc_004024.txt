The quinone metabolites of ISO that form superoxide anions and other reactive oxygen species (ROS) when they react with oxygen might cause oxidative stress by interfering with antioxidant enzymes [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR124">124</xref>]. For the prevention of oxygen-free radical-induced tissue damage, the presence of the endogenous antioxidant defense is crucial [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR125">125</xref>]. The principal free radical scavenging enzymes SOD, catalase (CAT), and GPx play a role in the first-line cellular defense against oxidative harm by breaking down oxygen (O2) and hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) before they combine to create the more dangerous hydroxyl radical [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR126">126</xref>].</p><p id="Par119">According to histopathological investigations, giving WS to patients dramatically lessens ischemia-related cardiac damage. Because of its anti-apoptotic qualities and ability to restore the normal balance of oxidative substances, ashwagandha has a cardioprotective effect [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR127">127</xref>]. Additionally, withaferin A, a part of Ashwagandha renowned for its anticancer activities, was investigated for its cardioprotective effects. By upregulating the mitochondrial anti-apoptotic pathway through an increase in AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK) phosphorylation and an increase in the Bcl-2/Bax ratio (AMPK), modest dosages of withaferin A were found to have a cardioprotective effect in this rat investigation [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR54">54</xref>]. Additionally, it is known that AMPK is triggered by caloric restriction and that it plays a role in a few aging-related processes and diseases that frequently affect older people. Since AMPK maintains energy balance, it is believed to increase life expectancy and quality of life [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR55">55</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR56">56</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR128">128</xref>].</p></sec><sec id="Sec14"><title>Effects of Ashwagandha in enhancing physical performance</title><p id="Par120">According to sources, WS has been utilized in the conventional Ayurvedic medical system to boost cognition, increase memory, and boost other aspects of physical performance [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR129">129</xref>–<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR132">132</xref>] (Fig. <xref rid="Fig3" ref-type="fig">3</xref>). Ashwagandha acts as a gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA) mimetic and shows cholinomimetic activity [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR133">133</xref>]. Additionally, it appears that ashwagandha’s secondary metabolites act as agonists for the nicotinic receptor α -7 [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR134">134</xref>]. <italic toggle="yes">Withania somnifera</italic> supplements have been found in animal trials to improve memory and cognitive function [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR133">133</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR135">135</xref>]. <italic toggle="yes">Withania somnifera</italic> has been related in human intervention studies to improved cognition in people with early dementia or bipolar illness [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR10">10</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR136">136</xref>]. However, there are few research on populations that are healthy [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR134">134</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR137">137</xref>]. For eight weeks, Indian adults with early dementia who were taking 600 mg of Ashwagandha-root extract saw improvements in their memory, executive function, sustained attention, and processing speed [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR136">136</xref>]. In male and female bipolar patients with an average age of 47, supplementing for eight weeks with 500 mg of <italic toggle="yes">Withania somnifera</italic> appeared to improve a select few cognitive abilities, including auditory-verbal working memory, a measure of reaction time, and a measure of social cognition [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR136">136</xref>]. Gopukumar et al. examined the impact of Ashwagandha SR capsules (300 mg) on cognitive functioning, stress levels, sleep quality, and overall wellbeing in randomized placebo-controlled clinical research. They claimed that stress impairs cognitive function and can cause a number of illnesses, including mental and anxiety problems, coronary heart disease, high blood pressure, and diabetes [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR138">138</xref>]. This study demonstrated how Ashwagandha SR can enhance cognition, lower stress levels, and enhance sleep quality. Given the negative effects of conventional anti-anxiety drugs (such benzodiazepines), which cause sleepiness and impair cognitive abilities, Ashwagandha SR’s dual efficacy on cognitive capacities and sleep quality is advantageous [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR139">139</xref>].
