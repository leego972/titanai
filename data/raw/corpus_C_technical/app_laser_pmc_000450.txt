For the calculation of improvement, only participants were included who had any such impairment at baseline. The parameters with the highest proportion of participants with improvement were degree of scarring (100%), atrophy (94.1%), skin surface impairment (80.4%) and hyperpigmentation (78.1%). After 12 months, 83.3% of the participants treated with BF‐200 ALA who had cosmetic impairment at baseline rated the cosmetic outcome as very good or good. Of these, the majority rated the cosmetic outcome as very good (60.4%).</p><p>As shown in Figure <xref rid="jdv20452-fig-0005" ref-type="fig">5</xref>, skin quality improved in all measured parameters during follow‐up in both groups. In the BF‐200 ALA group, the proportion of participants with any impairment decreased for all parameters during 12 months of follow‐up compared to baseline.
