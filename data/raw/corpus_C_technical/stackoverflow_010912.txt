Question: <p>I'm looking to create <code>favicon.ico</code> files programatically from Python, but PIL only has support for reading <code>ico</code> files.</p>

Answer: <p>I don't know if this applies for all cases, but on WinXP an .ico can be a bmp of size 16x16, 32x32 or 64x64. Just change the extension to ico from bmp and you're ready to go.</p>
