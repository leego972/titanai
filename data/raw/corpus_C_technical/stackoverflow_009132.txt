Answer: <p>OT: Using successive calls to <code>PyList_Append</code> is a performance issue. Since you know how many results you'll get in advance, you can use:</p>

<pre><code>PyObject *pyTimeList = PyList_New(NUM_DRAWERS);
int i;
for (i=0; i&lt;NUM_DRAWERS; i++){
    o = PyInt_FromLong(pdbHeader-&gt;last_good_test[i]);
    PyList_SET_ITEM(pyTimeList, i, o);
}
</code></pre>

<p>Observe that you may not decrease the refcount of <code>o</code> after calling <code>PyList_SET_ITEM</code>, because it "steals" a reference. Check the <a href="http://docs.python.org/c-api/list.html">docs</a>.</p>
