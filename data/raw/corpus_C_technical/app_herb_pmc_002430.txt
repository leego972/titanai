# PMC: phytochemical bioactive compound anticancer — Overview

Kaempferol demonstrates potential in treating obesity-related ailments by promoting lipolysis and suppressing adipogenesis in 3T3-L1 adipocytes, a mouse pre-adipocyte cell line (<xref rid="ref-40" ref-type="bibr">Muni Swamy et al., 2020</xref>). It has also been reported to inhibit the growth of pancreatic cancer cells through apoptosis induction (<xref rid="ref-74" ref-type="bibr">Zhang et al., 2008</xref>). Hesperitin, another major flavonoid found in <italic toggle="yes">S. rostratum</italic> (<xref rid="ref-29" ref-type="bibr">Ibarra-Alvarado et al., 2010</xref>) has been associated with reducing cancer cell proliferation by inducing cell cycle arrest and exhibiting cytostatic effects (<xref rid="ref-20" ref-type="bibr">De Oliveira, Santos &amp; Fernandes, 2019</xref>). Likewise, luteolin, a flavonoid in <italic toggle="yes">S. rostratum</italic> (<xref rid="ref-67" ref-type="bibr">Wang et al., 2015</xref>) has been linked to inhibiting tumor growth by influencing cellular processes such as apoptosis, angiogenesis, migration, and cell cycle progression (<xref rid="ref-14" ref-type="bibr">Çetinkaya &amp; Baran, 2023</xref>) as well as suppressing some proinflammatory mediators such as IL-6, IL-8, TNF-<italic toggle="yes">α</italic>, and COX-2 which in turn regulates different skin inflammatory processes (<xref rid="ref-25" ref-type="bibr">Gendrisch et al., 2021</xref>).</p><fig position="float" id="fig-5" orientation="portrait"><object-id pub-id-type="doi">10.7717/peerj.17112/fig-5</object-id><label>Figure 5</label><caption><title>Chemical structure of important flavonoids isolated and identified from <italic toggle="yes">S. rostratum</italic>.</title><p>(A) Quercetin.
