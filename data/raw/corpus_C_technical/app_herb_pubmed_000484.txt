Empirical antibacterial drug discovery--foundation in natural products.

Singh SB(1), Barrett JF.

Author information:
(1)Natural Products Chemistry, RY80Y-350, Merck Research Laboratories, P.O. 
2000, Rahway, NJ 07065, USA. sheo_singh@merck.com

Natural products have been a rich source in providing leads for the development 
of drugs for the treatment of bacterial infections. However, beyond the 
discovery of the natural product, thienamycin and the synthetic lead, 
oxazolidinone in the 1970s, there has been a dearth of new compounds. This 
commentary provides an overview of current antibiotic leads and their mechanism 
of action, and highlights tools that can be applied to the discovery of new 
antibiotics.
