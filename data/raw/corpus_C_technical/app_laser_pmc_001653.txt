Innovation often occurs at the boundaries of fields where there is a leveraging of ideas, knowledge, and technology from one field into another. The clearest example in OCT is the use of technology and ideas from fiber optic telecommunications. All of the lasers, detectors, many of the system concepts, and fiber optic components themselves came from telecom.
