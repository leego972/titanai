Single-Cell Migration Assay</title><p>HaCaT and HDF cells were seeded onto 10.61 kPa PAA gels at a density of 1000 cells/cm<sup>2</sup> to evaluate single-cell migration following LED treatment. After cell attachment to the gel, Hoechst 33,342 (1:1000; #<ext-link xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink" ext-link-type="pmc:entrez-nucleotide" xlink:href="H21492">H21492</ext-link>; Molecular Probes, Eugene, CA, USA) was used to stain the nuclei for 1 h. The medium was then replaced, and the cells were exposed to LED treatment for 20 min. Time-lapse images of cell migration were acquired every 10 min for 12 h using an E-VOS M700 imaging system (Thermo Fisher Scientific, Waltham, MA, USA) with a 10× objective lens (NA, 0.3; #AMEP4981; Thermo Fisher Scientific, Waltham, MA, USA). Cell trajectories were analyzed using TrackMate in FIJI and MATLAB-based program codes. Cell migration was quantified by calculating the mean square displacement (MSD), diffusion coefficient (D), and velocity (V) using previously established equations in MATLAB (MathWorks, Natick, MA, USA; version R2023b) [<xref rid="B72-ijms-26-06232" ref-type="bibr">72</xref>]. The MSD measures the average displacement of a cell over a specified time interval (Δt). The diffusion coefficient (D = slope/4) was derived from the linear regression of the MSD curves, representing cell dispersion speed. The mean absolute distance (MAD) was used to measure the average absolute displacement of a cell over a specified time interval. The Velocity (V = slope) was derived from the linear regression of MAD, indicating the speed of cell movement regardless of direction.
