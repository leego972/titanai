Question: <p>How can I find local IP addresses (i.e. 192.168.x.x or 10.0.x.x) in Python platform independently and using only the standard library?</p>

Answer: <p>On Debian (tested) and I suspect most Linux's..</p>

<pre><code>import commands

RetMyIP = commands.getoutput("hostname -I")
</code></pre>

<p>On MS Windows (tested) </p>

<pre><code>import socket

socket.gethostbyname(socket.gethostname())
</code></pre>
