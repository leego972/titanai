# Al-Azraq Treaty of 1245

"al-Azraq Our traitor" and "al-Azraq Our betrayer" since he had violated the terms of their surrender treaty. Al-Azraq returned to the Iberian Peninsula and al-Andulas in 1260 to help lead another Mudéjar revolt. He was killed at the Battle of Alcoi in 1276. The revolt of 1260 ended in much the same way as the one in 1247.
