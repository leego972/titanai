# Tobin Wolf

Theodore Walter "Tobin" Wolf (July 21, 1922 – June 21, 1999), also known as Ted Wolf, was an American writer who was known for creating the animated television series ThunderCats. He was also an inventor with several patents to his name. Wolf was born in 1922 in La Romana, Dominican Republic, and died in 1999, in Honolulu, Hawaii.
