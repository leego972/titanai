These compounds have antioxidant and free radical scavenging activities (<xref rid="bib68" ref-type="bibr">Li et al., 2012e</xref>). The flavonols in <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> include tricin (<bold>43</bold>), rutin (<bold>44</bold>), luteolin (<bold>45</bold>), quercetin (<bold>46</bold>), kaempferol (<bold>47</bold>), isoorientin (<bold>48</bold>), apigenin (<bold>49</bold>), hyperoside (<bold>50</bold>), vitexin (<bold>51</bold>) and quercetin-3-O-arabinoside (<bold>52</bold>) (<xref rid="bib175" ref-type="bibr">Xu et al., 2006</xref>, <xref rid="bib67" ref-type="bibr">Li and Lin, 1993</xref>, <xref rid="bib20" ref-type="bibr">Chen et al., 2000b</xref>, <xref rid="bib23" ref-type="bibr">Chen et al., 2001c</xref>). In addition, a novel flavonostilbene glycoside was isolated from <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> and identified as polygonflavanol A (<bold>53</bold>) (<xref rid="bib13" ref-type="bibr">Chen et al., 2012a</xref>).</p></sec><sec id="s0040"><label>4.4</label><title>Phospholipids</title><p id="p0080">
<italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> is rich in phospholipids and may be associated with the tonic effect of <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic>. These phospholipids include phosphatidyl ethanolamine (<bold>54</bold>), copaene (<bold>55</bold>), eicosane (<bold>56</bold>), hexanoic acid (<bold>57</bold>), hexadecanoic acid methyl ester (<bold>58</bold>), hexadecanoic acid ethyl ester (<bold>59</bold>), octadecanoic acid methyl ester (<bold>60</bold>), octadecanoic acid ethyl ester (<bold>61</bold>), ethyl oleate (<bold>62</bold>), docosanoic acid methyl ester (<bold>63</bold>), tetradecanoic acid ethyl ester (<bold>64</bold>), squalene (<bold>65</bold>) (<xref rid="bib23" ref-type="bibr">Chen et al., 2001c</xref>), 1,2-dihydroxynonadecone-3 (<bold>66</bold>) (<xref rid="bib20" ref-type="bibr">Chen et al., 2000b</xref>), 1-O-stearoyl-2-O-Δ<sup>4′,7′</sup>-dodecenoyl-3-O-phosphatidic acid-O-β-<sc>d</sc>-glucoside (<bold>67</bold>) and 1-O-stearoyl-2-O-Δ<sup>4′,7′</sup>-dodecenoyl-3-O-phosphatidic acid-O-(6″-O-α-<sc>d</sc>-glucoside)-β-<sc>d</sc>-glucoside (<bold>68</bold>) (<xref rid="bib21" ref-type="bibr">Chen et al., 2001b</xref>).</p></sec><sec id="s0045"><label>4.5</label><title>Other compounds</title><p id="p0085">There are also other components in <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic>, and these include the following polyphenolic compounds: catechin (<bold>69</bold>), epicatechin (<bold>70</bold>) (<xref rid="bib24" ref-type="bibr">Chen et al., 1999c</xref>), 3-O-galloyl-(-)-catechin (<bold>71</bold>), 3-O-galloyl-(-)-epicatechin (<bold>72</bold>), 3-O-galloyl-procyanidin B2 (<bold>73</bold>), 3,3′-di-O-galloyl-procyanidin B2 (<bold>74</bold>) (<xref rid="bib118" ref-type="bibr">Nonaka et al., 1982</xref>), gallic acid (<bold>75</bold>) (<xref rid="bib67" ref-type="bibr">Li and Lin, 1993</xref>) and methyl gallate (<bold>76</bold>) (<xref rid="bib185" ref-type="bibr">Yang et al., 1998</xref>).
