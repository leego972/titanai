A higher CI value indicates more uses of a species [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR31">31</xref>].</p></sec></sec><sec id="Sec6"><title>Results</title><sec id="Sec7"><title>Age and gender structure of the mastery of medical knowledge</title><p id="Par19">According to the survey, the number of medicinal materials that were mastered by men was much higher than that mastered by women (Fig. <xref rid="Fig2" ref-type="fig">2</xref>). The data showed that 299 herbs were provided by men, whereas only 128 herbs were provided by women, less than half of that provided by men. Additionally, men aged 61–80 years provided the most medicinal materials, whereas men aged 21–40 years provided the least (Fig. <xref rid="Fig2" ref-type="fig">2</xref>). Women aged 41–60 years provided the greatest amount of medicinal materials, whereas women aged 21–40 years provided the least (Fig. <xref rid="Fig2" ref-type="fig">2</xref>).
