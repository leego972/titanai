Question: <p>I know how to do this if I iterate through all of the characters in the string but I am looking for a more elegant method.</p>

<p>Thanks</p>

Answer: <p>You could always use a list comprehension and check the results with all, it would be a little less resource intensive than using a regex: <code>all([c in string.letters + string.digits + ["_", "-"] for c in mystring])</code></p>
