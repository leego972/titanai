The study was conducted on two groups of mice: a stressed group (<italic toggle="yes">n</italic> = 12) and a non-stressed group (<italic toggle="yes">n</italic> = 12), both receiving a placebo. The experimental timeline is reported in Fig. <xref rid="Fig2" ref-type="fig">2.1</xref>. During the first week (D1–D6), the mice were acclimated to the caretaker and trained to consume the gummies. On D6, a baseline anxiety level assessment was conducted using behavioral tests (EPM and OF tests). Starting on the eighth day (D8), all animals received a daily dose of the placebo. A second behavioral evaluation was performed on D19, prior to stress induction in order to evaluated to effect of exposition to gummies and daily manipulation. Between D20 and D26, stress was induced to the stressed group, while both groups continued to receive the placebo. On D26, a third and final anxiety level assessment was conducted using the same behavioral tests (EPM and OF tests).
