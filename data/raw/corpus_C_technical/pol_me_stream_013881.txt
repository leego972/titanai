# Marcin Kikut

Marcin Kikut (born 25 June 1983 in Barlinek) is a Polish former professional footballer who played as a defender or a midfielder.

Career
His former club was Amica Wronki. In August 2012, he joined Ruch Chorzów. Kikut was also a member of Poland U21 team. He made his debut for senior national team on 10 December 2010.

Honours
Lech Poznań
 Ekstraklasa: 2009–10
 Polish Cup: 2008–09
 Polish Super Cup: 2009
