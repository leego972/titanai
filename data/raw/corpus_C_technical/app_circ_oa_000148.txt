Title: A review of power converter topologies for wind generators

Abstract:
Wind energy conversion systems have become a focal point in the research of renewable energy<br> sources. This is in no small part due to the rapid advances in the size of wind generators as well as the development of power electronics and their applicability in wind energy extraction. This paper<br> provides a comprehensive review of past and present converter topologies applicable to permanent<br> magnet generators, induction generators, synchronous generators and doubly fed induction<br> generators. The many different generator–converter combinations are compared on the basis of<br> topology, cost, efficiency, power consumption and control complexity. The features of each<br> generator–converter configuration are considered in the context of wind turbine systems.<br>
