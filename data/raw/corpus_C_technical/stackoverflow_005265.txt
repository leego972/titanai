Question: <p>How do I convert a hex string to an int in Python? </p>

<p>I may have it as "<code>0xffff</code>" or just "<code>ffff</code>".</p>

Answer: <p><code>int(hexString, 16)</code> does the trick, and works with and without the 0x prefix:</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; int("a", 16)
10
&gt;&gt;&gt; int("0xa",16)
10
</code></pre>
