Grades II–III were combined in the analyses due to the low number of grade III lesions (<italic toggle="yes">n</italic> = 26).</p><p><italic toggle="yes">Treatment tolerability.</italic> Patient-estimated maximal pain during illumination was documented separately in AFXL-dPDT and dPDT treatments using a numerical rating scale (NRS) from 0 to 10. To further evaluate treatment tolerability, at the 6-month follow-up 37 patients were asked if they would agree to AFXL-dPDT treatment in the future. The first 19 patients were not asked this question, as it was added to the study protocol at a later stage.</p><p><italic toggle="yes">Aesthetic outcome.</italic> Hypopigmentation or hyperpigmentation 6 months after treatment was documented for 39 patients (the first 17 patients were not assessed for this).</p></sec><sec id="sec1.4"><title>Statistical analysis</title><p>Cross-tabulation and McNemar’s test were used for CC and PC analyses, and cross-tabulation and Pearson’s χ<sup>2</sup> for LSC analyses, adPDT-ndPDT comparison, and determination of confounding factors. The impacts of lesion grade or treatment history were determined using logistic regression. For pain calculations Wilcoxon signed-rank test was used. The analyses were performed by a trained statistician, with NCSS statistical software 12.0.17 (NCSS, Kaysville, UT, USA).
