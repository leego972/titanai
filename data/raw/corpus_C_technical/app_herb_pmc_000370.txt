Herbal extracts produced with phytoneering differ in quality and active substances but in terms of quality, safety and efficacy, these modern herbal medicines can be comparable to synthetically produced medicines, having much less unwanted side effects [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR80">80</xref>]. Specific herbal medicines such as BNO1016 extract, cineole and andrographis paniculata SHA-10 extract, have a significant effect on common cold symptoms without significant side effects. In post viral sinusitis, and CRS, BNO1016, may be a good alternative. Little by little herbal extracts gain their place among traditional medicines in Western world.
