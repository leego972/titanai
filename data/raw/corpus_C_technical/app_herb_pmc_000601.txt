Introduction</title><p>Since 1963, the fruits of <italic toggle="yes">Xanthium strumarium</italic> L. have been listed in the Pharmacopoeia of the People’s Republic of China (CH.P), and currently over 60 formulas containing the fruits of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> have been applied for treating various diseases, including rhinitis, nasal sinusitis, headache, gastric ulcer, urticarial, rheumatism, bacterial and fungal infections, and arthritis [<xref rid="B1-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>,<xref rid="B2-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B3-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>]. So far, many studies have been devoted to the pharmacological and phytochemical studies of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic>, and more than 170 chemical compounds have been isolated and identified from this plant, including sesquiterpene lactones, phenols, glycoside, alkaloids, fatty acid and others [<xref rid="B4-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">4</xref>]. In addition, increasing evidence has indicated that <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> possesses a wide spectrum of pharmacological activities including analgesic and anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, hypoglycemic, anti-cancer, antibacterial and antifungal, anti-trypanosomal, anti-tussive activities, and effects on nervous and digestive systems, as well as other effects [<xref rid="B1-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>]. Nowadays, the fruits of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> remains a common Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) listed in the CH.P, and atractyloside and chlorogenic acid are used as the quality indicator agents for evaluating quality of the fruits of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> [<xref rid="B5-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">5</xref>].</p><p>In this paper, we systematically summarize the traditional uses, botany, phytochemistry, pharmacology, pharmacokinetics as well as the safety aspects of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic>, hoping that it could propel the research forward for applying the medicinal values of this plant completely. Moreover, potential research directions and emphasis on <italic toggle="yes">Xanthium strumarium</italic> L. are discussed as well.</p></sec><sec id="sec2-molecules-24-00359"><title>2.
