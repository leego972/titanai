Low-level laser therapy in dentistry.

Sun G(1), Tunér J.

Author information:
(1)American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, 5401 World Dairy Drive, Madison, WI 
53718, USA. grace@sundds.com

Low-level laser therapy (LLLT) is a newly developing technique in dentistry, 
although it has been used among medical, dental, physiotherapy, and veterinary 
professions in some parts of the world for decades. LLLT can offer tremendous 
therapeutic benefits to patients, such as accelerated wound healing and pain 
relief. There is much to be learned about the mechanisms, recognition of the 
therapeutic window, and how to properly use these cellular phenomena to reach 
the treatment goals.
