Question: <p>How do I end a Tkinter program? Let's say I have this code:</p>

<pre><code>from Tkinter import *

def quit():
    # code to exit

root = Tk()
Button(root, text="Quit", command=quit).pack()
root.mainloop()
</code></pre>

<p>How should I define the <code>quit</code> function?</p>

Answer: <p>Illumination in case of confusion...</p>

<pre><code>def quit(self):
    self.destroy()
    exit()
</code></pre>

<p>A) destroy() stops the mainloop and kills the window, but leaves python running</p>

<p>B) exit() stops the whole process</p>

<p>Just to clarify in case someone missed what destroy() was doing, and the OP also asked how to "end" a tkinter program.</p>
