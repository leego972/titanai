Description:
2,5-dichloro-2,5-cyclohexadiene-1,4-diol is a cyclohexadienediol that is cyclohexa-2,5-diene-1,4-diol in which positions 2 and 5 are substituted by chlorines. It has a role as a bacterial xenobiotic metabolite. It is a cyclohexadienediol, an organic hydroxy compound and an organochlorine compound.
