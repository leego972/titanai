Question: <p>How would you go about finding out how much memory is being used by an object? I know it is possible to find out how much is used by a block of code, but not by an instantiated object (anytime during its life), which is what I want. </p>

Answer: <p>Try this:</p>

<pre><code>sys.getsizeof(object)
</code></pre>

<p><a href="https://docs.python.org/3/library/sys.html#sys.getsizeof" rel="nofollow">getsizeof()</a> calls the objectâs <code>__sizeof__</code> method and adds an additional garbage collector overhead <strong>if</strong> the object is managed by the garbage collector.</p>
