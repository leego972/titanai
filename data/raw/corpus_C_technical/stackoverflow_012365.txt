Question: <p>I would like to wipe out all data for a specific kind in Google App Engine. What is the
best way to do this?
I wrote a delete script (hack), but since there is so much data is
timeout's out after a few hundred records. </p>

Answer: <p>One tip. I suggest you get to know the <a href="http://code.google.com/appengine/articles/remote%5Fapi.html" rel="nofollow">remote_api</a> for these types of uses (bulk deleting, modifying, etc.).  But, even with the remote api, batch size can be limited to a few hundred at a time.</p>
