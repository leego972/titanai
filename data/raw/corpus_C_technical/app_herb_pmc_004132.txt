# PMC: adaptogen stress response HPA axis — Overview

ref-type="bibr">5</xref> It indicates that after half a century of development, the new concept of adaptogens has been officially recognized. Finally, in 2018, Panossian et al summarized “adaptogen” as a natural compound or plant extract that can improve the adaptability and survival of an organism under stress.<xref rid="cit0006" ref-type="bibr">6</xref> Compared to the previous definitions, this one is more explicit about the properties and medicinal results of adaptogens. Plant adaptogens are part of the classes of adaptogens.
