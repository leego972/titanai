This comprehensive narrative Review underscores the diverse health benefits of Ashwagandha, emphasizing its roles in immunomodulation, stress reduction, antioxidant effects, cardioprotection, anti-inflammatory, anti-tumor, and anti-cancer activities, neuroprotection, memory enhancement, cognitive function, physical performance, and sexual health improvement effects. Also, Ashwagandha has physical performance benefits, which indicate its potential to increase muscle strength, facilitate muscle regeneration, and improve cardiorespiratory endurance.
