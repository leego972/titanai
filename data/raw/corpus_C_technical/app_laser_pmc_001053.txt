For Logmar, VAD was determined as the best model, followed by GCIPL, VLD and FD. For Sloan 2.5% LCVA, mGCIPL was the strongest model, followed by VAD, VLD and FD, which were all stronger than pRNFL. It was conducted on a large cohort of predominantly relapsing patients and examined both optic neuritis and non-optic neuritis eyes. We compared associations found with OCTA against conventional OCT metrics to help understand cerebral damage mechanisms and clinical outcomes in MS and ascertain any complementary value of OCTA over OCT as a biomarker.</p><sec id="fcag007-s4.1"><title>Retinal microvasculature changes in multiple sclerosis compared with healthy controls</title><p>We observed abnormal retinal microvasculature in our POINT-MS cohort compared to age- and sex-matched healthy controls, as well as between optic neuritis and non-optic neuritis eyes, reinforcing previous studies that have reported reduced vessel density and altered vascular architecture in MS, independent of optic neuritis.<sup><xref rid="fcag007-B9" ref-type="bibr">9</xref>,<xref rid="fcag007-B11" ref-type="bibr">11</xref>,<xref rid="fcag007-B33" ref-type="bibr">33</xref></sup> Intrathecal immunity research suggests that retinal microvasculature rarefication correlates with increased activated B cells and pro-inflammatory cytokines in cerebrospinal fluid, indicating OCTA’s potential utility in detecting in vivo neuroinflammation.<sup><xref rid="fcag007-B34" ref-type="bibr">34</xref></sup> Moreover, previous studies have demonstrated distinct retinal vessel loss patterns between optic neuritis and non-optic neuritis eyes.
