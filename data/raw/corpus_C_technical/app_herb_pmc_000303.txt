Zhang et al. found that periplocin inhibited the proliferation of SMMC-7721 cells and arrested them at the G2/M phase by inhibiting Stat3 signal transduction in human hepatic cells [<xref rid="B54-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">54</xref>]. As a potential antitumor component, periplocin also showed an inhibition effect on BT-549 and TE-13, which suppressed proliferation and induced apoptosis of these cells significantly [<xref rid="B55-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">55</xref>,<xref rid="B56-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">56</xref>]. Cardiotonic Action</title><p>In this plant’s traditional usages, one important application is cardiotonic action. The main compounds exhibiting cardiotonic action are cardiac glycosides. Li et al. used the isolated heart perfusion technique to evaluate the cardiotonic action of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> [<xref rid="B57-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">57</xref>]. The study of Li et al. indicated the <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> extracts significantly increased LVSP and decreased LVEDP, which demonstrated that <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> may have a cardiac effect. Ma et al. found periplocin could improve the structure and function of the left ventricle in chronic heart failure in rats [<xref rid="B58-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">58</xref>]. Xie et al. proposed that cardiotonic glycosides shared the capacity to bind to the extra-cellular surface of the main ion transport protein in the cell [<xref rid="B59-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">59</xref>]. Therefore, the mechanism of its cardiotonic action is should be explored to promote clinical administration.</p></sec><sec id="sec5dot4-molecules-21-01702"><title>5.4. Effects on the Nervous System</title><p>Sakuma et al. suggested that glycoside K, glycoside H1, and glycoside H2 which were identified from the <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> show strong potentiation of NGF-mediated nerve fiber outgrowth in chicken embryonic dorsal root and sympathetic ganglia, especially glycoside H2. These glycosides were consequently expected to have an impressive effect on the neuronal system [<xref rid="B18-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">18</xref>].</p></sec><sec id="sec5dot5-molecules-21-01702"><title>5.5. Insecticidal Action</title><p>Zhao et al. isolated a highly potent insecticidal compound—called periplocoside NW (PSNW)—from <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> by bioactivity-guided screening method [<xref rid="B60-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">60</xref>]. They found cell damage was caused by PSNW in the midgut of <italic toggle="yes">Mythimna separata</italic> larvae. The results of insecticidal bioassay and immunoelectron microscopic localization showed that some gold particles appeared on the microvilli layer of the midgut of <italic toggle="yes">M. separata</italic> larvae, and accumulated gradually over time until it was destroyed. Therefore, they concluded that the mechanism might be related to the degeneration of brush border microvilli, and finally periplocoside NW was applied against insects. Shi et al. found that <italic toggle="yes">n</italic>-butyl alcohol extracts from <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> could kill <italic toggle="yes">Sohizaphis graminum</italic> effectively and possessed some stomach poison against <italic toggle="yes">Mythimna separata</italic> larvae. Hui et al. separated some active components from <italic toggle="yes">Periploca</italic> methyl alcohol extraction, which exhibited significant repellent activity for <italic toggle="yes">Soknopsis invicta</italic> [<xref rid="B61-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">61</xref>,<xref rid="B62-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">62</xref>].</p></sec></sec><sec id="sec6-molecules-21-01702"><title>6. Antirheumatic Effect</title><p>From time immemorial, <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> has been regarded as an available medicine to treat rheumatism. Because of its peculiarity, it is used as an external preparation, such as Huoxuezhitong sticking and Tianhezhuifeng sticking. Additionally, some medicinal liquors including <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> show distinct antirheumatic action.</p></sec><sec id="sec6dot2-molecules-21-01702"><title>6.2.
