# Centurians of Rome

of Rome is a 1981 gay pornographic film made during the Golden Age of Porn and directed by John Christopher, starring George Payne and Scorpio. The project, released by Hand In Hand Films, is notable as the most expensive gay porno at the time of its release, financed by George Bosque, later convicted of stealing almost $2 million.
