On the contrary, the systems biology and network pharmacology concepts provide ideal mechanistic tools for understanding and conceptualizing adaptogen modes and mechanisms of action. Adaptation to environmental challenges and senescence are multistep processes that involve diverse mechanisms and molecular interactions. Many molecular networks regulate and harmonize intracellular and extracellular communications, metabolic regulation of homeostasis of various cells, and physiological systems.</p><p>Multiple molecular targets of adaptogens, molecular networks, and adaptive stress response signaling pathways have recently been identified [<xref rid="B5-pharmaceuticals-15-01051" ref-type="bibr">5</xref>,<xref rid="B6-pharmaceuticals-15-01051" ref-type="bibr">6</xref>,<xref rid="B7-pharmaceuticals-15-01051" ref-type="bibr">7</xref>,<xref rid="B8-pharmaceuticals-15-01051" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>,<xref rid="B9-pharmaceuticals-15-01051" ref-type="bibr">9</xref>,<xref rid="B10-pharmaceuticals-15-01051" ref-type="bibr">10</xref>,<xref rid="B11-pharmaceuticals-15-01051" ref-type="bibr">11</xref>,<xref rid="B12-pharmaceuticals-15-01051" ref-type="bibr">12</xref>,<xref rid="B13-pharmaceuticals-15-01051" ref-type="bibr">13</xref>,<xref rid="B14-pharmaceuticals-15-01051" ref-type="bibr">14</xref>,<xref rid="B15-pharmaceuticals-15-01051" ref-type="bibr">15</xref>]. They are associated with chronic inflammation, atherosclerosis, neurodegenerative cognitive impairment, metabolic disorders, and cancer, which are more common with age [<xref rid="B9-pharmaceuticals-15-01051" ref-type="bibr">9</xref>].</p><p>Overall, adaptogens trigger <bold><italic toggle="yes">pleiotropic</italic></bold> genes, molecular mechanisms, and cellular signaling pathways that mediate adaptive and defense responses, resulting in multitarget modes of action simultaneously and, therefore, in <bold><italic toggle="yes">nonspecific pleiotropic</italic></bold> pharmacological activity.</p><p>Pleiotropy is the result of the effect of adaptogen on a single gene that impacts multiple signaling pathways, biological processes, physiological functions, and phenotype characteristics. Various cells use the gene transcription mechanism that triggers numerous downstream signaling pathways and molecular networks that collectively affect multiple molecular targets, resulting in many pharmacological activities (nonspecific effect).</p><p>Pharmacological activities of adaptogens (<xref rid="pharmaceuticals-15-01051-t001" ref-type="table">Table 1</xref>) depend on their
