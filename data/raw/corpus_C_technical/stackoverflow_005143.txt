Question: <p>For a website like reddit with lots of up/down votes and lots of comments per topic what should I go with?</p>

<p>Lighttpd/Php or Lighttpd/CherryPy/Genshi/SQLAlchemy?</p>

<p>and for database what would scale better / be fastest MySQL ( 4.1 or 5 ? ) or PostgreSQL?</p>

Answer: <p>I can't speak to the MySQL/PostgreSQL question as I have limited experience with Postgres, but my Masters research project was about high-performance websites with CherryPy, and I don't think you'll be disappointed if you use CherryPy for your site.  It can easily scale to thousands of simultaneous users on commodity hardware.</p>

<p>Of course, the same could be said for PHP, and I don't know of any reasonable benchmarks comparing PHP and CherryPy performance.  But if you were wondering whether CherryPy can handle a high-traffic site with a huge number of requests per second, the answer is definitely yes.</p>
