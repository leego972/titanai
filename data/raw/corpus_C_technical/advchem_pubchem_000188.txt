CID: 378
Source: ChEBI

Description:
4-nitrophenyl phosphate is an aryl phosphate resulting from the mono-esterification of phosphoric acid with 4-nitrophenol. It has a role as a mouse metabolite. It is functionally related to a 4-nitrophenol. It is a conjugate acid of a 4-nitrophenyl phosphate(2-).
