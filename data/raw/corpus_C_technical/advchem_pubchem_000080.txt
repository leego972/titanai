Source: ChEBI

Description:
8-amino-7-oxononanoic acid is the 8-amino-7-oxo derivative of nonanoic acid. It has a role as a Saccharomyces cerevisiae metabolite. It is an amino acid and a 7-oxo monocarboxylic acid. It is functionally related to a nonanoic acid. It is a conjugate acid of an 8-amino-7-oxononanoate. It is a tautomer of an 8-amino-7-oxononanoic acid zwitterion.
