They concluded that most water-soluble phytochemicals such as flavonoids may lack antimicrobial significance and phenolic compounds may only be significant as antioxidants. In addition, it was indicated that even though water is a universal solvent that is widely used by traditional healers, organic solvents such as methanol, ethanol and hexane have been known to extract more of the bioactive constituents or antibacterial agents and hence, they give consistent antimicrobial activity than water. It was observed in the current study that most extracts and/or oil with exception to the methanol extract had large zones of inhibition against gram-positive strains as opposed to gram-negative and thus, they were more potent towards the gram-positive bacteria.
