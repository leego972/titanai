Recent phytochemical analyses of <italic toggle="yes">C. japonica</italic> leaves have identified a variety of bioactive compounds, with phenolic compounds being the most abundant, along with carotenoids, terpenoids and fatty acids. These molecules exhibit a range of biological activities, including antioxidant, antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and anticancer effects. Nonetheless, certain constituents, such as saponins, triterpenes, and tannins, may exhibit anti-nutritional or mild toxic effects under specific conditions. This review specifically examines the bioactive compounds found in <italic toggle="yes">C. japonica</italic> leaves and their associated health benefits.
