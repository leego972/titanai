Clinical and preclinical photodynamic therapy.

Fisher AM(1), Murphree AL, Gomer CJ.

Author information:
(1)Clayton Ocular Oncology Center, Childrens Hospital Los Angeles, California 
90027, USA.

Photodynamic therapy (PDT) is a treatment modality that utilizes a 
photosensitizing drug activated by laser generated light, and is proving 
effective for oncologic and nononcologic applications. This report provides an 
overview of photosensitizers, photochemistry, photobiology, and the lasers 
involved in photodynamic therapy. Clinical and preclinical PDT studies involving 
Photofrin and various second generation photosensitizers are reviewed.
