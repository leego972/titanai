Conclusions</title><p>In this review, we focused on miRNA expression after LLLT and PDT. As mentioned above, only a few papers have been published regarding miRNA expression in this context, and those few reports discuss only a small number of laser therapy conditions. The ability of LLLT to induce growth-factor production, inhibition of inflammation, stimulation of angiogenesis, pain reduction and direct effects on stem cells suggests that there is an urgent need to combine this modality with regenerative medicine. PDT has been employed in the treatment of many tumor types, and its effectiveness as a curative and palliative treatment is well documented, especially in the context of skin cancer.
