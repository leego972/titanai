Description:
Amodiaquine is a quinoline having a chloro group at the 7-position and an aryl amino group at the 4-position. It has a role as an EC 2.1.1.8 (histamine N-methyltransferase) inhibitor, an anticoronaviral agent, a non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug, an antimalarial, a prodrug and a drug allergen. It is a member of phenols, an organochlorine compound, an aminoquinoline, a secondary amino compound and a tertiary amino compound. It is a conjugate base of an amodiaquine(1+).
