Question: <p>How can I output colored text to the terminal, in Python?
What is the best Unicode symbol to represent a solid block?</p>

Answer: <p>uses ANSI  </p>

<pre><code>RED = '\033[91m'
GREEN = '\033[92m'
YELLOW = '\033[93m'
LIGHT_PURPLE = '\033[94m'
PURPLE = '\033[95m'
END = '\033[0m'
</code></pre>

<p>Make your function :-</p>

<pre><code>def red(name):
    print ("\033[91m {}\033[00m" .format(name))
</code></pre>

<p>Call function :- </p>

<blockquote>
  <p>red("Good one")
  Good one  &lt;--  It will print in Red , </p>
</blockquote>

<p>Note :- not required any module </p>
