The extracts were well tolerated and no side effects were observed [<xref rid="B31-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">31</xref>,<xref rid="B32-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">32</xref>].</p><p>However, the most convincing evidences of the efficacy of adaptogens were found in studies related to its neuro-protective effects, effects on cognitive functions and mental performance in fatigue, and on its efficacy in asthenia and depression [<xref rid="B23-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">23</xref>]. The evidence points to adaptogens may be beneficial on neurodegenerative disorders [<xref rid="B54-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">54</xref>,<xref rid="B83-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">83</xref>,<xref rid="B84-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">84</xref>,<xref rid="B89-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">89</xref>,<xref rid="B135-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">135</xref>,<xref rid="B141-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">141</xref>].</p><sec><title>4.1. Adaptogens in Fatigue, Effect on Cognitive Functions</title><p>In total, more than 30 publications on the clinical efficacy of various <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> preparations can be found in the PubMed database. The majority of these studies are of varying methodological rigor and concern cognitive functions and mental performance under fatigue, <xref ref-type="table" rid="pharmaceuticals-03-00188-t005">Table 5</xref>.</p><p>The clinical trials using <italic toggle="yes">Schisandra</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> (13 studies) and <italic toggle="yes">Eleutherococcus</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">senticosus</italic> (11 studies) on mental performance in humans have been the subject of a recent review [<xref rid="B23-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">23</xref>]. A systematic review showed that adaptogens have a significant, beneficial and specific effect on stress-induced symptoms under fatigue [<xref rid="B23-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">23</xref>]. It was seen that <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic>, in particular, significantly reduced symptoms of fatigue and improved attention after four weeks of repeated administration [<xref rid="B122-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">122</xref>]. Furthermore, it was suggested that the inhibitory effect of <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> on the increased basal level of salivary cortisol results in an improvement of cognitive function. This is in line with other studies demonstrating that optimal corticosteroid levels are a requirement for efficient cognitive function. It has been shown that significant changes (upregulation or downregulation) of circulating levels of corticosteroids are associated with cognitive impairment [<xref rid="B168-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">168</xref>]. Moreover, studies on healthy volunteers receiving single and repeated doses of SHR-5 extract (<italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic>) have demonstrated an anti-fatigue effect and improvement in cognitive functions during fatigue and in stressful conditions [<xref rid="B143-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">143</xref>,<xref rid="B144-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">144</xref>,<xref rid="B145-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">145</xref>].
