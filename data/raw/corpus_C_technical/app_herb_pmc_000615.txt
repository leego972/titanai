Pharmacokinetics</title><p>Up to now, there are few reports on the pharmacokinetics of the extracts or monomers of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic>. Previous pharmacokinetics studies of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> mainly focused on its active compounds including xanthatin, cryptochlorogenic acid, and toxic ingredient such as atractyloside. In 2014, a sensitive, specific and rapid ultra-high performance liquid chromatography (UHPLC) tandem mass spectrometry (UHPLC-MS/MS) method was applied to research pharmacokinetic properties of xanthatin in rat plasma. After intravenous injection of xanthatin at a dose of 2.4 mg/200 g, 4.8 mg/200 g and 9.6 mg/200 g, respectively. The t<sub>1/2</sub> of three concentrations were found to be 108.58 ± 32.82, 123.50 ± 66.69, and 181.71 ± 148.26 min, respectively; and the peak plasma concentration (C<sub>max</sub>) values were 418.72 ± 137.51, 904.89 ± 193.53, and 1773.46 ± 1733.10 ng/mL, respectively. As the dose increased, the AUC<sub>0–t</sub> and AUC<sub>0–∞</sub> were gradually enlarged, and the AUC<sub>0–t</sub> of three doses were 14,340.20 ± 7122.41, 32,149.52 ± 11,259.44, and 49,524.28 ± 28,520.88 n gh/mL, respectively; furthermore, the AUC<sub>0–∞</sub> of three levels are 15,538.97 ± 7733.12, 36,431.22 ± 14,498.16, and 61,885.45 ± 30,704.80 n gh/mL, respectively. In addition, the total body CL were 0.13 ± 0.14, 0.17 ± 0.11, 0.22 ± 0.13 mL/min and V<sub>d</sub> were 46.85 ± 20.19, 159.99 ± 30.49, and 208.22 ± 85.97 mL of three concentrations [<xref rid="B134-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">134</xref>].</p><p>After intragastric administration of the atractyloside at doses of 11.4, 22.8, and 45.6 mg/kg, the peak time (T<sub>max</sub>) values were determined to be 0.38, 1.85, 0.27 h, respectively, the t<sub>1/2</sub> were 13.64, 9.62, 8.61 h, respectively, and the peak plasma concentration (C<sub>max</sub>) values were 41.98, 24.61, 263.40 µg/mL, respectively. In addition, the area under the concentration-time curve (AUC) was also determined, and the AUC<sub>0–t</sub> was 132.70, 222.90, and 345.20 µ gh/L. The results showed that the toxicokinetic behavior of atractyloside in rats was non-linear within the experimental dose range [<xref rid="B135-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">135</xref>]. </p><p>Furthermore, Shen et al. studied the pharmacokinetics of neochlorogenic acid and cryptochlorogenic acid in <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> and its processed products after intragastric administration in rats. The results showed that the T<sub>max</sub> of neochlorogenic acid and cryptochlorogenic acid in processed fruits of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> were 2.94 ± 0.18, and 3.00 ± 0.46 h, respectively; the t<sub>1/2</sub> of neochlorogenic acid and cryptochlorogenic acid in processed fruits of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> were 2.35 ± 1.11, 1.97 ± 0.66 h.
