Progress, Trends, Pitfalls, and Challenges in the Adaptogens Research</title><p>Adaptogens are botanicals that enhance adaptability, resilience, resistance, and survival in response to stress-induced and aging-related disorders [<xref rid="B2-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B3-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>].</p><p>The term adaptogen is derived from adaptation, which is coined for the physiological process of adaptability [<xref rid="B6-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">6</xref>,<xref rid="B7-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">7</xref>], <xref rid="pharmaceuticals-18-01346-f001" ref-type="fig">Figure 1</xref>, of the organism to repeated action of the botanical that triggers adaptive stress response via intracellular and extracellular adaptive signaling pathways and networks in neuroendocrine-immune, cardiovascular, and gastrointestinal systems and resulting in increased adaptability, resilience, resistance, and survival in stress-induced and aging-related disorders [<xref rid="B1-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>,<xref rid="B2-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B3-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>].</p><p>Adaptogens play a similar role in defending the plant against environmental challenges, including viruses, harmful bacteria, insect-borne diseases, excessive UV rays, and the physiological ravages of chronic stress [<xref rid="B3-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>].</p><p>Initially, the postulated definition of adaptogens was modified several times (<xref rid="pharmaceuticals-18-01346-t002" ref-type="table">Table 2</xref>), as our understanding of their mode and mechanisms of action, and the progress in biomedical sciences over the last few decades, improved [<xref rid="B1-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>,<xref rid="B2-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B3-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>]. Witnessing the progress in stress research, the evolution of the adaptogen concept and milestones of adaptogen research [<xref rid="B59-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">59</xref>,<xref rid="B60-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">60</xref>] reflects scientific knowledge to new findings over time: from discoveries of hormones, neurotransmitters, molecular chaperones (heat shock proteins), neuropeptide NPY, eicosanoids, and other mediators of intracellular and extracellular communications in response to adaptive hormetic response, to network pharmacology of OMICs and systems biology concepts [<xref rid="B1-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>,<xref rid="B2-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B3-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>].</p><table-wrap position="anchor" id="pharmaceuticals-18-01346-t002" orientation="portrait"><object-id pub-id-type="pii">pharmaceuticals-18-01346-t002_Table 2</object-id><label>Table 2</label><caption><p>Definitions of adaptogens *.</p></caption><table frame="hsides" rules="groups"><tbody><tr><td align="left" valign="middle" style="border-top:solid thin;border-bottom:solid thin" rowspan="1" colspan="1">• Adaptogens are medicinal substances causing the “state of nonspecifically increased resistance” of the organism.<break/>• Only those preparations that meet the following requirements may be included in the group of adaptogens: (a) An adaptogen should be innocuous and cause minimal disorders in the physiological functions of an organism; (b) The action of an adaptogen should be nonspecific, i.e., it should increase resistance to adverse influences of a wide range of factors of physical, chemical, and biological nature, (c) An adaptogen may possess normalizing action irrespective of the direction of the foregoing pathologic changes.<break/>• The adaptogens are nontoxic compounds with polyvalent mechanisms of action and pharmacological effects related to adaptability and survival.<break/>• Adaptogens are substances that elicit in an organism a state of nonspecifically raised resistance, allowing them to counteract stressor signals and to adapt to exceptional strain.<break/>• Adaptogens are metabolic regulators that increase the ability of an organism to adapt to environmental factors and to avoid damage from such factors.<break/>• Plant adaptogens are agents that reduce the damaging effects of various stressors due to the reduction in the reactivity of the host defense system.
