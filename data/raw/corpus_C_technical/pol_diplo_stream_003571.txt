# Northside School of Innovation, Technology, and Entrepreneurship

Northside School of Innovation, Technology, and Entrepreneurship (formerly Business Careers High School)  is a business and technology magnet high school part of the Northside Independent School District in San Antonio, Texas.  It is a "school within a school" located on the campus of Oliver Wendell Holmes High School.
