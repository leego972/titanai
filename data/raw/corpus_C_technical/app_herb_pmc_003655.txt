2021</meta-value></custom-meta></custom-meta-group></article-meta></front><body><sec id="Sec1"><title>Background</title><p id="Par11">In most developing countries, medicinal plants constitute the main materia medica for 70 to 95% of citizens [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR1">1</xref>–<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR3">3</xref>]. Because of outdated medical facilities and unaffordable medical expenses, traditional medicinal plants have become the first choice or supplement for medical alternatives in most developing countries [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR4">4</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR5">5</xref>]. In developed countries, increasing numbers of people are also utilizing traditional medicinal plants to treat diseases. With today’s globalized development, traditional medicine has kept pace with the times, which is of great value for the protection of human health. In 2015, for example, Tu Youyou won the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for the discovery of artemisinin, an extract from traditional Chinese medicine (TCM) <italic toggle="yes">Artemisia annua</italic> L. In 2018, China’s Tibetan medicinal bathing was listed as Intangible Cultural Heritage. To prevent COVID-19, the National Health Protection Commission of China had written the proprietary Chinese medicine Lianhua Qingwen Capsule and Qingfei Paidu Decoction into the guidelines for clinical diagnosis and treatment. All these cases have shown the important role played by traditional medicine in modern society. At the same time, Chinese ethnic medicines have also attracted the attention of researchers [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR6">6</xref>–<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR11">11</xref>].</p><p id="Par12">Furthermore, as the value of medicinal plants has risen, the traditional market has become an important source of income for citizens. Many studies of traditional herbal markets have been made conducted, such as the herbal markets in Africa and Europe [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR12">12</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR13">13</xref>], and Hunan [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR14">14</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR15">15</xref>], and Yunnan Provinces in China [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR16">16</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR17">17</xref>].
