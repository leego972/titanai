It is very likely that this herbal extract is taken in combination with conventional drugs. The present study aimed to evaluate the effects of <italic toggle="yes">H. helix</italic> extract on cytochrome P450 (CYP) enzyme-mediated metabolism to predict the potential for herb–drug interactions. A cocktail probe assay was used to measure the inhibitory effect of CYP. <italic toggle="yes">H. helix</italic> extracts were incubated with pooled human liver microsomes or CYP isozymes with CYP-specific substrates, and the formation of specific metabolites was investigated to measure the inhibitory effects. <italic toggle="yes">H. helix</italic> showed significant inhibitory effects on CYP2C8, CYP2C19 and CYP2D6 in a concentration-dependent manner. In recombinant CYP2C8, CYP2C19 and CYP2D6 isozymes, the IC<sub>50</sub> values of the extract were 0.08 ± 0.01, 0.58 ± 0.03 and 6.72 ± 0.22 mg/mL, respectively. Further investigation showed that <italic toggle="yes">H. helix</italic> extract has a positive time-dependent inhibition property on both CYP2C8 and CYP2C19 with IC<sub>50</sub> shift value of 2.77 ± 0.12 and 6.31 ± 0.25, respectively. Based on this in vitro investigation, consumption of herbal medicines or dietary supplements containing <italic toggle="yes">H. helix</italic> extracts requires careful attention to avoid any
