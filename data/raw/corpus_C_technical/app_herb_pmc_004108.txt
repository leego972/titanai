Discussion</title><p>The collected evidence indicates that plant adaptogens act through pleiotropic and multiaxial modulation of the organism’s stress response, which clearly distinguishes them from classical drugs targeting single molecular entities [<xref rid="B2-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B3-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>]. A key component of their activity is normalization of hypothalamic–pituitary–adrenal (HPA) axis function and integration of neuroendocrine, immune, and metabolic responses, leading to restoration of homeostatic balance under both acute and chronic stress conditions [<xref rid="B2-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B54-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">54</xref>,<xref rid="B66-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">66</xref>].</p><p>At the molecular level, a common denominator of adaptogenic activity is modulation of the PI3K/Akt, AMPK/SIRT1/PGC-1α, and Nrf2/NF-κB signaling pathways [<xref rid="B3-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>,<xref rid="B8-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>,<xref rid="B29-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">29</xref>,<xref rid="B61-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">61</xref>,<xref rid="B72-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">72</xref>]. This results in improved mitochondrial function, enhanced efficiency of energy metabolism, and attenuation of oxidative stress and neuroinflammation, which has been demonstrated in both preclinical and clinical models [<xref rid="B13-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">13</xref>,<xref rid="B33-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">33</xref>,<xref rid="B53-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">53</xref>,<xref rid="B72-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">72</xref>]. These mechanisms are directly reflected in the neuroprotective, antidepressant, anxiolytic, and anti-fatigue effects observed in experimental and human studies [<xref rid="B26-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">26</xref>,<xref rid="B39-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">39</xref>,<xref rid="B53-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">53</xref>,<xref rid="B69-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">69</xref>,<xref rid="B76-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">76</xref>].</p><p>An important contribution to the long-term effects of adaptogens is the induction of heat shock proteins, particularly Hsp70, and the associated phenomenon of cellular hormesis [<xref rid="B1-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>,<xref rid="B14-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">14</xref>]. Induction of Hsp70 and activation of Nrf2-dependent antioxidant responses have been described for Schisandra chinensis and Rhodiola rosea, supporting mitochondrial integrity and proteostasis under conditions of oxidative challenge [<xref rid="B8-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>,<xref rid="B14-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">14</xref>,<xref rid="B16-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">16</xref>]. These processes increase cellular resistance to damage and promote maintenance of tissue functional stability. At the same time, adaptogens differ in their molecular points of action, as shown by the selective modulation of PDE4D by withanolides [<xref rid="B38-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">38</xref>], FKBP51 and glucocorticoid receptors by ginsenosides [<xref rid="B54-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">54</xref>,<xref rid="B55-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">55</xref>], or cholinergic neurotransmission by bacosides [<xref rid="B72-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">72</xref>,<xref rid="B76-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">76</xref>].</p><p>Network pharmacology studies further confirm that the efficacy of adaptogens arises from the synergistic action of multiple bioactive compounds that collectively modulate complex gene and protein networks, rather than from the activity of single isolated constituents [<xref rid="B3-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>,<xref rid="B4-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">4</xref>]. Experimental validation of such synergistic transcriptional effects has been demonstrated for multicomponent adaptogenic preparations [<xref rid="B4-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">4</xref>].</p><p>Nevertheless, heterogeneity in extract composition and differences in standardization procedures, dosing regimens, and experimental models remain significant limitations of the available evidence [<xref rid="B2-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B3-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>,<xref rid="B26-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">26</xref>].
