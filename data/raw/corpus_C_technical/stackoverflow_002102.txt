Question: <p>I'm a little confused about how the standard library will behave now that Python (from 3.0) is unicode-based. Will modules such as CGI and urllib use unicode strings or will they use the new 'bytes' type and just provide encoded data?</p>

Answer: <p>There will be a two-step dance here.  See <a href="http://www.artima.com/weblogs/viewpost.jsp?thread=227041" rel="nofollow">Python 3000 and You</a>.</p>

<p>Step 1 is to get running under 3.0.</p>

<p>Step 2 is to rethink your API's to, perhaps, do something more sensible.</p>

<p>The most likely course is that the libraries will switch to unicode strings to remain as compatible as possible with how they used to work.</p>

<p>Then, perhaps, some will switch to bytes to more properly implement the RFC standards for the various protocols.</p>
