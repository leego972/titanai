# FK Sloga Meridian

Fudbalski klub Sloga Doboj (), known as Sloga Meridian for sponsorship reasons, is an association football club from the city of Doboj in northern Republika Srpska an entity of Bosnia and Herzegovina. The club competes in the Premier League of Bosnia and Herzegovina and plays its home matches at Stadion Luke, which has a capacity of 3,000.
