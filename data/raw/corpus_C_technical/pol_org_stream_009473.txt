# Francismar

Francismar Carioca de Oliveira (born 18 April 1984) is a Brazilian association footballer who plays for Ipatinga.

Career 
On 6 March 2009 the attacking midfielder, has left Esporte Clube Juventude to join Atlético Goianiense.

Club statistics

Achievements
 Campeonato Mineiro: 2006
 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D: 2014
