Answer: <p>You may also be interested in the GEOS library, which is available in Python through <a href="http://trac.gispython.org/lab/wiki/Shapely" rel="nofollow">Shapely</a> and <a href="http://geodjango.org/docs/geos.html" rel="nofollow">the GEOS API included in GeoDjango</a>.</p>
