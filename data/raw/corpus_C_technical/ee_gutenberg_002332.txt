ROSANILINE Ros*an"i*line ( or ), n. Etym: [Rose + aniline.] (Chem.) Defn: A complex nitrogenous base, C20H21N3O, obtained by oxidizing a mixture of aniline and toluidine, as a colorless crystalline substance which forms red salts.
