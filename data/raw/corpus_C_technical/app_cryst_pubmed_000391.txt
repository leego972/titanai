The COVID-19 pandemic has been disastrous to society and effective drugs are 
urgently needed. The papain-like protease domain (PLpro) of SARS-CoV-2 (SCoV2) 
is indispensable for viral replication and represents a putative target for 
pharmacological intervention. In this work, we describe the development of a 
potent and selective SCoV2 PLpro inhibitor, 19. The inhibitor not only 
effectively blocks substrate cleavage and immunosuppressive function imparted by 
PLpro, but also markedly mitigates SCoV2 replication in human cells, with a 
submicromolar IC50. We further present a convenient and sensitive activity 
probe, 7, and complementary assays to readily evaluate SCoV2 PLpro inhibitors 
in vitro or in cells. In addition, we disclose the co-crystal structure of SCoV2 
PLpro in complex with a prototype inhibitor, which illuminates their detailed 
binding mode. Overall, these findings provide promising leads and important 
tools for drug discovery aiming to target SCoV2 PLpro.
