Question: <p>How do I read text from the (windows) clipboard from python?</p>

Answer: <p>Use Pythons library <a href="https://pypi.python.org/pypi/clipboard/0.0.4" rel="nofollow">Clipboard </a> </p>

<p>Its simply used like this:</p>

<pre><code>import clipboard
clipboard.copy("this text is now in the clipboard")
print clipboard.paste()  
</code></pre>
