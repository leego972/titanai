<p>"^LThis is an example ^Gstring with multiple ^Jcharacter encodings"</p>

<ul>

<li>^J - Japanese</li>
<li>^C - Cyrillic</li>

<p>And so on... I need a way to convert this sort of string into Unicode, but I'm really not sure how to do it.
