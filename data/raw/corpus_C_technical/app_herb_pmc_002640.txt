</disp-formula></p></sec><sec id="sec008"><title>Total phenolics content (TPC)</title><p>TPC was measured using the Folin-Ciocalteu (FC) method, as described by [<xref rid="pone.0310298.ref015" ref-type="bibr">15</xref>]. Specifically: 50 μL of EA-AS was pipetted into test tubes and adjusted to 1 mL by D water. 0.5 mL of 1 N FC reagent was added to each tube, including the blank, and allowed to stand for 5 minutes at 25°C. Then, 2.5 mL of 5% Na<sub>2</sub>CO<sub>3</sub> sol was added. After 25°C for 40 minutes absorbance was measured at 725 nm. Total phenolic content was expressed as micrograms of gallic acid equivalent.</p></sec><sec id="sec009"><title>Total flavonoid content (TFC)</title><p>TFC of EA-AS was determined using the aluminum chloride (AlCl<sub>3</sub>) colorimetric method: 0.5 mL of the Launaea nudicaulis extract was pipetted into a series of test tubes. The volume in each tube was adjusted to 1 mL with DH<sub>2</sub>O. 150 μL of 5% NaNO<sub>2</sub> solution was added to all the tubes and allowed to incubate for 5 minutes at 25°C. Then, 150 μL of 10% AlCl<sub>3</sub> solution was added to all the test tubes and incubated for 6 minutes at 25°C. After this, 2 mL of 4% NaOH solution was added to all the tubes, and the final volume was made up to 5 mL with DH<sub>2</sub>O. The test tubes were vortexed and allowed to stand for 15 minutes and absorbance was measured at 510 nm [<xref rid="pone.0310298.ref016" ref-type="bibr">16</xref>]. The flavonoids content of extracts was estimated by using the quercetin standard calibration curve.</p></sec><sec id="sec010"><title>Tannin content (TC)</title><p>TC of the EA-AS was determined using the FD method, as described by Makkar [<xref rid="pone.0310298.ref015" ref-type="bibr">15</xref>].
