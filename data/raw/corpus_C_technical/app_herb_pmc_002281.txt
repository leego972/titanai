Black Seed (<italic toggle="yes">Nigella sativa</italic>), known historically within Unani and Ayurvedic medical systems, contains thymoquinone, a key compound with well-documented antioxidant, anticancer effects (<xref rid="ref-27" ref-type="bibr">Sadeghi, Imenshahidi &amp; Hosseinzadeh, 2023</xref>; <xref rid="ref-17" ref-type="bibr">Khalid et al., 2024</xref>). Other constituents, including flavonoids and essential oils, contribute to immune modulation and metabolic protection (<xref rid="ref-24" ref-type="bibr">Oubannin et al., 2024</xref>). Multiple studies have demonstrated its ability to inhibit cancer cell proliferation <italic toggle="yes">via</italic> oxidative stress modulation, apoptosis induction, and signal transduction interference (<xref rid="ref-29" ref-type="bibr">Sheikhnia et al., 2023</xref>; <xref rid="ref-36" ref-type="bibr">Zafar et al., 2023</xref>; <xref rid="ref-13" ref-type="bibr">Homayoonfal, Asemi &amp; Yousefi, 2022</xref>). Although each plant exhibits substantial biological activity individually, combining extracts may amplify therapeutic effects through synergistic interactions. Polyherbal formulations may enhance antioxidant capacity, reduce off-target effects, and strengthen cytotoxic actions against cancer cells by integrating complementary phytochemical pathways (<xref rid="ref-35" ref-type="bibr">Vicol &amp; Duca, 2023</xref>; <xref rid="ref-7" ref-type="bibr">Bouizgma et al., 2023</xref>; <xref rid="ref-15" ref-type="bibr">Jongrungraungchok et al., 2023</xref>; <xref rid="ref-33" ref-type="bibr">Slavova-Kazakova et al., 2021</xref>). Plant-derived extracts are increasingly studied not only for clinical therapeutic potential but also as emerging candidates in animal health and veterinary nutrition, where natural antioxidants and immunomodulatory agents may enhance physiological resilience, metabolic balance, and disease resistance. The herbal extract may therefore hold relevance across biomedical and livestock applications. Accordingly, this study evaluates the phytochemical composition, antioxidant activity, and cytotoxic potential of a combined extract of herbal extract, with relevance to both human therapeutic applications and prospective roles in veterinary and nutraceutical research.
