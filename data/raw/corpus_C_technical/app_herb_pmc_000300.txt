# PMC: herbal medicine pharmacology mechanism review — Overview

100 compounds have been identified from <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic>, some C<sub>21</sub>-steroidal glycosides and terpenoids have demonstrated special immunosuppressive activity [<xref rid="B1-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>,<xref rid="B4-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">4</xref>]. Besides its characteristic antirheumatic effect, it was shown to specifically treat heart failure. Research studies showed that the extracts of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> exhibited cardiotonic effects on the isolated heart of rats and could strengthen cardiotonic function [<xref rid="B5-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">5</xref>,<xref rid="B6-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">6</xref>]. In addition, more studies have been focused on its antitumoral effect. Studies reported that <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> showed a good effect on tumor cell apoptosis and the repression of tumor growth of SW480 cells, TE-13 cells, SMMC-7721 cells, MCF-7 cells, BGC-823 cells, etc. [<xref rid="B2-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B7-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">7</xref>,<xref rid="B8-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>]. However, poisoning incidences caused by using <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> have also been reported. As it is well known, periplocin is a poisonous component in <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic>, and it shows a special cardiotoxic effect. Sun et al. studied the LD<sub>50</sub> value of periplocin in mice and determined that it was 15.20 mg/kg by intraperitoneal injection. Studies also showed that it could obviously affect the electrocardiograms of guinea pigs, which indicated that it had a special toxicity [<xref rid="B9-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">9</xref>]. It was also found that there was a good correlation between the content of periplocin and the acute toxicity of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic>. It implied that the content of periplocin should be limited in order to reduce the toxicity response when it was used in clinic [<xref rid="B10-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">10</xref>].</p><p>Numerous components have been identified from this herb, and some of them have been proven to have an effective function for the treatment of different diseases for many years. Here, we summarized the available articles about the photochemistry, biological activities, and toxicity profile of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic>.</p></sec><sec id="sec2-molecules-21-01702"><title>2. Methods</title><p>All the information about this plant has been obtained through searching journals, books, and theses and collected via libraries or electronic databases including PubMed, Elsevier, Google Scholar, and Springer. Due to few publications of this herb in English literature, the main source of information of this plant was from China national knowledge infrastructure (CNKI) and other classic Chinese herbal literature.</p></sec><sec id="sec3-molecules-21-01702"><title>3. Phytochemistry</title><p>Many chemical constituents and active ingredients have been isolated from <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic>, since the 1970s by researchers [<xref rid="B11-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">11</xref>]. So far, more than 100 compounds have been isolated and identified from this plant, containing C<sub>21</sub>-steroidal glycosides, cardiac glycosides, terpenoids, volatile oils, fatty acids, etc.</p><sec id="sec3dot1-molecules-21-01702"><title>3.1. C<sub>21</sub>-Steroidal Glycosides</title><p>C<sub>21</sub>-steroidal glycosides account for the majority of the total chemical constituents of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic>. There are more than 30 C<sub>21-</sub>steroidal glycosides which have been identified from <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> (<xref ref-type="fig" rid="molecules-21-01702-f001">Figure 1</xref>, <xref rid="molecules-21-01702-t001" ref-type="table">Table 1</xref>). These steroids have five essential C<sub>21</sub> skeletons and their respective glycosides, including β-<sc>d</sc>-glucopyranosyl (Glc), β-cymaropyranosyl (Cym), β-canaropyranosyl (Can), and β-digitalopyranosyl (Dig).</p></sec><sec id="sec3dot2-molecules-21-01702"><title>3.2. Cardiac Glycosides</title><p>Another type of compound contained in this plant is cardiac components, including two glycosides and two aglycones, which were identified as periplocin, peripocymarin, periplogenin, and xysmalogenin [<xref rid="B23-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">23</xref>]. Research reported that periplocin was a special ingredient, which was not only a cardiotonic compound, but also the main toxic component in <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> [<xref rid="B5-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">5</xref>]. Therefore, it is necessary to develop more studies for the assessment of toxicity of periplocin in order to guarantee efficacy and safety when it is utilized in clinic.
