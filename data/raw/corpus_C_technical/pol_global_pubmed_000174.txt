The outbreak of COVID-19 has spread quickly across 114 
countries/territories/areas in six continents worldwide and has been announced 
as a pandemic by WHO. This study analyzed global COVID-19 epidemiological 
trends, examined impact of the pandemic on global health security, diplomacy, 
and social environment in China, and provided short- and long-term strategic 
policy recommendations for China's subsequent preparedness and responses.

Publisher: 
当前全球六大洲、114个国家、领土或地区受到新型冠状病毒肺炎疫情不同程度的影响，WHO已宣布本次疫情构成"全球大流行"。为进一步加强对全球新型冠状病毒肺炎疫情的防控，应对全球疫情升级对中国的挑战，本研究着重回答3个问题：新型冠状病毒肺炎全球流行现状及各国防疫情况；全球疫情对中国影响；中国应对全球疫情的短期预案和中长期策略建议。.
