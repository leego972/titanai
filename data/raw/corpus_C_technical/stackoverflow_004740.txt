Answer: <p>Here's another approach that is short. The disadvantage is that you can only break the outer loop, but sometimes it's exactly what you want.</p>

<pre><code>for a in xrange(10):
    for b in xrange(20):
        if something(a, b):
            # Break the inner loop...
            break
    else:
        # Continue if the inner loop wasn't broken.
        continue
    # Inner loop was broken, break the outer.
    break
</code></pre>
