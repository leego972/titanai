Most of the products were meant to be applied topically on the skin. Forty four samples were found to be contaminated; only 3 samples did not grow any bacteria in the cultures (Table <xref rid="Tab1" ref-type="table">1</xref>). Overall, 59 bacterial species were successfully isolated and identified (Fig. <xref rid="Fig1" ref-type="fig">1</xref>). Nine samples had staphylococcal bacteria (4 <italic toggle="yes">S. hominis</italic>, 3 <italic toggle="yes">S. auricularis</italic>, 1 <italic toggle="yes">S. haemolyticus</italic> and 1 <italic toggle="yes">S. schleiferi</italic>), and 7 had <italic toggle="yes">Pantoea agglomerans</italic>.</p><p id="Par22">
