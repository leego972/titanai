# Rodolfo Soares

Rodolfo dos Santos Soares, or simply Rodolfo Soares (born May 20, 1985) is a Brazilian football who plays as a defender for Maltese club St Lucia.

Club career
On 12 May 2018, he signed a two-year contract with Gżira United.
