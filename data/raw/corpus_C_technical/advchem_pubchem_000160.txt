Description:
Chloride is a halide anion formed when chlorine picks up an electron to form an an anion. It has a role as a cofactor, an Escherichia coli metabolite and a human metabolite. It is a halide anion and a monoatomic chlorine. It is a conjugate base of a hydrogen chloride.
