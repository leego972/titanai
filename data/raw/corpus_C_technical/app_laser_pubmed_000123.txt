Photodynamic therapy for cancers: a clinical trial of porfimer sodium in Japan.

Kato H(1), Horai T, Furuse K, Fukuoka M, Suzuki S, Hiki Y, Ito Y, Mimura S, 
Tenjin Y, Hisazumi H, et al.

Author information:
(1)Department of Surgery, Tokyo Medical College.

Photodynamic therapy utilizing Photofrin has proven to be an effective modality 
that can be used in the treatment of a wide variety of solid tumors and luminal 
cancers. An argon pumped dye laser or excimer dye laser was used to deliver 630 
nm light via quartz fibers passed through the biopsy channel subsequent to i.v. 
injection of photosensitizer. In this study, 64 patients with superficial 
cancers were treated in this manner but only 58 patients, including 21 with 
roentgenographically occult lung cancer, 8 with stage I lung cancer, 5 with 
esophageal cancer, 12 with gastric cancer, 8 with cervical cancer and 4 with 
bladder cancer were evaluable. Complete remission was obtained in 48 out of 58 
cases (82.8%). There was no serious complication except skin photosensitivity, 
which was seen in 13 patients. We conclude that photodynamic therapy is 
efficacious in the treatment of superficial cancers where complete remission may 
be achieved.
