Description:
Amlexanox is a pyridochromene-derived monocarboxylic acid having an amino substituent at the 2-position, an oxo substituent at the 5-position and an isopropyl substituent at the 7-position. It has a role as a non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug, an anti-ulcer drug and an anti-allergic agent. It is a monocarboxylic acid and a pyridochromene.
