In South Africa, <italic toggle="yes">E. natalensis</italic> is an ingredient of herbal concoction called <italic toggle="yes">imbiza</italic> also containing roots of <italic toggle="yes">Polygala fruticosa, Raphionacme</italic> spp., bulbous roots of <italic toggle="yes">Crinum</italic> spp. and <italic toggle="yes">Cyrtanthus obliquus</italic> and root barks of <italic toggle="yes">Zanthoxylum capense, Capparis tomentosa</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">Rauvolfia caffra</italic> used to purify the blood [<xref rid="B8-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>]. [<xref rid="B24-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">24</xref>] revealed that root decoction of <italic toggle="yes">E. natalensis</italic> is taken orally mixed with roots of <italic toggle="yes">Grewia hexamita</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">Pappea capensis</italic> as remedies for stomach complaints and reproductive problems in women such as infertility and painful menstruation. In Tanzania, root decoction of <italic toggle="yes">E. natalensis</italic> is mixed with other plant species such as <italic toggle="yes">Acacia brevispica, Acacia hockii, Acacia robusta, Aloe secundiflora, Asparagus flagellaris, Capparis fascicularis, Carrisa spinarum, Clerodendrum myricoides, Cymbopogon citratus, Dichrostachys cinerea, Eucalyptus</italic> spp., <italic toggle="yes">Gomphocarpus fruticosus, Harrisonia abyssinica, Kedrostis foetidissima, Mangifera indica, Pennisetum purpureum, Psidium guajava, Punica granatum, Musa</italic> spp., <italic toggle="yes">Sansevieria ehlenbergii, Withania somnifera, Ximenia caffra</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">Zanthoxylum chalybeum</italic> as herbal medicine for amoebic dysentery, opportunistic infections and venereal diseases [<xref rid="B25-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">25</xref>].</p></sec><sec id="sec4-molecules-22-02128"><title>4. Phytochemistry</title><p>Various chemical constituents have been isolated from <italic toggle="yes">E. natalensis</italic>, mainly compounds belonging to naphthoquinone and pentacyclic terpenoids classes (<xref rid="molecules-22-02128-t002" ref-type="table">Table 2</xref>). Lopes and Paul [<xref rid="B46-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">46</xref>] isolated two pentacyclic terpenoids, betulin <bold>1</bold> and lupeol <bold>2</bold> from the root bark of <italic toggle="yes">E. natalensis</italic>, while Tannock [<xref rid="B47-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">47</xref>] isolated naphthoquinones, namely isodiospyrin <bold>3</bold> and mamegakinone <bold>4</bold> from the same species (<xref rid="molecules-22-02128-t002" ref-type="table">Table 2</xref>). King et al. [<xref rid="B48-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">48</xref>] isolated natalenone <bold>5</bold> from the root bark of <italic toggle="yes">E. natalensis</italic>, while Ferreira et al. [<xref rid="B49-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">49</xref>] isolated natalenone <bold>5</bold>, 8′-hydroxydiospyrin <bold>6</bold>, euclanone <bold>7</bold>, galpinone <bold>8</bold>, methylnaphthazarin <bold>9</bold> and neodiospyrin <bold>10</bold> from the same species (<xref rid="molecules-22-02128-t002" ref-type="table">Table 2</xref>). Khan et al. [<xref rid="B50-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">50</xref>] isolated lupeol <bold>2</bold>, mamegakinone <bold>4</bold>, diospyrin <bold>11</bold> and 7-methyljuglone <bold>12</bold> from the root bark of <italic toggle="yes">E. natalensis</italic> while Khan [<xref rid="B51-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">51</xref>] isolated 4,8-dihydroxy-6-methyl-1-tetralone (shinanolone <bold>13</bold>) from the same species (<xref rid="molecules-22-02128-t002" ref-type="table">Table 2</xref>). Weigenand et al. [<xref rid="B52-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">52</xref>] isolated betulin <bold>1</bold>, lupeol <bold>2</bold>, shinanolone <bold>13</bold>, 20(29)-lupene-3β-isoferulate <bold>14</bold> and octahydroeuclein <bold>15</bold> from the root bark of <italic toggle="yes">E. natalensis</italic>. Similarly, Lall et al. [<xref rid="B53-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">53</xref>] isolated betulin <bold>1</bold>, lupeol <bold>2</bold>, shinanolone <bold>13</bold>, 20(29)-lupene-3β-isoferulate <bold>14</bold>, octahydroeuclein <bold>15</bold> and β-sitosterol <bold>16</bold> from the root bark of <italic toggle="yes">E. natalensis</italic> (<xref rid="molecules-22-02128-t002" ref-type="table">Table 2</xref>). Van der Kooy [<xref rid="B54-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">54</xref>] isolated isodiospyrin <bold>3</bold>, mamegakinone <bold>4</bold>, neodiospyrin <bold>10</bold>, diospyrin <bold>11</bold>, 7-methyljuglone <bold>12</bold>, shinanolone <bold>13</bold> and 5-hydroxy-4-methoxy-2-nathaldehyde <bold>17</bold> from the root bark of <italic toggle="yes">E. natalensis.</italic> Van der Kooy et al.
