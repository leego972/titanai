Question: <p>Is there an easy way to cache things when using urllib2 that I am over-looking, or do I have to roll my own?</p>

Answer: <p>If you don't mind working at a slightly lower level, httplib2 (<a href="http://code.google.com/p/httplib2/">http://code.google.com/p/httplib2/</a>) is an excellent HTTP library that includes caching functionality.</p>
