Question: <p>How do you extract an HD and Bios Unique ID, using python script?</p>

Answer: <p>Go Get Microsoft's <a href="http://www.microsoft.com/downloads/details.aspx?familyid=09dfc342-648b-4119-b7eb-783b0f7d1178&amp;displaylang=en" rel="nofollow">Scriptomatic</a></p>
