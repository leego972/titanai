Question: <p>If I have two dates (ex. <code>'8/18/2008'</code> and <code>'9/26/2008'</code>) what is the best way to get the difference measured in days?</p>

Answer: <p>Using the power of datetime:</p>

<pre><code>from datetime import datetime
date_format = "%m/%d/%Y"
a = datetime.strptime('8/18/2008', date_format)
b = datetime.strptime('9/26/2008', date_format)
delta = b - a
print delta.days # that's it
</code></pre>
