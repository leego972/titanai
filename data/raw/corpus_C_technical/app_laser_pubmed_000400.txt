The physical properties of tissue ablation with excimer lasers.

Cross FW(1), Bowker TJ.

Author information:
(1)Department of Surgery, National Medical Laser Centre, University College 
London, Rayne Institute, United Kingdom.

The current controversy regarding the interaction between the output of the 
excimer laser and human tissue concerns the relative importance of photothermal 
and photoablative effects. Two experiments using an excimer laser on vascular 
tissue, aimed at defining the precise laser-tissue interaction, were performed. 
The experiments argue strongly in favor of photoablative effects that result 
from multiple photon interactions with ablated tissue.
