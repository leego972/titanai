Description:
2-oxobutanoic acid is a 2-oxo monocarboxylic acid that is the 2-oxo derivative of butanoic acid. It is a short-chain fatty acid and a 2-oxo monocarboxylic acid. It is functionally related to a butyric acid. It is a conjugate acid of a 2-oxobutanoate.
