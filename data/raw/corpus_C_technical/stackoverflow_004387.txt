Question: <p>I've discovered that cElementTree is about 30 times faster than <code>xml.dom.minidom</code> and I'm rewriting my XML encoding/decoding code. However, I need to output XML that contains CDATA sections and there doesn't seem to be a way to do that with ElementTree.</p>

<p>Can it be done?</p>

Answer: <p>I've discovered a hack to get CDATA to work using comments:</p>

<pre><code>node.append(etree.Comment(' --&gt;&lt;![CDATA[' + data.replace(']]&gt;', ']]]]&gt;&lt;![CDATA[&gt;') + ']]&gt;&lt;!-- '))
</code></pre>
