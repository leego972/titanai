Question: <p>It appears the Python signal module doesn't have anything similar to the sighold and sigrelse functions found in C, using signal.h. Are there Python equivalents of <em>any</em> sort?</p>

<p>Many thanks!</p>

Answer: <p>There is no way to ``block'' signals temporarily from critical sections (since this is not supported by all Unix flavors).</p>
