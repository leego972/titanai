Question: <p>It seems like there should be a simpler way than:</p>

<pre><code>import string
s = "string. With. Punctuation?" # Sample string 
out = s.translate(string.maketrans("",""), string.punctuation)
</code></pre>

<p>Is there?</p>

Answer: <p>This question is over 6 years old, but I'd figured I chime in with a function I wrote. It's not very efficient, but it is simple and you can add or remove any punctuation that you desire:</p>

<pre><code>def stripPunc(wordList):
    """Strips punctuation from list of words"""
    puncList = [".",";",":","!","?","/","\\",",","#","@","$","&amp;",")","(","\""]
    for punc in puncList:
        for word in wordList:
            wordList=[word.replace(punc,'') for word in wordList]
    return wordList
</code></pre>
