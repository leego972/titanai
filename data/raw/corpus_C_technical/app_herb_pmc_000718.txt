Salvia Miltiorrhiza</title><p>Salvia miltiorrhiza (Danshen), a traditional Chinese herbal medicine, is commonly used for the prevention and treatment of cardiovascular disease. According to Yu et al. [<xref rid="B86" ref-type="bibr">86</xref>] intraperitoneal injection Salvia miltiorrhiza 100 mg/kg/d for 4 weeks improved the heart function of diabetic rats and protected against cardiomyopathy by downregulating thrombospondin-1 (TSP-1) and TGF-<italic toggle="yes">β</italic>1 in myocardial tissue. Cryptotanshinone is an active principal ingredient isolated from Salvia miltiorrhiza (Danshen). Oral administration of 10 mg/kg/d cryptotanshinone for 28 days attenuates the cardiac fibrosis in STZ-induced diabetic rats by inhibiting STAT3 pathway and MMP-9 expression [<xref rid="B87" ref-type="bibr">87</xref>].</p></sec><sec id="sec4.7"><title>4.7. Flavonoids</title><p>Chrysin, a PPAR-<italic toggle="yes">γ</italic> agonist, is a natural flavonoid present in honey, propolis, and various plant extracts. According to Rani et al. [<xref rid="B88" ref-type="bibr">88</xref>, <xref rid="B89" ref-type="bibr">89</xref>], chrysin attenuated isoproterenol-induced myocardial injury in diabetic rats by activating PPAR-<italic toggle="yes">γ</italic> and inhibiting AGE-RAGE-mediated inflammation and oxidative stress signaling pathway. Myricitrin (<xref ref-type="fig" rid="fig5">Figure 5(g)</xref>) is a flavone exact from the root bark of <italic toggle="yes">Myrica cerifera</italic>, Myrica esculenta, Ampelopsis grossedentata, and other plants. Zhang et al. [<xref rid="B90" ref-type="bibr">90</xref>] reported that pretreated AGE-cultured H9c2 cells with 25 <italic toggle="yes">μ</italic>g/ml Myricitrin for 12 h significantly decreased the AGE-induced inflammation cytokines and cell apoptosis by activating Nrf2 and inhibiting NF-<italic toggle="yes">κ</italic>B. Oral administration of Myricitrin 300 mg/kg/d for 8 weeks attenuated the cardiomyocyte apoptosis and inflammation of diabetic mice heart via regulation of AKt- and ERK-mediated Nrf2 pathways. Apigenin, a flavonoid derived in fruits and vegetables, has been shown to protect against isoproterenol-challenged diabetic myocardial injury by activation of PPAR-<italic toggle="yes">γ</italic> pathway [<xref rid="B91" ref-type="bibr">91</xref>]. Taxifolin is a flavonoid abound in <italic toggle="yes">Pseudotsuga taxifolia</italic>, Dahurian larch, and syn Larix dahurica Turoz. According to Sun et al. [<xref rid="B92" ref-type="bibr">92</xref>], Taxifolin at concentration 20 and 40 <italic toggle="yes">μ</italic>g/ml could decrease the apoptosis of high-glucose cultured H9c2 cells by inhibiting ROS generation. In vivo, Taxifolin attenuated the structure and function abnormalities by blocking NADPH oxidative activities. Troxerutin (<xref ref-type="fig" rid="fig5">Figure 5(h)</xref>), a bioflavonoid, protects against DCM through suppression of NF-<italic toggle="yes">κ</italic>B and JNK in a diabetic rat [<xref rid="B93" ref-type="bibr">93</xref>]. Hesperidin, a flavonoid isolated from citrus, has been shown to reduce oxidative stress and apoptosis and attenuate myocardial injury in isoproterenol-STZ rat via activation of PPAR-<italic toggle="yes">γ</italic> [<xref rid="B94" ref-type="bibr">94</xref>]. Nobiletin treatment (50 mg/kg/d p.o. 11 weeks) attenuates diabetic heart injury by suppression of oxidative stress, JNK, p38 MAPK, and NF-<italic toggle="yes">κ</italic>B pathways [<xref rid="B95" ref-type="bibr">95</xref>]. 80 <italic toggle="yes">μ</italic>M Naringin (4,5,7-trihydroxyflavonone-7-rhamnoglucoside, <xref ref-type="fig" rid="fig5">Figure 5(i)</xref>) pretreated for 2 hours protects H9c2 cells from high-glucose injury by ROS scavenging and MAPK cascade inhibiting [<xref rid="B96" ref-type="bibr">96</xref>]. Liquirtin, a major constituent of Glycyrrhiza radix, exerts various pharmacological activities. Liquirtin prevents myocardial injury induced by high fructose feeding by inhibiting NF-<italic toggle="yes">κ</italic>B and MAPK cascades [<xref rid="B97" ref-type="bibr">97</xref>, <xref rid="B98" ref-type="bibr">98</xref>].</p></sec><sec id="sec4.8"><title>4.8.
