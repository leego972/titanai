Question: <p>What's the <strong>easiest, tersest, and most flexible</strong> method or library for parsing Python command line arguments?</p>

Answer: <p>The new hip way is <code>argparse</code> for <a href="http://argparse.googlecode.com/svn/trunk/doc/argparse-vs-optparse.html">these</a> reasons.  argparse > optparse > getopt</p>

<p><strong>update:</strong> As of py2.7 <a href="http://docs.python.org/library/argparse.html">argparse</a> is part of the standard library and <a href="http://docs.python.org/library/optparse.html">optparse</a> is deprecated.</p>
