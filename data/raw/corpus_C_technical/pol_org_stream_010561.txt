Organization of the Corps of Cadets 
Overall command of the Corps of Cadets OCS-SAFTI MI falls on the shoulders of the Commander OCS, who also serves as commandant of the Corps. 

Majority of the Corps is made up of direct entry officer cadets straight out of basic military training at the BMT Centre, with graduates of the Specialist Cadet School and career soldiers and specialists forming the rest. Those who did not have collegiate degrees graduate from OCS as Second Lieutenants following their term, those that have graduated college are given a Lieutenant's commission. Technical service and DIS officers are commissioned Military Expert 4 after their graduation.

Given the short 6-month study period of officer cadets the whole Corps is structured into the following battalions:

 1st and 2nd Battalions are made up of the Army officer cadets of the courses that will graduate from OCS during the year. The battalions are changed whenever a new batch of officers have graduated from the OCS, which happens twice a year, and their personnel selected in the conditions set above, regardless of their company assignments. In the days when the study course was 9 months, the graduation and passing out parade was held once a year.

 3rd Battalion is made up of the officer cadets of the Navy, Air Force, and DIS. The battalion is renewed in like manner as the Army battalions.
