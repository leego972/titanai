Four trials recruited more than 50 participants, and the other 4 recruited less than 50 participants. The highest number of recruited patients was 260. One of the trials, <ext-link xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink" ext-link-type="pmc:clinical-trial" xlink:href="NCT00243022">NCT00243022</ext-link>, was terminated due to low patient accrual even though it is indicated as “has results” via ClinicalTrials.gov.</p><p><ext-link xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink" ext-link-type="pmc:clinical-trial" xlink:href="NCT00411762">NCT00411762</ext-link> concluded that a combination of capecitabine and PHY906 is safe for patients with solid tumors; the combination of cationic anti-microbial peptides and PHY906 has also shown insufficient evidence in patients with solid tumors for its effectiveness.<sup>
