Title: Design of 8-bit Low power & High performance SAR ADC using current steering DAC

Abstract:
A novel approach of 8-bit low power & high performance Successive Approximation analog to digital converter consumes a large amount of power. As compared to other Analog to Digital Converter, Successive Approximation Register Analog to Digital Converters (SAR ADC) are cheaper because of their compact circuitry. In digital to analog modules, high power dissipation has been observed. To achieve excellent power efficiency with relatively moderate resolution, modifications are proposed to Digital to Analog Converter (DAC) architecture. With its current steering digital to analog converter, this SAR ADC will be useful for low power applications with high speed and medium resolution. The ADC is designed in 90nm CMOS technology. In this thesis, 8-bit SAR ADC firstly designed in 90nm technology and comparing power consumption and delay with 45nm technology. Schematics designed using Cadence Virtuoso Tool. Circuit designs for these logics use 1V to 2.5V power supplies to operate the transistors in saturation region. To reduce power consumption, a low-power comparator is designed. The SAR ADC is chosen as it is low-power, and has a small die size compared to other types of ADCs. This allowed for a powerefficient design.
