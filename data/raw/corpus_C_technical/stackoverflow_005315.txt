Question: <p>How do I get the inverse of a matrix in python? I've implemented it myself, but it's pure python, and I suspect there are faster modules out there to do it.</p>

Answer: <p>Numpy will be suitable for most people, but you can also do <a href="http://docs.sympy.org/latest/tutorial/matrices.html" rel="nofollow">matrices in Sympy</a></p>

<p>Try running these commands at <a href="http://live.sympy.org/" rel="nofollow">http://live.sympy.org/</a></p>

<pre><code>M = Matrix([[1, 3], [-2, 3]])
M
M**-1
</code></pre>

<p>For fun, try <code>M**(1/2)</code></p>
