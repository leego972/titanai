This is related to a high incidence of gastrointestinal ailments in southern Ecuador [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR86">86</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR127">127</xref>], which may also be associated to stomach cancer, as this is the second most important cause of mortality in the country [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR128">128</xref>]. When comparing the large FIC values obtained in this study for the digestive system to the most important medical categories reported in previous surveys worldwide [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR113">113</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR115">115</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR116">116</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR129">129</xref>], the results reveal the importance of medicinal plant species in treating ailments of the digestive system, such as gastric complaints and abdominal pains.</p><p>Additionally, therapeutic uses related with the other two medical categories, culture-bound syndromes and general disorders, had relatively high values of use citations by market vendors. The medicinal plant species responsible for this particular status was <italic toggle="yes">Ruta graveolens</italic>, used to treat seven psychosomatic complaints, and <italic toggle="yes">Equisetum bogotense,</italic> used in five common ailments. As it was confirmed by participative observation, local people believe that these two medicinal plant species were efficient in the treatment of 12 particular local ailments, revealing the persistence of cultural believes syndromes and the necessity of preventive medicine to avoid common ailments.</p><p>Even the lowest FIC values registered in this study, which included hormonal system (0.81), musculoskeletal system (0.83), and gynecological system (0.85), are large when compared to other studies that use this same index [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR106">106</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR107">107</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR110">110</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR111">111</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR130">130</xref>]. These low FIC values show a low agreement amongst medicinal plant market vendors, specifically in the trade of medicinal plant species associated to the treatment of the symptoms related to these ailments. This data contrasts with the results of a market study in Venezuela [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR30">30</xref>], where a FIC = 0.91 was registered for the gynecological system, as obtained with 25 interviewed market vendors.</p><p>The FIC values between 0.81 and 0.92 demonstrate the strong levels of consensus amongst 196 vendors in the multiple uses of the 160 medicinal plant species sold. Incidentally, even when the taxa number prescribed for a specific illness or disease varied, the majority of the 33 traditional markets had a common pool of regional flora sold to clients within the 12 medical categories. As in Bolivia [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR19">19</xref>] and Peru [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR125">125</xref>], the most common medicinal plant species sold were for symptoms related to the digestive system, nervous system, respiratory system, and genitourinary system.</p></sec><sec id="Sec14"><title>Fidelity Level (FL)</title><p>The FL index determined 11 culturally important medicinal plant species in the local population of the Loja province (Table <xref rid="Tab4" ref-type="table">4</xref>), as based on the reported uses by 40 or more market vendors to treat 53 illnesses and diseases grouped in 12 medical categories. It was also useful for highlighting the most important species sold in each medical category. In this analysis, the 160 medicinal plant species mentioned by market vendors were considered, and the FL was calculated for each one.
