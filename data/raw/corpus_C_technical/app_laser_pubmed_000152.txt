Photodynamic therapy in head and neck cancer.

Photodynamic therapy is a nonsurgical, minimally invasive technique that uses a 
light source to activate light-sensitive drugs or photosensitizers to treat 
cancer and other diseases. The effect on living organisms caused by light in the 
presence of a photosensitizer and oxygen is known as the photodynamic effect or 
photodynamic action.
