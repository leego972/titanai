Sandra Nelson Winkler (born December 29, 1964), more commonly credited as Sandra Nelson, is an American actress. Born in Madison, Wisconsin, she is best known for her role as Phyllis Summers on The Young and the Restless that she played from 1997 to 1999. She also played Aliyah Farran in The Wolf of Wall Street.

Personal life
Nelson married television and film director and producer Charles Winkler on September 6, 1998. They divorced in 2012.Charles Winkler The Outer Limits, Directors They have a daughter, born in 2000, and a son born in 2002.
