Title: Constitutional boundaries after Verein KlimaSeniorinnen: Lessons on domestic rights‐based climate change litigation from the Swedish Supreme Court's Aurora Judgment

Abstract:
This case note analyses the Swedish Supreme Court's judgment in the Aurora case (Anton Foley and others v Sweden). The case is one of the first national apex court decisions to interpret and apply the European Court of Human Rights' (ECtHR) groundbreaking judgment in Verein KlimaSeniorinnen Schweiz and others v Switzerland. This case note examines how the Swedish Supreme Court applies the ECtHR's principles on victim status, access to court and the separation of powers and assesses the extent to which this aligns with the spirit and substance of Verein KlimaSeniorinnen. The article argues that while the Swedish Supreme Court's reasoning may appear formally compatible with the ECtHR's judgment in Verein KlimaSeniorinnen, its restrictive approach risks undermining meaningful judicial oversight envisioned by the ECtHR.
