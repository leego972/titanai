# List of CBS television affiliates (table) — Overview

The CBS television network is a television network based in the United States made up of 15 owned-and-operated stations and nearly 228 network affiliates. Stations are listed in alphabetical order by city of license. A blue background indicates an affiliate originating as a digital subchannel. A gray background indicates a low-power station or translator. A lavender blue background indicates an affiliate originating as a digital subchannel of a low-power station.
