Question: <p>I would like to know the best way to replace a standard textarea field with a rich text editor in Django Admin?</p>

Answer: <p>There's an <a href="http://pypi.python.org/pypi/django-tinymce/">add-on Django application</a> to provide <a href="http://tinymce.moxiecode.com/">TinyMCE</a> support for Django admin forms without having to muck around with admin templates or Django newform internals.</p>
