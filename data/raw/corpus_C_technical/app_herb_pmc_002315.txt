Cosmetic Industry</title><p>The cosmetics industry is one of the main beneficiaries of the advances made with <italic toggle="yes">CJ</italic>. In fact, <italic toggle="yes">CJ</italic> products have achieved relatively high technological readiness levels (TRL 8–9), with several formulations already commercialized due to demonstrated safety, stability, and efficacy in skincare applications. However, most of these currently available <italic toggle="yes">CJ</italic> products use other parts of the plant, such as the seeds, which are used to obtain oils with emollient and skin-regenerating properties. These oils are used in the formulation of anti-wrinkle creams, moisturizing serums, and hair care products [<xref rid="B96-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">96</xref>,<xref rid="B125-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">125</xref>,<xref rid="B126-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">126</xref>].</p><p><italic toggle="yes">CJL</italic> helps strengthen hair, reduce hair loss, and improve the overall health of the scalp. The combination of <italic toggle="yes">CJL</italic> extracts with other hair oils such as castor, black cumin, lavender, rosemary, cedarwood, and lemongrass has been the basis of various patented formulations in recent years, aimed at producing shampoos that protect hair from the effects of the elements, maintain its natural moisture, prevent frizz, and hydrate and restore long-lasting shine [<xref rid="B127-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">127</xref>,<xref rid="B128-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">128</xref>,<xref rid="B129-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">129</xref>]. <italic toggle="yes">CJL</italic> products currently marketed include small-scale productions such as Acemelia’s solid shampoo or internationally marketed shampoos such as Garnier’s Whole Blends, Palmolive, or Oshima Tsubaki shampoo.</p><p>In addition, the leaves also exhibit certain potential for the development of other cosmetic applications. Among these, it is worth highlighting the incorporation of their extracts into cosmetic products aimed at combating skin aging and preventing damage caused by exposure to environmental factors such as UV radiation, pollution, and stress [<xref rid="B86-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">86</xref>,<xref rid="B130-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">130</xref>]. This effect is attributed to the high concentration of antioxidants in <italic toggle="yes">CJL</italic>, primarily phenolic compounds, which stimulate cell regeneration and enhance the skin’s repair capacity, thereby improving its texture and firmness [<xref rid="B31-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">31</xref>]. The improved texture achieved with its cosmetic products is also due to <italic toggle="yes">CJL</italic>’s ability to act as a natural moisturizer, helping to maintain skin and hair hydration [<xref rid="B125-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">125</xref>,<xref rid="B131-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">131</xref>]. <italic toggle="yes">CJL</italic> can also be used in the development of cosmetic products with anti-inflammatory properties or that prevent redness or skin reactions in irritated or sensitive skin [<xref rid="B127-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">127</xref>,<xref rid="B132-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">132</xref>]. Other studies proved that <italic toggle="yes">CJL</italic> could be used in the formulation of cosmetic products to control excess oil due to its astringent properties and the fact that it helps regulate sebum production [<xref rid="B133-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">133</xref>,<xref rid="B134-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">134</xref>]. Furthermore, <italic toggle="yes">CJL</italic> can reduce enlarged pores, highly desirable characteristics for the formulation of toners, facial cleansers, and masks [<xref rid="B135-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">135</xref>,<xref rid="B136-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">136</xref>]. Another less developed use includes the formulation of cosmetics with antibacterial properties, making them suitable for products designed to treat skin problems such as acne and other superficial skin infections [<xref rid="B96-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">96</xref>,<xref rid="B132-nutrients-17-03382" ref-type="bibr">132</xref>].</p><p>All these properties make <italic toggle="yes">CJL</italic> a promising ingredient for the formulation of innovative cosmetic products, especially in anti-aging creams, toners, masks, hair care products, and solutions for sensitive or acne-prone skin. For this reason, several prestigious brands have incorporated this matrix into their formulations in recent years.
