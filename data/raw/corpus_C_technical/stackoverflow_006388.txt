Question: <p>What's the difference between the list methods <code>append()</code> and <code>extend()</code>?</p>

Answer: <p><code>append</code> adds an element to a list, <code>extend</code> concatenates the first list with another list (or another iterable not necessarily a list.)</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; li = ['a', 'b', 'mpilgrim', 'z', 'example']
&gt;&gt;&gt; li
['a', 'b', 'mpilgrim', 'z', 'example']

&gt;&gt;&gt; li.append("new")               
&gt;&gt;&gt; li
['a', 'b', 'mpilgrim', 'z', 'example', 'new']

&gt;&gt;&gt; li.insert(2, "new")            
&gt;&gt;&gt; li
['a', 'b', 'new', 'mpilgrim', 'z', 'example', 'new']

&gt;&gt;&gt; li.extend(["two", "elements"]) 
&gt;&gt;&gt; li
['a', 'b', 'new', 'mpilgrim', 'z', 'example', 'new', 'two', 'elements']
</code></pre>
