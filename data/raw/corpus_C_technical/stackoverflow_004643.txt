Question: <p>What is the regular expression to match strings (in this case, file names) that start with 'Run' and have a filename extension of '.py'?</p>

<p>The regular expression should match any of the following:</p>

<pre><code>RunFoo.py
RunBar.py
Run42.py
</code></pre>

<p>It should not match:</p>

<pre><code>myRunFoo.py
RunBar.py1
Run42.txt
</code></pre>

<p>The SQL equivalent of what I am looking for is <code>... LIKE 'Run%.py' ...</code>.</p>

Answer: <pre><code>/^Run.*\.py$/
</code></pre>

<p>Or, in python specifically:</p>

<pre><code>import re
re.match(r"^Run.*\.py$", stringtocheck)
</code></pre>

<p>This will match "Runfoobar.py", but not "runfoobar.PY". To make it case insensitive, instead use:</p>

<pre><code>re.match(r"^Run.*\.py$", stringtocheck, re.I)
</code></pre>
