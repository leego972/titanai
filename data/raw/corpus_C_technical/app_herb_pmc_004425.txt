# PMC: adaptogen stress response HPA axis — Overview

<xref rid="med21743-bib-0008" ref-type="ref"> <sup>8</sup> </xref>, <xref rid="med21743-bib-0009" ref-type="ref"> <sup>9</sup> </xref>, <xref rid="med21743-bib-0010" ref-type="ref"> <sup>10</sup> </xref>, <xref rid="med21743-bib-0011" ref-type="ref"> <sup>11</sup> </xref>, <xref rid="med21743-bib-0012" ref-type="ref"> <sup>12</sup> </xref>, <xref rid="med21743-bib-0013" ref-type="ref"> <sup>13</sup> </xref>, <xref rid="med21743-bib-0014" ref-type="ref"> <sup>14</sup> </xref>, <xref rid="med21743-bib-0015" ref-type="ref"> <sup>15</sup> </xref>, <xref rid="med21743-bib-0016" ref-type="ref"> <sup>16</sup> </xref>, <xref rid="med21743-bib-0017" ref-type="ref"> <sup>17</sup> </xref> on the efficacy and safety of adaptogenic plants have been published in the last several decades. The aim of this review is to summarize our knowledge about common concept relating to adaptogenic plants used as officinal medical preparations in the USSR/Russian and in traditional Chinese medicine (TCM), Ayurveda, Kampo, and other traditional medical systems (TMS) and alternative medical systems, and to analyze how such preparations have been studied scientifically. This provides a basis for assessing the use of adaptogens in the treatment of stress‐induced and aging‐related disorders.</p><p>Adaptogens must be innocuous and cause minimal disorder in the physiological functions of an organism, and have nonspecific actions, that is, increase resistance to adverse influences of a wide range of factors with physical, chemical, and biological properties. In addition, they typically possess normalizing actions irrespective of the direction of the foregoing pathologic changes.</p><sec id="med21743-sec-0020"><label>1.1</label><title>Evolution of the adaptogenic concept: From postulates to evidence‐based statements</title><p>The term adaptogens is currently widely used in alternative and complementary medicine, as well as in pharmacognosy, phytomedicine, and phytotherapy research.<xref rid="med21743-bib-0005" ref-type="ref">
