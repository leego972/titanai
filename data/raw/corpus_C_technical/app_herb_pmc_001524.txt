# PMC: phytochemical bioactive compound anticancer — Overview

weight [<xref rid="B44-molecules-30-01186" ref-type="bibr">44</xref>,<xref rid="B45-molecules-30-01186" ref-type="bibr">45</xref>].</p><p>Amino acids are essential for protein biosynthesis, nitrogen transport, and stress response mechanisms. Uminska et al. analyzed the amino acid profile of <italic toggle="yes">C. angustifolium</italic> using GC-MS, identifying L-alanine (2.350–6.090 mg/g) and L-phenylalanine as the dominant amino acids, with moderate amounts of L-leucine, L-isoleucine, and L-valine. Interestingly, sulfur-containing amino acids are absent, potentially impacting the synthesis of specific sulfur-rich secondary metabolites. This strategic allocation of amino acids highlights their diverse structural and functional roles [<xref rid="B46-molecules-30-01186" ref-type="bibr">46</xref>,<xref rid="B47-molecules-30-01186" ref-type="bibr">47</xref>].</p><p>Fatty acids contribute to membrane integrity and bioactive lipid functions. Key compounds in <italic toggle="yes">Chamaenerion</italic> include linoleic acid, palmitic acid [<xref rid="B48-molecules-30-01186" ref-type="bibr">48</xref>], and n-hexadecenoic acid [<xref rid="B35-molecules-30-01186" ref-type="bibr">35</xref>].
