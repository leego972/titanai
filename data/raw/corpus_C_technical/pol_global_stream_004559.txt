# Maryland Route 194

the intersection is between MD 26 to the west and MD 194 to the north; the southernmost portion of MD 194 became an extension of the MD 26 divided highway. This configuration was chosen because two-thirds of traffic passing through the intersection was between Frederick and Woodsboro.

Auxiliary routes
MD 194 has four existing auxiliary routes and two that no longer exist. MD 194A and MD 194B are in Woodsboro.  MD 194D and MD 194E are north of Taneytown. Former MD 194C and MD 194F were also north of Taneytown.
MD 194A is the designation for Main Street, which runs  between MD 194 on the south side of Woodsboro and MD 550 on the north side of Woodsboro. MD 194A is municipally maintained for  from Mt. Hope Cemetery north to Coppermine Road.
MD 194B is the designation for a  section of Main Street that is now a spur south from MD 194A just north of MD 194's southern end at MD 194.
MD 194C was the designation for an unnamed  connector between MD 194 and former MD 853D.  MD 194C and MD 853D were removed from the state highway system in 2004 due to the roadway being overgrown.
MD 194D is the designation for an unnamed  connector between MD 194 and MD 853E, the old alignment that parallels the northbound direction of the modern highway south of Angell Road.
MD 194E is the designation for an unnamed  connector between MD 194 and MD 853A, the old alignment that parallels the southbound direction of the modern highway south of the Pennsylvania state line.
MD 194F was the designation for an unnamed  connector between MD 194 and former MD 853D.  MD 194F and MD 853D were removed from the state highway system in 2004 due to the roadway being overgrown.
