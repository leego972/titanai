# PMC: adaptogen stress response HPA axis — Overview

In this short communication is mentioned that 31 patients suffering from pronounced clinical symptoms of extrapyramidal syndrome, which resulted from therapy by neuroleptics, received a high dose of <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic> <italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract (25–40 drops; 2–3 times daily) for a duration of 1–1.5 months. In addition, 19 of these patients received a combination of a high dose of <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract and cholinolytic Romparkin (Trihexylphenidyl), as this preparation alone failed to eliminate or soothe the clinical symptoms of the side effects in this group of patients. The results of this study showed that <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract had the most pronounced therapeutic effect on symptoms of Parkinsonism and asthenia.</p><p>In conclusion, the introduction of <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract in the USSR was based on the following findings:
<list list-type="simple"><list-item><label>(i)</label><p>as stimulants for essentially healthy people in a state of fatigue and for patients with asthenic states during the rehabilitation period following somatic or infectious diseases.</p></list-item><list-item><label>(ii)</label><p>for essentially healthy people <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>

Schisandra chinensis</title><p><italic toggle="yes">Schisandra</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> extracts (e.g., tincture, decoction and tablets) have been reported to be effective in the treatment of general asthenia, exhaustion and reduced physical and mental performance in open label studies on a population of 2,000 poorly characterized mixed patients. It was reported that the extracts showed improvement after 2–10 weeks of therapy [<xref rid="B165-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">165</xref>,<xref rid="B166-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">166</xref>]. Furthermore, the treatment showed a particularly remarkable effect on a group of patients (n = 200) with nervous disorders where an increase in general well-being and working capacity, as well as a decrease in sleepiness and exhaustion [<xref rid="B165-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">165</xref>,<xref rid="B166-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">166</xref>]. Another study [<xref rid="B180-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">180</xref>], which was based on 13 patients, concluded that two administrations of <italic toggle="yes">S.</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> extract per day was optimal and that prolonged treatment may cause negative effects. The author pointed out that the duration of treatment should be decided on an individual patient basis. Taken together, the results of the treatment showed considerably improved results in short term patients, in comparison to chronic patients [<xref rid="B180-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">180</xref>].</p><p>A comparative clinical pilot study of <italic toggle="yes">S.</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> seeds (tincture) in neurasthenic patients (n = 95) showed that there was a high efficiency present in the therapy (dosage: 15 drops during 25–28 days). The results showed that general weakness, poor sleep and appetite, high irritability and headaches disappeared almost completely in the treatment group, whilst 55% of the control group complained of these symptoms. In addition, the muscular force of the hands increased around 2.5 times in the treatment group, in comparison to the control group. It was also seen that the gain of vital lung capacity reached 19% in the treatment group, in comparison to 3% in the control group [<xref rid="B181-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">181</xref>].</p><p>Several studies have reported a positive therapeutic effect of <italic toggle="yes">S.</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> preparations on asternic-and astheno-depressive states (particularly in stress induced depressions) snd schizophrenia [<xref rid="B184-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">184</xref>,<xref rid="B185-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">185</xref>,<xref rid="B186-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">186</xref>,<xref rid="B187-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">187</xref>,<xref rid="B188-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">188</xref>,<xref rid="B189-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">189</xref>,<xref rid="B190-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">190</xref>,<xref rid="B191-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">191</xref>,<xref rid="B192-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">192</xref>].
