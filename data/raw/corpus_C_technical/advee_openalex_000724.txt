Title: Constructional design, manufacturing and evaluation of high power density medium frequency transformer prototypes

Abstract:
The aim of this project is to manufacture and test two 60 kW, 1.2/3.6 kV, 6 kHz 46/28\nμH prototype transformers.\n• Based on the prepared high power medium frequency transformers’ design data, the\nconstructional design of two prototype transformers is performed. During the\nconstructional design, determination of the type, shape and volume of the suitable\nmagnetic, thermally conductive and dielectric materials available in the market was\ndone. The materials were bought and processed, and two prototype transformers were\nmanufactured. The important technical data regarding the insulation characteristics of\ninsulation materials was examined at nominal wave shape and frequency considered\nfor the transformer application and the insulation coordination of the transformers was\nupdated accordingly. At the same time, to prepare for the design validation tests,\nevaluation of the available high voltage, high current and medium frequency square\nwave shape supplies and the voltage and current measuring equipment was performed.\nThe transformers were subjected to a set of design validation tests and the results were\nrecorded, analysed and compared with the calculated values.\n• Key Words: Solid state transformer, Medium frequency Power transformer, DC-DC\nconverter, DC grid collection, Transformer testing and design validation
