</p><p>The aim of this review is to summarize the clinical applications of OCT in the field of medicine and dentistry, and highlight its potential advantages and disadvantages. Google Scholar and PubMed databases were searched using keywords including “optical coherence tomography,” “OCT,” and “OCT and its clinical applications.” Our search revealed 500+ hits; all the articles published in languages other than English and conference abstracts were excluded. Only the relevant articles published in the last 10 years (2011-2021) were included to keep the information recent and contemporary. Finally, 83 articles were selected for this study and included in our review.</p></sec><sec id="sec2"><title>Clinical applications of OCT</title><sec id="sec3"><title>Medical applications</title><p>OCT has been utilized by medical professionals extensively for diagnostic purposes and has several medical applications in ophthalmology, cardiology, otology, and dermatology. These applications will now be discussed below.</p><p>
<italic toggle="yes">Ophthalmology</italic>
</p><p>This technique can be used to accurately analyze choroidal thickness in the eyes under conditions such as diabetic neuropathy and macular degeneration due to aging. <sup><xref rid="ref11" ref-type="bibr">11</xref></sup> In fact, it can be said with authority that this imaging tool is nowadays a routine part of ophthalmology practice to identify macular lesions. <sup><xref rid="ref12" ref-type="bibr">12</xref></sup> The role of OCT is also pivotal in detecting other eye-related conditions like hyper-reflectivity due to macular telangiectasia type-2,
<sup><xref rid="ref13" ref-type="bibr">13</xref></sup> optic neuropathies,
<sup><xref rid="ref14" ref-type="bibr">14</xref></sup> and glaucomatous retinal nerve fiber layer loss. <sup><xref rid="ref15" ref-type="bibr">15</xref></sup> Papilledema is a serious medical condition that leads to visual disturbances and occurs due to the optic disc's swelling because of increased intraocular pressure;
<sup><xref rid="ref16" ref-type="bibr">16</xref></sup>
<sup>,</sup>
<sup><xref rid="ref17" ref-type="bibr">17</xref></sup> OCT technique can be used to measure the amount of optic disc edema in this condition. <sup><xref rid="ref18" ref-type="bibr">18</xref></sup>
</p><p>
<italic toggle="yes">Cardiology</italic>
</p><p>OCT can be used for various applications in the field of cardiology. In a recent study, it has been reported that OCT-based fractional flow reserve learning can be used for enhanced treatment of intermediate coronary artery stenosis. <sup><xref rid="ref19" ref-type="bibr">19</xref></sup> This technique can also be used for coronary imaging,
<sup><xref rid="ref20" ref-type="bibr">20</xref></sup> guiding percutaneous coronary intervention,
<sup><xref rid="ref21" ref-type="bibr">21</xref></sup> diagnosing myocardial infarction with non-obstructive coronary arteries,
<sup><xref rid="ref22" ref-type="bibr">22</xref></sup> and observing healed coronary plaques. <sup><xref rid="ref23" ref-type="bibr">23</xref></sup>
</p><p>
<italic toggle="yes">Otology</italic>
</p><p>OCT can be used to accurately diagnose otology conditions such as otitis media and conductive hearing loss. <sup><xref rid="ref24" ref-type="bibr">24</xref></sup> Other otology conditions where OCT has been used in the literature include diagnosis of middle ear effusions,
<sup><xref rid="ref25" ref-type="bibr">25</xref></sup> visualizing middle ear exudate,
<sup><xref rid="ref26" ref-type="bibr">26</xref></sup> observing microstructures of the middle and inner ear through an extra tympanic approach,
<sup><xref rid="ref27" ref-type="bibr">27</xref></sup> visualization of intra-cochlear structures for future cochlear implant surgery,
<sup><xref rid="ref28" ref-type="bibr">28</xref></sup> and to study endolymphatic hydrops that develops due to noise trauma. <sup><xref rid="ref29" ref-type="bibr">29</xref></sup>
</p><p>
<italic toggle="yes">Dermatology</italic>
</p><p>OCT has the potential to be used to image skin layers and associated structures. In a recent study, Kato
<italic toggle="yes">et al.</italic> utilized OCT to measure sweating and reported that this technique could be used successfully to measure and quantify sweating in the body.
