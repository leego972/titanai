It will be performed according to the following aspects:</p><list list-type="simple"><list-item><label>1.</label><p>Form of the herbal medicine used, such as granule or decoction</p></list-item><list-item><label>2.</label><p>Name of the herbal medicine used</p></list-item><list-item><label>3.</label><p>Sex and age of patients</p></list-item><list-item><label>4.</label><p>Treatment duration</p></list-item><list-item><label>5.</label><p>Duration and severity of the disease</p></list-item><list-item><label>6.</label><p>Type of control, such as no treatment, placebo, or conventional treatment</p></list-item></list></sec><sec><label>2.4.9</label><title>Sensitivity analysis</title><p>We will perform a sensitivity analysis to verify the robustness of the study results. This will be achieved by assessing the impact of the sample size, high risk of bias, missing data, and selected models. Following the analyses, if the quality of a study is judged to be low, it will be removed to ensure the robustness of the results.</p></sec><sec><label>2.4.10</label><title>Ethics and dissemination</title><p>Formal ethical approval is not required in this protocol. We will collect and analyze data based on published studies, and since no patients are directly or specifically assessed in this study, individual privacy will not be a concern. The results of this review will be disseminated to peer-reviewed journals or presented at a relevant conference.</p></sec></sec></sec><sec><label>3</label><title>Discussion</title><p>AD adversely affects not only the physical function but also the quality of life, resulting in an extensively increasing burden for both the patients and their caregivers.<sup>[<xref rid="R10" ref-type="bibr">10</xref>]</sup> However, there is yet no disease-modifying treatment, and therefore, effective treatment strategies for AD are required.</p><p>Studies have shown that herbal medicine has the potential to treat the symptoms of AD, and this might be due to the multitarget intervention effects of herbal medicine. The possible mechanisms for this are thought to be anti-inflammatory, antiapoptotic, and antioxidant actions,<sup>[<xref rid="R11" ref-type="bibr">11</xref>]</sup> which include protecting neurons from amyloid-β damage, inhibiting amyloid-β secretion, and suppressing amyloid-β-induced oxidative stress and apoptosis.<sup>[<xref rid="R12" ref-type="bibr">12</xref>]</sup> In this regard, herbal medicine may have the potential to overcome the limitations of conventional medicine.</p><p>The present review will be conducted to assess the effectiveness and safety of herbal medicine in AD, aimed at establishing management strategies to benefit and lessen the burden of practitioners as well as patients and their families.</p></sec><sec><title>Acknowledgments</title><p>This study was supported by the Traditional Korean Medicine R&amp;D program funded by the Ministry of Health &amp; Welfare through the Korea Health Industry Development Institute (KHIDI, HB16C0051).</p></sec><sec><title>Author contributions</title><p><bold>Conceptualization:</bold> JiEun Lee, Seungwon Kwon</p><p><bold>Data curation:</bold> Chul Jin, Seung-Yeon Cho, Woo-Sang Jung</p><p><bold>Formal analysis:</bold> Chul Jin, Sang-Kwan Moon, Seong-Uk Park, Jung-Mi Park</p><p><bold>Funding acquisition:</bold> Ki-Ho Cho</p><p><bold>Project administration:</bold> Seungwon Kwon</p><p><bold>Writing – original draft:</bold> JiEun Lee, Seungwon Kwon</p><p><bold>Writing – review &amp; editing:</bold> Seungwon Kwon, Ki-Ho Cho, Chang-Nam Ko</p></sec></body><back><fn-group><fn fn-type="abbr"><p>Abbreviations: AD = Alzheimer disease, CI = confidence interval, MD = mean difference, MMSE = Mini-Mental State Examination, PRISMA = preferred reporting items for systematic reviews and meta-analysis, RCT = randomized controlled trial.</p></fn><fn fn-type="other"><p>How to cite this article: Lee J, Jin C, Cho SY, Park SU, Jung WS, Moon SK, Park JM, Ko CN, Cho KH, Kwon S.
