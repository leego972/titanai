# John B. McClelland

ring Lt. Col. John B. McClelland has been installed where his wife, Martha Dale McClelland (1741–1822); son, John B McClelland (1766–1849); and daughter-in-law, Rachel Orr McClelland (1770–1843); are buried, at Tent Presbyterian Church Cemetery (275 Tent Church Road, Uniontown, Pennsylvania).
