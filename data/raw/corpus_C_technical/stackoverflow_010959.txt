Question: <p>I've read some about .egg files and I've noticed them in my lib directory but what are the advantages/disadvantages of using then as a developer?</p>

Answer: <p>For simple Python programs, you probably don't need to use eggs. Distributing the raw .py files should suffice; it's like distributing source files for GNU/Linux. You can also use the various OS "packagers" (like py2exe or py2app) to create .exe, .dmg, or other files for different operating systems.</p>

<p>More complex programs, e.g. Django, pretty much require eggs due to the various modules and dependencies required.</p>
