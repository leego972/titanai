The A-line following phase correction (<xref rid="g002" ref-type="fig">Fig. 2(c)</xref>) shows that the distortion due to f<sub>LO</sub>-based phase discontinuities is largely suppressed. <xref rid="g002" ref-type="fig">Figure 2(d)</xref> and Fig. S1,a-c shows the result of applying this correction to a measured interferogram from a mirror with the same f<sub>OCT</sub> and down-converted with the same f<sub>LO</sub>. Phase correction yielded a distortion suppression of -8 dB with a PSF closely resembling the source A-line acquired without mixing. We explored the performance of the phase correction in the f<sub>OCT</sub> range of 235-1488 MHz (Fig. S1,d-h). The results reveal that distortion suppression is reduced with increasing f<sub>OCT</sub>.
