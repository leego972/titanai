Question: <p>I've got a web system where users log in, and it stores a cookie of their session. When they log in as someone else or log out I want to remove that original cookie that I stored. What's the best way to do that?
I'm using Python and Apache, though I suppose the answer will remain the same for most languages.</p>

Answer: <p>Set the cookie again, as if you hadn't set it the first time, but specify an expiration date that is in the past.</p>
