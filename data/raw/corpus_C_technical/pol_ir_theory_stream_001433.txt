# Eyre Crowe (painter)

The painting was not originally well received by the public due to its unusually unromantic depiction of Urban life for the time. Critics called it "Vulgar" and "Unpictoral". However, it is held by many current art historians as one of the more realistic depictions of urban working life of its time.
