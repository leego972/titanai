Clinic Application</title><sec id="sec6dot1-molecules-21-01702"><title>6.1. Antirheumatic Effect</title><p>From time immemorial, <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> has been regarded as an available medicine to treat rheumatism. Because of its peculiarity, it is used as an external preparation, such as Huoxuezhitong sticking and Tianhezhuifeng sticking. Additionally, some medicinal liquors including <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> show distinct antirheumatic action.</p></sec><sec id="sec6dot2-molecules-21-01702"><title>6.2. Chronic Congestive Heart Failure Action</title><p>Extraction of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> was made into tablets, 10 mg per tablet, oral administration three times a day for four or five days. In a 21 case study, 12 cases were found to be remarkable effective. It can be found that <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> has an effective action to treat chronic congestive heart failure [<xref rid="B5-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">5</xref>].</p></sec></sec><sec id="sec7-molecules-21-01702"><title>7. Toxicity</title><p>Based on the traditional Chinese medicine classes, <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> is prudently recorded as a toxic medicine. <italic toggle="yes">Sichuanzhongyaozhi</italic> labeled it as “toxic, not appropriate to use for long time”. It is obvious that people long ago recognized the toxicity of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic>. Sun et al. found that all components, water extraction components, and alcohol extraction components of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> showed the acute toxicity on mice [<xref rid="B63-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">63</xref>]. Furthermore, through accumulative toxicity experiments, rats indicated serious reactions under a large dosage of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> extraction [<xref rid="B64-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">64</xref>]. Many conditions may cause intoxication such as over-dosage, drug confusion, incompatibility with herbs, and improper decoction causing life-threatening complications. Hence, it is necessary to normalize its dosage and administration.</p><p>In the Chinese Pharmacopoeia, the dosage of “xiangjiapi” ranges from 3 g/60 kg/day to 6 g/60 kg/day. Periplocin is its main toxic component, which can express cardiotonic action with unsuitable dosages. In the usual application, people always notice its great tonic effect, but not its potential toxicity. Although Tong et al. established an RP-HPLC method to determine the content of periplocin, due to extreme differences of periplocin content in <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> from different provinces, it is difficult to control toxic effects [<xref rid="B65-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">65</xref>]. In recent years, many adverse events have often occurred. The toxic ingredients are also the effective constituents, so there is a need to strengthen knowledge about using this herb to prevent adverse outcomes. This will be beneficial in guiding reasonable clinical prescription.</p></sec><sec id="sec8-molecules-21-01702"><title>8. Conclusions</title><p>As a traditional Chinese herbal medicine, <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> has been widely used to treat autoimmune diseases, especially rheumatoid arthritis. In the field of phytochemistry, it is reported to contain many compounds, including pregnane glycosides, steroids, volatile oils, polysaccharides, fatty acids, and cardiac glycosides. Although more than 100 compounds were obtained from this herb, further systematic phytochemistry research should be conducted. In the study of pharmacology, the extracts and chemical components from this plant focused on anti-inflammatory, antitumor, and immune functions. Although many chemical constituent analyses and pharmacological activity studies of this herb have been reported, the pharmacological mechanism of action and the metabolites responsible for these activities should be studied further. In addition, <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> was proven to be toxic in its clinical application; while its cardiotonic components are useful for the treatment of heart failure. Therefore, clarifying the mechanism, ensuring effectiveness, and minimizing risk are of great significance to more effective utilization of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic>. More studies should be carried out and methods should be developed to ensure an effective therapeutic effect with minimal toxicity in clinical applications.</p></sec></body><back><ack><title>Acknowledgments</title><p>This research was supported by the Science &amp; Technology Commission of MOST of China (2014ZX09304307001), National Natural Science Foundation of China (81503213, 81303140 and 81673824), National Science and Technology Support Program Projects (2014BA105B01), and PCSIRT (IRT-14R41).</p></ack><fn-group><fn><p><bold>Sample Availability:</bold> Samples of the compounds are not available from the authors.</p></fn></fn-group><notes notes-type="COI-statement"><title>Conflicts of Interest</title><p>The authors declare no conflict of interest.
