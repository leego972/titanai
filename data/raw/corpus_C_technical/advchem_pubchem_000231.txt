Description:
Phosphonoacetaldehyde is a phosphonic acid consisting of acetaldehyde with the phospho group at the 2-position. It has a role as a mouse metabolite. It is functionally related to an acetaldehyde and a phosphonic acid. It is a conjugate acid of a phosphonoacetaldehyde(1-).
