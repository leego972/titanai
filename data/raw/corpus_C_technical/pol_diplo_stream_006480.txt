# Chotín

Chotín (, Hungarian pronunciation:) is a village and municipality in the Komárno District in the Nitra Region of south-western Slovakia.

Geography
The village lies at an altitude of 109 metres and covers an area of 20.425 km².
It has a population of about 1,410 people.

History
In the 9th century, the territory of Chotín became part of the Kingdom of Hungary.
In historical records the village was first mentioned in 1138.
After the Austro-Hungarian army disintegrated in November 1918, Czechoslovak troops occupied the area, later acknowledged internationally by the Treaty of Trianon. Between 1938 and 1945 Chotín once more became part of Miklós Horthy's Hungary through the First Vienna Award. From 1945 until the Velvet Divorce, it was part of Czechoslovakia. Since then it has been part of Slovakia.

Demographics
The village is about 87% Hungarian, 12.5% Slovak.

Facilities
The village has a public library, a gym and a football pitch.
