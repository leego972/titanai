Description:
O-phosphoserine is a serine derivative that is serine substituted at the oxygen atom by a phosphono group. It has a role as a human metabolite. It is an O-phosphoamino acid, a serine derivative and a non-proteinogenic alpha-amino acid. It is a conjugate acid of an O-phosphonatooxyserine(2-).
