Question: <p>I am opening a process (with os.popen() ) that, for some commands, detects certain keypresses (e.g. ESC - not the character, the key).  Is there a way to send keypress events to the process?</p>

Answer: <p>The obvious way would be to start the process in it's own shell.<br />
something like os.popen("sh command")  </p>
