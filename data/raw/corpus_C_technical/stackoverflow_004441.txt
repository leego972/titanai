Question: <p>For a list <code>["foo", "bar", "baz"]</code> and an item in the list <code>"bar"</code>, what's the cleanest way to get its index (1) in Python?</p>

Answer: <p>all indexes with zip function</p>

<pre><code>get_indexes = lambda x, xs: [i for (y, i) in zip(xs, range(len(xs))) if x == y]

print get_indexes(2,[1,2,3,4,5,6,3,2,3,2])
print get_indexes('f','xsfhhttytffsafweef')
</code></pre>
