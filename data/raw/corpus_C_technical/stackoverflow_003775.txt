Question: <p>If I have two dates (ex. <code>'8/18/2008'</code> and <code>'9/26/2008'</code>) what is the best way to get the difference measured in days?</p>

Answer: <p>Days until Christmas:</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; import datetime
&gt;&gt;&gt; today = datetime.date.today()
&gt;&gt;&gt; someday = datetime.date(2008, 12, 25)
&gt;&gt;&gt; diff = someday - today
&gt;&gt;&gt; diff.days
86
</code></pre>
