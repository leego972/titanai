Question: <p>I know <code>class foo(object)</code> is an old school way of defining a class. But I would like to understand in more detail the difference between these two.</p>

Answer: <p><code>class foo(object):</code> is the 'new' way of declaring classes.</p>

<p>This change was made in python 2.2, see <a href="https://docs.python.org/whatsnew/2.2.html" rel="nofollow">this PEP for an explanation</a> of the differences. </p>
