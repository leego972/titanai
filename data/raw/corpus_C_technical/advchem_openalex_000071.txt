Title: Recent Advances in Chan-Evans-Lam Coupling Reaction

Abstract:
Chan-Evans-Lam reaction has been served as a powerful cross-coupling reaction in organic synthesis since its discovery in the 1990s owing to the feature of mild reaction conditions, functional group tolerance and open-flask chemistry. Up to now, it has been not only extensively utilized in carbon-heteroatom or carbon-carbon bonds formation, but also successfully applied to synthesize natural products and alkaloids. The new strategies of Chan-Evans-Lam reaction in organic synthesis as well as applications in total synthesis in recent five years are discussesed.
