Question: <p>I know <code>class foo(object)</code> is an old school way of defining a class. But I would like to understand in more detail the difference between these two.</p>

Answer: <p>Subclassing <code>object</code> yields a new-style class. Two well known advantages of new-style classes are:</p>

<ul>
<li>Metaclasses (like class factories, but works transparently)</li>
<li>Properties (getters &amp; setters...)</li>
</ul>
