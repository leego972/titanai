[Personalized medicine: equity and access].

[Article in French]

Joly Y(1), Knoppers BM(1).

Author information:
(1)Centre de génomique et politique, université McGill, 740 avenue du 
Dr Penfield, Montréal, Québec H3A 0G1, Canada.

Personalized medicine has seen a recent increase in popularity amongst medical 
researchers and policymakers. Nevertheless, there are persistent legal, ethical, 
and social questions that need to be explored, particularly related to the 
criticism that personalized medicine constitutes an elitist model of healthcare. 
Investigating this critique the current manuscript argues that personalized 
medicine has the potential to become a positive force for equitable access to 
better healthcare at a national and international level.
