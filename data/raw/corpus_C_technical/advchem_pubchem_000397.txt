Description:
N-carbamoylglycine is a member of the class of ureas obtained by formal condensation of the carboxy group of carbamic acid with the amino group of glycine. It has a role as a xenobiotic metabolite and a rat metabolite. It is a N-acylglycine, a monocarboxylic acid and a member of ureas. It is functionally related to a glycine. It is a conjugate acid of a N-carbamoylglycinate.
