# Oliveira (surname)

ropean *hloywom. Its first documented use dates back to the 13th century, from Évora noble Pedro de Oliveira, and his son, Braga archbishop D. Martinho Pires de Oliveira. Further tracing of its origins show that it derives from  ancient Roman aristrocats from the gens Oliva.
