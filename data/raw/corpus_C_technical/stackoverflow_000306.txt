Question: <p>What's the <strong>easiest, tersest, and most flexible</strong> method or library for parsing Python command line arguments?</p>

Answer: <p>I prefer optparse to getopt. It's very declarative: you tell it the names of the options and the effects they should have (e.g., setting a boolean field), and it hands you back a dictionary populated according to your specifications.</p>
