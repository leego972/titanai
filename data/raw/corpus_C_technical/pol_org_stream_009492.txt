# Tkachuk

Tkachuk, Tkaczuk, or Tkatchuk  (Ukrainian: Ткачук) is a common Ukrainian surname in Ukraine and the Ukrainian diaspora. The name in Ukrainian stands for the name of occupation, weaver.
