# PMC: phytochemical bioactive compound anticancer — Overview

Flavonoids are well-known for their pharmacological properties and their consumption is associated with reduced risk of several chronic illnesses such as cancer, cardiovascular diseases and neurodegenerative disorders [<xref rid="B129-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">129</xref>]. For instance, the flavone apigenin (<bold>221</bold>), only reported in <italic toggle="yes">C. dactylon</italic>, is recognized for its anti-inflammatory (inhibition of E-selectin expression, IC<sub>50</sub> 17.7 µM) [<xref rid="B130-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">130</xref>], antianxiety (dietary administered, 10 mg/kg dose) [<xref rid="B131-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">131</xref>] and anticarcinogenic activities [<xref rid="B132-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">132</xref>,<xref rid="B133-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">133</xref>]. The flavonoid epicatechin (<bold>226</bold>) (only described in <italic toggle="yes">Z. aquatica</italic>) proved their value as an anti-fatigue agent, by inducing structural and metabolic changes in skeletal and cardiac muscles, ultimately leading to endurance capacity (dietary administered, 1 mg/kg dose) [<xref rid="B134-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">134</xref>]. This compound also possesses the ability to stimulate myocardial angiogenesis through rising protein levels and activation of canonical angiogenesis pathway (dietary administered, 1 mg/kg dose) [<xref rid="B135-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">135</xref>]. Kaempferol (<bold>231</bold>), present in <italic toggle="yes">C. dactylon</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">C. gayana</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">D. bipinnata</italic>, and <italic toggle="yes">Z. aquatica</italic>, is well-known for its antimicrobial activity with MIC values ranging from 32 μg/mL to 512 μg/mL and antioxidant activity with an IC<sub>50</sub> value of 52.48 μg/mL [<xref rid="B136-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">136</xref>]. The anti-inflammatory property of kaempferol is linked to activity towards NF-κB (nuclear factor kappa-light-chain-enhancer of activated B cells) pathway proteins (IC<sub>50</sub>: 10 µM) [<xref rid="B137-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">137</xref>]. This compound has also been able to inhibit the growth of HT-29 human colon cancer cells at a concentration of 60 µmol/L (IC<sub>50</sub>) [<xref rid="B138-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">138</xref>]. The flavanone-7-<italic toggle="yes">O</italic>-glycoside, naringin (<bold>235</bold>), is reported to display antioxidant (IC<sub>50</sub>: 0.5 mg/mL) [<xref rid="B139-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">139</xref>], anti-inflammatory (oral administered, 15.8 mg/kg/day dose) [<xref rid="B140-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">140</xref>], antihypertensive (dietary administered, 20, 40 and 80 mg/kg doses) [<xref rid="B141-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">141</xref>] and hypolipidemic activities [<xref rid="B142-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">142</xref>]. The same activities are also recognized in quercetin (<bold>238</bold>) [<xref rid="B143-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">143</xref>,<xref rid="B144-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">144</xref>]. The flavonol glycoside, rutin (<bold>241</bold>) has a vast array of health benefits and pharmacological activities which includes antinociceptive, antarthritic, anti-diabetic, hypercholesteraemic, antiplatelet aggregation and antiasthmatic activities. A recent and complete review on this aspect, led by Ganeshpurkar and Saluja, (2017), is available [<xref rid="B145-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">145</xref>]. These inherent properties of flavonoids categorize them as a class of beneficial compounds that have health-promoting and disease-preventing effects.</p><p>This is an important class of compounds from a pharmacological and nutritional point of view. There is evidence that points to lower risk of chronic diseases such as cancer and cardiovascular disorders for those who have a diet rich in flavonoids. Additionally, the species studied regarding this aspect showed a great diversity of compounds, enriching the nutraceutical value of halophytic grasses. </p></sec><sec id="sec2dot7-ijms-20-01067"><title>2.7.
