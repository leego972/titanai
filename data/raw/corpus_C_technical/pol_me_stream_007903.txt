# Scott Laird

three brothers, Craig, Jamie and Callum. Craig played soccer at the University of Tampa and also represented England at schoolboy level, whilst both Jamie and Callum have played semi-professional football. Laird's grandmother gave him a one pound coin every time he scored a goal.
