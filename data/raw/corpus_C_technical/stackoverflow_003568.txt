Answer: <p>The only difficulty I've seen people encounter with Python's in particular is when they try to mix non-functional features like variable reassignment with closures, and are surprised when this doesn't work:</p>

<pre><code>def outer ():
    x = 1
    def inner ():
        print x
        x = 2
    return inner
outer () ()
</code></pre>

<p>Usually just pointing out that a function has its own local variables is enough to deter such silliness.</p>
