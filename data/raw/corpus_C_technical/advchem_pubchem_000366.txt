Description:
Roxarsone is an organoarsonic acid where the organyl group is 4-hydroxy-3-nitrophenyl. It has a role as an agrochemical, a coccidiostat, an antibacterial drug and an animal growth promotant. It is an organoarsonic acid and a member of 2-nitrophenols. It is functionally related to a phenylarsonic acid.
