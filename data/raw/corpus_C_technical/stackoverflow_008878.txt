Question: <p>When I call the connect function of the Paramiko <code>SSHClient</code> class, it outputs some log data about establishing the connection, which I would like to suppress.</p>

<p>Is there a way to do this either through Paramiko itself, or Python in general?</p>

Answer: <p>I don't know what Paramiko is, and there must be a log level setting for sure, but if you are desperate and looking for a temporary solution and if your app is single threaded</p>

<pre><code>import sys
dev_null = sys.stdout = sys.stderr = open('/dev/null', 'w')
try:
.
. connect()
.
finally:
  dev_null.close()
</code></pre>

<p>you can use StringIO for output also, if you are on an OS not have a '/dev/null'</p>
