However, its phytochemical potential and pharmacological mechanisms have not yet been adequately investigated. This review consolidates current data on the bioactive chemicals and therapeutic potential of <italic toggle="yes">Poikilospermum</italic> spp. with an emphasis on <italic toggle="yes">Poikilospermum suaveolens</italic> (Blume) Merr.</p></sec><sec id="s2002"><title>Methodology</title><p>A comprehensive search was performed using the PubMed, Scopus, Google Scholar, and Web of Science databases. This study examined publications from 2015 to 2025 that explored the phytochemical composition and pharmacological properties of <italic toggle="yes">Poikilospermum</italic> species. Terms such as “Poikilospermum”, “inflammatory”, “phytochemicals”, “anticancer”, “ethnobotany”, “phytochemistry”, “pharmacological”, “antimicrobial”, and “antioxidant”.</p></sec><sec id="s2003"><title>Results</title><p>The investigation revealed various bioactive components such as flavonoids, tannins, alkaloids, and terpenoids, which contribute to the antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant capabilities of <italic toggle="yes">Poikilospermum</italic>. Initial data indicated possible anticancer properties of <italic toggle="yes">P. suaveolens</italic>; however, additional research is required to validate these effects and elucidate the underlying pathways.</p></sec><sec id="s2004"><title>Conclusion</title><p><italic toggle="yes">Poikilospermum</italic> species, particularly <italic toggle="yes">P. suaveolens</italic>, is a promising source of bioactives; priority next steps include standardized in vivo studies, toxicity assessment, and targeted mechanistic work. Subsequent studies should focus on clinical trials and more in-depth inquiries into the mechanisms of action of identified substances.</p></sec></abstract><abstract abstract-type="graphical"><title>Graphical Abstract</title><p>
