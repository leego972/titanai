Question: <p>What do I need to look at to see if I'm on Windows, Unix, etc?</p>

Answer: <p>Watch out if you're on Windows with Cygwin where <code>os.name</code> is <code>posix</code>.</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; import os, platform
&gt;&gt;&gt; print os.name
posix
&gt;&gt;&gt; print platform.system()
CYGWIN_NT-6.3-WOW
</code></pre>
