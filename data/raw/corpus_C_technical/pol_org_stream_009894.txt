# Stan Parrish

0 season.

Siena Heights
In April 2011, Parrish was appointed the quarterbacks coach at Siena Heights University prior to the inaugural season of the school's new football program under head coach Jim Lyall. His role was on a voluntary basis, which allowed him to coach without any recruiting duties.

Eastern Michigan
In January 2013 Parrish was named the offensive coordinator and quarterbacks coach for Eastern Michigan University under head coach Ron English for the upcoming season. Stan Parrish was named the interim head coach when English was fired after a 1–8 start to the 2013 season. The Eagles won their first game under Parrish's guidance, putting them in position to retain the Michigan MAC Trophy.

Personal life and death
He was married to Ruth Purdy, with whom he had a daughter and son. Parrish died on April 3, 2022, at the age of 75.
