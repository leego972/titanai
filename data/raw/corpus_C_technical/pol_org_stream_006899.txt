# Honda G engine

The Honda G-series engine is a family of slanted inline-five cylinder gasoline engines. The engine family features a single overhead cam with 4 valves per cylinder. They were originally used in the 1989 Honda Vigor, Honda Rafaga, Honda Ascot and Honda Inspire. They then carried over to the Vigor's successor; the Acura TL, which ran from 1995 to 1998 in North America, and the Honda Saber in Japan.
