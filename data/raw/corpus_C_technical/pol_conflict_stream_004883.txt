# Asa P. Blunt

1877 and 1888 Blunt was Commandant of the United States Disciplinary Barracks at Fort Leavenworth, Kansas.

Blunt was promoted to permanent major on September 29, 1889.  He died in Manchester, New Hampshire on October 4, 1889.
