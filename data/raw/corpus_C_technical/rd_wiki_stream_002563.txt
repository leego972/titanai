# Kazlų Rūda

Kazlų Rūda () is a city in Lithuania. It is located  north from Marijampolė. The city is surrounded by forests, but a railway line crosses the city and divides it into almost equal parts. Inter IKEA Holding operates a manufacturing plant in the city. The former Soviet Army Kazlų Rūda airbase is  northeast of Kazlų Rūda.
