(Biol.) Defn: The assimilation by one body or organism of the elements of another. METEOR Me"te*or, n. Etym: [F. météore, Gr. 1. Any phenomenon or appearance in the atmosphere, as clouds, rain, hail, snow, etc. Hail, an ordinary meteor. Bp. Hall. 2. Specif.: A transient luminous body or appearance seen in the atmosphere, or in a more elevated region. The vaulty top of heaven Figured quite o'er with burning meteors. Shak.
