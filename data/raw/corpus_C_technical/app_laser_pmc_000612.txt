Once ceased the excitation, the electrons escape from the traps and re-enter the conduction band under the stimulation of external factors, recombining with emitter to emit PersL (process 1′) [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR25">25</xref>]. Upon the stimulus of LED light, the electrons of emitter are excited from the ground state to the corresponding excited state. Subsequently, the electrons are captured by surrounding traps through the quantum tunneling (process 2). Once stopped the stimulation, the electrons in the trap are recombined with emitter through quantum tunneling, emitting PersL (process 2′) [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR26">26</xref>]. The PersL mechanism of X-ray or 980 nm laser excitation is similar to the above-mentioned mechanisms, except that there is a process of energy transfer under X-ray or 980 nm laser excitation. The excitation energy of X-ray or 980 nm laser is transferred to the emitter through the host or Yb<sup>3+</sup>-Er<sup>3+</sup>/Yb<sup>3+</sup>-Tm<sup>3+</sup> causing the above-mentioned series of electrons transition, capture, release, and recombination of the emitter, and finally produce PersL [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR22">22</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR27">27</xref>]. So far, the mechanism for radiopharmaceuticals is still unclear. However, most of radiopharmaceuticals can emit gamma radiation and Cerenkov luminescence during the decay of radionuclides[<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR28">28</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR29">29</xref>], where gamma ray is similar to X-ray but come from different parts of the atom [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR30">30</xref>], and Cerenkov luminescence have the emission in the range of 250–600 nm [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR29">29</xref>], thus we speculate that the mechanism of radiopharmaceuticals-excited PersL includes the PersL mechanisms of X-ray, UV light, and LED light.<fig id="Fig2" position="float" orientation="portrait"><label>Fig. 2</label><caption><p>Schematic diagrams of the PersL mechanism under different excitation sources</p></caption><graphic xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink" id="MO3" position="float" orientation="portrait" xlink:href="12951_2021_862_Fig2_HTML.jpg"/></fig></p></sec><sec id="Sec4"><title>Synthesis of PLNPs</title><p id="Par8">The traditional PersL materials are mainly synthesized by solid-state reaction at high temperature [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR9">9</xref>].
