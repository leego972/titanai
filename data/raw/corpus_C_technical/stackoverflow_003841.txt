Question: <p>Is there a framework equivalent to Guice (<a href="http://code.google.com/p/google-guice">http://code.google.com/p/google-guice</a>) for Python?</p>

Answer: <p>I haven't used it, but the <a href="http://springpython.webfactional.com/">Spring Python</a> framework is based on Spring and implements <a href="http://static.springsource.org/spring-python/1.2.x/sphinx/html/objects.html">Inversion of Control</a>.</p>

<p>There also appears to be a Guice in Python project: <a href="http://code.google.com/p/snake-guice/">snake-guice</a></p>
