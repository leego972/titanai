Question: <p>What is the library? Is there a full implementation? How is the library used? Where is its website?</p>

Answer: <p>Use LXML. LXML uses the full power of libxml2 and libxslt, but wraps them in more "Pythonic" bindings than the Python bindings that are native to those libraries. As such, it gets the full XPath 1.0 implementation. Native ElemenTree supports a limited subset of XPath, although it may be good enough for your needs.</p>
