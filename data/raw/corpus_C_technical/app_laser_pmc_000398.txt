In one of them, 10 days after two sessions of PDT imiquimod was applied, five times a week for three weeks. Clinical clearing was obtained without recurrence over 15 months [<xref rid="B105-ijms-16-25912" ref-type="bibr">105</xref>]. In another study, after three sessions of MAL-PDT, imiquimod was applied five times a week for six weeks. Three months later skin biopsies showed 22%–40% of lesion size reduction [<xref rid="B44-ijms-16-25912" ref-type="bibr">44</xref>]. Osiecka <italic toggle="yes">et al.</italic> [<xref rid="B106-ijms-16-25912" ref-type="bibr">106</xref>] treated 10 patients with ALA-PDT, and 24 patients with ALA-PDT plus imiquimod. Six patients (60%) were totally cured in the first group and four lesions (40%) decreased in size, whereas in the second group 18 lesions (75%) totally disappeared and six diminished. Requena <italic toggle="yes">et al.</italic> [<xref rid="B107-ijms-16-25912" ref-type="bibr">107</xref>] reported a case of a giant recurrent basal cell carcinoma on the face successfully treated by a combination of MAL-PDT and imiquimod.</p><p>Regarding the combination with lasers, Smucler and Vlk studied the effectiveness of three different methods for the treatment of recurring nodular BCCs: ALA-PDT, Er:YAG laser, and the combination of both; this was the most effective, with an efficacy rate of 98.97% <italic toggle="yes">vs.</italic> 94.85% (PDT) and 91.75% (Er:YAG laser), and the best aesthetic results [<xref rid="B108-ijms-16-25912" ref-type="bibr">108</xref>]. Later, the same group divided 67 patients into three groups based on tumor depth: &lt;2 mm (PDT), 2–3 mm (Er:YAG laser ablation and PDT), and &gt;3 mm (diode laser ablation and PDT). The treatment consisted of application of laser followed by MAL-PDT, repeated one and three weeks later. After six months, 100% clearance rate was observed in the group with the deepest tumors, 94.7% in that with tumors 2–3 mm in depth, and 81.2% in the group with the most superficial ones [<xref rid="B109-ijms-16-25912" ref-type="bibr">109</xref>]. In other study, twelve patients were treated using combined CO<sub>2</sub> laser and PDT, and after 18.1 months of follow-up no recurrences were present [<xref rid="B110-ijms-16-25912" ref-type="bibr">110</xref>].
