A one-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) test was performed to compare the differences between the three maturity levels of leaf extracts. The leaves in day-30 showed the lowest concentration of (Fe<sup>2+</sup>) (mM) while day-45 and day-60 had almost the same concentration of (Fe<sup>2+</sup>) (mM). There were no significant differences (<italic toggle="yes">p</italic> &gt; 0.05) in ferric reducing power between the three maturity levels of <italic toggle="yes">M. oleifera</italic> leaf extract.
