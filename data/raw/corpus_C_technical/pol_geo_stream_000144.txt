# Typhoon Xangsane

mmittee selected the name Leepi to replace Xangsane on the Western Pacific basin name lists beginning in 2008. In 2010 the name selected by PAGASA to replace Milenyo was Mario, but the name itself was retired and was replaced with "Maymay" after devastating Metro Manila and Northern Luzon.
