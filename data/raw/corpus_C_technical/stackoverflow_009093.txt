Question: <p>How do I connect to Gmail and determine which messages have attachments?  I then want to download each attachment, printing out the Subject: and From: for each message as I process it.</p>

Answer: <p>You should be aware of the fact that you need SSL to connect to GMail (both for POP3 and IMAP - this is of course true also for their SMTP-servers apart from port 25 but that's another story).</p>
