# UNIT: The Longest Night

UNIT: The Longest Night is a Big Finish Productions audio drama based on the long-running British science fiction television series Doctor Who. The Limited series (television) continues the story of UNIT (United Nations Intelligence Taskforce). "The Longest Night" is the fourth episode of five.

Plot 
The UNIT team are enjoying a night out in London when, suddenly, suicide bombers strike.
