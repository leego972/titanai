# Akeem Agbetu

Akeem Oriyomi Agbetu (born 10 March 1988) is a Nigerian professional footballer, who plays as a striker.

Career
For the 2014–2015 season Agbetu joined Le Havre AC of Ligue 2 agreeing a two-year contract.
