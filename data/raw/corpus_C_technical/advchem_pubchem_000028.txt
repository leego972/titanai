Description:
2,3-bisphosphoglyceric acid is a bisphosphoglyceric acid that is glyceric acid carrying two phospho substituents at positions 2 and 3. It has a role as a human metabolite. It is a bisphosphoglyceric acid and a tetronic acid derivative. It is functionally related to a glyceric acid. It is a conjugate acid of a 2,3-bisphosphoglycerate.
