Israel placed fourth in the final, scoring 154 points.

The semi-final received a market share of 22.5% in Israel with a peak of 31.1%, while the final received a market share of 39.5% in Israel with a peak of 47.9%. Both shows were televised live on Channel 1. The Israeli spokesperson, who announced the Israeli votes during the final, was Dana Herman.
