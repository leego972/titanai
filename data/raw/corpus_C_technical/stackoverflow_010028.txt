Answer: <p>Thanks for lots of nice anwser, this is a hackerrank challenge about <strong>groupby</strong>, this is <a href="https://www.hackerrank.com/challenges/compress-the-string/submissions/code/18455859" rel="nofollow">link</a>.</p>
