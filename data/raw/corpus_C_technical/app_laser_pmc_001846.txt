It should be noted that, increased expression of this growth factor was greater in cells treated with the combination therapy simultaneously (DNC and LLLT treatment at the same time) than combination therapy of 4 hours apart (laser irradiation 4 hours after DNC treatment).</p><p>Formerly, researchers have shown that curcumin [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref039" ref-type="bibr">39</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref060" ref-type="bibr">60</xref>] and laser therapy [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref043" ref-type="bibr">43</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref051" ref-type="bibr">51</xref>] at specific doses and under certain conditions were able to enhance angiogenesis by increasing the expression of the most important pro-angiogenic ligand called VEGF specifically in the influential cells (fibroblasts) producing this ligand for endothelial cells at the site of the lesion. VEGF has been well known for its pro-angiogenic potency through stimulation of endothelial cell proliferation, migration, and tube formation activities [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref045" ref-type="bibr">45</xref>]. According to the results obtained, a significant increase in VEGF expression post-treated with DNC (0.75μM) and LLLT 450 nm (0.95 j/cm<sup>2</sup>) was perceived. In addition, the observations of this study suggest that the increased expression of this gene in simultaneous combination treatment was more pronounced.</p><p>Inflammation has been considered as the vital second phase of the wound healing process manipulated by cytokines and growth factors. While key inflammatory cells at the site of injury assumed to be the immune cells that clean the wound from infection and damaged structural proteins as well as play chemotactic role in inviting fibroblasts and other cells involved, damaged fibroblasts from nonhealing wounds have been shown to produce higher levels of pro-inflammatory cytokines and represent poor responses to growth factors as well. Previous reports have shown that prolonged up-regulation of inflammatory cytokines such as TNF-α which induces IL-6 production as well can cause local inflammation and persistent localized inflammation have the potency to damage the tissue [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref031" ref-type="bibr">31</xref>]. Inevitably, controlling the prolonged inflammation may optimize the wound healing process accelerating the proliferation phase entrance [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref061" ref-type="bibr">61</xref>–<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref063" ref-type="bibr">63</xref>]. Many researchers have demonstrated that curcumin, as well as blue light at 450 nm at very low doses, modulates inflammation by reducing the expression of pro-inflammatory cytokines including TNF-α and IL-6 [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref018" ref-type="bibr">18</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref064" ref-type="bibr">64</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref065" ref-type="bibr">65</xref>]. Herein, we obtained a significant decline in the expression of TNF-α and IL-6 in MEFs under treatment of DNC (0.5, 0.75 μM) and LLLT 450 nm (0.95 j/cm<sup>2</sup>). Remarkably, a more pronounced decrease in expression of these two factors was observed in simultaneous combination treatment compared to DNC and LLLT groups alone and the control group. According to previous research studies, blue 470 nm light irradiation [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref037" ref-type="bibr">37</xref>] and curcumin [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref066" ref-type="bibr">66</xref>] revealed a decrease in IL-6 concentrations compared to the non-treated groups. In line with these observations, lower concentrations of IL-6 secreted from MEFs were found following the uptake of DNC and LLLT 450 nm exposure both alone and in combination.</p><p>The production of ROS such as superoxide radical (O<sub>2</sub><sup>−</sup>), hydroxyl radical (OH) and hydrogen peroxide (H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>2</sub>) by aerobic metabolism at a certain level is natural and [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref067" ref-type="bibr">67</xref>] executing intracellular secondary messenger roles in signaling pathways specially the ones involved in wound healing as well as being required for immune system defense against pathogens. However, excessive accumulation of intracellular ROS is considered to be the major cause of inflammation during wound healing activity; Cause damage to cellular components, delay the wound healing process and cause non-healing ulcers [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref038" ref-type="bibr">38</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref039" ref-type="bibr">39</xref>]. Previous studies have reported that laser irradiation within 456–808 nm spectrum had generated a high concentration of ROS in various cells primarily induced signaling pathways required for proper wound healing however, the prolonged production and accumulation of that resulted in oxidative stress [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref068" ref-type="bibr">68</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref069" ref-type="bibr">69</xref>].
