# Simon Donnelly

grew up in Burnside, Rutherglen and attended Stonelaw High School. His father Tom Donnelly was also a footballer, who failed to break into the team at Rangers in the late 1960s but played for Motherwell and East Stirlingshire over the next decade before becoming a school teacher.
