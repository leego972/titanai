Question: <p>I've been working on a very simple crud generator for pylons. I came up with something that inspects </p>

<pre><code>SomeClass._sa_class_manager.mapper.c
</code></pre>

<p>Is it ok to inspect this (or to call methods begining with underscore)? I always kind of assumed this is legal though frowned upon as it relies heavily on the internal structure of a class/object. But hey, since python does not really have interfaces in the Java sense maybe it is OK.</p>

Answer: <p>If it works, why not? You could have problems though when _sa_class_manager gets restructured, binding yourself to this specific version of SQLAlchemy, or creating more work to track the changes. As SQLAlchemy is a fast moving target, you may be there in a year already.</p>

<p>The preferable way would be to integrate your desired API into SQLAlchemy itself.</p>
