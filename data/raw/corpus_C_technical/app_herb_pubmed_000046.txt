Bioguided isolation of pharmacologically active plant components, still a 
valuable strategy for the finding of new lead compounds?

Pieters L(1), Vlietinck AJ.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences, University of Antwerp, 
Universiteitsplein 1, B-2610 Antwerp, Belgium.

The position of natural products research in the drug discovery and development 
process nowadays is discussed. A flow chart for the study of plants used in 
traditional medicine, and the composition of a multidisciplinary research team 
for the investigation of medicinal plants are presented, and some 
recommendations are made for the future of bioassay-guided isolations and the 
use of standardised plant extracts in developing countries.
