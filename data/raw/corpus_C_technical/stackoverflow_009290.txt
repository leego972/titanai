Question: <p>I am writing a crawler in Python, in order to make Ctrl+C not to cause my crawler to start over in next run, I need to save the processing deque in a text file (one item per line) and update it every iteration, the update operation needs to be super fast. In order not to reinvent the wheel, I am asking if there is an established module to do this?</p>

Answer: <p>You should be able to use <a href="http://docs.python.org/library/pickle.html" rel="nofollow">pickle</a> to serialize your lists.</p>
