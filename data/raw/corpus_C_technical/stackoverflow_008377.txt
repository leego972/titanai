Question: <p>Is there a way to access a list(or tuple, or other iterable)'s next, or previous element while looping through with for loop?</p>

<pre><code>l=[1,2,3]
for item in l:
    if item==2:
        get_previous(l,item)
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>Iterators only have the next() method so you cannot look forwards or backwards, you can only get the next item.</p>

<p>enumerate(iterable) can be useful if you are iterating a list or tuple.</p>
