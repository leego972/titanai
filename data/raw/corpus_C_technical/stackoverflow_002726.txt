Question: <p>For example, the standard division symbol '/' rounds to zero:</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; 4 / 100
0
</code></pre>

<p>However, I want it to return 0.04. What do I use?</p>

Answer: <p>You might want to look at Python's <a href="http://docs.python.org/lib/module-decimal.html" rel="nofollow">decimal</a> package, also.  This will provide nice decimal results.</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; decimal.Decimal('4')/100
Decimal("0.04")
</code></pre>
