At the same time, 5 patients with OL and severe dysplasia developed new lesions, such as ulcers (Fig. <xref rid="Fig4" ref-type="fig">4</xref>). TCT analysis of these 5 patients revealed that no nuclear heterogeneous cells were seen at t1, while atypical squamous epithelial cells were observed at t2 (Fig. <xref rid="Fig5" ref-type="fig">5</xref>). DNA ploidy analysis showed that compared with t0, the number of aneuploidies was significantly reduced at t1, while the number of aneuploidies was obviously increased at t2 (Fig. <xref rid="Fig5" ref-type="fig">5</xref>). HE staining confirmed that at t2, severe epithelial dysplasia and even carcinoma in situ appeared in other parts of the oral cavity (Fig. <xref rid="Fig6" ref-type="fig">6</xref>), mostly on the tongue.
