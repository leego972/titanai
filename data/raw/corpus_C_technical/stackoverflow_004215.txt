Answer: <p>From <a href="http://bugs.mymediasystem.org/?do=details&amp;task_id=676" rel="nofollow">http://bugs.mymediasystem.org/?do=details&amp;task_id=676</a> suggests that Py_ssize_t was added in python 2.5, so it won't work (without some modifications) with python 2.4.</p>

<p>Either install/compile your own copy of python 2.5/2.6, or work out how to change P4Python, or look for an alternative python-perforce library.</p>
