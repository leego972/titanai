# PMC: laser photodynamic therapy cancer mechanism — Overview

<sup>27</sup> </xref>, <xref rid="jdv20452-bib-0046" ref-type="bibr"> <sup>46</sup> </xref> Within the scope of the presented study, we showed continuous improvement of all parameters of skin quality during follow‐up, particularly in the BF‐200 ALA group. Thus, we were able to show that skin quality improvements were long‐lasting.</p><p>In sum, we demonstrated that the high efficacy of field‐directed PDT with BF‐200 ALA and narrow‐spectrum red light is maintained over 1 year. Few AKs had developed in the treatment field despite the extent of field cancerization. One limitation of the study might be that the sample size was insufficient to draw additional meaningful conclusions from the subgroup analysis. Taken together, the promising efficacy data and improvement of all parameters of skin quality during the follow‐up period of 12 months indicate that field‐directed PDT is a beneficial long‐lasting treatment for AK patients.</p></sec><sec id="jdv20452-sec-0029"><title>AUTHOR CONTRIBUTIONS</title><p>UR, WGP‐D, TD, RO, RA, CB and R‐MS: investigation, writing – review and editing.
