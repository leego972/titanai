Small molecule inhibition of microbial natural product biosynthesis-an emerging 
antibiotic strategy.

Cisar JS(1), Tan DS.

Author information:
(1)Tri-Institutional Training Program in Chemical Biology, Memorial 
Sloan-Kettering Cancer Center, 1275 York Avenue, Box 422, New York, NY 10065, 
USA.

A variety of natural products modulate critical biological processes in the 
microorganisms that produce them. Thus, inhibition of the corresponding natural 
product biosynthesis pathways represents a promising avenue to develop novel 
antibiotics. In this tutorial review, we describe several recent examples of 
designed small molecule inhibitors of microbial natural product biosynthesis and 
their use in evaluating this emerging antibiotic strategy.
