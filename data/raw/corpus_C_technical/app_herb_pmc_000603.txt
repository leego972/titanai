Phytochemistry</title><p>So far, many phytochemical studies of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> have been conducted, and more than 170 compounds have been isolated and identified from this plant. Among them, sesquiterpenes and phenylpropanoids are the most abundant and major bioactive constituents in <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic>, and are considered as the characteristic constituents of this plant. In addition to the chemical constituents found in fruits, constituents in other parts of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> were also comprehensively reported, including leaves, roots and stems, etc. In this section, the identified compounds are listed in the following table and the corresponding structures are also comprehensively presented. Sesquiterpenoids and Triterpenoids</title><p>Sesquiterpenoids have many important biological functions and physiological activities, which are abundant in <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic>. Sesquiterpene lactones, the main characteristic components of plants in the Asteraceae family, exhibit strong activities with anti-microbial, antiviral, anti-tumor and anti-inflammation [<xref rid="B55-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">55</xref>,<xref rid="B56-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">56</xref>]. The predominant sesquiterpene lactones are the guaiane type and seco-guaiane type, of which xanthanolides are the important active constituent. In 2015, eight sesquiterpenes were isolated from the fruits of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic>, including sibirolide A (<bold>1</bold>), sibirolide B (<bold>2</bold>) and norxanthantolide A–F (<bold>3–8</bold>) [<xref rid="B13-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">13</xref>]. In addition, 1β-hydroxyl-5α-chloro-8-epi-xanthatin (<bold>9</bold>) and 11α, 13-dihydro-8-epi-xanthatin (<bold>10</bold>) were isolated from the aerial parts of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> [<xref rid="B14-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">14</xref>]. Moreover, xanthinin (<bold>11</bold>), xanthumin (<bold>12</bold>), xanthanol (<bold>13</bold>), xanthanol acetate (<bold>14</bold>), isoxanthanol (<bold>13</bold>), xanthumanol (<bold>16</bold>), deacetoxylxanthumin (<bold>17</bold>), xanthatin (<bold>18</bold>), xanthinosin (<bold>19</bold>), tomentosin (<bold>20</bold>) were isolated from the leaves of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> [<xref rid="B15-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">15</xref>,<xref rid="B16-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">16</xref>].
