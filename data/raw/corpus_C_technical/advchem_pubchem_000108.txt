Description:
Ammonium is an onium cation obtained by protonation of ammonia. It has a role as a cofactor, a Saccharomyces cerevisiae metabolite, an Escherichia coli metabolite and a human metabolite. It is a nitrogen hydride, an onium cation and a monovalent inorganic cation. It is a conjugate acid of an ammonia.
