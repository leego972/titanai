</p></fn></table-wrap-foot></table-wrap></sec><sec><title>Biological activities associated with <italic toggle="yes">S. rostratum</italic></title><sec><title>Antioxidant activity</title><p>An antioxidant is a substance that inhibits or reduces oxidative damage caused by free radicals in the body that are associated with cell damage, aging, and chronic diseases. Free radicals are highly reactive molecules or ions that can damage cells and contribute to various health issues, including aging and chronic diseases specifically in male. <italic toggle="yes">S. rostratum</italic> are good anti-oxidants because of their richness in flavonoids like 5,7,8, 4′-tetrahydrox y-3-m ethoxyflavon e-8-O- <italic toggle="yes">β</italic>-d-xylopyranoside, querceti n-3-O-<italic toggle="yes">β</italic>-D-g alactopyranoside, quercetin, quercetin 3′-methox y-3-O-<italic toggle="yes">β</italic>-D-g alactopyranoside and kaempfero l-3-O-<italic toggle="yes">β</italic>-D-g lucopyranoside (<xref rid="ref-28" ref-type="bibr">Huang et al., 2017</xref>) having the potential to be used as promoters for insulin secretion and enhancement of insulin resistance in man (<xref rid="ref-72" ref-type="bibr">Yi et al., 2021</xref>). Notably, quercetin, a flavonoid in <italic toggle="yes">S. rostratum</italic>, has demonstrated significant inhibitory activity against the stable free radical compound 2,2-diphenyl-1-picrylhydrazyl(DPPH), surpassing the effectiveness of ascorbic acid (<xref rid="ref-10" ref-type="bibr">Azeem et al., 2023</xref>). <xref rid="ref-44" ref-type="bibr">Omar et al. (2018)</xref>, in their examination of phytochemical constituents in <italic toggle="yes">S. rostratum,</italic> also investigated its antioxidant activity where the result revealed the chloroform extract of the plant exhibited an exceptional radical scavenging ability with an IC<sub>50</sub> value of 0066 ± 0.001 mg/ml. Additionally, the isolation of isorhamnetin-3-O-glucoside, a flavonoid glycoside, has been reported to possess the potential to treat cardiovascular-related diseases by regulating specific signaling pathways such as phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase/ protein kinase B(PI3K/AKT/PKB) signaling pathways, nuclear factor kappa-light-chain-enhancer of activated B cells (NF-<italic toggle="yes">κ</italic>B) transcription factor protein, mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) and the expression of associated kinases and cytokines (<xref rid="ref-44" ref-type="bibr">Omar et al., 2018</xref>).</p><p>In animals like rat, <italic toggle="yes">S.
