Cytotoxic Activity</title><p>The drugs used for cancer therapy are toxic and affect both cancer cells and normal cells. Hence, it is necessary to use compounds isolated from natural sources to reduce cancer risk in several cancer types, including colon cancer, lung cancer, gastric cancer, and breast cancer. The significant antiproliferative and preventative effect of <italic toggle="yes">Brassica</italic> vegetables seeds on tumor cells, most consistently colon and lung cancers [<xref rid="B143-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">143</xref>,<xref rid="B144-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">144</xref>], are related to their richness in bioactive components such as phenolics, flavonoids, glucosinolates, and their degradation products allyl isothiocyanate acid, sulforaphane, and indole-3-methanol. In addition to their antioxidant capacity, phenolics and flavonoids have been found to possess antitumor activity [<xref rid="B145-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">145</xref>,<xref rid="B146-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">146</xref>]. Quercetin and kaempferol have been shown to inhibit cell growth in human intestinal cancer lines in a synergistic manner [<xref rid="B147-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">147</xref>]. In addition, quercetin and rutin have shown antiproliferative activity against largeous spectrum of different cancers [<xref rid="B148-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">148</xref>,<xref rid="B149-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">149</xref>]. It has also been reported that sinigrin inhibits the proliferation of breast cancer cells [<xref rid="B150-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">150</xref>] and liver cancer cells [<xref rid="B151-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">151</xref>]. As it is known, glucosinolates and their degradation products such as allyl isothiocyanate, benzyl isothiocyanate, phenethylnate, and sulforaphane inhibit the activity of various cancer cells [<xref rid="B152-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">152</xref>,<xref rid="B153-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">153</xref>,<xref rid="B154-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">154</xref>,<xref rid="B155-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">155</xref>,<xref rid="B156-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">156</xref>]. Sulforaphane, 3,3′-diindolylmethane, and indole-3-carbinol inhibit several carcinoma cells by inhibiting the transcription factors (STAT) and by suppressing cyclin-dependent kinase CDK2/4/6, cyclin D, and P27Kip expression [<xref rid="B157-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">157</xref>,<xref rid="B158-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">158</xref>,<xref rid="B159-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">159</xref>].</p><p>The reported anticarcinogenic activity of mustard seeds is shown in <xref rid="molecules-27-06008-t006" ref-type="table">Table 6</xref>. Compared to other <italic toggle="yes">Brassica</italic> seeds, the oil seed of <italic toggle="yes">B. juncea</italic> var. <italic toggle="yes">raya</italic> revealed a remarkable anti-proliferative activity against a large spectrum of different human cancer cell lines, mainly breast (MCF-7 and MDA-MB-231), prostate (PC-3), lung (A-549), cervix (HeLa), and colon (HCT-116) with a high inhibition in MCF-7 cells with an IC<sub>50</sub>=32.93 μg/mL. It was proven that seed oil reduced the increase in cancer cells in a dose-dependent manner, resulting in apoptosis and cell death [<xref rid="B97-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">97</xref>]. Another study in <italic toggle="yes">B. oleracea var. capitata</italic> seed fractions revealed moderate anticancer activities of fractions II (IC<sub>50</sub>=27.32 μg/mL) and III (IC<sub>50</sub>=15.56 μg/mL) on A-549 cells compared to those of sulforaphane (IC<sub>50</sub>=3.53 μg/mL) &gt; iberin (IC<sub>50</sub>=4.93 μg/mL) &gt; iberverin (IC<sub>50</sub>=7.07 μg/mL). They also induced cell apoptosis by increasing early apoptosis and late apoptosis/necrosis and activating caspase-3, -8, and -9 [<xref rid="B160-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">160</xref>]. Hexane, ethyl acetate, ethanol, and methanol extracts of broccoli seeds exhibited high anticancer activity on A-549, LAC, HeLa, HepG-2, and Caco-2 with a high inhibition of LAC with IC<sub>50</sub> of 10.38 mg/g [<xref rid="B63-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">63</xref>,<xref rid="B77-molecules-27-06008" ref-type="bibr">77</xref>].
