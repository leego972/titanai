id="s0005"><label>1</label><title>Introduction</title><p id="p0005"> <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> ( <xref rid="f0005" ref-type="fig">Fig. 1</xref>) is one of the most popular traditional Chinese medicines and is an ingredient in many medicines and prescriptions. It has been widely used to treat various diseases that have been commonly associated with aging for many centuries in China. A recent study proved that it can exhibit antioxidative activity (<xref rid="bib110" ref-type="bibr">Lv et al., 2007</xref>, <xref rid="bib148" ref-type="bibr">Wang, 2005a</xref>, <xref rid="bib161" ref-type="bibr">Wang et al., 2008</xref>, <xref rid="bib149" ref-type="bibr">Wang et al., 2009a</xref>), mainly due to its flavonoid and phenolic acid constituents. The pharmacological effects of stilbene in <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> have been reported to promote anti-aging effects (<xref rid="bib104" ref-type="bibr">Long and Dougherty, 2003</xref>, <xref rid="bib85" ref-type="bibr">Lin et al., 2008</xref>, <xref rid="bib19" ref-type="bibr">Chen et al., 2001a</xref>, <xref rid="bib6" ref-type="bibr">Chan et al., 2002</xref>, <xref rid="bib146" ref-type="bibr">Um et al., 2006</xref>, <xref rid="bib27" ref-type="bibr">Cheung et al., 2014</xref>) and hepatoprotective activities (<xref rid="bib52" ref-type="bibr">Huang et al., 2007</xref>, <xref rid="bib90" ref-type="bibr">Liu et al., 1992</xref>). Anthraquinones, another main component of this plant, also have many biological activities, such as effects against cancer (<xref rid="bib113" ref-type="bibr">Ma et al., 2012</xref>, <xref rid="bib158" ref-type="bibr">Wang et al., 2011b</xref>, <xref rid="bib162" ref-type="bibr">Way et al., 2014</xref>, <xref rid="bib196" ref-type="bibr">Yu et al., 2013</xref>, <xref rid="bib93" ref-type="bibr">Liu et al., 2009a</xref>, <xref rid="bib86" ref-type="bibr">Lin et al., 2006</xref>, <xref rid="bib143" ref-type="bibr">Tabolacci et al., 2010</xref>), developmental anomalies (<xref rid="bib192" ref-type="bibr">Yon et al., 2013</xref>), and tonic tension (<xref rid="bib83" ref-type="bibr">Lim et al., 2014</xref>). However, an increasing number of recently published studies have demonstrated the adverse effects of <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic>. Some researchers have found that <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> shows not only hepatotoxicity but also a possible drug interaction with warfarin to result in bone marrow suppression. Specifically, the long-term use of <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> may lead to liver and kidney toxicity. The toxicity of emodin has been detailed by the U.S. National Toxicology Program (<xref rid="bib119" ref-type="bibr">NTP technical report, 2001</xref>).
