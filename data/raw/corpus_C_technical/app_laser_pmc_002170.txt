In this analysis, LLLT was demonstrated to have therapeutic effects on the body through the promotion of wound healing and pain alleviation. It has further illustrated its efficacy in treating chronic or slow-healing wounds such as DFUs. It would be beneficial to determine the parameters for the LLLT, including the wavelength, output power and energy density, which provide optimal treatment effects.
