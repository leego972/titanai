Regarding the diversity of classes reported in the different species, <italic toggle="yes">C. dactylon</italic> has 11 classes, followed by <italic toggle="yes">S. spontaneum</italic> with 8, <italic toggle="yes">A. donax</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">C. ciliaris</italic> with 7, and <italic toggle="yes">I. cylindrica</italic> with 6, the remaining species have between 1 and 4 classes of compounds reported (<xref ref-type="fig" rid="ijms-20-01067-f002">Figure 2</xref>). This puts in evidence the lack of research in some of the species discussed in this review. For instance, in <italic toggle="yes">P. clandestinum</italic>, only 4 compounds were described although distributed in 2 classes (<xref ref-type="app" rid="app1-ijms-20-01067">Table S1</xref>). In spite of this, the phytochemical investigations have revealed that many compounds are highly bioactive. The complete description and distribution of the compounds reported in each species are illustrated in <xref ref-type="app" rid="app1-ijms-20-01067">Table S1</xref>. Alkanes and Alkenes</title><p>Alkanes and alkenes (compounds <bold>1</bold>–<bold>29</bold>, <xref ref-type="app" rid="app1-ijms-20-01067">Table S1</xref>) were reported in <italic toggle="yes">A. donax</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">C. ciliaris</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">C. dactylon</italic>, and <italic toggle="yes">S. spontaneum</italic> species. In general, 29 molecules from this class were described: 18 in <italic toggle="yes">C. ciliaris</italic> [<xref rid="B56-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">56</xref>,<xref rid="B57-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">57</xref>], 12 in <italic toggle="yes">A. donax</italic> [<xref rid="B43-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">43</xref>] and only 1 in both <italic toggle="yes">C. dactylon</italic> [<xref rid="B58-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">58</xref>] and <italic toggle="yes">S. spontaneum</italic> [<xref rid="B59-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">59</xref>] (<xref ref-type="app" rid="app1-ijms-20-01067">Table S1</xref>). In the first species, these were obtained from its essential oils [<xref rid="B57-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">57</xref>] as well as from the lipophilic, methanol and ethyl acetate root extracts [<xref rid="B56-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">56</xref>] while in <italic toggle="yes">A. donax</italic> the alkanes were obtained from the lipophilic extracts [<xref rid="B43-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">43</xref>]. </p><p>Alkanes represent between 70% to 80% of the wax cuticle constitution, which is indispensable to prevent uncontrolled water loss [<xref rid="B60-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">60</xref>]. Therefore, their role in abiotic stresses such as salinity stress is crucial [<xref rid="B61-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">61</xref>]. The well-reported presence of these compounds in <italic toggle="yes">C. ciliaris</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">A. donax</italic> can be a sign of their adaptation to high salinity environments. Consequently, more studies must be conducted in order to completely characterize the presence of alkanes in other halophytic species. Some of the compounds included in this class are biologically active; for instance, tetradecane (<bold>5</bold>) [<xref rid="B56-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">56</xref>], hexadecane (<bold>7</bold>) [<xref rid="B56-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">56</xref>], heptadecane (<bold>8</bold>) [<xref rid="B56-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">56</xref>], nonadecane (<bold>10</bold>) [<xref rid="B56-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">56</xref>] and eicosane (<bold>11</bold>) [<xref rid="B56-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">56</xref>] exhibited antimicrobial activities. In addition, nonacosane (<bold>20</bold>) seems to inhibit human gastric cancer cells BGC823 at 5 µM after 72 hours [<xref rid="B62-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">62</xref>]. It was also found that hentriacontane (<bold>22</bold>) significantly reduced all the parameters of inflammation in the conducted experiments at all the tested concentrations: 10 μM, 5 μM and 1 μM (in vitro) and 5 mg/kg, 2 mg/kg and 1 mg/kg (in vivo) [<xref rid="B63-ijms-20-01067" ref-type="bibr">63</xref>].
