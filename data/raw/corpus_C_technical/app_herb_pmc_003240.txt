In Pakistan, a decoction of the plant is used to treat fever and nervous problems [<xref rid="B50-molecules-27-00492" ref-type="bibr">50</xref>]. Taken together, <italic toggle="yes">Artemisia herba-alba</italic>, if the traditionalists are proved correct in clinical trials, by itself can possibly assuage many of the symptoms of COVID-19 as well as serious comorbidities such as malaria, bronchitis, diabetes, diarrhea, hypertension, neuralgia, abdominal pain, and high fever. </p></sec><sec id="sec3dot2-molecules-27-00492"><title>3.2. Pharmacological Aspects of Artemisia herba-alba</title><p>A recent clinical study with a limited number of patients (<italic toggle="yes">n</italic> = 21) showed that deceased severely ill COVID-19 patients (<italic toggle="yes">n</italic> = 7) displayed higher plasma oxidative stress levels (i.e., higher plasma 4-hydroxynonenal, HNE) than their survived counterparts (<italic toggle="yes">n</italic> = 14) [<xref rid="B51-molecules-27-00492" ref-type="bibr">51</xref>]. HNE is a compound produced by lipid peroxidation in cells. Furthermore, their immunohistochemistry data showed increased edema in the alveoli of deceased patients with eosinophilic liquid rich in HNE [<xref rid="B52-molecules-27-00492" ref-type="bibr">52</xref>]. Noticeably, the authors of this study reported a low (below the detection limit) total antioxidant capacity of all of the severely ill COVID-19 patients (<italic toggle="yes">n</italic> = 21) reported in their study [<xref rid="B52-molecules-27-00492" ref-type="bibr">52</xref>]. Thus, the results from the study indicated that the intake of antioxidant-rich food or dietary materials might help to protect people from oxidative damages caused by COVID-19 or related viral infections. A number of scientific reviews have detailed the pharmacological activities of <italic toggle="yes">Artemisia herba-alba</italic> and its parts, but only reviews pertinent to the alleviation of COVID-19 symptoms and comorbidities will be presented and discussed. One review mentions the activities as being antioxidant, anti-venom, anti-fungal, nematocidal, antibacterial, antispasmodic, anthelmintic, anti-leishmanial, anti-neurological disorders (Alzheimer’s disease, epilepsy, and depression), and hypoglycemic [<xref rid="B31-molecules-27-00492" ref-type="bibr">31</xref>]. A second review mentions the pharmacological activities of the plant as anti-diabetic, anti-hypertensive, antioxidant, anti-fungal, neurological, antimalarial, antispasmodic, immunomodulatory, and anti-mycoplasmal [<xref rid="B53-molecules-27-00492" ref-type="bibr">53</xref>]. The aqueous extract of the aerial parts of the plant was reportedly active against <italic toggle="yes">Escherichia coli</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Serratia marcescens</italic> W225, <italic toggle="yes">Enterobacter cloacae</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Shigella dysenteriae</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Proteus vulgaris</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Bacillus subtilis</italic>, and <italic toggle="yes">Staphylococcus aureus</italic> [<xref rid="B54-molecules-27-00492" ref-type="bibr">54</xref>]. In a clinical trial with 15 diabetic patients, 14 patients showed the lowering of elevated blood sugar and the good remission of diabetic symptoms following the administration of the plant extract [<xref rid="B55-molecules-27-00492" ref-type="bibr">55</xref>]. A 70% ethanol extract of the dried plant has been shown to inhibit α-amylase activity in vitro and lower elevated plasma glucose levels in alloxan-diabetic rats in vivo [<xref rid="B56-molecules-27-00492" ref-type="bibr">56</xref>]. Overall, a conclusion can be arrived at regarding the results of pharmacological activity studies on the plant, which corroborate well with the plant’s ethnic uses, and a number of the various ailments that ethnic uses and pharmacological activities correspond to match with the comorbidities or symptoms generally observed in COVID-19 patients. If this conclusion is correct, then that justifies further in silico studies on the phytochemicals of <italic toggle="yes">Artemisia herba-alba</italic> against the SARS-CoV-2 virus itself.</p></sec><sec id="sec3dot3-molecules-27-00492"><title>3.3.
