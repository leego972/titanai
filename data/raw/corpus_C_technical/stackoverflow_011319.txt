Question: <p>How do I select a(or some) random row(s) from a table using SQLAlchemy? </p>

Answer: <p>If you are using the orm and the table is not big (or you have its amount of rows cached) and you want it to be database independent the really simple approach is.</p>

<pre><code>import random
rand = random.randrange(0, session.query(Table).count()) 
row = session.query(Table)[rand]
</code></pre>

<p>This is cheating slightly but thats why you use an orm.</p>
