In clinical studies, many investigators have found contradictory results of the effects of LLLT on wound healing. In laboratory animals accelerated wound closure, increased wound epithelialisation and improved tensile strength of scars were seen.[<xref rid="ref5" ref-type="bibr">5</xref><xref rid="ref6" ref-type="bibr">6</xref><xref rid="ref7" ref-type="bibr">7</xref><xref rid="ref8" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>] Many other studies showed no improvement of the healing process through LLLT and the above-mentioned affects could not be reproduced. According to experimental studies, low-level laser radiation activates individual cells via three principle effects:</p><p>
<list list-type="order"><list-item><p>The photobiological action mechanism via activation of the respiratory chain: Primary photoacceptors are terminal oxidases as well as NADH-dehydrogenase.</p></list-item><list-item><p>Activation of other redox chains in cells: In phagocytic cells irradiation initiates a nonmitochondrial respiratory burst (production of reactive oxygen species, especially superoxide anion) through activation of NADPH-oxidase located in the plasma membrane of these cells. The irradiation effects on phagocytic cells depend on the physiological status of the host organism as well as on radiation parameters.</p></list-item><list-item><p>Indirect activation of cells via secondary messengers released by directly activated cells: Reactive oxygen species produced by phagocytes, lymphokines and cytokines produced by various subpopulations of lymphocytes, or NO produced by macrophages or as a result of NO-haemoglobin photolysis of blood cells.[<xref rid="ref9" ref-type="bibr">9</xref>]</p></list-item></list>
</p><p>Also, <italic toggle="yes">in vitro</italic> data suggests that LLLT facilitates collagen synthesis[<xref rid="ref10" ref-type="bibr">10</xref>], keratinocyte cell motility[<xref rid="ref11" ref-type="bibr">11</xref>], and growth factor release.[<xref rid="ref12" ref-type="bibr">12</xref>] and transforms fibroblasts to myofibroblasts.[<xref rid="ref13" ref-type="bibr">13</xref>] However, the benefits of LLLT in wound healing are still controversial.
