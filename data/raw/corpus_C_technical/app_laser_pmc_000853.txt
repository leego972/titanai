The entire export process took a total of approximately 202 h to complete over a period of 24 days. In a smaller sample, using the macro to download the scans was significantly faster than manually downloading the scans, averaging 3.88 vs. 11.08 min/file, respectively (<italic toggle="yes">t</italic> = 8.59, <italic toggle="yes">p</italic> &lt; 0.001).
