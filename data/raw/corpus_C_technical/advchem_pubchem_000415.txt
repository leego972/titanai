Description:
Naphthazarin is a naphthoquinone that is 1,4-naphthoquinone in which the hydrogens at positions 5 and 8 are replaced by hydroxy groups. It has a role as a geroprotector, an acaricide, an antibacterial agent, an antineoplastic agent, an apoptosis inducer and a plant metabolite.
