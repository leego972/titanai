# Bulgaria–Greece relations

Republic of Greece
Sofia (Embassy)

Embassies 
The Embassy of Bulgaria is located in Athens. The Embassy of Greece is located in Sofia.

Border 

The border between Bulgaria and Greece was established in 1919. A 15 to 45 kilometre deep surveillance zone was established.
