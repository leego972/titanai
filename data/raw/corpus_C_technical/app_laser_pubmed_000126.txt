Oncologic photodynamic therapy photosensitizers: a clinical review.

Allison RR(1), Sibata CH.

Author information:
(1)21st Century Oncology, Greenville, NC 27834-3764, USA.

A myriad of naturally occurring and synthetic structures are capable of 
transferring the energy of light. Few, however, allow for this energy transfer 
to enable a type II photochemical reaction which, as currently practiced, is a 
fundamental component of photodynamic therapy. Even fewer of these agents, aptly 
termed photosensitizers, have found success in the treatment of patients. This 
review will focus on the oncologic photosensitizers that have come to clinical 
trial with outcomes published in peer reviewed journals. Based on a clinical 
orientation the qualities of successful photosensitizers will be examined, how 
current drugs fare and potential future options explored.
