Answer: <p>Since there are so many different variation's of how people write their names, but here's how a basic way to get the first/lastname via regex.</p>

<pre><code>import re
p = re.compile(r'^(\s+)?(Mr(\.)?|Mrs(\.)?)?(?P&lt;FIRST_NAME&gt;.+)(\s+)(?P&lt;LAST_NAME&gt;.+)$', re.IGNORECASE)
m = p.match('Mr. Dingo Bat')
if(m != None):
  first_name = m.group('FIRST_NAME')
  last_name = m.group('LAST_NAME')
</code></pre>
