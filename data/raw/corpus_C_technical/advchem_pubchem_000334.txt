Description:
Baclofen is a monocarboxylic acid that is butanoic acid substituted by an amino group at position 4 and a 4-chlorophenyl group at position 3. It acts as a central nervous system depressant, GABA agonist and muscle relaxant. It has a role as a central nervous system depressant, a muscle relaxant and a GABA agonist. It is a monocarboxylic acid, a gamma-amino acid, a primary amino compound and a member of monochlorobenzenes. It is a tautomer of a baclofen zwitterion.
