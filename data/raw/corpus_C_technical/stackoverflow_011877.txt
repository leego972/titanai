Question: <p>Which is better to use for timing in Python? time.clock() or time.time()? Which one provides more accuracy?</p>

<p>for example:</p>

<pre><code>start = time.clock()
... do something
elapsed = (time.clock() - start)
</code></pre>

<p>vs.</p>

<pre><code>start = time.time()
... do something
elapsed = (time.time() - start)
</code></pre>

Answer: <p><a href="http://stackoverflow.com/questions/85451#85511">Others</a> have answered re: time.time() vs. time.clock(). </p>

<p>However, if you're timing the execution of a block of code for benchmarking/profiling purposes, you should take a look at the <a href="https://docs.python.org/library/timeit.html" rel="nofollow"><code>timeit</code> module</a>.</p>
