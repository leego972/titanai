# Abdullah Al-Waked — Overview

Abdullah Al-Waked Al-Shahrani () (born 29 September 1975) is a Saudi Arabian football player. He played most of his career for Al Shabab and Al Ittihad and is playing now with Al-Nasr. He played for the Saudi Arabia national football team and was a participant at the 2002 FIFA World Cup. He participated in the 1996 Summer Olympics. Honours
