# For the Cause (Star Trek: Deep Space Nine)

he second scene of Act 2 was writer Ron Moore's tribute to the original series episode, "The Tholian Web", in which those aliens first appeared. The reference by Jake Sisko in Act 3 to the 1961 Yankees and the 1978 Red Sox was a tribute by producer Ira Steven Behr to his two favorite baseball teams.

Reception 
In 2020, James Whitbrook, writing for io9, included the episode in a list of "must-watch" episodes.
