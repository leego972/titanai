Question: <p>How should I base64 encode a PDF file for transport over XML-RPC in Python?</p>

Answer: <p>Looks like you might be able to use the <a href="http://docs.python.org/library/binascii.html" rel="nofollow">binascii</a> module</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>binascii.b2a_base64(data)</p>
  
  <p>Convert binary data to a line of ASCII characters in base64 coding. The return value is the converted line, including a newline char. The length of data should be at most 57 to adhere to the base64 standard.</p>
</blockquote>
