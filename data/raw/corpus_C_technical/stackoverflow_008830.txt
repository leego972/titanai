Question: <p>What is the nicest/shortest way to pad a string with zeroes to the left, so the string length has a specific length?</p>

Answer: <pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; '99'.zfill(5)
'00099'
&gt;&gt;&gt; '99'.rjust(5,'0')
'00099'
</code></pre>

<p>if you want the opposite:</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; '99'.ljust(5,'0')
'99000'
</code></pre>
