Question: <p>Is there a benefit to using one over the other? They both seem to return the same results.</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; 6/3
2
&gt;&gt;&gt; 6//3
2
</code></pre>

Answer: <p><code>//</code> is floor division, it will always give you the integer floor of the result.  The other is 'regular' division.</p>
