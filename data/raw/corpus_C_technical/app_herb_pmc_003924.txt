Thus, cortisol is considered a primary target of substances that reduce chronic stress, and reducing cortisol levels to a near-normal level is the goal of reducing the risk of subsequent health implications.</p><p><italic toggle="yes">Ocimum tenuiflorum</italic> is an adaptogenic herb with multiple health benefits. Therefore, we evaluated its stress managing properties using swim endurance test in mice, and forced swim test in rats, cortisol release assay <italic toggle="yes">in vitro</italic> and CRF1 antagonistic effect <italic toggle="yes">in vitro</italic>. Several studies have already shown the antistress property of <italic toggle="yes">Ocimum tenuiflorum</italic>. Ethanol extract from the leaves of <italic toggle="yes">Ocimum tenuiflorum</italic> showed antistress effects in both acute stress and chronic unpredictable stress models at a dose of 200 mg/kg body weight in rats. The extract was reported to have significantly prevented the stress induced increase in plasma corticosterone levels [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref037" ref-type="bibr">37</xref>].</p><p>The phytochemical constituents that are analysed as part of Ocimum Bioactive Complex (patent-pending), including rosmarinic acid and luteolin, was hypothesized to contribute to the antistress effect of Holixer<sup>TM</sup>. The individual phytoconstituents of OBC have been well researched for its various pharmacological activity, in particular the anti-stress effect. Rosmarinic acid has been reported to reduce plasma corticosterone levels and increase the expression of hippocampal mineralocorticoid receptors, glucocorticoid receptors and brain-derived neurotrophic factor in the hippocampus of rats induced with HIV-1 viral protein tat injection and subsequent repetitive restrain stress [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref038" ref-type="bibr">38</xref>]. Rosmarinic acid has also been reported to reduce maternal stress induced corticosterone increase [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref039" ref-type="bibr">39</xref>], chronic unpredictable stress induced increase in corticosterone [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref040" ref-type="bibr">40</xref>] and reverse stress induced changes of SUV39H1, Mkp-1, and BDNF expression in the mice hippocampus [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref041" ref-type="bibr">41</xref>]. Luteolin-7-O-glucuronide, another constituent of OBC, has been found to reduce blood corticosterone in a mouse sleep deprivation model, wherein the compound improved depression-like and stress coping behaviours [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref042" ref-type="bibr">42</xref>].</p><p>An ethanol extract of leaves of <italic toggle="yes">Ocimum sanctum</italic> was reported to prevent the noise-stress induced increase in corticosterone in Wistar rats at an intraperitoneal dose of 100 mg/kg body weight [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref043" ref-type="bibr">43</xref>]. When restraint-stressed rats, were evaluated in elevated plus maze test, tail suspension test, and forced swimming test after treatment with ethanol extract of <italic toggle="yes">Ocimum tenuiflorum</italic> leaves, 200 mg/kg body weight dose had better antistress effects [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref044" ref-type="bibr">44</xref>]. Aqueous extract of <italic toggle="yes">Ocimum tenuiflorum</italic> leaves at a dose of 100 mg/kg body weight has shown antistress effects in rats subjected to restraint stress [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref014" ref-type="bibr">14</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0285012.ref045" ref-type="bibr">45</xref>]. Aqueous leaf extract of <italic toggle="yes">Ocimum tenuiflorum</italic> was found to reduce stress-induced gastric ulcers in rats at a dose of 100 and 200 mg/kg body weight [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref046" ref-type="bibr">46</xref>]. The antistress activity was also reported for the methanol extract of the whole plant of <italic toggle="yes">Ocimum tenuiflorum</italic> extract at doses from 50 to 200 mg/kg body weight in rats subjected to chronic variable stress [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref022" ref-type="bibr">22</xref>]. The same extract was found to show CRF1 antagonist activity of 63% at 100 μg/ml concentration and cortisol release inhibition of 89% at 100 μg/ml concentration. Methanol extract of the roots of <italic toggle="yes">Ocimum sanctum</italic> was reported to increase swimming time in mice at an intraperitoneal dose of 400 mg/kg body weight [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref047" ref-type="bibr">47</xref>].
