Question: <p>Which is better to use for timing in Python? time.clock() or time.time()? Which one provides more accuracy?</p>

<p>for example:</p>

<pre><code>start = time.clock()
... do something
elapsed = (time.clock() - start)
</code></pre>

<p>vs.</p>

<pre><code>start = time.time()
... do something
elapsed = (time.time() - start)
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>Depends on what you care about. If you mean WALL TIME (as in, the time on the clock on your wall), time.clock() provides NO accuracy because it may manage CPU time. </p>
