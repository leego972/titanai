# Jacek Kiełb

Jacek Kiełb (born 10 January 1988 in Siedlce) is a Polish former professional footballer who played as a midfielder. After retirement in 2023, he joined Korona Kielce's coaching staff.

Career

Club
Kiełb began his career 2004 with Pogoń Siedlce and joined 2005 in the youth team of Korona Kielce, who made one year later his professional debut in the Ekstraklasa.

On 10 June 2010, Lech Poznań announced the signing of Kiełb on a four-year deal. In August 2011, he was loaned back to Korona for a year.
