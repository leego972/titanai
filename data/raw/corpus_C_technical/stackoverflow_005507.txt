Question: <p>Which types of objects fall into the domain of "subscriptable"?</p>

Answer: <p>Off the top of my head, the following are the only built-ins that are subscriptable:</p>

<pre><code>string:  "foobar"[3] == "b"
tuple:   (1,2,3,4)[3] == 4
list:    [1,2,3,4][3] == 4
dict:    {"a":1, "b":2, "c":3}["c"] == 3
</code></pre>

<p>But mipadi's answer is correct; any class that implements __getitem__ is subscriptable</p>
