Question: <p>Is there a benefit to using one over the other? They both seem to return the same results.</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; 6/3
2
&gt;&gt;&gt; 6//3
2
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>The answer of the equation is rounded to the next smaller integer or float with .0 as decimal point. </p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt;print 5//2
2
&gt;&gt;&gt; print 5.0//2
2.0
&gt;&gt;&gt;print 5//2.0
2.0
&gt;&gt;&gt;print 5.0//2.0
2.0
</code></pre>
