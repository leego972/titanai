Description:
(2-aminoethyl)phosphonic acid is a phosphonic acid in which the hydrogen attached to the phosphorus of phosphonic acid is substituted by a 2-aminoethyl group. It has a role as a metabolite, a mouse metabolite and a human metabolite. It is a member of phosphonic acids and a primary amino compound. It is functionally related to a phosphonic acid. It is a tautomer of a (2-aminoethyl)phosphonic acid zwitterion.
