Answer: <p>How about this, to use letters, figures and punctuation (all usable to form a Django key):</p>

<pre><code>import random
import string

chars = string.letters + string.digits + string.punctuation
chars_len = len(chars)
n = 40

print(''.join([chars[random.randint(0, chars_len)] for i in range(n)]))
</code></pre>
