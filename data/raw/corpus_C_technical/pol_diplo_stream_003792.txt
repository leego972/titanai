# Philip Ward

Ward was president of the South of England Agricultural Society (1994–95), Governor of the Church Lads' and Church Girls' Brigade (1979–86), and Patron of the Chichester Cathedral Trust in 1995. Sir Philip was president of the D-Day and Normandy Fellowship from 1990 to 2001.

In 1993, he laid the foundation stone for new site of Tanbridge House School in Horsham.

He married Pamela Ann Glennie in 1948. She survived him with their two sons and two daughters.
