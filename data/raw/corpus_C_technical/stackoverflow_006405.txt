Question: <p>What's the difference between the list methods <code>append()</code> and <code>extend()</code>?</p>

Answer: <p>The method "append" adds its parameter as a <strong>single element</strong> to the list, while "extend" gets a list and adds its content.</p>

<p>for example,  </p>

<blockquote>
  <p><strong>extend</strong></p>
</blockquote>

<pre><code>    letters = ['a', 'b']  
    letters.extend(['c', 'd'])   
    print(letters)   # ['a', 'b', 'c', 'd']
</code></pre>

<blockquote>
  <p><strong>append</strong>  </p>
</blockquote>

<pre><code>    letters.append(['e', 'f'])   
    print(letters)    # ['a', 'b', 'c', 'd', ['e', 'f']]
</code></pre>
