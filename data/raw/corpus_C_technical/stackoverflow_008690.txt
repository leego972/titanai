Question: <p>How do you change the size of figure drawn with matplotlib?</p>

Answer: <p>This resizes the figure immediately even after the figure has been drawn (at least using Qt4Agg/TkAgg - but not MacOSX - with matplotlib 1.4.0):</p>

<pre><code>matplotlib.pyplot.get_current_fig_manager().resize(width_px, height_px)
</code></pre>
