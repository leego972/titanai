Description:
3-sulfinylpyruvic acid is a pyruvic acid compound having a 3-sulfinyl substituent. It has a role as a mouse metabolite and a human metabolite. It is a sulfur-containing carboxylic acid, a 2-oxo monocarboxylic acid and an organosulfinic acid. It is functionally related to a pyruvic acid. It is a conjugate acid of a 3-sulfinatopyruvate(2-).
