In the present study, we reported CYP enzyme inhibition by <italic toggle="yes">H. helix</italic> extracts for the first time. <italic toggle="yes">H. helix</italic> extract inhibited the enzyme activities of CYP2C8, CYP2C19 and CYP2D6 in a concentration-dependent manner, while it showed time-dependent inhibition for CYP2C8 and CYP2C19.
