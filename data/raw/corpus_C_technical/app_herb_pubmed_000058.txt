Potential of Natural Products of Herbal Origin as Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors.

Orhan IE(1).

Author information:
(1)Department of Pharmacognosy, Faculty of Pharmacy, Gazi University, 06330 
Ankara, Turkey. iorhan@gazi.edu.tr.

Monoamine oxidase (MAO, E.C. 1.4.3.4) is a flavin-adenine type of enzyme with 
two isoforms referred to MAO-A and MAO-B that function for oxidation of 
monoamines. While MAO-A inhibitors are effective as antidepressant and 
anxiolytic drugs (e.g. chlorgyline, moclobemide, and lazabemide), inhibitors of 
MAO-B (e.g. Ldeprenyl, pargyline, and rasagiline) are used against 
neurodegenerative diseases such as Parkinson's and Alzheimer's diseases. 
Considering the need for novel MAO inhibitors due to side effects of the current 
ones, natural products have become attractive targets for researchers. Up till 
now, many studies revealed strong MAO inhibitory activity of flavonoid, 
xanthone, alkaloid, and coumarin derivatives from herbal sources, which also 
become good models for the synthetic MAO inhibitors. For this purpose, the 
present review focuses on examples of in vitro and in vivo MAO-inhibiting 
natural compounds of plant origin from a wide variety of chemical classes 
isolated mainly between 2000 - 2015.
