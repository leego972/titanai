Phenolic constituents from Dalbergia cochinchinensis.

Shirota O(1), Pathak V, Sekita S, Satake M, Nagashima Y, Hirayama Y, Hakamata Y, 
Hayashi T.

Author information:
(1)Division of Pharmacognosy and Phytochemistry, National Institute of Health 
Sciences, 1-18-1 Kamiyoga, Setagaya-ku, Tokyo 158-8501, Japan. 
shirota@nihs.go.jp

Three new phenolic compounds (1-3), along with five known phenolics, 
4'-hydroxy-2'-methoxychalcone (4), latinone (5), dalbergiphenol (6), 
7-hydroxyflavanone, and dalbergin (7), have been isolated from the stems of 
Dalbergia cochinchinensis. The structures of 1-3 were established by 
spectroscopic techniques including 1D and 2D NMR methods. The inhibitory 
activity against testosterone 5 alpha-reductase, which causes androgen-dependent 
diseases, was also examined for selected compounds.
