Etym: [L. rete a net + -form. cf. F. rétiforme.] Defn: Composed of crossing lines and interstices; reticular; netlike; as, the retiform coat of the eye. RETINA Ret"i*na, n. Etym: [NL., from L. rete a net. Cf. Reticule.] (Anat.) Defn: The delicate membrane by which the back part of the globe of the eye is lined, and in which the fibers of the optic nerve terminate. See Eye. Note: The fibers of the optic nerve and the retinal blood vessels spread out upon the front surface of the retina, while the sensory layer (called Jacob's membrane), containing the rods and cones, is on the back side, next the choroid coat.
