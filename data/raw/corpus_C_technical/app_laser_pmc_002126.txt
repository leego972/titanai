Therefore, the next translational step should focus on rigorously characterized chronic animal models capable of reproducing this pathophysiological complexity.</p><sec id="cbf70161-sec-0220"><label>4.1</label><title>Study Limitations</title><p>This review presents important methodological limitations. The main one is the impossibility of normalizing power density between LED and LASER devices, due to the absence, in most studies, of essential information for calculating optical dose (power in the target plane, beam area and profile, and pulse parameters). This gap prevents the standardization of energy between modalities and limits more robust quantitative comparisons. There was also great heterogeneity in irradiation parameters and outcomes evaluated, reducing the internal consistency of the evidence. Furthermore, although the protocol foresaw the inclusion of studies with control, placebo, or sham groups, this review focused only on direct comparisons between LASER and LED.</p></sec></sec><sec sec-type="conclusions" id="cbf70161-sec-0230"><label>5</label><title>Conclusion</title><p>In general, evidence indicates that both LED therapy and low‐level laser therapy (LLLT) stimulate cellular and molecular mechanisms essential for tissue repair. Although LLLT demonstrates quantitatively greater effects on parameters such as blood vessel density and caliber and type I collagen deposition, both modalities show similar preclinical effects.</p></sec><sec id="cbf70161-sec-0250"><title>Ethics Statement</title><p>International Prospective Register of Systematic Review Registration approved this study number: CRD420251086145.</p></sec><sec sec-type="COI-statement" id="cbf70161-sec-0260"><title>Conflicts of Interest</title><p>The authors declare no conflicts of interest.</p></sec></body><back><ack id="cbf70161-sec-0240"><title>Acknowledgments</title><p>The authors express their gratitude to the Piauí State Research Support Foundation (FAPEPI), the Coordination for the Improvement of Higher Education Personnel (CAPES), and the Federal University of Delta do Parnaíba, which supported and facilitated this study.</p></ack><sec sec-type="data-availability" id="cbf70161-sec-0280"><title>Data Availability Statement</title><p>The data that support the findings of this study are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request.</p></sec><ref-list content-type="cited-references" id="cbf70161-bibl-0001"><title>References</title><ref id="cbf70161-bib-0001"><label>1</label><mixed-citation publication-type="journal" id="cbf70161-cit-0001">
