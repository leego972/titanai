# Mark Mwandosya

of. Mwandosya is a distinguished member of the Advisory Board of NatureNews, Africa's foremost independent newspaper that is focused on Environment, Climate Change and Sustainable Earth. In 2021, he gave the maiden anniversary lecture of NatureNews titled "Climate Change: Mitigation and Resilience."
