Steroids</title><p>A few studies have been conducted investigating the steroids in <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic>. In 2010, β-sitostenone (<bold>124</bold>), β-sitosterol (<bold>125</bold>), daucosterol (<bold>126</bold>), stigmast-4-en-β-ol-3-one (<bold>127)</bold>, and 5α,8α-epidioxy-22E-ergosta-6,22-dien-3β-ol (128) were isolated from <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> [<xref rid="B39-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">39</xref>]. Furthermore, Chen et al. found 6β-hydroxy-stigmast-4,22-dien-3-one (<bold>129</bold>), 6β-hydroxy-stigmast-4-en-3-one (<bold>130</bold>), 3-oxo-Δ4,5-sitostenone (<bold>131</bold>), β-daucosterol (<bold>132</bold>), β-stigmastero (<bold>133</bold>) and 7-ketositosterol (<bold>134</bold>) from the roots of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> [<xref rid="B28-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">28</xref>]. </p><p>Lately, stigmasterol (<bold>135</bold>), β-sitosterol-3-O-β<sc>-d-</sc>glucopyranoside (<bold>136</bold>) [<xref rid="B31-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">31</xref>], ergosterol (<bold>137</bold>), taraxasteryl acetate (<bold>138</bold>) [<xref rid="B30-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">30</xref>], 7α-hydroxy-β-sitosterol(stigmast-5-ene-3β,7α-diol) (<bold>139</bold>), stigmast-4-ene-3β,6α-diol (<bold>140</bold>) [<xref rid="B24-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">24</xref>] and 14-methyl-12,13-dehydro-sitosterol-heptadeconate (<bold>141</bold>) [<xref rid="B32-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">32</xref>] were isolated and identified in <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic>. Glycosides</title><p>In 1962, Song et al. isolated a toxic glycoside component named AA<sub>2</sub> from the fruits of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic>, which has been authenticated as atractyloside (<bold>142</bold>) by Wang in 1983 [<xref rid="B49-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">49</xref>,<xref rid="B59-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">59</xref>]. Subsequently, John et al. found another toxic ingredient known as carboxyatractyloside (<bold>143</bold>) in 1975 [<xref rid="B50-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">50</xref>]. Research showed that the content of atractyloside in <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium</italic> could be reduced after stir-flying, and its toxicity could be reduced. [<xref rid="B60-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">60</xref>] Lately, seven other glycosides were separated from the fruits of <italic toggle="yes">X. strumarium,</italic> such as 3β-norpinan-2-one 3-<italic toggle="yes">O</italic>-β<sc>-d-</sc>apiofuranosyl-(1→6)-β<sc>-d-</sc>glucopyranoside (<bold>144</bold>), (6Z)-3-hydroxymethyl-7-methylocta-1,6-dien-3-ol 8-<italic toggle="yes">O</italic>-β<sc>-d-</sc>glucopyranoside (<bold>145</bold>), (6E)-3-hydroxymethyl-7-methylocta-1,6-dien-3-ol 8-<italic toggle="yes">O</italic>-β<sc>-d-</sc>glucopyranoside (<bold>146</bold>), 7-[(β<sc>-d-</sc>apiofuranosyl-(1→6)-β<sc>-d-</sc>glucopyranosyl)oxymethy]-8,8-dimethyl-4,8-dihydrobenzo[1,4]thiazine-3,5-dione (<bold>147</bold>) [<xref rid="B41-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">41</xref>], 3’,4’-dedisulphated-atractyloside (<bold>148</bold>) [<xref rid="B46-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">46</xref>], 2-methyl-3-buten-2-ol-phated-atractylosideimethy<sc>-d-</sc>glucopyranoside (<bold>149</bold>), everlastoside C (<bold>150</bold>) [<xref rid="B51-molecules-24-00359" ref-type="bibr">51</xref>], and all glycosides are displayed in <xref ref-type="fig" rid="molecules-24-00359-f008">Figure 8</xref>.</p></sec><sec id="sec4dot6-molecules-24-00359"><title>4.6.
