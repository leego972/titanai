Answer: <p>Thanks for posting the question and answers. I have two versions of Python installed, but root was pointing to the /usr/bin version, and I wanted to use the 2.7 version of Python in /usr/local/bin. After rebuilding/reinstalling Django and mysqldb, all is well and I'm not getting the error.</p>
