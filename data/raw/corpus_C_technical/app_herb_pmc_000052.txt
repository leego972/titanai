Chemical Constituents</title><p>The wide spectrum of pharmacological effects was closely associated with various biologically active compounds of <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> roots, stem, leaves and even bark. Kuo <italic toggle="yes">et al.</italic>, reported the isolation of sixty five phenolic compounds from the <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> root [<xref rid="B36-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">36</xref>]. <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> is a rich source of various classes of bioactive compounds, which includes quassinoids, β-carboline alkaloids, canthin-6-one alkaloids, triterpene-type tirucallane, squalene derivatives, and eurycolactone, eurycomalactone, laurycolactone, biphenyl neolignan and bioactive steroids [<xref rid="B7-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">7</xref>,<xref rid="B36-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">36</xref>,<xref rid="B42-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">42</xref>,<xref rid="B43-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">43</xref>,<xref rid="B44-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">44</xref>,<xref rid="B45-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">45</xref>]. Among these, bitter tasting quassinoid phytoconstituents account for a major portion in the <italic toggle="yes">E</italic>. <italic toggle="yes">longifolia</italic> root contents. The quassinoids are a group of nortriterpenoids with dynamic pharmacological properties [<xref rid="B40-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">40</xref>]. Quassinoids, are even effective at inhibiting cell growth in nanomolar and subnanomolar concentrations [<xref rid="B41-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">41</xref>]. The presence of tirucallane and squalene-type triterpenes might be the quassinoids’ biological precursors. β-Carboline and Canthin-6-one alkaloids formed as metabolic by-products are natural amine compounds that repel herbivores and insects [<xref rid="B46-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">46</xref>]. The metabolite type and concentration in <italic toggle="yes">E</italic>. <italic toggle="yes">longifolia</italic> plant extracts, depend on the processing temperature as well as geographical factors. For standardization, it is crucial to ensure the consistency of the chemical bioactive components, particularly for the efficacy of herbal medicines [<xref rid="B47-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">47</xref>]. Summarized here are some major constituents of <italic toggle="yes">E</italic>. <italic toggle="yes">longifolia</italic> with their secondary metabolites:</p><p>Quassinoids, including various types of eurycomanone (pasakbumin-A), eurycomanols, pasakbumin-B, hydroxyklaineanones, eurycomalactones, eurycomadilactones, eurylactones, laurycolactones, longilactones, and hydroxyglaucarubol have been isolated from the roots of <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> [<xref rid="B38-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">38</xref>,<xref rid="B39-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">39</xref>,<xref rid="B48-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">48</xref>,<xref rid="B49-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">49</xref>,<xref rid="B50-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">50</xref>,<xref rid="B51-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">51</xref>,<xref rid="B52-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">52</xref>].</p><p>The squalene derivatives include teurilene, eurylene; 14-deacetyleurylene; and longilene peroxide [<xref rid="B53-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">53</xref>,<xref rid="B54-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">54</xref>].</p><p>The biphenyl neolignans class includes; 2-hydroxy-3,2,6-trimethoxy-4-(2,3-epoxy-1-hydroxypropyl)-5-(3-hydroxy-1-propenyl)-biphenyl; two isomeric 2,2-dimethoxy-4-(3-hydroxy-1-propenyl)-4-(1,2,3-trihydroxypropyl) diphenyl ethers; and 2-hydroxy-3,2-dimethoxy-4-(2,3-epoxy-1-hydroxypropyl)-5-(3-hydroxy-1-propenyl)biphenyl [<xref rid="B55-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">55</xref>].</p><p>Alkaloids included 5,9-dimethoxycanthin-6-one; 9,10-dimethoxycanthin-6-one, 11-hydroxy-10-methoxycanthin-6-one; 10-hydroxy-9-methoxycanthin-6-one; and 9-methoxy-3-methylcanthin-5,6-dione [<xref rid="B45-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">45</xref>,<xref rid="B56-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">56</xref>,<xref rid="B57-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">57</xref>].</p><p>Major isolated chemical constituents with metabolites from <italic toggle="yes">E.
