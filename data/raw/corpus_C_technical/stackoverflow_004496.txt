Question: <p>I have a series of datetime objects and would like to calculate the average delta between them.</p>

<p>For example, if the input was <code>(2008-10-01 12:15:00, 2008-10-01 12:25:00, 2008-10-01 12:35:00)</code>, then the average delta would be exactly 00:10:00, or 10 minutes.</p>

<p>Any suggestions on how to calculate this using Python?</p>

Answer: <p>You can subtract each successive date from the one prior (resulting in a timedelta object which represents the difference in days, seconds).  You can then average the timedelta objects to find your answer.</p>
