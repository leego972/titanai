Question: <p>I would like to assign a global hotkey to my Python application, running in Gnome. How do I do that? All I can find are two year old posts saying, well, pretty much nothing :-)</p>

Answer: <p>There is python-keybinder which is that same code, but packaged standalone. Also available in debian and ubuntu repositories now.</p>
