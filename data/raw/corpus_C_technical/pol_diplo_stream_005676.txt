Later years
Subsequently, Heceta returned to Spain, fighting in various naval battles against France and Great Britain in Europe.  He died there in 1807 with the rank of the lieutenant general.

Legacy
Heceta Island in Alaska, and Heceta Head and the Heceta Head Light on the Oregon Coast, are named after him. In Oregon it is pronounced "Ha–SEE–Ta" and in Alaska it is pronounced "HECK–ah–Ta."
