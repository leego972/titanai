The state of the higher nervous activity was tested in these patients with the help of a verbal test and the conditional motor reflexes were tested with verbal reinforcement, in conjunction with routine clinical examinations. The results of the verbal tests showed that the latent period of verbal reactions in most of the subjects was long-up to 1.8–6.0 s (a normal period is 1.5 s). Indeed, results of the motor-verbal test prior to treatment showed that two thirds of the patients performed poorly in mental work situations. However, after a course of therapy with <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract (10 drops; 3 times daily for 10 days) the excitation and inhibitory processes intensified and mobility normalized. In fact, the conditional motor reflexes also changed. It was seen that their value and stability increased and that the latent period reduced, whilst concentration improved and the interaction of both signal systems were normalized. Furthermore, the extract therapy reduced the latent time of verbal reactions and improved attention and memory.</p><p>Indeed, the authors observed a clinical improvement, such as disappearance of irritability and unpleasant sensations in the heart region and improvement of sleep and appetite, in these patients. Similar clinical results were obtained in another study with 177 patients suffering from vascular hypotension. The authors observed that a course of therapy with <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract stabilized, completed or partially normalized 92% of the hypotensive patients. Thus, the patients felt better, headaches disappeared, sleep became normal and they recovered their working capacity.</p><p>Several clinical studies of the dynamics of asthenic symptoms have showed that <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract was able to diminish or eliminate depressive symptoms. For example, Mikhailova reported that an open label clinical trial of <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic> extract (15 drops three times daily for a month or up to four months in some cases) in 58 patients with stress induced depression (asthenia of exogenous organic genesis), showed a remarkable symptomatic improvenment was observed [<xref rid="B178-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">178</xref>]. Their general weakness or feeling of being worn out in the morning with a high fatigability, and hypersomnia during the day (without disturbance of the following night’s sleep) either disappeared or became considerably reduced. In addition, no side effects were observed except in one patient who showed sleep disturbance. In this case, the dose was reduced to 6 drops twice a day and the adverse symptoms immediately disappeared. Indeed, most patients (39/58) suffering from pre-and intra somnia (<italic toggle="yes">i.e.,</italic> difficulties in falling asleep, waking up during the night) noted sleep improvements. However, it should be noted that sleep normalisation and reduction of other asthenic symptoms depended on the intensity of these disorders [<xref rid="B178-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">178</xref>].</p><p>Furthermore, it was seen that patients in depressive states of different genesis normally treated with tricyclic anti-depressants reduced the time spent in hospital whilst using them in combination with <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> [<xref rid="B179-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">179</xref>]. The authors observed adjuvant therapy with <italic toggle="yes">R.</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> increased general activity, intellectual and physical productivity of patients, and decreases side effects induced by the tricyclic anti-depressants.</p><p>In was reported that <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract was successfully used in the alleviation of side effects after psychotropic therapy in schizophrenic patients [<xref rid="B176-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">176</xref>]. In this short communication is mentioned that 31 patients suffering from pronounced clinical symptoms of extrapyramidal syndrome, which resulted from therapy by neuroleptics, received a high dose of <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract (25–40 drops; 2–3 times daily) for a duration of 1–1.5 months.
