Answer: <p>I've never used these APIs, but digging through the code, it looks like the class passed in is used to register the name of the class in the registry, so you can't do any initialization of your own.  But there's a method called GetServiceCustomOption that may help:</p>

<p><a href="http://mail.python.org/pipermail/python-win32/2006-April/004518.html">http://mail.python.org/pipermail/python-win32/2006-April/004518.html</a></p>
