Question: <p>How can I bind arguments to a Python method to store a nullary functor for later invocation?  Similar to C++'s <code>boost::bind</code>.</p>

Answer: <p>I'm not overly familiar with boost::bind, but the <code>partial</code> function from <code>functools</code> may be a good start:</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; from functools import partial

&gt;&gt;&gt; def f(a, b):
...     return a+b

&gt;&gt;&gt; p = partial(f, 1, 2)
&gt;&gt;&gt; p()
3

&gt;&gt;&gt; p2 = partial(f, 1)
&gt;&gt;&gt; p2(7)
8
</code></pre>
