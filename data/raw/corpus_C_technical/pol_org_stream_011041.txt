Science 
 For most of his life, Neruda was fascinated by butterflies. In 1976, a sub-group of the South American genus Heliconius was named after him (see Neruda (genus)) though not currently in use in the primary scientific literature.
 A crater on Mercury was formerly named Neruda in his honor. (As of 2023, it is still named Neruda, but has been reattributed to be named after two Czechs, 19th century poet and writer Jan Neruda, and 18th century composer Johann Baptist Georg Neruda.)
