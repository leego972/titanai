# Leipzig-class cruiser

The  class was a class of two light cruisers of the German  and later ; the class comprised , the lead ship, and , which was built to a slightly modified design. The ships were improvements over the preceding s, being slightly larger, with a more efficient arrangement of the main battery and improved armor protection.  was built between 1928 and 1931, and  followed between 1934 and 1935.

Both ships participated in the non-intervention patrols during the Spanish Civil War in 1936 and 1937. After the outbreak of World War II in September 1939, they were used in a variety of roles, including as minelayers and escort vessels. On 13 December, both ships were torpedoed by the British submarine . Steering was controlled by a single balanced rudder, which gave the ships excellent maneuverability.
