# Bas Sibum

Bas Sibum (born 26 December 1982) is a Dutch professional football manager and former player who is the head coach of  club Roda JC.

Playing career
A defensive midfielder during his playing days, he made his professional debut as part of Emmen in the 2000–01 season. He also played for FC Twente, Roda JC, NEC, Alemannia Aachen, SK Beveren and Heracles Almelo.

Managerial career
Sibum started coaching Emmen's youth in February 2017, where he was appointed coach of the under-19 team. He was since promoted to assistant coach of the first team.

On 9 May 2023, Eerste Divisie club Roda JC announced Sibum as their new head coach, replacing Edwin de Graaf. The move marked a return to the club, where he played between 2007 and 2008.
