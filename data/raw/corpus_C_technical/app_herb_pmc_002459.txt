The general reactions in this assay signaled the presence or absence of major phytochemical phytoconstituents in the extracts of <italic toggle="yes">N</italic>. <italic toggle="yes">sativa</italic> seeds.</p><sec id="sec006"><title>Test for alkaloids (Wagner’s reagents)</title><p>An amount of 1.5 ml of 1% hydrochloric acid (HCl) was added to 2.0 ml of each extract in a test tube. 6 drops of Wagner’s reagent were added after heating the test tube content over the water bath. The presence of alkaloids was showed by the formation of an orange precipitate.</p></sec><sec id="sec007"><title>Test for flavonoids</title><p>A few drops of ferric chloride hexahydrate (FeCl<sub>3</sub>∙6H<sub>2</sub>O) solution were added to 2.0 ml of each extract. The formation of an intense green color indicates the presence of flavonoid.</p></sec><sec id="sec008"><title>Test for phenols</title><p>A few drops of 5% FeCl<sub>3</sub>∙6H<sub>2</sub>O solution were added to 2.0 ml of each extract. The presence of tannins was showed by a deep blue-black color.</p></sec><sec id="sec009"><title>Test for tannins</title><p>One ml of each extract was mixed with 2.0 ml of distilled water. To this mixture, 2.0 ml of 5% FeCl<sub>3</sub>∙6H<sub>2</sub>O solution and the resulting brownish-green or dark-green solution confirms the presence of tannin.</p></sec><sec id="sec010"><title>Test for cardiac glycosides</title><p>Three mL of glacial acetic acid (CH<sub>3</sub>COOH) were added to 2.0 ml of each extract in the test tube followed by addition of 1 drop of 5% FeCl<sub>3</sub>∙6H<sub>2</sub>O. 0.5 mL of concentrated sulphuric acid (H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>) was added carefully by the side of the test tube. The blue color was formed in CH<sub>3</sub>COOH indicating the presence of Cardiac glycosides.</p></sec><sec id="sec011"><title>Test for steroids</title><p>Five ml of chloroform (CHCl<sub>3</sub>) and 2.0 ml acetic anhydride ((CH₃CO)₂O) were added to 2.0 ml of each extract followed by concentrated H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>. The reddish-brown coloration at the interface shows the presence of steroids.</p></sec><sec id="sec012"><title>Test for saponins</title><p>Each extract was diluted with distilled water and shaken in a test tube for 15 minutes. The presence of saponins is indicated by the formation of a layer of foam.</p></sec><sec id="sec013"><title>Test for terpenoids</title><p>Two ml of CHCl<sub>3</sub> were mixed with 1.0 ml of extract and 3.0 ml concentrated H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> were carefully added to form a layer. The presence of terpenoids was indicated by a reddish-brown coloration at the interface.</p></sec></sec><sec id="sec014"><title>Quantitative analysis of phytochemicals in aqueous and methanol extracts</title><sec id="sec015"><title>Determination of Total Tannin Contents (TTC)</title><p>The standard tannic acid solution was prepared by dissolving 50 mg of tannic acid (LD Didactic) in 250 ml of distilled water. Serial dilution was peformed using distilled water to prepare different concentrations (0,10, 20, 40, 60, 80 and 100 mg/L) of tannic acid standard in 50 ml volumetric flasks for the standard curve of tannic acid. The TTC was determined using Van-Burden and Robinson method [<xref rid="pone.0272457.ref019" ref-type="bibr">19</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0272457.ref020" ref-type="bibr">20</xref>]. Five ml of each standard (0, 10, 20, 40, 60, 80 and 100 mg/L of tannic acid) and one ml of each extract were added to different test tubes. The content of each test tube was mixed with 2 ml of 0.1 M FeCl<sub>3</sub>∙6H<sub>2</sub>O (LD Didactic) followed by 4 drops of 0.1 N HCl (Promark) and 3 drops of 8 mM C<sub>6</sub>FeK<sub>4</sub>N<sub>6</sub> (LD Didactic). Distilled Water was used as a blank.
