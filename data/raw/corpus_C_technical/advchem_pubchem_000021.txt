Description:
Hydroxymalonic acid is a dicarboxylic acid that is malonic acid substituted by a hydroxy group at position 2. It has a role as a plant metabolite. It is a dicarboxylic fatty acid and a 2-hydroxydicarboxylic acid. It is functionally related to a malonic acid. It is a conjugate acid of a hydroxymalonate(1-).
