Question: <p>Convert mysql timestamp to epoch time in python - is there an easy way to do this?</p>

Answer: <p>I use something like the following to get seconds since the epoch (UTC) from a MySQL date (local time):</p>

<pre><code>calendar.timegm(
   time.gmtime(
      time.mktime(
         time.strptime(t, 
                       "%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S"))))
</code></pre>
