# Corey Gaines

f the 2018–19 season, the Detroit Pistons hired Gaines as a Coaching Consultant. Before the start of the 2019–20 season, Gaines was hired as an assistant coach with the Washington Wizards.

Personal life
Gaines' father is African-American and his mother is of Japanese descent.
