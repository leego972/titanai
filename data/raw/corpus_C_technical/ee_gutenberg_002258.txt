See Rule.] 1. Conformed to a rule; agreeable to an established rule, law, principle, or type, or to established customary forms; normal; symmetrical; as, a regular verse in poetry; a regular piece of music; a regular verb; regular practice of law or medicine; a regular building. 2. REGULATIVE Reg"u*la*tive (rg"*l*tv), a. 1. Tending to regulate; regulating. Whewell. 2. (Metaph.)
