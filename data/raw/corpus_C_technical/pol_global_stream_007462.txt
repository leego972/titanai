# Pakistani passport

یلی اور ملک سے روانگی کی اطلاع سفارت خانہ یا قونصل خانہ کو دینی ضروری ہے ۔ نیز یہ کہ اگر بیرون ملک پاکستانی شہری کے کوئی بچہ پیدا ہو تو اس کا نام بھی قریب ترین پاکستانی سفارت خانہ یا قونصل خانہ میں مندرجہ بالا قواعد کے تحت رجسٹر کرائیں تاکہ بوقت ضرورت انھیں سفارت خانہ کی امداد اور مشورہ حاصل ہوسکے ۔

 English Translation (not present on the actual passport):
 Regulations:
 This passport is owned by the Government of Pakistan.
 Compliance with the existing immigration regulations in foreign countries will be mandatory on every passport holder. One must obtain a visa before entering the countries for which it is necessary to obtain a visa.
 Revoked or overdue passport should be kept with the passport for up to two years as the person concerned will need it while applying for foreign exchange.
 If the passport is lost or wasted, the police and the nearest Pakistani passport issuing office should be notified immediately. Such information should be given to the nearest Pakistani embassy or consulate in foreign countries.
 Only authorized officer can change or add to the passport. According to the law, persons can be punished according to the change or alteration of their passport, as well as those who have a passport in their possession which is not given to them legally.
 It is a criminal offence to send a passport from one country to another by mail or otherwise.
 Registration:
 Pakistani citizens residing in foreign countries for more than three months are required to register their name at the nearest Pakistani embassy or consulate every year under the Pakistan Rules of Reputation 1952. Change of address and departure from the country information must be given to the embassy or consulate. Also, if any child of a Pakistani citizen is born abroad, his name should be registered in the nearest Pakistani embassy or consulate under the above rules so that he can get the help and advice of the embassy in case of need.

History of Pakistani Passport 

The Henley Passport Index (HPI) is a yearly global assessment that meticulously evaluates and ranks countries according to the travel privileges extended to their citizens. In the HPI's 2023 report, the Pakistani passport was positioned at the 103rd spot, showcasing its travel freedoms on the international stage. This marks a subtle shift from its position at 102 in 2019. The HPI has been conducting this ranking and analysis since 2006, providing a comprehensive overview of passports from over a hundred nations based on the number of destinations their holders can access without the need for a visa.

Regrettably, the Pakistani passport has persistently remained in the lower echelons of this ranking. It's a testament to the importance of travel freedom and the complexities of international relations in today's world. The passport's standing in this global index reflects the challenges Pakistani citizens might face when it comes to visa requirements and access to various countries.

What makes the HPI particularly intriguing is its potential to offer historical insights. Prior to the index's inception in 2006, it's believed that if the strength of the Pakistani passport were to be retroactively assessed using the HPI ranking and scoring model, the Pakistani passport would have likely been positioned among the top 30 passports globally from 1961 to 1974. This underscores the historical significance of the document and the fluctuations in its international influence over the years.
