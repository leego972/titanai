Question: <p>If I have two dates (ex. <code>'8/18/2008'</code> and <code>'9/26/2008'</code>) what is the best way to get the difference measured in days?</p>

Answer: <p>You want the datetime module. </p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; from datetime import datetime 
&gt;&gt;&gt; datetime(2008,08,18) - datetime(2008,09,26) 
datetime.timedelta(4) 
</code></pre>
