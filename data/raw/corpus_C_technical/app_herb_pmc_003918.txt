# PMC: adaptogen stress response HPA axis — Overview

In addition, we explored the mechanism of action of Holixer<sup>TM</sup> on the HPA axis using two <italic toggle="yes">in vitro</italic> cell-based assays to check for its inhibitory effect on cortisol release and CRF1 receptor antagonistic activity. <italic toggle="yes">Ocimum tenuiflorum</italic> extract enhanced the swimming time in mice, reduced the stress-induced increase in immobility time, and prevented the increase in corticosterone in rats subjected to the forced swim test. that everyone undergoes in day-to-day life [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref001" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>]. Stress is a normal physiological response that helps us to notice unusual or unexpected threats in and around us. Stressful situations either make people vulnerable or increase their capacity to deal with stressful situations depending on the duration of stress and the adaptogenic capacity of people. The term “stress” was first introduced by Hens Selye into the medical world to represent the effects of anything that seriously threatens homeostasis and is stated as “nonspecific response of the body to any demand [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref002" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0285012.ref003" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>]. Acute stress (short-term extreme situations requiring a fight-or-flight response) is a survival strategy in healthy individuals [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref004" ref-type="bibr">4</xref>–<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref006" ref-type="bibr">6</xref>]. However, prolonged unmanageable stress called chronic stress leads to many diseases like cardiovascular disease [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref007" ref-type="bibr">7</xref>], diabetes [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref008" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0285012.ref009" ref-type="bibr">9</xref>] and depression [<xref rid="pone.0285012.ref008" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0285012.ref010" ref-type="bibr">10</xref>].
