The water and ethanol extracts of the leaves were prepared and for some plants, stems and roots were also prepared. Of the ethanol extracts, the highest TPC was found in <italic toggle="yes">Breynia oblongifolia</italic> leaf (128.36 ± 14.09 GAE/g extract), followed by <italic toggle="yes">Coleus amoenus</italic> root and stem with 119.67 ± 3.03 and 94.23 ± 10.16 GAE/g extract, respectively. Of the aqueous extracts, the <italic toggle="yes">C. amoenus</italic> root extract showed the highest TPC (99.88 ± 4.47 GAE/g extract), followed by <italic toggle="yes">Dodonaea lanceolata var. subsessilifolia</italic> stem extract, and <italic toggle="yes">Exocarpos latifolius</italic> root extract with 48.96 ± 3.70 and 46.66 ± 11.96 GAE/g extract, respectively (Fig. <xref rid="Fig3" ref-type="fig">3</xref>). Overall, <italic toggle="yes">Coleus amoenus</italic> had the highest TPC for the ethanol and aqueous extracts.
