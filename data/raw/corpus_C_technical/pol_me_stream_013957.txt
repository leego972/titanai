# Educação Sentimental

Educação Sentimental () is the second studio album released by Brazilian pop music band Kid Abelha. It was originally released in 1985 by WEA and sparked some successful hits in Brazilian airplay such as "Lágrimas e Chuva", "Educação Sentimental (Parts I & II)", "Garotos" and "A Fórmula do Amor".

It was listed by Rolling Stone Brazil as one of the 100 best Brazilian albums in history.
