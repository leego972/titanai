Species with high RFC values were interpreted as locally important MPs and considered priority taxa for further ethnopharmacological investigation and conservation attention.</p><p id="Par51">The Cultural Value Index (CVI) was calculated to quantify the overall cultural significance of each medicinal plant species within the study community [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR29">29</xref>]. This index integrates three key dimensions of ethnobotanical relevance: the proportion of informants citing a species, the diversity of medicinal uses reported, and the number of distinct use categories in which the species is employed. By combining these components, CVI provides a holistic measure of how deeply a plant is embedded in local traditional knowledge and its role in everyday healthcare practices. FL reflects how consistently a species is reported for a given therapeutic application and serves as an indicator of the plant’s perceived effectiveness and specificity within the local traditional healthcare system.
