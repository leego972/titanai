Question: <p>How do I convert a hex string to an int in Python? </p>

<p>I may have it as "<code>0xffff</code>" or just "<code>ffff</code>".</p>

Answer: <p>To convert a DWORD from hex to a signed integer , implement two's complement like this:</p>

<pre><code>~ (0xffffffff - int('0xdeadbeef', 16)) + 1
</code></pre>
