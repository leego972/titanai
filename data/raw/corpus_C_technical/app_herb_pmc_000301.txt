Fatty Acids and Volatile Oils</title><p>Li et al. [<xref rid="B25-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">25</xref>] analyzed the fatty acid compositions in the <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> extract using a gas chromatography-mass spectrometer, and identified 14 types of fatty acids (40–53), mainly including palmitic acid, linoleic acid, oleic acid, and linolenic acid. Shi et al. isolated 15 types of volatile oils (57–66) from <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> and 10 of them were purified and identified [<xref rid="B26-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">26</xref>]. Moreover, other researchers [<xref rid="B27-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">27</xref>] also found several different fatty acids and volatile oils in <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> (<xref ref-type="fig" rid="molecules-21-01702-f003">Figure 3</xref>, <xref rid="molecules-21-01702-t003" ref-type="table">Table 3</xref>). 4-methoxysalicylaldehyde has a unique fragrance and expresses antimicrobial and antioxidant activities [<xref rid="B28-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">28</xref>]. It can be deemed suitable as a standard in evaluating the quality of this plant [<xref rid="B29-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">29</xref>].</p></sec><sec id="sec3dot4-molecules-21-01702"><title>3.4. Terpenoids</title><p>Just like some other herbs, a small number of terpenoids were isolated from <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic>. In 1971, Mitsuhash et al. found ursolic acid, oleanolic acid, lupeol, and lupeol acetate in <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> [<xref rid="B31-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">31</xref>]. Furthermore, hederagenin, α-amyrinacetate, β-amyrinacetate, α-amyrin, β-amyrin, and scopoletin were identified in <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic>. Later, (24<italic toggle="yes">R</italic>)-9,19-cycloart-25-ene-3β,24-diol, (24<italic toggle="yes">S</italic>)-9,19-cycloart-25-ene-3β,24-diol, and cycloeucalenol were isolated from the ethyl acetate extracts of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> (<xref rid="molecules-21-01702-t004" ref-type="table">Table 4</xref>, <xref ref-type="fig" rid="molecules-21-01702-f004">Figure 4</xref>).</p></sec><sec id="sec3dot5-molecules-21-01702"><title>3.5. Others</title><p>In addition, other chemical constituents also exist in <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic>, including saccharides, aldehydes, flavonoids, etc. (<xref rid="molecules-21-01702-t005" ref-type="table">Table 5</xref>, <xref ref-type="fig" rid="molecules-21-01702-f005">Figure 5</xref>).</p></sec></sec><sec id="sec4-molecules-21-01702"><title>4. Pharmacokinetics</title><p>Studies on the pharmacokinetics (PK) of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> are somewhat limited. Available literature mainly focused on its toxic component, periplocin. There is a report on the PK study of periplocin with its two metabolites (periplocymarin and periplogenin) in rat plasma and periplogenin in rat plasma. He et al. [<xref rid="B6-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">6</xref>] studied the PK of <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic> in rat’s serum. This was studied to determine the concentrations of periplocin and two metabolites simultaneously in rats’ plasma by using LC-MS/MS. The rats were given 50 mg/kg periplocin dissolved in water by gavage. As shown from the results, they found that the concentration of periplocin could only be detected at a few time points; however, its two metabolites (periplocymarin and periplogenin) reached the maximum plasma concentration after 8–10 h. The Cmax for periplocymarin and periplogenin were 1655.63 ± 404.26 ng/mL and 32.94 ± 9.16 ng/mL, respectively. Furthermore, research was done using the same method coupled with high performance liquid chromatography tandem mass spectrometry to validate the quantifying method of periplogenin in rat plasma using psoralen as an internal standard (IS) [<xref rid="B48-molecules-21-01702" ref-type="bibr">48</xref>]. All these studies provided the pharmacokinetics (PK) information of the toxic compounds in <italic toggle="yes">Cortex Periplocae</italic>.
