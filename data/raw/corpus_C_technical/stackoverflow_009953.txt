Answer: <p>This is the exact behavior you would get if your Python script does not have the executable permission set.</p>

<p>Try:</p>

<pre><code>chmod a+x foo.py
</code></pre>

<p>(where foo.py is your script name).</p>

<p>See the <a href="http://httpd.apache.org/docs/1.3/howto/cgi.html#filepermissions">Apache tutorial</a> for more information.</p>
