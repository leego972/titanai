To overcome this major barrier and enhancing half-life and cellular permeability of curcumin, we employed an amphipathic and biodegradable nanocarrier which was previously synthesized by our group called dendrosomal nanocurcumin (DNC) [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref020" ref-type="bibr">20</xref>–<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref022" ref-type="bibr">22</xref>].</p><p>The cutaneous wound healing process is generally considered to have four distinct phases: 1. haemostasis, 2. inflammation, 3. proliferation and 4. Remodeling. Numerous immune and non-immune cells, chemical agents and factors are involved in each phase [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref023" ref-type="bibr">23</xref>]. Among cells, fibroblasts have been introduced as one of the key factors and have been investigated in the field of wound healing [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref024" ref-type="bibr">24</xref>]. Additionally, the most important factors pointed out in several studies are transforming growth factor beta (TGF-β) as a growth factor involved in growth, survival, differentiation, migration, angiogenesis and as an anti-inflammatory cytokine [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref025" ref-type="bibr">25</xref>] as well as vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) as the principal growth factor required in the angiogenesis process giving rise to vessel formation by supplying key nutrition supplements for the regenerated tissue [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref026" ref-type="bibr">26</xref>]. Over-expression of these two factors from fibroblasts can help to improve the wound healing process [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref024" ref-type="bibr">24</xref>]. On the other side, Tumor necrosis factor alpha (TNF-α) and Interleukin-6 (IL-6) are the most important pro-inflammatory cytokines in the process of wound healing released from haematopoietic and non- haematopoietic population such as fibroblasts. During the process of wound healing, TNF-α stimulates inflammation and growth factor production from immune cells primarily, the excess production of this cytokine provokes persistent inflammation yielding impaired wound healing as long as tissue damage [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref027" ref-type="bibr">27</xref>]. Stimuli like persistent DNA damage and tissue destruction activates secretion of IL-6 by variety of cells including fibroblasts [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref028" ref-type="bibr">28</xref>–<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref030" ref-type="bibr">30</xref>]. Over and above that, IL-6 expression is induced by TNF-α at the inflammatory stage as well [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref030" ref-type="bibr">30</xref>]. Therefore, reducing inflammatory factors which are prolonged in chronic wounds may play a key role in shortening the inflammation phase, and also in promoting tissue repair and healing process by stimulating the proliferation stage [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref031" ref-type="bibr">31</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref032" ref-type="bibr">32</xref>].</p><p>According to previous studies, LLLT and curcumin each their own were able to accelerate the wound healing process by stimulating growth factor production and therefore proliferation and migration of cells involved in repair process such as fibroblasts, reduction of inflammatory factors and shortening of the inflammatory phase, inducing angiogenesis, granulation tissue formation [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref033" ref-type="bibr">33</xref>–<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref037" ref-type="bibr">37</xref>]. Also, it was declared that the progressive effect of these two therapeutic strategies was through regulating the production and accumulation of Reactive Oxygen Species (ROS) [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref038" ref-type="bibr">38</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref039" ref-type="bibr">39</xref>]. The natural antioxidant curcumin can inhibit oxidative stress by scavenging free radicals like ROS or by inhibiting ROS-generating enzymes as well as activating ROS-neutralizing enzymes [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref039" ref-type="bibr">39</xref>]. And LLLT, along with blue light, is suggested to increase levels of ROS and these alterations activate some transcription factors which lead growth factor production and cell proliferation [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref015" ref-type="bibr">15</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref040" ref-type="bibr">40</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref041" ref-type="bibr">41</xref>]. Despite that, prolonged oxidative stress has been evident to mutilate wound healing process by inducing cell apoptosis. Thereby balance between the favorable act of ROS and their detrimental effects is demanded for proper wound repair [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref038" ref-type="bibr">38</xref>].</p><p>On the basis of the concentration exerted, curcumin can exhibit antioxidant or pro-oxidant effects, therefor this study was performed to explore whether the antioxidant concentration of the compound (DNC) is able to induce proliferation and migration in MEFs cells in combination with LLLT (450 nm).
