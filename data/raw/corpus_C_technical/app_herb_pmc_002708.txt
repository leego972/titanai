We recorded 17 different categories in which plants are used, the five more important ways of uses are: boiled (135 species), infusion (98 species), chewing (38 species), milled (13 species), and smeared (12 species) (Figure <xref ref-type="fig" rid="F5">5</xref>).</p><fig id="F4" position="float" orientation="portrait"><label>Figure 4</label><caption><p>Main categories of use of plants and number of species per category in Rayones, Nuevo, León, México.</p></caption><graphic xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink" position="float" orientation="portrait" xlink:href="1746-4269-10-62-4.jpg"/></fig><fig id="F5" position="float" orientation="portrait"><label>Figure 5</label><caption><p>Main categories and number of species per category in which plants are used in Rayones, Nuevo, León, México.</p></caption><graphic xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink" position="float" orientation="portrait" xlink:href="1746-4269-10-62-5.jpg"/></fig></sec><sec><title>Parts of plants most commonly used</title><p>People from Rayones utilize almost all parts of the plant, however, the leaves, are by far, the most commonly used part, we recorded 126 species which the leaves are used in different ways, followed by the logs (stems) (57 species), branches (52) and fruits (51) (Figure <xref ref-type="fig" rid="F6">6</xref>).</p><fig id="F6" position="float" orientation="portrait"><label>Figure 6</label><caption><p>Morphological constituents of the plant most commonly used in Rayones, Nuevo León, México.</p></caption><graphic xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink" position="float" orientation="portrait" xlink:href="1746-4269-10-62-6.jpg"/></fig></sec><sec><title>Medicinal plants</title><p>Of all the species recorded, 160 of them are used as medicinal, they includes 64 families and 139 genera. The families with the highest number of medicinal species are Asteraceae (19 species), Lamiaceae (11), Cactaceae (9), Euphorbiaceae (9), Rutaceae (7), Agavaceae (6), Fabaceae (6) and Rosaceae (6). The genera with the higher number of medicinal species are: <italic toggle="yes">Agave</italic> (5), <italic toggle="yes">Acacia</italic> (3), <italic toggle="yes">Citrus</italic> (3), <italic toggle="yes">Euphorbia</italic> (3), <italic toggle="yes">Opuntia</italic> (3), <italic toggle="yes">Allium</italic> (2), <italic toggle="yes">Artemisia</italic> (2), <italic toggle="yes">Commelina</italic> (2), <italic toggle="yes">Cucurbita</italic> (2) and <italic toggle="yes">Dyssodia</italic> (2). Twelve of the species has at least 10 different medicinal uses (Figure <xref ref-type="fig" rid="F7">7</xref>). According to the information recorded, several species are used to heal different ailments, such as gastrointestinal ills, renal diseases, respiratory diseases, menstrual cramps, and diabetes (Figure <xref ref-type="fig" rid="F8">8</xref>).</p><fig id="F7" position="float" orientation="portrait"><label>Figure 7</label><caption><p>Medicinal plants with 10 or more uses in Rayones, Nuevo León, Mexico.</p></caption><graphic xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink" position="float" orientation="portrait" xlink:href="1746-4269-10-62-7.jpg"/></fig><fig id="F8" position="float" orientation="portrait"><label>Figure 8</label><caption><p>Main illness recorded and number of medicinal species used to heal them in Rayones, Nuevo León, Mexico.</p></caption><graphic xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink" position="float" orientation="portrait" xlink:href="1746-4269-10-62-8.jpg"/></fig></sec><sec><title>Food plants</title><p>Food plants are used in 11 different ways such as edible fruits (40 species), condiment (20), tea (16), green salad (13), food supplement (13) and confectionery (11). The families most commonly used were Rosaceae (11 species), Lamiaceae (10), Cactaceae (6), Rutaceae (6), Asteraceae (4) and Solanaceae (4). The genera with the higher number of food plants are <italic toggle="yes">Agave</italic> (5), <italic toggle="yes">Opuntia</italic> (4), <italic toggle="yes">Citrus</italic> (4), <italic toggle="yes">Prunus</italic> (4) and <italic toggle="yes">Cucurbita</italic> (3).
