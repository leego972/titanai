Description:
2,4-diaminobutyric acid is a diamino acid that is butyric acid in which a hydrogen at position 2 and a hydrogen at position 4 are replaced by amino groups. It is a gamma-amino acid, a diamino acid and a non-proteinogenic alpha-amino acid. It is functionally related to a butyric acid.
