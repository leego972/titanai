Title: A MEMS OPTICAL MOIRÉ SHEAR STRESS SENSOR FOR HARSH ENVIRONMENT APPLICATIONS

Abstract:
This paper describes the development of a shear stress sensor for use in harsh environments as well as experimental characterization of the sensor system. The microelectromechanical systems (MEMS) sensor utilizes optical moir transduction of a floating element structure created using a silicon-on-Pyrex fabrication process.
