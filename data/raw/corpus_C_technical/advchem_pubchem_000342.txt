CID: 5022
Source: ChEBI

Description:
(-)-N(6)-(2-phenylisopropyl)adenosine is a hydrocarbyladenosine in which adenosine is substituted at N-6 by a (2R)-1-phenylpropan-2-yl group. It is a potent adenosine A1 receptor agonist that exhibits neuroprotective and antinociceptive properties. It has a role as a neuroprotective agent and an adenosine A1 receptor agonist. It is a member of benzenes, a hydrocarbyladenosine, an aromatic amine and a secondary amino compound. It is functionally related to an adenosine.
