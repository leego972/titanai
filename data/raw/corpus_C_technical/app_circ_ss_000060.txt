Title: 1.8-V Low Power, High-Resolution, High-Speed Comparator With Low Offset Voltage Implemented in 45nm CMOS Technology

Abstract:

It is a custom-made comparator for a highly linear 4-bit Flash A/D Converter (ADC). The outlined design can operate on a nominal supply of 1.8 V. The comparator offset voltage was elevated because of this mismatch. To compensate for the offset voltage, we followed a decent approach to design the circuits.
