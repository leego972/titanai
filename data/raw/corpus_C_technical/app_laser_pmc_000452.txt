<sup>36</sup>
</xref> Thus, the chronic nature of the disease and recurrence rates are a major health issue. The clearance and recurrence rates in this study have solely been observed on visible lesions due to standardized assessments. However, it should be noted that the treatment was applied field‐directed on an area with presumably plenty of subclinical lesions. Field‐directed treatment might therefore be an excellent tool to treat subclinical lesions to prevent their occurrence and progression. Over the course of this 12‐month follow‐up (ALA‐AK‐CT007), only 7.4% of participants had developed new AK in the treatment field. This is in agreement with observations made in a study with a similar field‐directed PDT protocol used for AK in the periphery.<xref rid="jdv20452-bib-0021" ref-type="bibr">
<sup>21</sup>

<sup>37</sup>
</xref> showed that repeated field‐directed PDT treatments can eliminate subclinical lesions in sun‐exposed but clinically normal skin in organ transplant recipients, thereby delaying the first appearance of AK.
