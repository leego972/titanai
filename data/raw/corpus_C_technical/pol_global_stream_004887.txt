# Immigrant communities in Northern Ireland

Poland joined the European Union, with many coming on a short-term basis for work. A recent report showed that in the Police Service of Northern Ireland's most recent recruitment drive, nearly 1,000 Polish people applied to become officers. There are a lot of Polish people in Antrim, County Antrim.

Notable people
There are a number of well known people from Northern Ireland who are from an ethnic minority background. Chaim Herzog, President of Israel from 1983 to 1993, was born in Belfast. Others include the broadcaster Lata Sharma (who is Asian), Andre Shoukri, a convicted UDA terrorist whose father is from Egypt, the Liberal Democrat politician Lembit Opik, who is of Estonian parentage, and the Alliance Party assembly member for South Belfast, Anna Lo MLA, who is Hong Kong Chinese. Freddie Scappaticci and Angelo Fusco were two IRA members of Italian ancestry. Lilian Seenoi-Barr is the first Black Politician in NI and was selected for a Foyleside seat in Derry Council by the SDLP in 2021
