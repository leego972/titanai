# Hamish Brown

2006, he released the book The Mountains Look on Marrakech an account of a 90-day end to end trek of the Atlas Mountains. In 2008, Brown took a break from writing books on walking when he released The Scottish Graveyard Miscellany, a book about the design and art of gravestones throughout Scotland.
