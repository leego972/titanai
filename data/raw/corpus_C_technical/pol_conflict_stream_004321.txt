# Winkie (novel)

He travels to Cairo, Egypt with Françoise – a lesbian cleaning woman who mended him after he was shot during his arrest in the forest – where he begins to accept and understand why everything has happened to him.

Reception
The Dallas Morning News praised the character of Winkie, saying that he is "one [of] the most memorable stuffed animals since the Velveteen Rabbit." Time Out Chicago praised Clifford's writing, saying that " what elevates Chase's work above simple cleverness is the magic of the prose itself, which derives beauty from all things. Publishers Weekly placed Chase  "in the same league as David Sedaris," noting that his Winkie "is way too odd to be sentimental."
