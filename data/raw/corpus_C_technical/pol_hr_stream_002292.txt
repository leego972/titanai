# Human Rights Act 2004

ney-General and Commission

The following amendment specifies who the Supreme Court should communicate with, in the event, a case is heard that relates to an individual exercising a Human Right. It is made explicit, that both the Attorney-General and the Human Rights Commissioner should be notified.
