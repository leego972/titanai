Knowledge of traditional medicine and modern practices of processing botanical agents, form the basis of modern phytotherapy.</p><p id="Par61">The use of innovative pharmaceutical techniques and processes plays a vital role in the research and production of herbal medicines. This production process is named phytoneering and it refers to a special link between nature and plants (phytos) on one hand, and science and technology on the other (mechanics) [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR80">80</xref>]. At the same time, it represents an international orientation of turning the highest quality of standards for herbal medicines into a reality. A key element of this concept is the continuous optimization and certification of all production processes which starts with the selection of the seeds, continues with the cultivation of the plants, the isolation of the active substances from the parts of the plant and the final production of the medicinal preparation [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR80">80</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR83">83</xref>].</p><p id="Par62">In phytoneering, every phase of production is based on scientific efficiency and cutting-edge technical methods [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR80">80</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR83">83</xref>]. The production as a whole is adapted to fulfilling the special requirements necessary in the processing of herbal substances. Continuous quality control measures ensure the manufacturing process, from the cultivation of the plants to the release of the medicinal product and delivery to pharmacies [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR80">80</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR83">83</xref>]. The high concentration of active chemical compounds in the final product is the result of this optimized high-tech production process, which is the foundation basis of phytoneering [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR80">80</xref>].</p><p id="Par63">Phytoneering also ensures that the company's herbal medicines are tested for efficacy and safety [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR80">80</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR83">83</xref>]. Herbal medicines are tested in preclinical and clinical studies that meet the same strict criteria for synthetically produced prescription substances. Thus, specific pharmaceutical companies carry out randomized studies according to GCP (good clinical practice) and in this way excellent efficacy documentation is obtained. However, the optimization of the production process also requires up to date knowledge of the underlying mechanisms [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR80">80</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR83">83</xref>]. This is a basic axis which is supported by the conduction of studies in institutes and universities [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR80">80</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR83">83</xref>].</p><p id="Par64">Therefore, due to the systematic application of the phytoneering concept, herbal preparations differ in quality and active substances, with these modern herbal medicines being at least on par with synthetically produced medicines, in terms of quality, safety and efficacy, but having significantly less unwanted side effects [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR80">80</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR83">83</xref>]. In conclusion, phytoneering is the link between traditional botanology and modern medicine [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR80">80</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR83">83</xref>].</p><p id="Par65">The composition of the natural dry extract BNO 1016, in terms of herbs, is identical to that of BNO 1011, however, the dry extract BNO 1016 contains comparatively higher concentrations of the active ingredients, since it was prepared with a 59% ethanol extract (V/V) (with a drug: extract ratio of 4.2:1) [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR84">84</xref>–<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR86">86</xref>]. Both formulations were manufactured using a validated GMP manufacturing process, applying comprehensive specifications and standardized production processes, to guarantee high product quality from batch to batch [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR84">84</xref>–<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR86">86</xref>]. The quality of herbal medicines is determined according to the relevant EMA (European Medicines Agency) guidelines for herbal medicinal products.</p></sec><sec id="Sec19"><title>Conclusion</title><p id="Par66">Phytoneering is an innovative pharmaceutical technique in the field of research and production of herbal medicines [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR84">84</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR85">85</xref>]. Herbal extracts produced with phytoneering differ in quality and active substances but in terms of quality, safety and efficacy, these modern herbal medicines can be comparable to synthetically produced medicines, having much less unwanted side effects [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR80">80</xref>].
