Thus, the lateral size of the dura mater defect was estimated as 15 μm × 113 frames– 1.7 mm. The same calculation showed the lateral size of the right punctured hole was approximately 1.4 mm (<xref ref-type="fig" rid="pone.0172149.g006">Fig 6B</xref>). The average size of 1.55 mm was comparable to the size of the needle tip (1.47 mm). The OCT image could be used to target an epidural blood patch (EBP) and check hematoma formation within the space just after the procedure. We have recently shown that the ES can be identified from external tissues, based on the visual details of subsurface morphological information on 2D OCT images [<xref rid="pone.0172149.ref008" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>].
