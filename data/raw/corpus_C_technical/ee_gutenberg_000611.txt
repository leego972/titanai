COMPLAIN Com*plain", v. i. [imp. & p. p. Complained; p. pr. & vb. n. To give utterance to expression of grief, pain, censure, regret. etc.; to lament; to murmur; to find fault; -- commonly used with of. Also, to creak or squeak, as a timber or wheel.
