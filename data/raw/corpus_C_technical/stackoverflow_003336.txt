Question: <p>In C# I could easily write the following:</p>

<pre><code>string stringValue = string.IsNullOrEmpty( otherString ) ? defaultString : otherString;
</code></pre>

<p>Is there a quick way of doing the same thing in Python or am I stuck with an 'if' statement?</p>

Answer: <p><a href="http://www.diveintopython.net/power_of_introspection/and_or.html#d0e9975" rel="nofollow">Chapter 4 of diveintopython.net</a> has the answer.  It's called the and-or trick in Python.</p>
