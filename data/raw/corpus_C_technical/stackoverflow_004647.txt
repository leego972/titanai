Question: <p>What is the regular expression to match strings (in this case, file names) that start with 'Run' and have a filename extension of '.py'?</p>

<p>The regular expression should match any of the following:</p>

<pre><code>RunFoo.py
RunBar.py
Run42.py
</code></pre>

<p>It should not match:</p>

<pre><code>myRunFoo.py
RunBar.py1
Run42.txt
</code></pre>

<p>The SQL equivalent of what I am looking for is <code>... LIKE 'Run%.py' ...</code>.</p>

Answer: <p>If you write a slightly more complex regular expression, you can get an extra feature: extract the bit between "Run" and ".py":</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; import re
&gt;&gt;&gt; regex = '^Run(?P&lt;name&gt;.*)\.py$'
&gt;&gt;&gt; m = re.match(regex, 'RunFoo.py')
&gt;&gt;&gt; m.group('name')
'Foo'
</code></pre>

<p>(the extra bit is the parentheses and everything between them, except for '.*' which is as in Rob Howard's answer)</p>
