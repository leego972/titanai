# List of NBC television affiliates (table) — Overview

The National Broadcasting Company is a television network based in the United States made up of 12 owned-and-operated stations and nearly 223 network affiliates. Stations are listed in alphabetical order by city of license. A blue background indicates an affiliate originating as a digital subchannel. A gray background indicates a low-power station. A lavender blue background indicates an affiliate originating as a digital subchannel of a low-power station. (**) – Indicates station was built and signed on by NBC. Owned-and-operated stations
