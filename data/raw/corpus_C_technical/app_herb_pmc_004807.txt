Salidroside also inhibited the production of several inflammatory cytokines, including TNF-α, IL-6, and IL-1β, and NF-κB DNA-binding activation after LPS challenge [<xref rid="b54-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">54</xref>]. These results indicated that salidroside exerts a protective effect on LPS-induced acute lung injury in mice. Another study reported that salidroside downregulated the phosphorylation of LPS-induced NF-κB p65 and an inhibitor of NF-κB α (IκBα) in the NF-κB signal pathway and suppressed the phosphorylation of p38, ERK, and c-jun NH(2)-terminal kinase (JNK) in MAPK signaling pathways [<xref rid="b55-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">55</xref>,<xref rid="b56-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">56</xref>]. Salidroside is an effective suppressor of inflammation and may be a candidate for the prophylaxis of mastitis.</p><p>Salidroside pretreatment reduced the ratio of concanavalin-A-induced aspartate transaminase to alanine transaminase (also called the aspartate-aminotransferase-to-alanine-aminotransferase ratio) markedly and suppressed the secretion of proinflammatory cytokines by downregulating the activity of NF-κB [<xref rid="b57-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">57</xref>]. Salidroside altered the distribution of CD4<sup>+</sup> and CD8<sup>+</sup> T lymphocytes in the liver and spleen by regulating CXCL-10 and reduced the severity of liver injuries [<xref rid="b57-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">57</xref>].</p></sec><sec sec-type="other"><title>6. Antinociceptive effect</title><p>Studies have reported that <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic> extract exhibits analgesic activity. The antinociceptive effect of <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic> extract was evaluated using the hot-plate test, Randall-Selitto test, and formalin test in male Wistar rats. In the hot-plate test, orally administering <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola rosea</italic> at 50 mg/kg and 100 mg/kg body weight increased the latency reaction [<xref rid="b48-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">48</xref>]. In the Randall-Selitto test, <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola rosea</italic> caused a significant increase in pressure reaction at 50 mg/kg [<xref rid="b48-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">48</xref>]. In the formalin test, <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola rosea</italic> significantly reduced the paw-licking time during the first phase at 100 mg/kg [<xref rid="b48-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">48</xref>]. The studies indicated that <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola rosea</italic> extract exhibited significant analgesic activity in different pain models, inhibiting thermal pain, mechanical hyperalgesia, and formalin-induced pain behavior.</p></sec><sec sec-type="other"><title>7. Immune system</title><p><italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic> aqueous extract significantly enhanced tetanus toxoid-specific immunoglobulin levels in rats [<xref rid="b58-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">58</xref>] and the level of ovalbumin-induced antibody responses. Tetanus toxoid and ovalbumin in combination with complete Freund's adjuvant or <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic> aqueous extract were observed to evoke a significant delayed-type hypersensitivity response [<xref rid="b58-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">58</xref>]. <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic> aqueous extract did not suppress the swelling response in an adjuvant-induced arthritis model [<xref rid="b58-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">58</xref>]. <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic> aqueous extract exhibits adjuvant and immunopotentiating activity.</p><p><italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola imbricata</italic> rhizome extract inhibited the proliferation of the human T-cell lymphoma cell line EL-4 and erythroleukemic cell line HL-60 [<xref rid="b59-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">59</xref>]. Furthermore, treating human peripheral blood mononuclear cells (hPBMCs) with LPS and <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola imbricata</italic> extract suppressed regulated upon activation, normal T cell expressed and secreted production [<xref rid="b59-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">59</xref>]. However, the number of TNF-α spots in <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola imbricata</italic>-rhizome-extract-treated hPBMCs increased. <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola imbricata</italic> extract upregulated TLR-4 mRNA expression in the splenocytes of rats [<xref rid="b59-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">59</xref>].</p><p>Aqueous and 50% hydroalcoholic extracts of <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola quadrifida</italic> stimulated granulocyte activity <italic toggle="yes">in vitro</italic> and increased the response of lymphocytes to mitogens in mice and rats [<xref rid="b60-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">60</xref>].
