Question: <p>How can i receive and send email in python? A 'mail server' of sorts.</p>

<p>I am looking into making an app that listens to see if it recieves an email addressed to foo@bar.domain.com, and sends an email to the sender.</p>

<p>Now, am i able to do this all in python, would it be best to use 3rd party libraries? </p>

Answer: <p>Depending on the amount of mail you are sending you might want to look into using a real mail server like postifx or sendmail (*nix systems) Both of those programs have the ability to send a received mail to a program based on the email address. </p>
