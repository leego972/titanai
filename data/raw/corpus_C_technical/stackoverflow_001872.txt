Answer: <p>My guess is as follows.  The PythonWin editor offers autocomplete for an object, i.e. when you type <code>myobject.</code> it offers a little popup of all the availble method names.  So I think when you type <code>X().</code> it's creating an instance of <code>X</code> in the background and doing a <code>dir</code> or similar to find out the attributes of the object.</p>

<p>So the constructor is only being run <em>once for each object</em> but to give you the interactivity it's creating objects silently in the background without telling you about it.</p>
