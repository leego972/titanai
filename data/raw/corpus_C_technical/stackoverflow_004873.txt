Question: <p>How can I distribute a standalone Python application in Linux?</p>

<p>I think I can take for granted the presence of a recent Python interpreter in any modern distribution. The problem is dealing with those libraries that do not belong to the standard library, i.e. wxPython, scipy, python cryptographic toolkit, reportlab, and so on.</p>

<p>Is there a working Linux counterpart to, say, py2exe (which, by the way, I have never tried)?
Is there a free, opensource one?</p>

Answer: <p>You might want to look at the dependency declarations in <a href="http://peak.telecommunity.com/DevCenter/setuptools?action=highlight&amp;value=EasyInstall#declaring-dependencies">setuptools</a>.  This might provide a way to assure that the right packages are either available in the environment or can be installed by someone with appropriate privileges.</p>
