Question: <p>What is the regular expression to match strings (in this case, file names) that start with 'Run' and have a filename extension of '.py'?</p>

<p>The regular expression should match any of the following:</p>

<pre><code>RunFoo.py
RunBar.py
Run42.py
</code></pre>

<p>It should not match:</p>

<pre><code>myRunFoo.py
RunBar.py1
Run42.txt
</code></pre>

<p>The SQL equivalent of what I am looking for is <code>... LIKE 'Run%.py' ...</code>.</p>

Answer: <p>You don't need a regular expression, you can use glob, which takes wildcards e.g. Run*.py</p>

<p>For example, to get those files in your current directory...</p>

<pre><code>import os, glob
files = glob.glob( "".join([ os.getcwd(), "\\Run*.py"]) )
</code></pre>
