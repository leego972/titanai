These two species have considerable agreement amongst market vendors on their particular use and credibility and therefore could be further analyzed for developing pharmafood or pharmaceutical products.</p><p>The market vendors had a tendency to rely on 11 medicinal plant species to treat ailments related to nine medical categories.
