# PMC: adaptogen stress response HPA axis — Overview

<italic toggle="yes">R. rosea</italic> HRE contains salidroside and rosavin (Supplementary data, Fig. <xref rid="fsn31249-sup-0001" ref-type="supplementary-material">S1</xref>A). UHPLC‐MS analysis confirmed the presence of these two compounds (peaks 7 and 15) (Fig. <xref rid="fsn31249-sup-0001" ref-type="supplementary-material">S1</xref>B and Table <xref rid="fsn31249-sup-0002" ref-type="supplementary-material">S1</xref>). Three monoterpene glycosides corresponding to rhodiolosides E, B (or C) and rosiridin (peaks 13 and 24) and several phenylpropane derivatives, including rosarin and rosin, were identified (peaks 15–16 and 18).
