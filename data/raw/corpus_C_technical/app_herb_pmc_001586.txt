Novel Chalcones</title><p>A new chalcone isolated from <italic toggle="yes">Millettia pachycarpa</italic> (compound <bold>101</bold>, <xref rid="molecules-27-08307-f008" ref-type="fig">Figure 8</xref>) showed more potent antiproliferative effects (at 2 µM) against HeLa cell than cisplatin [<xref rid="B141-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">141</xref>]. Recently, two newly synthesized derivatives of compound <bold>101</bold> have been reported to have a very potent cytotoxicity than compound <bold>18</bold> in vitro and in vivo (at nanomolar concentration) against many cancer cell lines. These compounds induced apoptosis via G<sub>2</sub>/M arrest, inhibited tububin polymerization, repressed MDR phenotype, and had little cytotoxicity against normal cells [<xref rid="B142-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">142</xref>,<xref rid="B143-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">143</xref>]. Two new chalcone dimers (compound <bold>102</bold> and <bold>103</bold>, <xref rid="molecules-27-08307-f008" ref-type="fig">Figure 8</xref>) isolated from <italic toggle="yes">Helichrysum zivojinii</italic> inhibited the growth of both sensitive and resistant lung carcinoma NCI-H460, NCI-H460/R, and HaCaT cells [<xref rid="B144-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">144</xref>]. Compound <bold>102</bold> suppressed the topoisomerase IIα and significantly enhanced doxorubicin activity (a typical anticancer drug that suppresses topoisomerase IIα) when combined. Compounds <bold>103</bold> increased the efficacy of tipifarnib (farnesyltransferase inhibitor used in the treatment of leukemia) against MDR cancer cells [<xref rid="B144-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">144</xref>].</p><p>A novel antiproliferative phytochemical belonging to polyprenylated benzophenone (Compound <bold>104</bold>, <xref rid="molecules-27-08307-f009" ref-type="fig">Figure 9</xref>) was isolated from <italic toggle="yes">Garcinia epunctata</italic> [<xref rid="B145-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">145</xref>]. The compound showed anti-MDR effects with relative reversal (RR) values of 0.5, 0.73, and 0.86 in HCT116 (p53−/−), CEM/ADR5000, and U87MG.ΔEGFR glioblastoma cell lines, respectively, and a collateral resistance (CR) value of 0.22 in MDA-MB-231-BCRP cancer cell [<xref rid="B146-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">146</xref>]. A novel polyene (compound <bold>105</bold>, <xref rid="molecules-27-08307-f009" ref-type="fig">Figure 9</xref>) isolated from the root bark of Oplopanax horridus [<xref rid="B147-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">147</xref>] was found to inhibit growth of HCT-116, MCF-7, and SW-480 cancer cells at &lt;10 µM [<xref rid="B148-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">148</xref>]. A new phytosterol (compound <bold>106</bold>, <xref rid="molecules-27-08307-f009" ref-type="fig">Figure 9</xref>) belonging to the withanolides was recently isolated from <italic toggle="yes">Datura inoxia.</italic> This phytosterol was reported to inhibit the growth of HCT15 lung carcinoma at 4 µM [<xref rid="B149-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">149</xref>]. A juglone analogue (compound <bold>107</bold>, <xref rid="molecules-27-08307-f009" ref-type="fig">Figure 9</xref>) isolated from the root of <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum cuspidatum</italic> inhibited the growth of hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) and HCC cancer stem cells via the blockage of the STAT3 signaling pathway [<xref rid="B150-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">150</xref>]. New phytochemicals isolated from <italic toggle="yes">Brugmansia suaveolens</italic> (compounds <bold>108</bold> and <bold>109</bold>, <xref rid="molecules-27-08307-f009" ref-type="fig">Figure 9</xref>) showed immunomodulatory potentials against PBMC-immunostimulated MCF7, A549, and HL-60 cell lines via enhanced IL-2 and IFN-γ secretions and IL-1β reduction [<xref rid="B151-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">151</xref>,<xref rid="B152-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">152</xref>].</p><p>Finally, polysaccharides from various plants have been reported to have anticancer effects due to their scavenging, antioxidant, and reducing ability, as well as their cytotoxicity [<xref rid="B153-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">153</xref>,<xref rid="B154-molecules-27-08307" ref-type="bibr">154</xref>].
