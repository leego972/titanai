Question: <p>In python, under what circumstances is SWIG a better choice than ctypes for calling entry points in shared libraries? Let's assume you don't already have the SWIG interface file(s). <br><br>What are the performance metrics of the two?</p>

Answer: <p>Something to keep in mind is that SWIG targets only the CPython implementation.  Since ctypes  is also supported by the PyPy and IronPython implementations it may be worth writing your modules with ctypes for compatibility with the wider Python ecosystem.</p>
