Question: <p>In Python, how do I jump to a file in the Windows Explorer? I found a solution for jumping to folders:</p>

<pre><code>import subprocess
subprocess.Popen('explorer "C:\path\of\folder"')
</code></pre>

<p>but I have no solution for files.</p>

Answer: <p>For some reason, on windows 7 it always opens the users Path, for me following worked out:</p>

<pre><code>import subprocess
subprocess.call("explorer C:\\temp\\yourpath", shell=True)
</code></pre>
