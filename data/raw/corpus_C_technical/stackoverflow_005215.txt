Answer: <p>I haven't tried it myself (yes, I just used Google and followed some links), but <a href="http://www.lag.net/paramiko/" rel="nofollow">http://www.lag.net/paramiko/</a> seems to be the recommended solution. From a cursory glance, it's an SSH implementation in pure Python, which allows tunneling for things like FTP.</p>

<p>Update: a commenter pointed out that I mixed up sftp and ftps, sorry. I still suggest at least investigating Paramiko briefly to see if it matches the requirements.</p>
