Comment in
    Aust Dent J. 1997 Dec;42(6):414. doi: 10.1111/j.1834-7819.1997.tb06089.x.

Despite more than 30 years of experience with low level laser therapy (LLLT) or 
'biostimulation' in dentistry, concerns remain as to its effectiveness as a 
treatment modality. Controlled clinical studies have demonstrated that while 
LLLT is effective for some specific applications, it is not a panacea. This 
paper provides an outline of the biological basis of LLLT and summarizes the 
findings of controlled clinical studies of the use of LLLT for specific soft 
tissue applications in dentistry. Areas of controversy where there is a pressing 
need for further research are identified.
