Dana. DOME Dome, n. Etym: [See Doom.] Defn: Decision; judgment; opinion; a court decision. [Obs.] Chaucer. DOMEBOOK Dome"book`, n. Etym: [Dome doom + book.] (O. Eng. Law) Defn: A book said to have been compiled under the direction of King Alfred. It is supposed to have contained the principal maxims of the common law, the penalties for misdemeanors, and the forms of judicial proceedings. Domebook was probably a general name for book of judgments. Burrill. DOMED Domed, a. Defn: Furnished with a dome; shaped like a dome. DOMESDAY Domes"day`, n. Defn: A day of judgment. See Doomsday. [Obs.] Domesday Book, the ancient record of the survey of most of the lands of England, made by order of William the Conqueror, about 1086.
