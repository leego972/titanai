Question: <p>Say we have normal distribution n(x): mean=0 and \int_{-a}^{a} n(x) = P.</p>

<p>What is the easiest way to compute standard deviation of such distribution? May be there are standard libraries for python or C, that are suitable for that task?</p>

Answer: <p><a href="http://www.scipy.org/" rel="nofollow">SciPy</a> has a <a href="http://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/tutorial/#scipy-organization" rel="nofollow">stats</a> sub-package.</p>
