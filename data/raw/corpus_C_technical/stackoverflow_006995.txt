Question: <p>What is the Python equivalent of Perl's <code>chomp</code> function, which removes the last character of a value?</p>

Answer: <p>And I would say the "pythonic" way to get lines without trailing newline characters is splitlines().</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; text = "line 1\nline 2\r\nline 3\nline 4"
&gt;&gt;&gt; text.splitlines()
['line 1', 'line 2', 'line 3', 'line 4']
</code></pre>
