MMP-Responsive 'Smart' Drug Delivery and Tumor Targeting.

Matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs) are major extracellular enzymes involved in 
cancer initiation, progression, and metastasis. MMPs are widely used as cancer 
biomarkers and therapeutic targets. Recently, MMPs have been investigated as 
robust tumor microenvironmental stimuli for 'smart' MMP-responsive drug delivery 
and tumor targeting and have shown great potential in cancer diagnosis and 
therapy. In this article, we review the newly emerging MMP-responsive strategies 
and major MMP-responsive nanomaterials and nanocarriers used for tumor-targeted 
delivery of drugs and imaging agents at the tissue, cellular, and intracellular 
levels. We also discuss the challenges and critical considerations in their 
development and clinical translation.
