As the schooner slipped through the narrow passage, he stood on the low, sandy point, and waved his hand in farewell. * * * * * A week later the little vessel dropped her anchor in the lagoon again, and Rua Manu and his crew came ashore to seek him. They found him lying under the shade of the puka-tree with his revolver in his hand and a bullet-hole in his temple.
