[<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR109">109</xref>]</td></tr></tbody></table></table-wrap></p><p id="Par31"><italic toggle="yes">Yunnan Baiyao</italic> is a traditional Chinese plant sold and used as an alternative hemostatic agent for humans and animals. In 2014, <italic toggle="yes">Yunnan Baiyao</italic> was used to prepare an ointment by mixing it with 45S5 bioactive glass, which showed accelerated recovery of diabetes-impaired skin wounds [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR95">95</xref>]. Moreover, in another study, the surface of the bioactive glass was modified with gallic acid and natural polyphenols extracted from red grape skin and green tea leaves in which a fast in vitro bioactivity has been observed [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR96">96</xref>].</p><p id="Par32">Icariin is a flavonoid compound isolated from a traditional Chinese herb (<italic toggle="yes">Epimedium sagitattum</italic>). It has been used for the treatment of fractures, bone, and joint diseases for hundreds of years. Icariin-loaded-hollow bioactive glass/chitosan therapeutic scaffolds were found to be a promising bone material for enhanced osteogenic differentiation and new bone regeneration [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR97">97</xref>]. Polymeric and glass-modified composite films were successfully loaded with polyphenols extracted from sage (<italic toggle="yes">Salvia officinalis</italic> L). The modified material exhibited cytocompatibility, and significantly increased expression of bone extracellular matrix proteins (osteocalcin and osteopontin). Furthermore, polyphenol-loaded bioactive glass showed anti-biofilm properties against Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacterial strains [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR98">98</xref>].</p><p id="Par33">Modification of bioactive glass with several ayurvedic plants such as Neem, rice hulls, Tulsi, turmeric, and <italic toggle="yes">Lawsonia inermis</italic> has been performed by many studies.
