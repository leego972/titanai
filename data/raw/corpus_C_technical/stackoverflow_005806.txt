Question: <p>How do I force Python's print function to output to the screen?</p>

Answer: <p>Also as suggested in <a href="http://algorithmicallyrandom.blogspot.com/2009/10/python-tips-and-tricks-flushing-stdout.html">this blog</a> one can reopen <code>sys.stdout</code> in unbuffered mode:</p>

<pre><code>sys.stdout = os.fdopen(sys.stdout.fileno(), 'w', 0)
</code></pre>

<p>Each <code>stdout.write</code> and <code>print</code> operation will be automatically flushed afterwards.</p>
