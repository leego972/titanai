Question: <p>What's the difference between the list methods <code>append()</code> and <code>extend()</code>?</p>

Answer: <p>extend(L) extends the list by appending all the items in the given list L.</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; a
[1, 2, 3]
a.extend([4)  #is eqivalent of a[len(a):] = [4]
&gt;&gt;&gt;a
[1, 2, 3, 4]
a =[1,2,3]
&gt;&gt;&gt; a
[1, 2, 3]
&gt;&gt;&gt; a[len(a):] = [4]
&gt;&gt;&gt; a
[1, 2, 3, 4]
</code></pre>
