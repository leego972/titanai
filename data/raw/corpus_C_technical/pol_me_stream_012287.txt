# Younes Ali

Younes Ali Rahmati (; born 3 January 1983) is a former Qatari footballer. He played as a midfielder and was a member of the Qatar national football team.

Ali is nicknamed "Guardiola" because he used to play alongside Pep Guardiola, whom he considered his role model, in Al Ahli.
