The linear mixed model yielded no significant difference in the mean rate of clearance between patients treated with DL-PDT (63.5%, 95% CI 52.6%/74.5%) and those treated with cryosurgery (52.4%, 95% CI 41.3%/63.5%), <italic toggle="yes">p</italic> = 0.154 (<xref rid="t0003" ref-type="table"><bold>Table III</bold></xref>). The mean percentage of clearance differed among the Olsen grades (Olsen grade I: mean 74.7% (95% CI 65.9%/83.5%), Olsen grade II: mean 64.0% (95% CI 55.5%/72.5%), Olsen grade III: mean 35.2% (95% CI 16.9%/53.5%), <italic toggle="yes">p</italic> = 0.001). The mean percentage of clearance was significantly lower in Olsen grade III AKs than in Olsen grade I AKs (<italic toggle="yes">p</italic> &lt; 0.001) and Olsen grade II AKs (<italic toggle="yes">p</italic> = 0.005). The interaction between Olsen grade and treatment was significant, <italic toggle="yes">p</italic> = 0.045 (<xref rid="t0003" ref-type="table">Table III</xref>). Within Olsen grade I (<italic toggle="yes">p</italic> = 0.533) and II (<italic toggle="yes">p</italic> = 0.594), the treatment groups did not significantly differ in the mean percentage of clearance of AKs (<xref rid="t0003" ref-type="table">Table III</xref>). However, Olsen grade III AKs responded significantly better to DL-PDT than to cryosurgery (<italic toggle="yes">p</italic> = 0.021).</p><table-wrap position="float" id="t0003"
