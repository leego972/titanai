# PMC: low level laser therapy healing review — Overview

id="Sec18"><title>Study design and setting</title><p id="Par27">All trials were randomized where five trials were single-blinded [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR35">35</xref>–<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR37">37</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR39">39</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR41">41</xref>], three trials were double-blinded [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR38">38</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR40">40</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR44">44</xref>] and six trials not reporting the blinding of participants and evaluator [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR12">12</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR14">14</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR33">33</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR34">34</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR42">42</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR43">43</xref>]. Based on the study setting, four studies were conducted in Iran [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR37">37</xref>–<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR40">40</xref>], and the others were each from Brazil [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR34">34</xref>], India [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR36">36</xref>], Sweden [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR41">41</xref>], Bulgaria [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR33">33</xref>], Turkey [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR35">35</xref>], Malaysia [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR42">42</xref>], Egypt [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR43">43</xref>], Italy [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR44">44</xref>], China [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR14">14</xref>], and Iraq [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR12">12</xref>]. Summary for study population and study design presented is presented in Table <xref rid="Tab1" ref-type="table">1</xref>. <table-wrap id="Tab1" position="float" orientation="portrait"><label>Table 1</label><caption><p>Characteristics of population and study design</p></caption><graphic xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink" position="anchor" id="MO2" orientation="portrait" xlink:href="13643_2024_2595_Tab1_HTML.jpg"/><table-wrap-foot><p><italic toggle="yes">N/A</italic> not applicable</p></table-wrap-foot></table-wrap></p></sec><sec id="Sec19"><title>Intervention and comparators</title><p id="Par28">Four studies reported CO2 laser [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR36">36</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR37">37</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR39">39</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR40">40</xref>], while the other four used diode laser [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR12">12</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR14">14</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR43">43</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR44">44</xref>] as an intervention. Two studies reported the use of InGaAIP [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR34">34</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR38">38</xref>], two studies used Er,Cy: YSGG [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR35">35</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR42">42</xref>], one study used GaAIAs [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR41">41</xref>], and one study used Nd: YAG [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR33">33</xref>]. Eight studies were placebo-controlled (sham treatment) [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR12">12</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR35">35</xref>–<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR41">41</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR43">43</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR44">44</xref>], four were in comparison with topical medication (three with glucocorticoid and one with wound healing promoting agent) [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR14">14</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR33">33</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR34">34</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR42">42</xref>], and two studies were in comparison with both topical medication and placebo (one with glucocorticoid and sham treatment [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR37">37</xref>] while the other one with antimicrobial and motivation only [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR12">12</xref>]).
