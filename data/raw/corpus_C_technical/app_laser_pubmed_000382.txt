A Deep Learning Method for Automatic Identification of Drusen and Macular Hole 
from Optical Coherence Tomography.

Pace T(1), Degan N(1), Giglio Md R(2), Tognetto Md D(2), Accardo A(1).

Author information:
(1)Department of Engineering and Architecture, University of Trieste, Trieste, 
Italy.
(2)Eye Clinic, University of Trieste, Trieste, Italy.

Deep Learning methods have become dominant in various fields of medical imaging, 
including ophthalmology. In this preliminary study, we investigated a method 
based on Convolutional Neural Network for the identification of drusen and 
macular hole from Optical Coherence Tomography scans with the aim to assist 
ophthalmologists in diagnosing and assessing retinal diseases.
