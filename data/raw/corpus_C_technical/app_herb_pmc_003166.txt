Furthermore, the use of the plants in broad geographic regions adds value to the therapeutic potential of the medicinal plants in Yalo Woreda [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR12">12</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR17">17</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR23">23</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR34">34</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR43">43</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR69">69</xref>]. However, 29 medicinal plants are new reports by Yalo Woreda informants compared to other studies conducted in Afar region, which are used as a treatment for different health conditions in Ethiopia and elsewhere.
