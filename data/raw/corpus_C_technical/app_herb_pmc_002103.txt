Chemical Analysis</title><p>Triplicates analyses (CV ≤ 10%) were performed in all three dried PMG samples, using official AOAC methods for ash, protein, fat, moisture, and carbohydrate (by difference) contents.</p></sec><sec id="sec2dot4-biomolecules-12-01649"><title>2.4. Non-Phenolic Antioxidants</title><p>Carotenoids [α-carotene, all-trans-β-carotene, cryptoxanthin, lutein, and zeaxanthin] were extracted (methanol + tetrahydrofuran) from all dried PMG samples and quantified (μg. 100 g<sup>−1</sup>) by HPLC-DAD (450 nm) as recently described [<xref rid="B12-biomolecules-12-01649" ref-type="bibr">12</xref>]; ascorbic acid (mg/100 g DM) was extracted (HPO3: glacial acetic acid: H<sub>2</sub>O) and quantified (μg. 100 g<sup>−1</sup>) by normal phase-HPLC as recommended by Doner and Hicks [<xref rid="B13-biomolecules-12-01649" ref-type="bibr">13</xref>]. Tocols [Tocopherols (T) and tocotrienols (T3), α, β, γ, δ] were stepwise-extracted (hexane–metanol) and quantified (μg. 100 g<sup>−1</sup>) by normal-phase HPLC as recently described [<xref rid="B14-biomolecules-12-01649" ref-type="bibr">14</xref>]. Pure standards were used to build calibration curves, and inter-run variation was ≤15%.</p></sec><sec id="sec2dot5-biomolecules-12-01649"><title>2.5. Phenolic Compounds (PC)</title><p>PC were extracted from 1 g of sample with either H2O–methanol (20:80 <italic toggle="yes">v</italic>/<italic toggle="yes">v</italic>; 20 mL; total phenols, total flavonoids) or acidified H2O–methanol [20:80 <italic toggle="yes">v/v</italic> with 2% <italic toggle="yes">v/v</italic> formic acid; 20 mL; monomeric anthocyanins (ANTO)], centrifuged (3000 rpm, 15 min, 2–5 °C), filtered (0.22–0.45 µM) and quantified [Total phenols (675 nm), mg of gallic acid equivalents (GAE). 100 g<sup>−1</sup> dwb; total flavonoids (510); mg of quercetin equivalents (QAE). 100 g<sup>−1</sup> dwb; ANTO (520, 700 nm); mg of Cyanindin-3-O-glucoside (Cy3GE). 100 g<sup>−1</sup> dwb] in a FLUOstar Omega multifunctional microplate reader (BMG LABTECH Inc., Durham, NC, USA), according to standard colorimetric methods [<xref rid="B15-biomolecules-12-01649" ref-type="bibr">15</xref>,<xref rid="B16-biomolecules-12-01649" ref-type="bibr">16</xref>,<xref rid="B17-biomolecules-12-01649" ref-type="bibr">17</xref>].</p></sec><sec id="sec2dot6-biomolecules-12-01649"><title>2.6. UPLC-DAD-MS<sup>2</sup></title><p>Samples (1 g) were extracted [10 mL, H<sub>2</sub>O (1.8)/formic acid (0.2)/methanol (8.0)], sonicated (30 min), centrifuged (10,000 rpm, 4 °C, 25 min) twice under dark conditions, and combined supernatants (<italic toggle="yes">n</italic> = 2) were filtered (0.22–0.45 µm). Individual PCs were identified by HUPLC-DAD-MS<sup>2</sup> using a Vion IMS QT of the mass spectrometer (HUPLC Acquity Class I, 2.1 × 100 mm, 1.7 µm BEH C<sub>18</sub> column; Waters Corp., Milford, MA, USA) according to Amaya-Cruz et al. [<xref rid="B18-biomolecules-12-01649" ref-type="bibr">18</xref>]. Running conditions were as follows: column temperature (35 °C), mobile phase = solvent A (H<sub>2</sub>O (99.9)-formic acid (0.1) and solvent B (acetonitrile), flow rate (400 μL. min<sup>−1</sup>), gradient = 5% B (2 min), stepwise increment ran up to 95% B (2–22 min) and then was held for 3 min, acquisition [positive (ANTO) and negative (all other PC) mode; of 50–1800 Da at 260, 280, 320, 360, and 520 nm], capillary energy (3.5 kV), low collision energy (5 eV), and high energy ramp (15 to 45 eV), mass calibration standard (leucine-enkephalin; infusion of 10 µL.min<sup>−1</sup>, 556.677 <italic toggle="yes">m</italic>/<italic toggle="yes">z</italic>), source temperature (120 °C), gas (Ar) flow (800 L.h<sup>−1</sup>). Tentative chemical identity was assigned by comparing their retention times (<italic toggle="yes">rt</italic>) with those reported in the literature, and by the mass fragmentation pattern ([M]<italic toggle="yes"><sub>m</sub></italic><sub>/<italic toggle="yes">z</italic></sub>, fragments), using the UNIFI scientific information system and the Progenesis QI software (Waters Corps., Milford, MA, USA). Results were expressed as a relative abundance (%) to the total chromatogram peak area per PMG sample.</p></sec><sec id="sec2dot7-biomolecules-12-01649"><title>2.7.
