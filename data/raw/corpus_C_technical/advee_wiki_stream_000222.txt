# Kamil Oziemczuk

Kamil Oziemczuk (born 29 March 1988 in Lublin) is a Polish professional footballer who plays as a striker for Tur Milejów.

Career

Club
Oziemczuk began his career at Górnik Łęczna before moving to AJ Auxerre in June 2006. In September 2008 he joined Motor Lublin.

International
He also appeared at the 2006 UEFA European U-19 Championship.
