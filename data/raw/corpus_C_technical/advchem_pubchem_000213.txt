Description:
Mevalonic acid is a racemate composed of equimolar amounts of (R)- and (S)-mevalonic acid. It has a role as a mouse metabolite and a human urinary metabolite. It contains a (R)-mevalonic acid and a (S)-mevalonic acid. It is a conjugate acid of a mevalonate.
