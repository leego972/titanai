<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract in patients suffering from illnesses, such as asthenia syndrome, neuroses, vascular dystonia, hypotension, and schizophrenia (remissions of asthenic type). The advised dosage of the extract was 5–25 drops (in a quarter of a glass of water) three times daily and should be taken 15–30 minutes before meals. Although, the duration of the therapy was individually determined and varied from 10 days to 4 months [<xref rid="B173-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">173</xref>,<xref rid="B174-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">174</xref>].</p><p>The symptomatic characteristics of asthenic syndrome is general weakness, reduced working capacity, low memory, irritability, headaches, insomnia, and poor appetite. These symptoms are frequently observed after intensive work, which requires high levels of mental exertion. Indeed, the <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract was reported to be a valuable medicine in essentially healthy people placed under heavy physical or mental workloads [<xref rid="B175-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">175</xref>,<xref rid="B176-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">176</xref>]. Furthermore, to take advantage of the prophylactic measures of <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract it has been advised that doses should be taken in the morning, or in the morning and the afternoon, for at least a few days prior to a period of intensive mental work for a during 2–3 weeks. In cases of hard mental work, it has been recommended that duration of <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> therapy last for the duration of the period. It has been reported that therapy with <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract prevented exhaustion and fatigue during work requiring long intensive mental activity [<xref rid="B175-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">175</xref>,<xref rid="B176-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">176</xref>].</p><p>The positive therapeutic effect of <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract was also observed in an open label, uncontrolled study in 128 patients with pronounced states of fatigue of different genesis [<xref rid="B175-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">175</xref>]. For example, 128 patients between 17 and 55 years of age (male/female: 75/53) were treated with an <italic toggle="yes">R.</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract and as a result the clinical symptoms of fatigue were considerably improved or disappeared completely. Their diagnosis of fatigue was confirmed by psychological examinations and increased mental work capacity. Indeed, there are also therapeutic effects of <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract in exhaustion due to psychogenic and somatogenic origin (82%) or in patients convalescing from somatic and infectious diseases (80%) [<xref rid="B175-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">175</xref>]. For example, patients suffering from post-influenza fatigue showed improved mental and physical working capacity already on the third day after intake of <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract. Thus, these patients improved in focusing their attention, and headaches vanished. Furthermore, <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract reduced and eliminated general weakness and fatigue in cases of traumatic cerebrastenia injury and also significantly facilitated the normalisation of the autonomic functions in this group of patients [<xref rid="B175-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">175</xref>].</p><p>That <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">rosea</italic> extract has a pronounced therapeutic effect on neuroses (exhaustings depression) was reported in an open label study on 65 poorly characterized mixed patient population [<xref rid="B177-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">177</xref>] suffering from insomnia, high irritability and various somatic disorders.
