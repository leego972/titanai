</p></sec><sec id="sec6"><title>Future clinical applications</title><p>Considering the potential benefits of OCT, it can be predicted that its use in the medical field will increase further in the coming years. The combination of OCT with other techniques like scanning laser ophthalmoscopy (SLO) could prove to be useful for eye imaging when compared with the former approach alone. <sup><xref rid="ref1" ref-type="bibr">1</xref></sup> The use of OCT angiography (OCTA) technique to observe human vasculature could increase in the future
<sup><xref rid="ref80" ref-type="bibr">80</xref></sup> as unlike SLO-based angiography methods; OCTA can provide depth information and isolation of vascular retinal
<sup><xref rid="ref81" ref-type="bibr">81</xref></sup> and various choroidal layers. <sup><xref rid="ref82" ref-type="bibr">82</xref></sup> It will be interesting to discover the role of artificial intelligence (AI) while using OCT to diagnose skin diseases, particularly basal cell carcinoma. <sup><xref rid="ref33" ref-type="bibr">33</xref></sup> Deep learning algorithms have been applied previously to the OCT technique,
<sup><xref rid="ref83" ref-type="bibr">83</xref></sup> but AI's role in improving OCT has not yet gained clinical maturity. It would be exciting to see the part of AI-related to OCT in the future, as it can decrease the cost and time required to train an operator. <sup><xref rid="ref33" ref-type="bibr">33</xref></sup>
</p></sec><sec sec-type="conclusions" id="sec7"><title>Conclusions</title><p>OCT is a non-invasive technique that has several clinical applications and distinct advantages. It is an explorative diagnostic method that provides clinically pertinent data, is fast, and thus it is appropriate for research and clinical practice. OCT is still an emerging technology, and considering its potential benefits, its use in medical and dental clinics should be encouraged.</p></sec><sec sec-type="data-availability" id="sec8"><title>Data availability</title><p>No data is associated with this article.</p></sec></body><back><ref-list><title>References</title><ref id="ref1"><label>1</label><mixed-citation publication-type="journal">
