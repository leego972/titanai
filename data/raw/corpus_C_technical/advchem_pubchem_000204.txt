Description:
Aspartic acid is an alpha-amino acid that consists of succinic acid bearing a single alpha-amino substituent. It has a role as a fundamental metabolite. It is a polar amino acid, an alpha-amino acid and a C4-dicarboxylic acid. It contains a carboxymethyl group. It is a conjugate acid of an aspartate and an aspartate(1-).
