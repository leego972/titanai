Title: Enhancing regional convergence in the European Union

Abstract:
Progress in regional convergence in the EU has been uneven over the last two decades. While Central and Eastern Europe has been catching up, Southern Europe has often lost ground, especially after the global financial crisis. Furthermore, within most countries, gaps between large cities and rural areas have widened. Some challenges to convergence have stemmed from worldwide factorssuch as globalisation, digitalisation, global warming, and, more recently, COVID19but others are European-specific, like incomplete financial integration, less effective fiscal governance and subpar innovation performance.
