# Junko

is a feminine Japanese given name.

Possible writings
Junko can be written using different kanji characters and can mean any of the following:
純子, "pure, child"
順子, "order, child"
淳子, "pure, child"
潤子, "rich/favor/wet, child"
準子, "conform, child"
洵子, "truth, child"
The name can also be written in hiragana or katakana.
