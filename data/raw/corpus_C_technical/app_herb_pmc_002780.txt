Use of plant specimens as a focal point for structured interviews has been referred to as the ‘plant interview’ method of ethnobotanical research [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR56">56</xref>].</p><p id="Par50">Data from the interviews were correlated in the aggregate to determine the relative importance of each taxon using the Relative Importance Index (RI), developed for ranking of medicinal plants by their pharmacological properties, and by the body systems affected [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR57">57</xref>].
