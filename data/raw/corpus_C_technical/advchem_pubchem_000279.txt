Description:
Aminoglutethimide is a dicarboximide that is a six-membered cyclic compound having ethyl and 4-aminophenyl substituents at the 3-position. It has a role as an antineoplastic agent, an anticonvulsant, an adrenergic agent and an EC 1.14.14.14 (aromatase) inhibitor. It is a dicarboximide, a member of piperidones and a substituted aniline. It is functionally related to a piperidine-2,6-dione.
