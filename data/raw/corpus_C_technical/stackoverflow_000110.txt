Question: <p>In many places, <code>(1,2,3)</code> and <code>[1,2,3]</code> can be used interchangeably.</p>

<p>When should I use one or the other, and why?</p>

Answer: <p><code>(1,2,3)</code> is a tuple and <code>[1,2,3]</code> is a list. You either of the two represent sequences of numbers but note that tuples are immutable and list are mutable Python objects. </p>
