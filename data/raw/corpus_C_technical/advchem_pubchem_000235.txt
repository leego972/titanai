Description:
5-oxoproline is an oxoproline having the oxo group placed at the 5-position. It is an intermediate metabolite in the glutathione cycle. It has a role as a human metabolite. It is an oxoproline, a pyrrolidinemonocarboxylic acid and a member of pyrrolidin-2-ones. It is a conjugate acid of a 5-oxoprolinate.
