Kessel et al. concluded no significant difference comparing PDL-PDT with cPDT in a split-face manner on 57 patients (<xref rid="cit0009" ref-type="bibr">9</xref>). At 6 months, 62% of PDL-PDT-treated and 64% of cPDT-treated AKs had healed, and at 12 months, the corresponding values were 48% and 56%.
