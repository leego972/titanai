Question: <p>Does anyone know of a really simple way of capitalizing just the first letter of a string, regardless of the capitalization of the rest of the string?</p>

<p>For example:</p>

<pre><code>asimpletest -&gt; Asimpletest
aSimpleTest -&gt; ASimpleTest
</code></pre>

<p>I would like to be able to do all string lengths as well.</p>

Answer: <pre><code>str = str[0].upper() + str[1:]
</code></pre>

<p>This should work with every string, except for the empty string (<code>""</code>).</p>
