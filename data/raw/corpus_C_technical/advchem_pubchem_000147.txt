Description:
Carnitine is an amino-acid betaine that is butanoate substituted with a hydroxy group at position C-3 and a trimethylammonium group at C-4. It has a role as a mouse metabolite and a human metabolite. It is functionally related to a butyrate. It is a conjugate base of a carnitinium.
