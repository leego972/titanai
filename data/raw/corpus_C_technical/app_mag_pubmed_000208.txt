Transcranial magnetic stimulation.

Reid V(1).

Author information:
(1)Department of Neurology, Beaumont Hospital, Dublin 9, Ireland. 
vreid@partners.org

Transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) is a noninvasive and painless method of 
stimulating the cerebral cortex that allows the study of cortical excitability 
and inhibition. This article describes the technique, the parameters most 
commonly studied, the clinical applications of TMS, and potential research 
applications.
