The primary indicators include the Plaque Index (PI) and Bleeding on Probing (BOP), which provide insights into plaque accumulation and gingival inflammation. Additionally, reductions in periodontal pocket depth (PPD) and gingival recession (GR) are used to measure improvements in periodontal structure. At a molecular level, changes in biomarkers such as matrix metalloproteinases (MMP-8 and MMP-9), prostaglandin E2 (PGE2), and interleukin-1β (IL-1β) in gingival crevicular fluid (GCF) serve as objective indicators of inflammation modulation. In several clinical studies, the efficacy of plant-derived mouthwashes and gels has been confirmed through these parameters, showing significant reductions in inflammatory markers and improvements in periodontal health compared to conventional treatments. PD management typically involves specialist visits, home hygiene, CHX mouthrinses, and antibiotics like metronidazole. However, prolonged CHX use may cause mucosal and dental staining, taste loss, irritation, and antibiotic resistance [<xref rid="B27-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">27</xref>,<xref rid="B28-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">28</xref>,<xref rid="B29-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">29</xref>,<xref rid="B30-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">30</xref>].</p><p>In recent years, the scientific community has developed an interest in the use of natural products, rich in bioactive substances with antimicrobial, bacteriostatic, immunomodulatory, and restorative properties, known since ancient times, capable of slowing down, controlling, or preventing PD [<xref rid="B31-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">31</xref>,<xref rid="B32-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">32</xref>,<xref rid="B33-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">33</xref>]. About 35,000 medicinal plants are known, but only 20% are analyzed phytochemically, and of these, only 10% are biologically screened to ensure quality and non-toxicity [<xref rid="B34-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">34</xref>,<xref rid="B35-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">35</xref>,<xref rid="B36-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">36</xref>,<xref rid="B37-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">37</xref>,<xref rid="B38-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">38</xref>,<xref rid="B39-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">39</xref>]. Extracts, concentrated forms of phytochemicals known as nutraceuticals, have received particular attention [<xref rid="B40-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">40</xref>,<xref rid="B41-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">41</xref>].</p><p>There are many ways to extract substances from plants, from long boiling to cold infusion, or by using alcohol, vinegar, hot water, or other solvents such as acetone and chloroform [<xref rid="B42-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">42</xref>,<xref rid="B43-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">43</xref>,<xref rid="B44-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">44</xref>].</p><p>Nutraceuticals are bioactive substances that can be used as main therapeutic agents or as a basis for the synthesis of other drugs (e.g., morphine from opium, extracted from <italic toggle="yes">Papaver somniferum</italic>) [<xref rid="B45-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">45</xref>,<xref rid="B46-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">46</xref>,<xref rid="B47-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">47</xref>].</p><p>Many natural substances have been the subject of research for the treatment of oral cavity inflammation. The use of herbal extracts alone or in combination with other natural herbal extracts (triphala, sage, green tea, tulsi patra, jyestiamadh, neem, clove oil, pudina, ajwain, ginger, calendula, aloe vera, propolis, mangosteen extract complex, elderberry) in the form of mouthwashes, gels, and toothpastes (<xref rid="nutrients-17-00762-f001" ref-type="fig">Figure 1</xref>) have been scientifically tested for various oral health problems: gingivitis, bleeding, bad breath, soft-tissue ulcers, infections, and prevention of the onset of DC [<xref rid="B8-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>,<xref rid="B48-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">48</xref>,<xref rid="B49-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">49</xref>,<xref rid="B50-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">50</xref>,<xref rid="B51-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">51</xref>,<xref rid="B52-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">52</xref>,<xref rid="B53-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">53</xref>].
