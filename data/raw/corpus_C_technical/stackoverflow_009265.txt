Question: <p>Does anyone know how to do this? I need to add a header of the form:</p>

<p>
  
    value1
    value2
  
</p>

Answer: <p>As the question is phrased, it's hard to guess what the intention (or even the intended semantics) is. For setting headers, try the following:</p>

<pre><code>import SOAPpy
headers = SOAPpy.Types.headerType()
headers.value1 = value2
</code></pre>

<p>or</p>

<pre><code>[...]
headers.foo = value1
headers.bar = value2
</code></pre>
