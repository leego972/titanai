Question: <p>If I have two dates (ex. <code>'8/18/2008'</code> and <code>'9/26/2008'</code>) what is the best way to get the difference measured in days?</p>

Answer: <p>If you have two date objects, you can just subtract them.</p>

<pre><code>from datetime import date

d0 = date(2008, 8, 18)
d1 = date(2008, 9, 26)
delta = d0 - d1
print delta.days
</code></pre>
