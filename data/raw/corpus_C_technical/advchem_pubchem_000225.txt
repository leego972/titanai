Source: ChEBI

Description:
4-(2-aminophenyl)-2,4-dioxobutanoic acid is a 2-oxo monocarboxylic acid, a 4-oxo monocarboxylic acid, a dioxo monocarboxylic acid, a substituted aniline, a beta-diketone and an aromatic ketone. It has a role as a mouse metabolite. It is functionally related to a butyric acid. It is a conjugate acid of a 4-(2-aminophenyl)-2,4-dioxobutanoate.
