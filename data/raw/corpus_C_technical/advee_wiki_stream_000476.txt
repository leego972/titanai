# Gummo (soundtrack)

Gummo is the soundtrack album to the film of the same name and contains songs that were recorded specifically for the film or were featured in the film.

The album is mostly made up of various forms of metal, ranging from black metal to powerviolence to grindcore and sludge metal. The soundtrack also includes a few eccentric selections of music which include industrial metal, power electronics, drum and bass, bluegrass, classical and folk selections.
