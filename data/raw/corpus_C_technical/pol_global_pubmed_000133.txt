Strengthening Global Health Security Under the Biden-Harris Administration.

Pace L(1), Kim SC(1).

Author information:
(1)Office of Global Affairs, U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.

Global health security in the Biden-Harris Administration has been a dynamic 
area of engagement, starting with the COVID-19 response, to strengthening and 
reforming the World Health Organization, to bolstering regional partnerships, 
and securing financing for pandemic preparedness. Sustained commitment to 
bilateral, regional, and multilateral cooperation will ensure that the United 
States stands ready to address any future health challenges.
