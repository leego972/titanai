    def kill(self):
    	del self

&lt;__main__.A instance at 0x01F23170&gt;

Answer: <p>what you could do is take the name with you in the class and make a dictionairy:</p>

<pre><code>class A:
  def __init__(self, name):
    self.name=name
  def kill(self)
    del dict[self.name]

dict={}
dict["a"]=A("a")
dict["a"].kill()
