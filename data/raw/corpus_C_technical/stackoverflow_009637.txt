Question: <p>I am using Django and the Google Web Toolkit (GWT) for my current project. I would like to pass a ModelForm instance to GWT via an Http response so that I can "chop" it up and render it as I please. My goal is to keep the form in sync with changes to my models.py file, yet increase control I have over the look of the form. However, the django classes for serialization, serializers and simplejson, cannot serialize a ModelForm. Neither can cPickle. What are my alternatives? </p>

Answer: <p>If your problem is just to serialze a ModelForm to json, just write your own simplejson serializer subclass.</p>
