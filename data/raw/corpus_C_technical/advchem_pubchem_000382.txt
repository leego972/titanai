Description:
SB-202190 is a member of the class of imidazoles that is 1H-imidazole in which the hydrogens at positions 2, 4, and 5 are replaced by 4-hydroxyphenyl, pyridin-4-yl, and 4-fluorophenyl groups, respectively. It is a widely used inhibitor of mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) alpha and beta. It has a role as an apoptosis inducer and an EC 2.7.11.24 (mitogen-activated protein kinase) inhibitor. It is a member of imidazoles, a member of pyridines, a member of phenols and an organofluorine compound.
