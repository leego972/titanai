Question: <p>If I have two dates (ex. <code>'8/18/2008'</code> and <code>'9/26/2008'</code>) what is the best way to get the difference measured in days?</p>

Answer: <p></p>

<pre><code>from datetime import date
def d(s):
  [month, day, year] = map(int, s.split('/'))
  return date(year, month, day)
def days(start, end):
  return (d(end) - d(start)).days
print days('8/18/2008', '9/26/2008')
</code></pre>

<p>This assumes, of course, that you've already verified that your dates are in the format <code>r'\d+/\d+/\d+'</code>.</p>
