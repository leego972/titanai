Antimicrobial Effects</title><p>Farouk <italic toggle="yes">et al.</italic>, showed that the alcoholic and acetone extracts of the leaves and stem were active on both the Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria <italic toggle="yes">Escherichia coli</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">Salmonella typhi</italic>. The root extracts had no antibacterial activity against the Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria tested. Aqueous leaves extract showed antibacterial activity against <italic toggle="yes">Staphylococcus aureus</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">Serratia marscesens</italic> [<xref rid="B162-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">162</xref>].</p><p>Extracts from <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">L. pumila</italic> leaves were evaluated and analyzed for their antibacterial activity against human pathogenic Gram positive (<italic toggle="yes">Staphylococcus aureus</italic>) and Gram negative (<italic toggle="yes">Pseudomonas aeruginosa</italic>) bacteria. The extracts were prepared in different solvents (acetone, methanol, ethanol, and phosphate buffer) and at various concentrations ranging from 5 to 100 mg/mL. Most of the extracts showed relatively high antibacterial activity against the tested bacteria with inhibition zone diameters ranging between 7 and 25 mm. The minimum concentration of <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">L. pumila</italic> extracts which inhibited the growth of <italic toggle="yes">S. aureus</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">P. aeruginosa</italic> was 75 mg/mL in ethanol and 25 mg/mL in a phosphate buffer, respectively [<xref rid="B163-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">163</xref>].</p><p>Kong <italic toggle="yes">et al.</italic> screened natural extracts from six plants, including <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic>, that improved the survival of <italic toggle="yes">S. aureus</italic>-infected worms by at least 2.8-fold, suggesting that these extracts could possibly activate host immunity to eliminate the bacteria or possibly interfere with the factor/s that prevent pathogen accumulation [<xref rid="B164-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">164</xref>].</p></sec><sec id="sec5dot5-molecules-21-00331"><title>5.5. Anti-Inflammatory Effects</title><p>It was demonstrated that the β-carboline alkaloid 7-MCPA (7-methoxy-(9<italic toggle="yes">H</italic>-β-carbolin-1-yl)-(<italic toggle="yes">E</italic>)-1-propenoic acid) isolated from <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> hairy-root cultures activated Nrf2 via a ROS-dependent p38 MAPK pathway and 7-MCPA anti-inflammatory effects was associated with 7-MCPA-induced activation of the Nrf2/HO-1 pathway. This study clarified the molecular mechanisms underlying the anti-inflammatory activities of β-carboline alkaloids of <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic>, which may be useful to prevent or treat inflammatory diseases [<xref rid="B165-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">165</xref>].</p><p>Eurycomalactone, 14,15β-dihydroklaieanone, and 13,21-dehydroeurycomanone were identified as potent NF-κB inhibitors with IC50 values of &lt;1 μM [<xref rid="B45-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">45</xref>]. Varghese <italic toggle="yes">et al</italic>, studied hydroalcoholic extract of <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> Jack for its antioxidant and <italic toggle="yes">in vitro</italic> anti-inflammatory properties. The antioxidant activity (free radical scavenging) was evaluated to determine the total antioxidant capacity of extract <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic>. The DPHH assay showed significant antioxidant activity in all concentrations used (<italic toggle="yes">i.e.,</italic> 10, 25, 50, 100 and 250 µg/mL). The human RBC (HRBC) stabilization method was utilized to evaluate the <italic toggle="yes">in vitro</italic> anti-inflammatory activity of the extract, and it was found that this anti-inflammatory activity increased in a concentration dependent manner [<xref rid="B82-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">82</xref>].</p></sec><sec id="sec5dot6-molecules-21-00331"><title>5.6.
