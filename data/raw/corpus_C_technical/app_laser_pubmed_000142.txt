48. J Dermatolog Treat. 2003;14 Suppl 3:15-22. doi: 10.1080/jdt.14.s3.15.22.

Clinical efficacy of methyl aminolevulinate (Metvix) photodynamic therapy.

Foley P(1).

Author information:
(1)University of Melbourne Department of Medicine (Dermatology) Australia.

Several studies have reported the efficacy of photodynamic therapy (PDT) in the 
treatment of non-melanoma skin cancer (NMSC) and pre-cursor lesions, such as 
actinic keratosis (AK). Recent studies investigating the use of methyl 
aminolevulinate (MAL, Metvix PDT have made comparisons to existing standard 
treatments for basal cell carcinoma (BCC) and AK in terms of efficacy, adverse 
events and cosmetic outcome. This review considers these studies as a body of 
evidence that supports the use of MAL PDT over traditional therapies in these 
conditions. The data thus far show PDT to be superior to cryotherapy, 
5-fluorouracil (5FU) and excisional surgery in terms of cosmetic outcome, and 
equivalent in efficacy to other treatment modalities in managing AK and BCC. The 
studies also show an advantage in the treatment of extensive lesions.
