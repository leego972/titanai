Elucidating biosynthetic pathways of plant specialized metabolites is a tricky 
but essential task for the biotechnological production of plant drugs. In a new 
report, Li et al. used a single-cell multi-omics approach to provide an 
integrative view of the architecture and regulation of anticancer alkaloid 
routes in Madagascar periwinkle.
