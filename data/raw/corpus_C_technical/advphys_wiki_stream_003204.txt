# Ettore Majorana Foundation and Centre for Scientific Culture

Ettore Majorana Foundation and Centre for Scientific Culture (acronym EMFCSC) is a scientific organization based in Erice, Sicily (Italy). The President of EMFCSC is Prof. Antonino Zichichi. It sponsors the International School of Subnuclear Physics whose Director is Prof. Antonino Zichichi.
