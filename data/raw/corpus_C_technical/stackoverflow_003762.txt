Question: <p>I understand that these methods are for pickling/unpickling and have no relation to the reduce built-in function, but what's the difference between the 2 and why do we need both?</p>

Answer: <p><code>__reduce_ex__</code> is what <code>__reduce__</code> should have been but never became.  <code>__reduce_ex__</code> works like <code>__reduce__</code> but the pickle protocol is passed.</p>
