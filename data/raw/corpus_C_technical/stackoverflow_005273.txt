Question: <p>How do I convert a hex string to an int in Python? </p>

<p>I may have it as "<code>0xffff</code>" or just "<code>ffff</code>".</p>

Answer: <p>In Python 2.7, <code>int('deadbeef',10)</code> doesn't seem to work. </p>

<p>The following works for me:</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;a = int('deadbeef',16)
&gt;&gt;float(a)
3735928559.0
</code></pre>
