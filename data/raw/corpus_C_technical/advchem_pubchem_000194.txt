Description:
2-nitropropane is a secondary nitroalkane that is propane in which a hydrogen at position 2 has been replaced by a nitro group. Mainly used as a solvent (b.p. 120C). It has a role as a xenobiotic, a polar aprotic solvent, a carcinogenic agent and a hepatotoxic agent.
