# Toyota UR engine

Vortices Series roots-type supercharger kit for the Tundra and Sequoia which bumps power up to  and  of torque. The supercharger kit could be installed by dealers and was covered under warranty. Later on, Magnuson offered an improved version of the supercharger kit which raised the output to 550hp.

Applications:
 2007–2021 Toyota Tundra
 2007–2022 Toyota Sequoia
 2007–2021 Toyota Land Cruiser 200 Series – US and  Middle East markets only
 2007–2021 Lexus LX 570
