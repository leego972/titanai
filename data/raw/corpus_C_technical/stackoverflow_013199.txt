Question: <p>Given:</p>

<pre><code>a = 1
b = 10
c = 100
</code></pre>

<p>I want to display a leading zero for all numbers with less than 2 digits, i.e.:</p>

<pre><code>01
10
100
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>Use a format string - <a href="http://docs.python.org/lib/typesseq-strings.html" rel="nofollow">http://docs.python.org/lib/typesseq-strings.html</a></p>

<p>For example:</p>

<pre><code>python -c 'print "%(num)02d" % {"num":5}'
</code></pre>
