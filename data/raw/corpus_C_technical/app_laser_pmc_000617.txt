The UV pre-excited PLNPs were intravenously injected for in vivo tumor PersL imaging at 2 h. After the PersL signal of the tumor site is decayed, the red LED was used for <italic toggle="yes">in-situ</italic> re-excitation, which can restore the PersL signal of the tumor site and realize in vivo re-excitation imaging of the tumor (Fig. <xref rid="Fig5" ref-type="fig">5</xref>). Later, Pan et al. intravenously injected c(RGDyK) peptide conjugated LiGa<sub>5</sub>O<sub>8</sub>:Cr<sup>3+</sup> in 4T1 tumor model and used white LED to in situ stimulate tumor accumulated LiGa<sub>5</sub>O<sub>8</sub>:Cr<sup>3+</sup> for PersL imaging up to 24 h [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR45">45</xref>]. Besides, Yan et al. reported FA modified Zn<sub>1.25</sub>Ga<sub>1.5</sub>Ge<sub>0.25</sub>O<sub>4</sub>:Cr<sup>3+</sup>,Yb<sup>3+</sup>,Er<sup>3+</sup> for actively targeting of MCF-7 tumor by oral administration [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR39">39</xref>]. With the excitation of 650 nm LED, the tumor regions achieve long time (160 min) and high sensitive (SNR &gt; 20) PersL imaging. Therefore, rare-earth doped PLNPs have been developed for NIR laser excitation. Zhang et al. reported that the ultrasmall ZnGa<sub>2</sub>O<sub>4</sub>:Cr,Eu and Zn<sub>2</sub>SnO<sub>4</sub>:Cr,Eu can be easily modified with folic acid molecules through a simple condensation reaction [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR36">36</xref>, <xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR84">84</xref>]. Highly sensitive targeted imaging of tumor can be achieved by injecting ultraviolet pre-excited PLNPs into mice. After the signal of the tumor site is decayed, the tumor site was re-excited or re-stimulated by the light source of 808 nm with stronger tissue penetration, which can achieve high sensitive tumor imaging. Hao et al. reported a novel 980 nm laser-activated upconverted PLNPs (Zn<sub>3</sub>Ga<sub>2</sub>GeO<sub>8</sub>:Yb/Er/Cr) for in vivo PersL imaging <bold>(</bold>Fig. <xref rid="Fig6" ref-type="fig">6</xref>). Owing to the efficient energy transfer (Er<sup>3+</sup>-Cr<sup>3+</sup>), the produced NIR PersL remained up to 15 h. Meanwhile, these upconverted PLNPs also can be effectively recharged in vivo under 980 nm laser’s excitation [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR85">85</xref>]. Chang et al. developed (Zn<sub>2</sub>SiO<sub>4</sub>:Mn): Y<sup>3+</sup>, Yb<sup>3+</sup>, Tm<sup>3+</sup> upconverting PLNPs for deep tumor imaging under 980 nm laser [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR86">86</xref>]. Besides, Li et al. constructed hybrid nanoparticles composed of upconversion nanoparticles and PLNPs, which can be excited by a 980 nm laser and showed PersL emission at 700 nm to realize NIR to NIR upconverted PersL imaging [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR87">87</xref>].
