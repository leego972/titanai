Question: <p>What is the best way to get a list of all files in a directory, sorted by date [created | modified], using python, on a windows machine?</p>

Answer: <p>There is an <code>os.path.getmtime</code> function that gives the number of seconds since the epoch
and should be faster than os.stat.</p>

<pre><code>os.chdir(directory)
sorted(filter(os.path.isfile, os.listdir('.')), key=os.path.getmtime)
</code></pre>
