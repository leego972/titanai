Question: <p>In many symbolic math systems, such as Matlab or Mathematica, you can use a variable like <code>Ans</code> or <code>%</code> to retrieve the last computed value. Is there a similar facility in the Python shell?</p>

Answer: <p>Underscore.</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; 5+5
10
&gt;&gt;&gt; _
10
&gt;&gt;&gt; _ + 5
15
&gt;&gt;&gt; _
15
</code></pre>
