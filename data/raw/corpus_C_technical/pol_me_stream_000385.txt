# Goffle Brook

a swimming hole in Midland Park.  North of Canterbury Lane in Wyckoff a dam used to exist which held back Maple Lake, now a wetland in danger of development.  Two more dams create large lakes on the north and south sides Wyckoff Ave, again in Wyckoff.
