# Eduard Sergienko

Eduard Sergienko (; born 18 February 1983) is a Ukrainian-born Kazakhstani former football midfielder. He has been capped for the Kazakhstan national football team 19 times.

Career
In January 2016, Sergienko signed for FC Akzhayik.
