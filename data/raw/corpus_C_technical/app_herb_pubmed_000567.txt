An evidence-based systematic review of acai (Euterpe oleracea) by the Natural 
Standard Research Collaboration.

Ulbricht C(1), Brigham A, Burke D, Costa D, Giese N, Iovin R, Grimes Serrano JM, 
Tanguay-Colucci S, Weissner W, Windsor R.

Author information:
(1)Massachusetts General Hospital, Boston, MA, USA. ulbricht@naturalstandard.com

An evidence-based systematic review of acai (Euterpe oleracea) by the Natural 
Standard Research Collaboration consolidates the safety and efficacy data 
available in the scientific literature using a validated, reproducible grading 
rationale. This article includes written and statistical analysis of clinical 
trials, plus a compilation of expert opinion, folkloric precedent, history, 
pharmacology, kinetics/dynamics, interactions, adverse effects, toxicology, and 
dosing.
