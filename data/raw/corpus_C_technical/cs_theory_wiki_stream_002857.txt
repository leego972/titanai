# Wildcat Den State Park

Wildcat Den State Park is a state park of Iowa, US, in Muscatine County.  The park features  cliffs, rock formations, and several historic structures.  The 1848 Pine Creek Gristmill and Pine Mill Bridge are both on the National Register of Historic Places.  The campground has 20 campsites, but lacks modern restroom facilities. Campsites and two open shelters can be reserved ahead of time through the online reservation system, iowastateparks.reserveamerica.com.  Additional campsites and other recreational facilities can be found  away at the Fairport State Recreation Area, which is directly on the Mississippi River.
