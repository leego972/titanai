# Tomasz Brzyski

Tomasz Brzyski (born 10 January 1982) is a Polish former professional footballer who played as a left back or midfielder.

Career
On 14 January 2013 he terminated his contract with Polonia Warsaw by mutual agreement and on the same day joined Legia Warsaw.

On 29 June 2018, Brzyski signed a contract with III liga club Motor Lublin. His final competitive appearance came on 17 June 2023, in the III liga match between KS Lublinianka and Stal Stalowa Wola. Prior to the start of the match, he received his club's shirt with his name on it, as well as a commemorative plaque. While leaving the pitch in the 67th minute of the match, he was given a farewell in a line made up by players of both teams.
