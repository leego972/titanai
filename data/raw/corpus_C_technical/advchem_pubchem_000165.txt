Description:
4-hydroxymandelic acid is a 2-hydroxy carboxylic acid that is mandelic acid bearing a phenolic hydroxy substituent at position 4. It has a role as a metabolite. It is a member of phenols and a 2-hydroxy carboxylic acid. It is functionally related to a mandelic acid. It is a conjugate acid of a 4-hydroxymandelate.
