Title: Creating Secure Spaces in the Face of Hybrid Warfare. Selected Aspects of Crisis Management

Abstract:
Problems of security need to be examined and solved jointly by many actors who are affected by security-related processes and their dynamics. Security management must take place at different levels. The most important one concerns the individual, where security is associated with their life, health and status, wealth or freedom. The second level involves security specified by the state and its sphere of values, and also material and physical capabilities and the scope of operations of institutions. The information coming from Ukraine confirms that crisis management during the war is a complex and multidimensional process, which includes both the actions of the Ukrainian government and mechanisms addressed to society itself. The war is run through methods of hybrid warfare primarily outside the combat area. As a consequence, responding to threats related to military operations must also take into account the protection of the population, ensuring continuity of state operations and assistance to state structures. Sheltered and conditions for planned evacuation are created along with infrastructure through which food, water and medicines can be distributed. At the local plane, threats caused by hybrid warfare mechanisms are reduced through crisis management processes implemented by actors operating at different levels and this society’s resilience is built up.
