In popular culture
Russians in Hong Kong have also been the subject of works of fiction; The Back Door, an 1897 war novel, imagined a naval invasion of Hong Kong by the Russians and the French. The autobiographical Gweilo by Martin Booth covering his childhood in 1950's Hong Kong reminisces of the Russian bakeries and discusses an elderly White Russian street woman who may have been Russian royalty.
