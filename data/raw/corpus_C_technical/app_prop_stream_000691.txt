Castor 1200 The CASTOR 1200 motor is a proposed booster based on the Space Shuttle Solid Rocket Booster and was intended to be used as the first stage of the OmegA's heavy configuration. The inaugural flight of the new motor was suggested to occur in the 2020s. It has also been proposed to replace the 5 segment RSRMVs on the Block 2 Space Launch System.
