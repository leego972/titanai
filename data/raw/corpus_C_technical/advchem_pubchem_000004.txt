Description:
1-chloro-2,4-dinitrobenzene is a C-nitro compound that is chlorobenzene carrying a nitro substituent at each of the 2- and 4-positions. It has a role as a sensitiser, an allergen and an epitope. It is a C-nitro compound and a member of monochlorobenzenes.
