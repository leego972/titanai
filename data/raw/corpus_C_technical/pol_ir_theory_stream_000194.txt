# Guatemalan Air Force

Guatemala requested credit approval of $166 million to buy six EMB-314, radar and equipment.  On 3 July 2019, Guatemala ordered two IA-63 Pampa jet trainers from Argentina, but Guatemala cancelled the order 9 days after due to "lack of transparency surrounding the deal."
