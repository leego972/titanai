Question: <p>What is the nicest/shortest way to pad a string with zeroes to the left, so the string length has a specific length?</p>

Answer: <p>You could also repeat "0", prepend it to str(n) and get the rightmost width slice. Quick and dirty little expression.</p>

<pre><code>def pad_left(n, width, pad="0"):
    return ((pad * width) + str(n))[-width:]
</code></pre>
