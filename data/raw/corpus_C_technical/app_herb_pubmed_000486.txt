New natural products as new leads for antibacterial drug discovery.

Brown DG(1), Lister T(2), May-Dracka TL(2).

Author information:
(1)AstraZeneca Pharmaceuticals, 35 Gatehouse Dr, Waltham, MA 02451, United 
States. Electronic address: dean.brown@astrazeneca.com.
(2)AstraZeneca Pharmaceuticals, 35 Gatehouse Dr, Waltham, MA 02451, United 
States.

Natural products have been a rich source of antibacterial drugs for many 
decades, but investments in this area have declined over the past two decades. 
The purpose of this review article is to provide a recent survey of new natural 
product classes and the mechanisms by which they work.
