Question: <p>I've got a file whose format I'm altering via a python script.  I have several camel cased strings in this file where I just want to insert a single space before the capital letter - so "WordWordWord" becomes "Word Word Word".</p>

<p>My limited regex experience just stalled out on me - can someone think of a decent regex to do this, or (better yet) is there a more pythonic way to do this that I'm missing?</p>

Answer: <p>Have a look at my answer on <em><a href="http://stackoverflow.com/questions/155303/net-how-can-you-split-a-caps-delimited-string-into-an-array#155487">.NET - How can you split a âcapsâ delimited string into an array?</a></em></p>

<p><strong>Edit:</strong> Maybe better to include it here.</p>

<pre><code>re.sub(r'([a-z](?=[A-Z])|[A-Z](?=[A-Z][a-z]))', r'\1 ', text)
</code></pre>

<p>For example:</p>

<pre><code>"SimpleHTTPServer" =&gt; ["Simple", "HTTP", "Server"]
</code></pre>
