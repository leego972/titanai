Question: <p>What do I need to look at to see if I'm on Windows, Unix, etc?</p>

Answer: <p>Check the available tests with module platform and print the answer out for your system:</p>

<pre><code>import platform

print dir(platform)

for x in dir(platform):
    if x[0].isalnum():
        try:
            result = getattr(platform, x)()
            print "platform."+x+": "+result
        except TypeError:
            continue
</code></pre>
