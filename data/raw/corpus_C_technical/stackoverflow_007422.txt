<pre><code>import smtplib

<pre><code>msg.get_payload()

<p>but if its a multipart message your going to need to do a little more processing, luckly a recursive solution is perfect for this situation</p>

<pre><code>def process_multipart_message(message):
    rtn = ''
    if message.is_multipart():
        for m in message.get_payload():
            rtn += process_multipart_message(m)
    else:
        rtn += message.get_payload()
    return rtn
</code></pre>

<pre><code>msg_contant = process_multipart_message(msg)
</code></pre>
