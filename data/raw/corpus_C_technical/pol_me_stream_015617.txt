# Aren Maeir

pedition to Gath, excavating the ancient site of Tell es-Safi, which is identified as Canaanite and Philistine Gath (one of the five cities of the Philistines mentioned in the Bible, the home of Goliath).
Over the years, he has written and edited, close to 20 volumes and published around 300 papers.
