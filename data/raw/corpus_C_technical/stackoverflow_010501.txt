Question: <p>In the transition to newforms admin I'm having difficulty figuring out how specify core=False for ImageFields.</p>

<p>I get the following error:</p>

<pre><code>TypeError: __init__() got an unexpected keyword argument 'core'
</code></pre>

<p>[Edit] However, by just removing the core argument I get a "This field is required." error in the admin interface on attempted submission. How does one accomplish what core=False is meant to do using newforms admin?</p>

Answer: <p>This is simple. I started getting this problems a few revisions ago. Basically, just remove the "core=True" parameter in the ImageField in the models, and then follow the instructions <a href="http://docs.djangoproject.com/en/dev/ref/contrib/admin/#inlinemodeladmin-objects" rel="nofollow">here</a> to convert to what the newforms admin uses.</p>
