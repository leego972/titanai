Question: <p>What's the difference between the list methods <code>append()</code> and <code>extend()</code>?</p>

Answer: <p>Append add the entire data at once. The whole data will be added to the newly created index. On the other hand Extend as it name suggests extends the current array. 
for example</p>

<pre><code>list1 = [123, 456, 678]
list2 = [111,222]
</code></pre>

<p>when append we get:</p>

<pre><code>result = [123,456,678,[111,222]]
</code></pre>

<p>while on extend we get:</p>

<pre><code>result = [123,456,678,111,222]
</code></pre>
