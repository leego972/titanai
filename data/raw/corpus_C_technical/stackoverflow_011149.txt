Question: <p>I am trying to implement AJAX in my Google App Engine application, and so I am looking for a good AJAX framework that will help me. Anyone has any idea?</p>

<p>I am thinking about Google Web Toolkit, how good it is in terms of creating AJAX for Google App Engine?</p>

Answer: <p>If you want to be able to invoke method calls from JavaScript to Python, <a href="http://json-rpc.org/wiki/python-json-rpc" rel="nofollow">JSON-RPC</a> works well with Google App Engine.  See Google's article, "<a href="http://code.google.com/appengine/articles/rpc.html" rel="nofollow">Using AJAX to Enable Client RPC Requests</a>", for details.</p>
