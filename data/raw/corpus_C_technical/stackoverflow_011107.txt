Question: <p>I have a long line of code that I want to break up among multiple lines. What do I use and what is the syntax?</p>

<p>For example, adding a bunch of strings,</p>

<pre><code>e = 'a' + 'b' + 'c' + 'd'
</code></pre>

<p>and have it like this:</p>

<pre><code>e = 'a' + 'b' +
    'c' + 'd'
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>You can break lines in between parenthesises and braces. Additionally, you can append the backslash character <code>\</code> to a line to explicitly break it:</p>

<pre><code>x = (tuples_first_value,
     second_value)
y = 1 + \
    2
</code></pre>
