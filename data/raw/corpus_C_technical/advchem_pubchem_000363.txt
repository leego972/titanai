Description:
Ronidazole is a carbamate ester that is 5-nitroimidazole in which the hydrogens at positions 1 and 2 are replaced by methyl and (carbamoyloxy)methyl groups, respectively. An antiprotozoal agent, it is used in veterinary medicine for the treatment of histomoniasis and swine dysentery. It has a role as an antiparasitic agent and an antiprotozoal drug. It is a carbamate ester, a member of imidazoles and a C-nitro compound.
