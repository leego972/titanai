Question: <p>I wonder what is the time complexity of pop method of list objects in Python (in CPython particulary). Also does the value of N for list.pop(N) affects the complexity?  </p>

Answer: <p>Yes, it is O(1) to pop the last element of a Python list, and O(N) to pop an arbitrary element (since the whole rest of the list has to be shifted).</p>
