Pharmacokinetics</title><p>A HPLC-DAD method was established and applied successfully to the pharmacokinetic study of quercitrin and isoquercitrin in rat plasma after oral administration of 80% ethanol extract of <italic toggle="yes">H. japonicum</italic> at a dose of 23.0 g/kg, equivalent to 48.3 and 62.1 mg/kg of quercitrin and isoquercitrin, respectively. The pharmacokinetic results suggested that the maximum concentrations of quercitrin and isoquercitrin occured after approximately 1.30 and 1.17 h, respectively. Quercitrin and isoquercitrin were still detectable in rat plasma for at least 24 h after oral administration of the extract. These findings provided useful evidence for evaluating the clinical efficacy of <italic toggle="yes">H. japonicum</italic> [<xref rid="B68-molecules-19-10733" ref-type="bibr">68</xref>].</p></sec><sec><title>6. Conclusions</title><p>Pharmacological studies on flavonoids have been performed <italic toggle="yes">in vitro</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">in vivo</italic> in animals, while the pharmacological studies on other main bioactive components such as phloroglucinols and xanthones are rare. Though several pharmacological mechanisms related to biological activity have already been explained, the comprehensive pharmacological mechanisms of <italic toggle="yes">H. japonicum</italic> need to be elucidated. Based on phytochemical and pharmacological research, the flavonoids responsible for the good hepatoprotective, anti-tumor and antibacterial activities were selected as chemical markers to evaluate the quality of <italic toggle="yes">H. japonicum</italic> and its products. Meanwhile, various methods have been successfully applied to the simultaneous analysis of the bioactive compounds in <italic toggle="yes">H. japonicum</italic>. However, pharmacokinetics studies on the main components, especially the bioactive components are still largely lacking, therefore firm evidence for further clinical application is necessary in order to assess the therapeutic potential of <italic toggle="yes">H. japonicum</italic> and its pharmaceutical commodities.</p></sec></body><back><ack><title>Acknowledgments</title><p>We thank Miss Qian-Yi
