# Beira–Lobito Highway

through DR Congo and Angola to Lobito as a trade corridor (the Lobito Corridor) in order to link the landlocked Copperbelt Region with its closest ocean (the Atlantic Ocean) for trade, as Zambia and DR Congo are two large producers of both copper and cobalt.
