# Yang Yin

Yang Yin (楊愔) (511–560), courtesy name Zhunyan (遵彦), nickname Qinwang (秦王), was a high-level official of the Chinese Northern Qi dynasty.

Background

Service under Gao Huan and Gao Cheng
