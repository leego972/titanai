Description:
Antazoline is a member of the class of imidazolines that is 2-aminomethyl-2-imidazoline in which the exocyclic amino hydrogens are replaced by benzyl and phenyl groups. Antazoline is only found in individuals that have taken the drug. It has a role as a xenobiotic, a H1-receptor antagonist and a cholinergic antagonist. It is an aromatic amine, a tertiary amino compound and a member of imidazolines.
