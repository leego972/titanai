Question: <p>How do you change the size of figure drawn with matplotlib?</p>

Answer: <p><a href="http://matplotlib.sourceforge.net/api/figure_api.html#matplotlib.figure.Figure">figure</a> tells you the call signature:</p>

<pre><code>figure(num=None, figsize=(8, 6), dpi=80, facecolor='w', edgecolor='k')
</code></pre>

<p>So <code>figure(figsize=(1,1))</code> creates an inch-by-inch image, which will be 80-by-80 pixels unless you also give a different dpi argument.</p>
