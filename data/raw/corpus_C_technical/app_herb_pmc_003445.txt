Ten key informants were selected to compare and rank these plants according to their efficiency and healing potential. The informants assigned higher ranks to plants with greater healing abilities and lower ranks to those with less effectiveness. The scores given by the informants were summed and organized in order of effectiveness. <italic toggle="yes">C. macrostachyus</italic> received the highest score, earning the top rank, followed by <italic toggle="yes">V. myriantha</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">C. arabica</italic>, indicating that these species are regarded as the most effective and preferred for treating wound infections.
