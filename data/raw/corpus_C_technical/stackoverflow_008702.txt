<p>In some places I see people that want to split the <strong>text</strong> doing:</p>

<pre><code>string.split(text)
</code></pre>

<p>In other places I see people just doing:</p>

<pre><code>text.split()
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>Use whichever you like, but realize that str.split is the recommended way of doing it.  :-)</p>

<p>string.split is a tad older method of doing the same thing.</p>

<p>str.split is a bit more efficient (since you don't have to import the string module or look up any names from it), but not enough to make a huge difference if you prefer string.split.</p>
