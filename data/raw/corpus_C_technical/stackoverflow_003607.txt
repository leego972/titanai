Question: <p>I need a compact representation of an array of booleans, does Python have a builtin bitfield type or will I need to find an alternate solution?</p>

Answer: <p>If you want to use ints (or long ints) to represent as arrays of bools (or as sets of integers), take a look at  <a href="http://sourceforge.net/projects/pybitop/files/" rel="nofollow">http://sourceforge.net/projects/pybitop/files/</a></p>

<p>It provides insert/extract of bitfields into long ints; finding the most-significant, or least-significant '1' bit; counting all the 1's; bit-reversal; stuff like that which is all possible in pure python but much faster in C.</p>
