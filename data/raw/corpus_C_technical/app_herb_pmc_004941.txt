# PMC: adaptogen stress response HPA axis — Overview

As sustained stress produces depletion of monoamines in the brain, this leads to memory deficit and depression. The improvement in learning task and retention of memory by EASF of <italic toggle="yes">M. alba</italic> may be due to an increase in the levels of depleted monoamines.</p><p>Chronic administration of EASF of <italic toggle="yes">M. alba</italic> significantly decreased the duration of immobility. Thus, the extract was effective in relieving endogenous depression. Diazepam also improved stress-induced depression. The antidepressant activity of the extract may be exerted in a similar manner by the increase in the level of depleted monoamines.</p><p>Administration of EASF of <italic toggle="yes">M. alba</italic> significantly reversed the elevated glucose level. Diazepam also reduced hyperglycemia. EASF of <italic toggle="yes">M. alba</italic> significantly decreased the adrenal gland hypertrophy and also significantly improved atrophy of the spleen.</p><p>Pre-treatment with EASF of <italic toggle="yes">M. alba</italic> significantly reversed the stress-induced UI. The decrease in the UI shows its potential role in attenuating the activation of the HPA-axis during stress. This demonstrates its potential antistress activity. Diazepam also inhibited the ulcerogenic effects of CS.</p></sec><sec id="sec1-5" sec-type="conclusions"><title>Conclusion</title><p>The results of the study indicate that EASF of <italic toggle="yes">M. alba</italic> roots possess significant antistress activity, as shown by its mitigating effects on CS-induced neurological, behavioral and biochemical perturbations. Thus, <italic toggle="yes">M. alba</italic> may provide an alternative to conventional therapy for the treatment of stress.</p></sec></body><back><ack><p>The authors are thankful to the All India
