On TLC developed in PE: EtOAc (10:2), the Rf was 0.40 and appeared purple in anisaldehyde spray reagent. The IR spectrum revealed absorption bands of hydroxyl (3280.90 cm<sup>− 1</sup>), carbonyl (1724.65 cm<sup>− 1</sup>) and olefinic C = C stretch (1687.91 cm<sup>− 1</sup>). <sup>1</sup>H and <sup>13</sup>C NMR (Supplementary Information, Figures S8, S9) data were similar with those of 2, indicating structural similarities. The significant difference was an additional quaternary carbon peak, δ<sub>C</sub> 171.8 (C-21) in compound 3 due to an ester carbonyl peak at δ<sub>C</sub> 171.8 (C21) and the acetyl carbon at δ<sub>C</sub> 21.7 (C-22). The position of the ester group at C-19 was supported by the HMBC correlations of H-20a and H-20b to C-16, C-18 and C-19, suggesting that 3 is xylopic acid, also previously isolated from <italic toggle="yes">X. aethiopica</italic> [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR47">47</xref>]. </p><p id="Par62">X-ray crystallography data gave an empirical formula of C<sub>22</sub>H<sub>32</sub>O<sub>4</sub> and M = 360.47 g/mol.
