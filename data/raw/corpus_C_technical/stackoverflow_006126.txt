Question: <p>Is there a way to get the Unicode Block of a character in python? The <a href="http://www.python.org/doc/2.5.2/lib/module-unicodedata.html" rel="nofollow">unicodedata</a> module doesn't seem to have what I need, and I couldn't find an external library for it.</p>

<p>Basically, I need the same functionality as <a href="http://java.sun.com/javase/6/docs/api/java/lang/Character.UnicodeBlock.html#of(char)" rel="nofollow"><code>Character.UnicodeBlock.of()</code></a> in java.</p>

Answer: <p>unicodedata.name(chr)</p>
