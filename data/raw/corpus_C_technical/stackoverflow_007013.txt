Question: <p>What is the Python equivalent of Perl's <code>chomp</code> function, which removes the last character of a value?</p>

Answer: <p>Just use : </p>

<pre><code>line = line.rstrip("\n")
</code></pre>

<p>or</p>

<pre><code>line = line.strip("\n")
</code></pre>

<p>You don't need any of this complicated stuff</p>
