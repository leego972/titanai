Question: <p>Because regular expressions scare me, I'm trying to find a way to remove all HTML tags and resolve HTML entities from a string in Python.</p>

Answer: <p>Regular expressions are not scary, but writing your own regexes to strip HTML is a sure path to madness (and it won't work, either).  Follow the path of wisdom, and use one of the many good HTML-parsing libraries.</p>

<p>Lucas' example is also broken because "sub" is not a method of a Python string.  You'd have to "import re", then call re.sub(pattern, repl, string).  But that's neither here nor there, as the correct answer to your question does not involve writing any regexes.</p>
