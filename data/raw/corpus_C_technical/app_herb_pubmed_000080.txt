Bio-Active Compounds from Teucrium Plants Used in the Traditional Medicine of 
Kurdistan Region, Iraq.

Herbal medicine is still widely practiced in the Kurdistan Region, Iraq, 
especially by people living in villages in mountainous regions. Seven taxa 
belonging to the genus Teucrium (family Lamiaceae) are commonly employed in the 
Kurdish traditional medicine, especially to treat jaundice, stomachache and 
abdominal problems. We report, in this paper, a comprehensive account about the 
chemical structures and bioactivities of most representative specialized 
metabolites isolated from these plants. These findings indicate that Teucrium 
plants used in the folk medicine of Iraqi Kurdistan are natural sources of 
specialized metabolites that are potentially beneficial to human health.
