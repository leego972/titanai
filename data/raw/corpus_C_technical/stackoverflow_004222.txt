Question: <p>What is the best way to get a list of all files in a directory, sorted by date [created | modified], using python, on a windows machine?</p>

Answer: <pre><code>sorted(filter(os.path.isfile, os.listdir('.')), 
    key=lambda p: os.stat(p).st_mtime)
</code></pre>

<p>You could use <code>os.walk('.').next()[-1]</code> instead of filtering with <code>os.path.isfile</code>, but that leaves dead symlinks in the list, and <code>os.stat</code> will fail on them.</p>
