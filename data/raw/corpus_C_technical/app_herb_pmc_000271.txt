Methanolic and hexane extracts of <italic toggle="yes">R. graveolens</italic> showed potent inhibition of AChE and butyryl cholinesterase (BuChE) <italic toggle="yes">in-vitro</italic>[<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="B48">48</xref>]. Rue contains rutin, which, widely used as a drug to improve blood circulation and expected to contribute for such activities.</p><p>Sage is a common name for <italic toggle="yes">Salvia</italic> species, and highly appreciated over all the world, it is used for treatment of many diseases and also proved to have strong activity against AD, in Egypt <italic toggle="yes">S. triloba</italic> is called Maramaria and it is used as condiment and tea. The plant has been reported in old Arabic literature to improve the mental power [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="B49">49</xref>,<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="B50">50</xref>].</p><p>Black pepper (<italic toggle="yes">Piper nigrum</italic>) is a flowering vine in the family Piperaceae [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="B51">51</xref>]. The plant has been used effectively for the treatment of AD. Piperine is a major plant alkaloid present in black pepper (<italic toggle="yes">Piper nigrum</italic>) and long pepper (<italic toggle="yes">Piper longum</italic>), which are among the most common spices consumed by a large number of people worldwide. This plant is known to possess several pharmacological actions, such as antimicrobial, antifungal, anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="B52">52</xref>]. Piperine demonstrated in <italic toggle="yes">in vitro</italic> studies to protect against oxidative damage by inhibiting or quenching free radicals and ROS, lower lipid peroxidation <italic toggle="yes">in vivo</italic> and beneficially influence cellular thiol status, antioxidant molecules and antioxidant enzymes in a number of experimental situations of oxidative stress [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="B53">53</xref>].</p><p>A recent <italic toggle="yes">in vivo</italic> work conducted by our group, revealed a significant reduction of the oxidative stress status and amelioration of the neurodegeneration characteristic of Alzheimer’s diseases in rats using <italic toggle="yes">P. nigrum</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">S. triloba</italic>. It is noteworthy that <italic toggle="yes">S. triloba</italic> extract showed more interest in improvement of AD in rats [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="B54">54</xref>].</p><p><italic toggle="yes">Terminalia</italic> species belong to the family combretaceae. They are extensively used in Unani, Ayurveda and homeopathic medicine. <italic toggle="yes">T. chebula</italic> is a popular traditional medicine in many countries including Egypt. It has a wide spectrum of pharmacological activities and reported as antioxidant, antidiabetic, antibacterial, antiviral, antifungal …etc. According to Unani medicine, emulsifying of one fruit every day prevents ageing and keeps the person very healthy. Recent literature supported the anti-ageing properties of <italic toggle="yes">Terminalia</italic> species. Phenolic constituents from <italic toggle="yes">T. chebula</italic> showed strong AChE and BChE inhibitory activities, and antioxidant activity [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="B55">55</xref>]. <italic toggle="yes">T. chebula</italic> has been recommended for old age diseases [<xref ref-type="bibr" rid="B56">56</xref>].
