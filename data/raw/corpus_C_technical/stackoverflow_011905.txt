Question: <p>I am relatively new to Python, and I have always used the standard cpython (v2.5) implementation.  </p>

<p>I've been wondering about the other implementations though, particularly Jython and IronPython.  What makes them better?  What makes them worse?  What other implementations are there?</p>

<p>I guess what I'm looking for is a summary and list of pros and cons for each implementation.</p>

Answer: <p>An additional benefit for Jython, at least for some, is <a href="http://zyasoft.com/pythoneering/2008/06/realizing-jython-25.html">it lacks the GIL</a> (the Global Interpreter Lock) and uses Java's native threads.  This means that you can run pure Python code in parallel, something not possible with the GIL.</p>
