# List of the largest trading partners of the United States

The 30 largest trade partners of the United States represent 87.9 percent of U.S. exports, and 87.4 percent of U.S. imports . These figures do not include services or foreign direct investment.

In 2023, Mexico became the largest trading partner of the United States.
