The Effect of Low-Level Laser Therapy on the Healing of Soft Tissue Graft Donor 
and Recipient Sites: A Randomized Clinical Trial.

BACKGROUND: Keratinized gingiva is crucial for periodontal health, and 
insufficient width can cause gingival recession. Free gingival graft (FGG) and 
connective tissue graft (CTG) commonly address this issue. Low-level laser 
therapy (LLLT) has been suggested to enhance wound healing and reduce 
complications after graft surgery. This study aimed to evaluate the effects of 
810-nm diode laser therapy on bleeding, pain, and epithelialization at donor and 
recipient sites over four weeks.
MATERIALS AND METHODS: In this double-blinded randomized clinical trial, 40 
patients (20 in each group) who underwent FGG or CGT surgery were assigned to 
either the laser or control group. In the laser group, LLLT using a 660-nm diode 
laser with a power output of 40 mW was applied to the donor and recipient sites 
immediately after surgery and at 24 h, 3 days, and 7 days post-surgery. 
Bleeding, pain levels, and epithelialization were assessed weekly for four 
weeks. Data were analyzed using SPSS version 22. T-tests, Chi-square tests, and 
Fisher's exact were used with a significance level 0.05.
RESULTS: No bleeding occurred at the donor or recipient sites, and pain levels 
were similar between the laser and control groups throughout the study. 
Epithelialization showed no significant differences in the first two weeks and 
the fourth week in both groups. By week three, the laser group had significantly 
more epithelialization at the donor site (p = 0.018), while the control group 
had better epithelialization at the recipient site (p = 0.047). No differences 
were observed by graft type.
CONCLUSION: LLLT accelerated epithelialization at the donor site; however, the 
results were reversed at the recipient site. Furthermore, laser treatment did 
not significantly affect pain levels at either site. These findings suggest that 
LLLT may benefit certain aspects of soft tissue healing after graft 
surgery.Trial registration: Trial Registry Code: IRCT20231213060351N1, 22 June 
2024. http://irct.ir.
