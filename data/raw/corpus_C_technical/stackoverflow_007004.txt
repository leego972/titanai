Question: <p>What is the Python equivalent of Perl's <code>chomp</code> function, which removes the last character of a value?</p>

Answer: <p>If your question is to clean up all the line breaks in a multiple line str object (oldstr), you can split it into a list according to the delimiter '\n' and then join this list into a new str(newstr).</p>

<p><code>newstr = "".join(oldstr.split('\n'))</code>    </p>
