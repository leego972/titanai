Description:
Arecoline is a tetrahydropyridine that is 1,2,5,6-tetrahydropyridine with a methyl group at position 1, and a methoxycarbonyl group at position 3. An alkaloid found in the areca nut, it acts as an agonist of muscarinic acetylcholine. It has a role as a metabolite and a muscarinic agonist. It is a methyl ester, a pyridine alkaloid, a tetrahydropyridine and an enoate ester.
