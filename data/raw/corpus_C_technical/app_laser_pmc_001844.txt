(C) Histograms represent changes in the intensity of DCF fluorescence, arising from production and accumulation of intracellular ROS in MEFs.</p></caption><graphic xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink" position="float" orientation="portrait" xlink:href="pone.0247098.g008.jpg"/></fig></sec></sec><sec sec-type="conclusions" id="sec032"><title>Discussion</title><p>Pressure ulcers are one of most dominant problems occurring in patients with mobility limitations. Affecting numerous people around the world and imposing considerable medical expenses on both individuals and health services of countries each year [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref046" ref-type="bibr">46</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref047" ref-type="bibr">47</xref>], this challenge has attracted considerations. Although several therapeutic approaches are using in order to heal such wounds [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref008" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref009" ref-type="bibr">9</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref048" ref-type="bibr">48</xref>], there is still obstacles in appropriate healing which necessitates new practical alternative or adjunctive. Plenty of studies done by researches have shown the potent beneficial effects of curcumin as a herbal medicine as well as low-level laser therapy (LLLT) as novel therapeutic approaches [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref020" ref-type="bibr">20</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref035" ref-type="bibr">35</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref049" ref-type="bibr">49</xref>]. Since curcumin works on different stages of the wound healing process [<xref rid="pone.0247098.ref017" ref-type="bibr">17</xref>, <xref rid="pone.0247098.ref018" ref-type="bibr">18</xref>], we sought to investigate the anti-oxidant, anti-inflammation and proliferative effects of curcumin along with LLLT on important events in wound healing process including proliferation, migration and alteration of cytokines and growth factors involved as well as modulating the oxidative stress, through in vitro studies on mouse embryonic fibroblast cells (<xref rid="pone.0247098.t001" ref-type="table">Table 1</xref>). In order to overcome curcumin’s poor bioavailability and to get the maximum therapeutical effects of it, we utilized dendrosome nanoparticles in the present study.</p><table-wrap
