Question: <p>Is there a way to send a file using POST from a Python script?</p>

Answer: <p>You may also want to have a look at <a href="http://code.google.com/p/httplib2/" rel="nofollow">httplib2</a>, with <a href="http://bitworking.org/projects/httplib2/doc/html/libhttplib2.html#examples" rel="nofollow">examples</a>.
