Question: <p>How can I output colored text to the terminal, in Python?
What is the best Unicode symbol to represent a solid block?</p>

Answer: <p>I'm surprised no one has mentioned the <a href="http://pypi.python.org/pypi/termcolor">Python termcolor module</a>. Usage is pretty simple:</p>

<pre><code>from termcolor import colored

print colored('hello', 'red'), colored('world', 'green')
</code></pre>

<p>It may not be sophisticated enough, however, for game programming and the "colored blocks" that you want to do...</p>
