# Dic Edwards

ales Trinity Saint David, Lampeter, where he was a lecturer until 2019. He is the editor and founder of the literary magazine The Lampeter Review.

Personal life
Edwards is married to Gwenda and has three children and eight grandchildren. He lives in Aberaeron in West Wales.
