Antidepression</title><p>Extracts of SHR-5 from <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola rosea</italic> rhizomes alleviated depressive symptoms in patients with mild or moderate depression, yielding no severe side effects [<xref rid="b20-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">20</xref>]. Orally administering salidroside (20 mg/kg and 40 mg/kg) for 2 weeks notably alleviated olfactory-bulbectomy-induced hyperactivity in an open-field test and reduced immobility time in a forced swimming test [<xref rid="b64-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">64</xref>]. Chronic treatment with salidroside greatly reduced TNF-α and IL-1β levels in the hippocampus [<xref rid="b64-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">64</xref>]. Salidroside increased glucocorticoid receptor and brain-derived neurotrophic factor expression in the hippocampus of rats. In addition, salidroside attenuated corticotropin-releasing hormone expression in the hypothalamus and the levels of serum corticosterone [<xref rid="b64-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">64</xref>]. Salidroside significantly reduced depression-like behavior in olfactory bulbectomized rats; the mechanisms of this reduction might be associated with the anti-inflammatory effects of and regulation of hypothalamic–pituitary–adrenal axis activity by salidroside.</p></sec><sec sec-type="other"><title>9.
