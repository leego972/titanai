CUPELLATION Cu`pel*la"tion (k`pl-l"shn), n. Etym: [See Cupel.] Defn: The act or process of refining gold or silver, etc., in a cupel. Note: The process consist in exposing the cupel containing the metal to be assayed or refined to a hot blast, by which the lead, copper, tin, etc., are oxidized, dissolved, and carried down into the porous cupel, leaving the unoxidizable precious metal.
