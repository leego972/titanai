    title = models.CharField(max_length=20)
    slug = models.SlugField()
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>Outside the admin, see <a href="http://www.djangosnippets.org/snippets/690/">this django snippet</a>. Put it in your <code>.save()</code>, and it'll work with objects created programmatically. Inside the admin, as the others have said, use <code>prepopulated_fields</code>.</p>
