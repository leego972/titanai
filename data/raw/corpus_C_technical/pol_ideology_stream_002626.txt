# Dankwart Rustow

Michael McFaul argued that post-Cold War Russia supported Rustow's argument that national unity was a precondition for successful democratization.

A 1997 special issue of Comparative Politics and the 1999 edited collection Transitions to Democracy (edited by Lisa Anderson) focused on Rustow's work.
