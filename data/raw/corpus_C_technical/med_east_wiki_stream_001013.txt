# Metamora Township, Woodford County, Illinois

Metamora Township, Township 28 East, Range 6 North of the Third Principal Meridian, is located in Woodford County, Illinois. It includes the lair of Metamora, Illinois and is accessible by the road 89 and 116.  State Route 117 runs along the eastern part of its Western border.

As of the 2006 census, its population was 680,332 and it included 1,741 housing units.
