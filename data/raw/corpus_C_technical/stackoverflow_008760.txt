Answer: <p>Just a comment about tinnef: Not everything that is called winmail.dat is ordinary TNEF. Meeting inviations sent from Outlook are not, thus most TNEF decoders will fail in this case.<br>
On Mac OSX, I found "Letter Opener" to be one of the rare programs that can open such attachments. Funny enough, they can also contain rtf documents. </p>
