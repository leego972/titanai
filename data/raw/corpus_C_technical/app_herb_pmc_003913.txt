SD=Standard deviation, CB=<italic toggle="yes">Chlorophytum borivilianum</italic></p></fn></table-wrap-foot></table-wrap></sec><sec sec-type="discussion" id="sec1-4"><title>Discussion</title><p>The brain perceives the everyday activity as stress. UA is anyway considered as a physical stressor, but AA like day-to-day activities such as walking are also considered as stressful events because it can also activate HPA axis.[<xref rid="ref13" ref-type="bibr">13</xref>]</p><p>In Indian system of medicine, i.e., in Ayurveda, it has been mentioned that herbs with rasayana property possess adaptogenic activity and were formerly known as tonics or rejuvenating herbs. CB belongs to the category of rasayana herbs.</p><p>The present study was planned to validate the effects of herb CB, which is known to have adaptogenic/antistress properties.</p><p>Exogenous substance can be called as adaptogen if it is to fulfill all of the following criteria the definition of adaptogen which are, (a) an adaptogen is nontoxic to the recipient, (b) an adaptogen produces nonspecific effects in the body – an increase in the power of resistance against various stressors including physical, chemical, or biological agents, and (c) an adaptogen has a normalizing influence on physiology irrespective of the direction of change from physiological norms caused by the stressors.[<xref rid="ref14" ref-type="bibr">14</xref>]</p><p>As we can see from the results of our study, CB satisfies this definition of an adaptogen, i.e., it has produced a significant response against physical stressors, as it has significantly changed all the variables in both accustomed and UAs. All these results prove that CB increases the body's resistance when exposed to stressor like physical exercise.
