# Flávio Minuano

Flávio Almeida da Fonseca (born July 9, 1944, in Porto Alegre), best known as Flávio Minuano, is a former footballer from Brazil.

Flávio played for Fluminense, Internacional and Santos in the Campeonato Brasileiro.

Career 
Internacional: 1961 - 1964
Corinthians: 1964 - 1969
Fluminense: 1969 - 1971
Porto: 1972 - 1975
Internacional: 1975 - 1976
Santos: 1977
Pelotas: 1977 - 1978
Brasília: 1979
Jorge Wilstermann: 1980
