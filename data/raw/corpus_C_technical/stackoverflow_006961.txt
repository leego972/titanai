Question: <p>Python 3.0 breaks backwards compatibility with previous versions and splits the language into two paths (at least temporarily). Do you know of any other language that went through such a major design phase while in maturity?</p>

<p>Also, do you believe that this is how programming languages should evolve or is the price to pay simply too high?</p>

Answer: <p>The python team has worked very hard to make the lack of backward compatibility as painless as possible, to the point where the 2.6 release of python was created with a mind towards a painless upgrade process.   Once you have upgraded to 2.6 there are scripts that you can run that will move you to 3.0 without issue.  </p>
