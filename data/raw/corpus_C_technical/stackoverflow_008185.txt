<p>Assuming my email is "email@gmail.com" and the password is "password," how should this look?  I tried <code>M.login(getpass.getuser(email@gmail.com), getpass.getpass(password))</code>
and it timed out.  Complete newb here, so it's very likely I missed something obvious (like creating an iMAP object first?  Not sure).</p>

Answer: <p>Instead of <code>M.login(getpass.getuser(email@gmail.com), getpass.getpass(password))</code> you need to use <code>M.login('email@gmail.com', 'password')</code>, i.e. plain strings (or better, variables containing them). Your attempt actually shouldn't have worked at all, since <a href="https://docs.python.org/2/library/getpass.html" rel="nofollow"><code>getpass</code></a>'s <code>getuser</code> doesn't take arguments but merely returns the user login name. And <code>email@gmail.com</code> isn't even a valid variable name (you didn't put it into quotes)...</p>
