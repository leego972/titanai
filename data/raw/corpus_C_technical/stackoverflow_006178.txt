Question: <p>Imagine I have these python lists:</p>

<pre><code>keys = ['name', 'age']
values = ['Monty', 42, 'Matt', 28, 'Frank', 33]
</code></pre>

<p>Is there a direct or at least a simple way to produce the following list of dictionaries ?</p>

<pre><code>[
    {'name': 'Monty', 'age': 42},
    {'name': 'Matt',  'age': 28},
    {'name': 'Frank', 'age': 33}
]
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>It's not pretty but here's a one-liner using a list comprehension, zip and stepping:</p>

<pre><code>[dict(zip(keys, a)) for a in zip(values[::2], values[1::2])]
</code></pre>
