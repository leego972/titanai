# Ralahine

n Vandeleur went bankrupt due to his gambling losses. Members of the Ralahine commune who were paid by Vandeleur by "labour notes" were reimbursed by Edward Craig himself, out of his own fortune. The collapse of the Ralahine Commune led to Edward Craig's reputation and legacy being damaged.
