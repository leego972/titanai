Question: <p>How can I fetch more than 1000 record from data store and put all in one single list to pass to django?</p>

Answer: <p>To add the contents of the two queries together:</p>

<pre><code>list1 = first query
list2 = second query
list1 += list2
</code></pre>

<p>List 1 now contains all 2000 results.</p>
