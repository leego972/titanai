[Herbal medicines--evidence and drug interactions in clinical practice].

We present an evidence-based literature review of five commonly used herbs in 
Denmark: St John's wort, ginkgo biloba, valerian, garlic, and ginseng. Various 
drug interactions are associated with the intake of some herbal medicines, and 
may result in many clinical conditions. We bring this to the attention of 
clinical practitioners. Attention to clinical practice and recommendations for 
discontinuation of the five herbs are given before surgery. Physicians should be 
aware of and report potential drug interactions and adverse effects, so as to 
throw more light on this subject.
