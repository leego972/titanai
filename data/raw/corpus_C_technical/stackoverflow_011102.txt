Question: <p>I have a long line of code that I want to break up among multiple lines. What do I use and what is the syntax?</p>

<p>For example, adding a bunch of strings,</p>

<pre><code>e = 'a' + 'b' + 'c' + 'd'
</code></pre>

<p>and have it like this:</p>

<pre><code>e = 'a' + 'b' +
    'c' + 'd'
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>Put a <code>\</code> at the end of your line or enclose the statement in parens <code>( .. )</code>. From <a href="http://www.ibm.com/developerworks/opensource/library/os-python5/">IBM</a>:</p>

<pre><code>b = ((i1 &lt; 20) and
     (i2 &lt; 30) and
     (i3 &lt; 40))
</code></pre>

<p>or</p>

<pre><code>b = (i1 &lt; 20) and \
    (i2 &lt; 30) and \
    (i3 &lt; 40)
</code></pre>
