Title: Youth Protagonism in Latin American Climate Litigation

Abstract:
Climate litigation has emerged as a powerful tool to compel governments and private entities to ensure environmental protection and a safe climate for current and future generations. With the rise of climate litigation cases focused on human rights, there has been an increase in efforts to protect children’s rights amid the climate crisis. As climate change threatens the well-being of younger generations, legal actions initiated by children and youth to hold governments accountable have surged. This paper analyses youth-led climate litigation in Latin America, considering the region’s unique legal framework and rights-based traditions. It explores how Latin American constitutional elements and international human rights instruments support youth litigation. The study draws on regional climate litigation databases and literature on intergenerational climate litigation in the region.
