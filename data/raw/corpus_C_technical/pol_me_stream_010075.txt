In November 2021, Agnew followed Bruce out the door at Newcastle United, following the appointment of Eddie Howe and his backroom staff.

Bruce was appointed manager of Championship club West Bromwich Albion in February 2022, and was again accompanied by Agnew and Clemence. They left with him when he was sacked eight months later. on 30 January 2023 he join  Aberdeen as interim assistant manager to interim manager Barry Robson following sacking of Jim Goodwin, signing on 1 May 2023 with Barry for a further two and a half years until end of season 2024–25.
