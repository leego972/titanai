Description:
Aminoparathion is an organic thiophosphate that is 4-aminophenol in which the hydroxy group is replaced by a (diethoxyphosphorothioyl)oxy group. It is a metabolite of parathion. It has a role as a human xenobiotic metabolite, a bacterial xenobiotic metabolite and an environmental contaminant. It is an organothiophosphate insecticide, an organic thiophosphate and a substituted aniline. It is functionally related to a 4-aminophenol and a parathion.
