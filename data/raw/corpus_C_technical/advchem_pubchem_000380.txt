Description:
SB220025 is am member of the class of imidazoles carrying piperidin-4-yl, 4-fluophenyl and 2-aminopyrimidin-4-yl substituents at posiitons 1, 4 and 5 respectively. It has a role as an angiogenesis inhibitor, an anti-inflammatory agent and an EC 2.7.11.24 (mitogen-activated protein kinase) inhibitor. It is a member of imidazoles, a member of piperidines, an organofluorine compound and an aminopyrimidine.
