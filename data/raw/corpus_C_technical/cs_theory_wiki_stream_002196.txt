# Lists of exoplanets

These are lists of exoplanets.  Most of these were discovered by the Kepler space telescope. There are an additional 1,984 potential exoplanets from Kepler's first mission yet to be confirmed, as well as 977 from its "Second Light" mission and 4,540 from the Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS) mission.
