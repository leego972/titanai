</code></pre>

Answer: <p>It does the same:</p>

<pre><code>cursor.execute('INSERT INTO mastertickets (%s, %s) VALUES (:%s, :%s)' % \
    tuple(sourcedest + sourcedest), dict(zip(sourcedest, (self.tkt.id, n))))
</code></pre>
