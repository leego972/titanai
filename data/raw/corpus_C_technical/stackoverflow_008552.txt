Question: <p>Can someone give me some example code that creates a surface with a transparent background in pygame?</p>

Answer: <p>This should do it:</p>

<pre><code>image = pygame.Surface([640,480], pygame.SRCALPHA, 32)
image = image.convert_alpha()
</code></pre>

<p>Make sure that the color depth (32) stays explicitly set else this will not work.</p>
