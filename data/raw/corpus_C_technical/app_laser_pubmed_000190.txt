Photodynamic therapy of cholangiocarcinoma cancer.

Ortner MA(1).

Author information:
(1)Endoscopy Unit, Department of Gastroenterology/Hepatology/Endocrinology, 
Humboldt University Charite CCN, Berlin, Germany. marianne.ortner@charite.de

Prognosis of nonresectable cholangiocarcinoma is not good, and in Bismuth type 
III and IV tumors relief of jaundice is seldom achieved, despite successful 
endoprosthesis insertion. Additional photodynamic therapy seems to be a 
promising new approach to these tumors. First pilot studies show relief of 
jaundice, mainly because of opening of the intrahepatic ducts and improvements 
in quality of life indices. Survival time seems to be long; however, randomized 
studies are necessary to confirm these results.
