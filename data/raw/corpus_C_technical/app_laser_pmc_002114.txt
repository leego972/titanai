LLLT interventions used wavelengths in the red and infrared ranges, whereas LEDs included red, infrared, green, and blue spectra. Wavelengths ranged from 460 to 950 nm, with 660–830 nm being the most commonly used range [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0021" ref-type="bibr">21</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0022" ref-type="bibr">22</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0023" ref-type="bibr">23</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0024" ref-type="bibr">24</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0026" ref-type="bibr">26</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0029" ref-type="bibr">29</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0030" ref-type="bibr">30</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0031" ref-type="bibr">31</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0032" ref-type="bibr">32</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0033" ref-type="bibr">33</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0034" ref-type="bibr">34</xref>]. Energy densities ranged from 1 to 60 J/cm², with most studies using 1–10 J/cm². Continuous‐mode irradiation was used in most studies [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0021" ref-type="bibr">21</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0022" ref-type="bibr">22</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0023" ref-type="bibr">23</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0024" ref-type="bibr">24</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0025" ref-type="bibr">25</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0028" ref-type="bibr">28</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0029" ref-type="bibr">29</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0030" ref-type="bibr">30</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0031" ref-type="bibr">31</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0032" ref-type="bibr">32</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0033" ref-type="bibr">33</xref>], five studies did not specify the mode [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0026" ref-type="bibr">26</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0027" ref-type="bibr">27</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0034" ref-type="bibr">34</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0035" ref-type="bibr">35</xref>, <xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0036" ref-type="bibr">36</xref>], and only one study directly compared continuous LLLT and pulsed LED [<xref rid="cbf70161-bib-0029" ref-type="bibr">29</xref>].
