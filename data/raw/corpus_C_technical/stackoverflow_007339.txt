Answer: <p>I'm not familiar enough with the Decimal class to help you out, but your problem is due to the fact that decimal fractions can often not be accurate represented in binary, so what you're seeing is the closest possible approximation; there's no way to avoid this problem without using a special class (like Decimal, probably).</p>
