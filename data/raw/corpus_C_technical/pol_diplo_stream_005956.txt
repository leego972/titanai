# Margot Käßmann

er the start of the 2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine, Käßmann repeatedly criticized the delivery of weapons to Ukraine, calling for a diplomatic solution to the conflict instead. In February 2023, Käßmann was one of the initial signers of a petition calling for an end to military support to Ukraine.
