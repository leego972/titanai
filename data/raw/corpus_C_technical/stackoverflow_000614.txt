<p>When I go to <a href="http://tred">http://tred</a> I get a directory listing rather than the rendered website. I think I have followed the tutorials correctly but it is obviously not right. What can I do to make this work?</p>

Answer: <p>Note that Alias and WSGIScriptAlias directives do not have the same precedence. Thus, they will not be processed in file order as written. Instead, all Alias directives get precedence over WSGIScriptAlias directives. Thus, it wouldn't have mattered if the Alias for '/' appeared after WSGIScriptAlias, it would still have taken precedence.</p>
