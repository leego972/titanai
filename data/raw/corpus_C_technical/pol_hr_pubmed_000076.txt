Humanitarian medical aid to the Syrian people: Ethical implications and 
dilemmas.

Zarka S, Farhat M, Gidron T.

Medical professionals providing humanitarian aid in times of crisis face 
complicated ethical and clinical challenges. Today, humanitarian aid is given in 
accordance with existing guidelines developed by international humanitarian 
organizations and defined by international law. This paper considers the ethical 
aspects and frameworks of an atypical humanitarian project, namely one that 
provides medical support through an Israeli civilian hospital to Syrian Civil 
War casualties. We explore new ethical questions in this unique situation that 
pose a serious challenge for the medical community and conventional ethical 
norms, a challenge Israeli medical staff meet on a daily basis. Before 
discussing the ethical challenges, we give a description of the project and its 
unique status.
