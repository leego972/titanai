Description:
Ornithine is an alpha-amino acid that is pentanoic acid bearing two amino substituents at positions 2 and 5. It has a role as an Escherichia coli metabolite, a human metabolite, a Daphnia magna metabolite and an algal metabolite. It is a conjugate base of an ornithinium(1+). It is a conjugate acid of an ornithinate.
