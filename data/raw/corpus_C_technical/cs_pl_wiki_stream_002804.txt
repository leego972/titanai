# Cricket coqui

The cricket coqui, green coqui, or coqui grillo (Eleutherodactylus gryllus) is a species of frog in the family Eleutherodactylidae endemic to Puerto Rico.
Its natural habitats are subtropical or tropical moist lowland forest and subtropical or tropical moist montane forest.
