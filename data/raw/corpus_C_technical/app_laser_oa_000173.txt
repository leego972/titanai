Title: Design And Implementation A System Communication Of Optical Fiber By Using Interval Wavelength (532nm-820nm) Of Diode Laser

Abstract:
The optical fiber communication system has different designs and implementations. In this work, two light sources were used in the optical system , a diode laser of a wavelength (532nm) and a light emitting diode of a wavelength 820nm . Multimode optical fiber is used as a channel and PIN photodiode is used as an optical detector . For each light source, the power budget for the optical design and the power-current curve have been measured by using the experimental results.
