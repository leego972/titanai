100. Breastfeed Rev. 2016 Jul;24(2):27-31.

Low level laser therapy for breastfeeding problems.

Buck ML, Eckereder G, Amir LH.

Breast and nipple pain, nipple damage and mastitis are common reasons given by 
women for their early cessation of breastfeeding. There are a limited number of 
effective therapies available to support healing of damaged nipples during 
lactation. Low level laser therapy is a painless treatment, which appears to 
accelerate wound healing and ease pain. We present two case studies, which 
demonstrate the use of low level laser therapy in clinical practice.
