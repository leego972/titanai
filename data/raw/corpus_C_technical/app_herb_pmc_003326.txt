Roxb.) / NAD-027</td></tr></tbody></table></alternatives></table-wrap><p>The total number of species within a given family has been calculated to obtain their FUV. Our results showed that Aloaceae had a high FUV (0.86), followed by Acoraceae (0.80), Piperaceae (0.69), and Euphorbiaceae (0.65). Other families represented low FUV (&lt; 0.60) (<xref rid="pone.0235886.t002" ref-type="table">Table 2</xref>). High values of FUV might be because the plant species were cited by a large number of people in the study area. In addition, some reports have described similar results. For example, <italic toggle="yes">A</italic>. <italic toggle="yes">vera</italic> or locally named as crocodile’s tongues has been frequently used in some regions such as Southern Africa [<xref rid="pone.0235886.ref037" ref-type="bibr">37</xref>], Asia [<xref rid="pone.0235886.ref038" ref-type="bibr">38</xref>], Nigeria [<xref rid="pone.0235886.ref039" ref-type="bibr">39</xref>], and India [<xref rid="pone.0235886.ref040" ref-type="bibr">40</xref>] to treat dry skin, for improving skin integrity, and to decrease the appearance of acne, skin burn, and wrinkles.</p><p><italic toggle="yes">A</italic>. <italic toggle="yes">calamus</italic> is also cited by other ethnobotanical studies around the world including China [<xref rid="pone.0235886.ref041" ref-type="bibr">41</xref>], India [<xref rid="pone.0235886.ref042" ref-type="bibr">42</xref>], Nepal [<xref rid="pone.0235886.ref043" ref-type="bibr">43</xref>] to treat fever, diarrhea, bronchitis, tumors, skin diseases, and cough treatment [<xref rid="pone.0235886.ref029" ref-type="bibr">29</xref>]. Reported species from Piperaceae has also highly cited in the previous study [<xref rid="pone.0235886.ref011" ref-type="bibr">11</xref>]. Finally, another high FUV in this study was obtained by Euphorbiaceae with only one species (<italic toggle="yes">Jatropha curcas</italic>). The Tengger people use this Barbados nut species to treat mouth ulcers and hyperuricemia. Our data supports other studies; for example, Abdelgadir and Staden [<xref rid="pone.0235886.ref044" ref-type="bibr">44</xref>] reported that its latex is used for ailments such as headache, toothache, mouth ulcers, cold, and cough. Abu Bakar <italic toggle="yes">et al</italic>. [<xref rid="pone.0235886.ref045" ref-type="bibr">45</xref>] also reported that <italic toggle="yes">J</italic>. <italic toggle="yes">curcas</italic> is potentially used to treat hyperuricemia.</p></sec><sec id="sec015"><title>Fidelity level</title><p>According to Imran <italic toggle="yes">et al</italic>.
