Question: <p>How do I convert a hex string to an int in Python? </p>

<p>I may have it as "<code>0xffff</code>" or just "<code>ffff</code>".</p>

Answer: <p>Adding to Dan's answer above: if you supply the int() function with a hex string, you will have to specify the base as 16 or it will not think you gave it a valid value. Specifying base 16 is unnecessary for hex numbers not contained in strings.</p>

<pre><code>print int(0xdeadbeef) # valid

myHex = "0xdeadbeef"
print int(myHex) # invalid, raises ValueError
print int(myHex , 16) # valid
</code></pre>
