Question: <p>What is the nicest/shortest way to pad a string with zeroes to the left, so the string length has a specific length?</p>

Answer: <p>For the ones how came here to understand and not just can a quick answer.
I do these especially for time strings:</p>

<pre><code>hour = 4
minute = 3
"{:0&gt;2}:{:0&gt;2}".format(hour,minute)
# prints 04:03

"{:0&gt;3}:{:0&gt;5}".format(hour,minute)
# prints '004:00003'

"{:0&lt;3}:{:0&lt;5}".format(hour,minute)
# prints '400:30000'

"{:$&lt;3}:{:#&lt;5}".format(hour,minute)
# prints '4$$:3####'
</code></pre>

<blockquote>
  <p>"0" symbols what to replace with the "2" padding characters, the default is an empty space</p>
  
  <p>">" symbols allign all the 2 "0" character to the left of the string</p>
  
  <p>":" symbols the format_spec</p>
</blockquote>
