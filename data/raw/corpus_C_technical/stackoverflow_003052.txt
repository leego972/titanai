Question: <p>for working with MS word files in python, there is python win32 extensions, which can be used in windows. How do I do the same in linux?
Is there any library?</p>

Answer: <p>benjamin's answer is a pretty good one.  I have just consolidated...</p>

<pre><code>import zipfile, re

docx = zipfile.ZipFile('/path/to/file/mydocument.docx')
content = docx.read('word/document.xml')
cleaned = re.sub('&lt;(.|\n)*?&gt;','',content)
print cleaned
</code></pre>
