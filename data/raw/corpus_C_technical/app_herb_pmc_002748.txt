The tender shoots of selected bamboo species like <italic toggle="yes">Dendrocalamus hamiltonii </italic>Hook. f. collected in bulk was prepared by cutting it into strips or pieces and boiled. The boiled shoots are chopped finely and packed in jars, bamboo tubes (<italic toggle="yes">Chunga</italic>) or even in plastic buckets and was kept for 5-10 days for fermentation. After fermentation, the taste of chopped shoots becomes sour. Fresh bamboo shoots and its fermented products were sold in the local market as edible foodstuffs. Important domestic uses concerned presently cultivated species of <italic toggle="yes">Livistona jenkensiana </italic>Griff. used for thatching and <italic toggle="yes">Bambusa tulda </italic>Roxb. and <italic toggle="yes">B. pallida </italic>Munro., for house building work. Traditional dyeing of clothes and food items were derived from plant species namely <italic toggle="yes">Illicium griffithii </italic>Hook. and <italic toggle="yes">Rubia cordifolia </italic>L. These plant species are currently cultivated in the gardens to meet regular use due to their less accessibility.
