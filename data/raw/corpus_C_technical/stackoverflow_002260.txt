Question: <p>I've seen several examples of code like this:</p>

<pre><code>if not someobj:
    #do something
</code></pre>

<p>But I'm wondering why not doing:</p>

<pre><code>if someobj == None:
    #do something
</code></pre>

<p>Is there any difference? Does one have an advantage over the other?</p>

Answer: <p>These two comparisons serve different purposes. The former checks for boolean value of something, the second checks for identity with None value.</p>
