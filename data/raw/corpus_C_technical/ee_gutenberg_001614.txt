LIGROIN Lig"ro*in, n. Defn: A trade name applied somewhat indefinitely to some of the volatile products obtained in refining crude petroleum. It is used as a solvent, as a carburetant for air gas, and for illumination in special lamps. LIGSAM Lig"sam, n. Etym: [Cf. The English adverbial ending-ly is from the same adjective. Cf. Each, Such, Which.] 1. Having the same, or nearly the same, appearance, qualities, or characteristics; resembling; similar to; similar; alike; -- often with in and the particulars of the resemblance; as, they are like each other in features, complexion, and many traits of character.
