# Minister of Conservation (Manitoba)

The Minister of Environment and Climate Change (formerly the Minister of Environment, Climate and Parks, Minister of Sustainable Development, Conservation and Climate and Enviroment and Climate) is the member of the Executive Council of Manitoba who oversees the Department of Environment and Climate, which is responsible for issues related to Manitoba's environment and biodiversity of natural resources (e.g., water, wildlife, and forests).

The current Environment and Climate Change Minister is Tracy Schmidt and the Deputy Minister is Jan Forster.
