Nowadays, “Attarin” drug shops and the public use mainly the Unani medicinal system for treatment of their health problems including improvement of memory and old age related diseases. Numerous medicinal plants have been described in old literature of Arabic traditional medicine for treatment of Alzheimer’s disease (AD) (or to strengthen memory).</p></sec><sec><title>Methods</title><p>In this study, some of these plants were evaluated against three different preliminary bioassays related to AD to explore the possible way of their bio-interaction. Twenty three selected plants were extracted with methanol and screened <italic toggle="yes">in vitro</italic> against acetylcholinesterase (AChE) and cycloxygenase-1 (COX-1) enzymes. In addition, anti-oxidant activity using DPPH was determined.</p></sec><sec><title>Results</title><p>Of the tested plant extracts; <italic toggle="yes">Adhatoda vasica</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">Peganum harmala</italic> showed inhibitory effect on AChE at IC<sub>50</sub> 294 μg/ml and 68 μg/ml respectively. Moreover, <italic toggle="yes">A. vasica</italic> interacted reversibly with the enzyme while <italic toggle="yes">P. harmala</italic> showed irreversible inhibition. <italic toggle="yes">Ferula assafoetida</italic> (IC<sub>50</sub> 3.2 μg/ml), <italic toggle="yes">Syzygium aromaticum</italic> (34.9 μg/ml) and <italic toggle="yes">Zingiber officinalis</italic> (33.6 μg/ml) showed activity against COX-1 enzyme. Potent radical scavenging activity was demonstrated by three plant extracts <italic toggle="yes">Terminalia chebula</italic> (EC<sub>50</sub> 2.2 μg/ml), <italic toggle="yes">T. arjuna</italic> (3.1 μg/ml) and <italic toggle="yes">Emblica officinalis</italic> (6.3 μg/ml)<italic toggle="yes">.</italic></p></sec><sec><title>Conclusion</title><p>Interestingly, differential results have been obtained which indicate the variability of the mode of actions for the selected plants. Additionally, the reversible interaction of <italic toggle="yes">A. vasica</italic> against AChE and the potent activity of <italic toggle="yes">F. assafoetida</italic> against COX-1 make them effective, new and promising agents for treatment of AD in the future, either as total extracts or their single bioactive constituents.</p></sec></abstract><kwd-group><kwd>Egyptian herbal
