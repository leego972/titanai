Diffuse reflectance spectroscopy (DRS) and UV-Vis-NIR spectroscopy are also two common methods for studying the optical properties of nanostructures that were carried out to investigate the optical properties of Au-H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> NTs and the results are depicted in Fig. <xref rid="Fig5" ref-type="fig">5</xref>a,b, respectively. According to Fig. <xref rid="Fig5" ref-type="fig">5</xref>a, the absorption of Au-H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> NTs contains two peaks centered at 249 and 435 nm, and the first one is more intense. The first absorption peak is related to the absorption of H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> nanotubes and the second is due to the LSPR phenomenon occurring in gold nanoparticles. The lower absorption peak at 435 nm compared to 249 nm can be understood by the lower value of gold in the Au-H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> NTs compared to H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> which was demonstrated by the EDX analysis results (0.01% of Au and 79.12% of Ti in the sample).</p><p id="Par26">Also, the sample reflectance in Fig. <xref rid="Fig5" ref-type="fig">5</xref>b shows that the reflectance of the Au-H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> NTs in the visible region is about 80%. In addition, significant low reflections are observed around the wavelengths of 200 nm (R∼40%) and 1.02 microns (R∼65%) which are mentioned in the inset of Fig. <xref rid="Fig5" ref-type="fig">5</xref>b.
