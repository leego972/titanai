These converging mechanisms accelerate neuronal vulnerability, promote dendritic degeneration within the hippocampus, and ultimately lead to deficits in cognitive performance [<xref rid="B5-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">5</xref>].</p><p>Enhancing the body’s ability to adapt to physiological stress and maintain internal equilibrium has become an important area of interest in herbal research and integrative health sciences [<xref rid="B6-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">6</xref>,<xref rid="B7-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">7</xref>]. Several plant-derived compounds are recognized as adaptogens—substances that enhance resistance to physical, chemical, or biological stressors and support the maintenance of homeostasis [<xref rid="B8-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>]. At the molecular level, adaptogens exert their effects through multiple mechanisms, including modulation of the HPA axis, regulation of immune responses, reduction in neuroinflammation, reinforcement of endogenous antioxidant systems, and preservation of neuronal integrity [<xref rid="B8-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>,<xref rid="B9-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">9</xref>]. These modern concepts parallel the principles of traditional Thai medicine, which emphasize the balance of the three fundamental elements, <italic toggle="yes">Tri-dhatu</italic>—<italic toggle="yes">Vata</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Pitta</italic>, and <italic toggle="yes">Sleshma</italic>. Disruption of this balance is traditionally associated with stress-induced deterioration of physiological and mental functions [<xref rid="B10-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">10</xref>]. The restoration of elemental harmony forms the basis of traditional rejuvenating remedies known as “Ya Aayu-Wattana” [<xref rid="B10-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">10</xref>,<xref rid="B11-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">11</xref>]. AYW-KK-04 is derived from this concept, providing an ethnopharmacological rationale for evaluating its adaptogenic and neuroprotective effects against chronic stress-induced cognitive impairment.</p><p>The AYW-KK-04 formulation was developed based on these principles by selecting herbal components known to support elemental balance, strengthen the body, and contribute to antioxidative and immunomodulatory activity. AYW-KK-04 consists of 22 herbal ingredients as shown in <xref rid="pharmaceuticals-19-00339-t001" ref-type="table">Table 1</xref>, most of which are listed in the Thai National List of Essential Medicines for traditional remedies. A key component of the formulation is Triphala—a combination of <italic toggle="yes">Terminalia chebula</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Terminalia bellirica</italic>, and <italic toggle="yes">Phyllanthus emblica</italic>—which accounts for approximately 26.5% of the formulation. Experimental studies in animal models have shown that Triphala exhibits strong anti-stress and antioxidative effects, including the reduction in lipid peroxidase (LPO) levels, an important indicator of oxidative damage [<xref rid="B12-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">12</xref>,<xref rid="B13-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">13</xref>,<xref rid="B14-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">14</xref>]. Additionally, individual constituents of Triphala demonstrate significant neuroprotective and cognitive-enhancing properties; <italic toggle="yes">Phyllanthus emblica</italic> has been reported to exert antioxidant and neuroprotective effects on neuronal integrityand function [<xref rid="B15-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">15</xref>], and <italic toggle="yes">Terminalia chebula</italic> has been shown to alleviate cognitive impairment through modulation of oxidative stress and neuroinflammation [<xref rid="B16-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">16</xref>]. Moreover, aqueous extract of <italic toggle="yes">Terminalia bellirica</italic> fruit has been observed to improve cognitive performance in rodent models, potentially via its antioxidant and neuroprotective actions [<xref rid="B17-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">17</xref>]. Other key component in the formulation is Trikatu-a combination of <italic toggle="yes">Zingiber officinale</italic> Roscoe., <italic toggle="yes">Piper retrofractum</italic> Vahl., and <italic toggle="yes">Piper nigrum</italic> L., which accounts for approximately 16.07% of the remedy, has also been reported to possess antioxidant properties [<xref rid="B18-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">18</xref>,<xref rid="B19-pharmaceuticals-19-00339" ref-type="bibr">19</xref>].</p><p>These components collectively suggest that AYW-KK-04 may exert broad biological activity through the reduction in reactive oxygen species (ROS), the enhancement of endogenous antioxidant enzyme systems, the protection of cellular structures from oxidative injury, and the support of fundamental physiological energy processes.
