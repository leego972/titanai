Description:
Formate is a monocarboxylic acid anion that is the conjugate base of formic acid. Induces severe metabolic acidosis and ocular injury in human subjects. It has a role as a Saccharomyces cerevisiae metabolite and a human metabolite. It is a conjugate base of a formic acid.
