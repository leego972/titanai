Question: <p>I am looking to do some tinkering with openGL and Python and haven't been able to find good reasons for using PyOpenGl versus pyglet</p>

<p>Which would you recommend and why?</p>

Answer: <p>I'd say that Pyglet is actually more evolved than PyOpenGL. It has a nice API of it's own, and it has a full wrapper around OpenGL accessed through the pyglet.gl module! PyOpenGL doesn't even wrap all the functions OpenGL has.
Pyglet also has a great library for rendering 2D with hardware acceleration through OpenGL, and it's really well made.</p>

<p>If you want a powerful ready made 3D engine you've got <a href="http://www.ogre3d.org/" rel="nofollow">Ogre</a> and such</p>
