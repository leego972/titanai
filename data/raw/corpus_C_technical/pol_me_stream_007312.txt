Mohajer-6 

The Mohajer-6 () retains the same form as prior Mohajer versions, but is significantly larger. It is capable of carrying guided bombs and more advanced surveillance equipment. It has been used in combat by Iran and exported to several Iranian allies. See the main article for more details.
