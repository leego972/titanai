# Ediglê

Ediglê Quaresma Farias (born 13 May 1978), simply known as Ediglê, is a Brazilian footballer who plays for São Raimundo as a central defender.

Ediglê has previously played for Internacional in the Campeonato Brasileiro. He also spent one season in the Primeira Liga playing for Marítimo.
