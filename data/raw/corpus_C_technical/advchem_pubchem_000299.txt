Description:
Anabasine is a pyridine alkaloid that is pyridine substituted by a piperidin-2-yl group at position 3. It has a role as a nicotinic acetylcholine receptor agonist, a teratogenic agent and a plant metabolite. It is a piperidine alkaloid and a pyridine alkaloid.
