Question: <p>How can I output colored text to the terminal, in Python?
What is the best Unicode symbol to represent a solid block?</p>

Answer: <p>I ended up doing this, I felt it was cleanest: </p>

<pre><code>formatters = {             
    'RED': '\033[91m',     
    'GREEN': '\033[92m',   
    'END': '\033[0m',      
}

print 'Master is currently {RED}red{END}!'.format(**formatters)
print 'Help make master {GREEN}green{END} again!'.format(**formatters)
</code></pre>
