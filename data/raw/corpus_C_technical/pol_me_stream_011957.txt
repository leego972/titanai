# Paweł Sasin

Paweł Sasin (born 2 October 1983 in Radom) is a Polish professional footballer who plays as a full-back or a defensive midfielder for III liga club Ślęza Wrocław.

Career

Club
In February 2011, he was released from Cracovia.

After leaving Cracovia, he joined Górnik Łęczna.

In July 2011, he signed a contract with Warta Poznań and moved to ŁKS Łódź on 10 January 2012.

National team
Sasin is a former member of the Poland U21.

Honours
Górnik Łęczna
 II liga: 2019–20
