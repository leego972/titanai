# Robert Torrens (economist)

orrens Island, in 1837. The explorer Edward Eyre named the large salt lake in the north of the colony Lake Torrens in 1839, and the suburb of Torrensville and the districts of East and West Torrens also commemorated Robert Torrens.

Torrens Park, however, was named after his son, Sir Robert Torrens.
