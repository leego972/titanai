In Alzheimer’s disease models, schisandrin B inhibits β-amyloid accumulation through suppression of the RAGE/NF-κB/MAPK signaling pathway and by increasing the expression of Hsp proteins and beclin-1, key markers of autophagy [<xref rid="B16-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">16</xref>].</p><p><italic toggle="yes">S. chinensis</italic>, including schisandrin B, influences the PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling pathway, modulating autophagy and conferring protection against atherosclerosis through the reduction of oxidative stress and inflammation [<xref rid="B17-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">17</xref>,<xref rid="B18-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">18</xref>]. Schisandrin C from <italic toggle="yes">S. chinensis</italic> attenuates indomethacin-induced intestinal injury via this pathway, decreasing apoptosis and improving intestinal barrier integrity [<xref rid="B19-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">19</xref>]. In Huntington’s disease models, <italic toggle="yes">S. chinensis</italic> seed extracts reduce disease symptoms by activating Nrf2 and inhibiting NF-κB/MAPK signaling, thereby limiting microglial migration [<xref rid="B20-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">20</xref>].</p><p>Network pharmacology studies have demonstrated that <italic toggle="yes">S. chinensis</italic> exerts its effects through the AGEs/RAGE pathway, leading to attenuation of inflammatory responses in diabetic kidney disease (DKD) [<xref rid="B21-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">21</xref>]. A mixture of <italic toggle="yes">S. chinensis</italic> fruits modulates the expression of RAGE, NF-κB, and pro-inflammatory cytokine genes, as confirmed by molecular docking and experimental validation [<xref rid="B21-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">21</xref>]. This mixture improves renal function by reducing fibrosis and oxidative stress in DKD models [<xref rid="B21-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">21</xref>].</p><p>Schisandrin B protects dopaminergic neurons against 6-hydroxydopamine (6-OHDA)-induced toxicity by limiting endoplasmic reticulum stress related to protein misfolding and by activating sirtuin 1 (SIRT1) [<xref rid="B22-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">22</xref>]. In oxidative stress models, schisandrin B increases the survival of PC12 cells through inhibition of NF-κB and activation of Nrf2 signaling [<xref rid="B16-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">16</xref>]. Clinically, <italic toggle="yes">S. chinensis</italic> extracts have been shown to improve cognitive performance and reduce fatigue in randomized clinical trials [<xref rid="B14-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">14</xref>].</p><p><italic toggle="yes">S. chinensis</italic> also exhibits cardioprotective effects mediated by its lignans, including schisandrin B, which reduce oxidative stress and inflammation in myocardial tissue [<xref rid="B23-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">23</xref>]. In obesity models, schisandrin B prevents skeletal muscle atrophy through regulation of the AMPK/mTOR pathway [<xref rid="B24-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">24</xref>]. Collectively, these findings underscore the pleiotropic adaptogenic properties of <italic toggle="yes">Schisandra chinensis</italic> [<xref rid="B3-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>].</p></sec><sec id="sec3dot3-nutrients-18-00931"><title>3.3. Withania somnifera (L.) Dunal</title><p><italic toggle="yes">Withania somnifera</italic> (L.) Dunal, commonly known as ashwagandha, Indian ginseng, or winter cherry, is one of the most important medicinal plants in Ayurvedic medicine. It has traditionally been used as a <italic toggle="yes">rasayana</italic> (rejuvenating tonic) to promote vitality, stress resilience, and longevity (<xref rid="nutrients-18-00931-f003" ref-type="fig">Figure 3</xref>) [<xref rid="B25-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">25</xref>,<xref rid="B26-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">26</xref>]. Ashwagandha contains withanolides as its principal bioactive constituents responsible for adaptogenic activity, including withaferin A, withanolide A, withanolide D, and sitoindosides, which exhibit broad and multitarget pharmacological effects [<xref rid="B27-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">27</xref>,<xref rid="B28-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">28</xref>,<xref rid="B29-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">29</xref>]. Withanolides are steroidal lactones with an ergostane skeleton composed of 28 carbon atoms, derived from the mevalonate pathway and steroid biosynthesis [<xref rid="B30-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">30</xref>].</p><p>Withaferin A, the most extensively studied withanolide, displays potent neuroprotective, anticancer, anti-inflammatory, and stress-modulating properties through its effects on multiple signaling pathways [<xref rid="B27-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">27</xref>,<xref rid="B31-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">31</xref>,<xref rid="B32-nutrients-18-00931" ref-type="bibr">32</xref>].
