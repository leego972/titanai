Description:
Secobarbital is a member of the class of barbiturates that is barbituric acid in which the hydrogens at position 5 are substituted by prop-2-en-1-yl and pentan-2-yl groups. It has a role as a sedative, a GABA modulator and an anaesthesia adjuvant. It is functionally related to a barbituric acid. It is a conjugate acid of a secobarbital(1-).
