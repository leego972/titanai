</p></sec><sec id="Sec17"><title>3.7.2.2 Prostate cancer (LNCap cells)</title><p id="Par54">In this section, the effects of different lasers including NIR (808 nm) and green (532 nm) lasers are investigated on embedded Au-H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> NTs in prostate cancer cells (LNCap cells), and the efficiencies of the proposed prostate cancer treatment are studied.</p></sec><sec id="Sec18"><title>NIR laser</title><p id="Par55">NIR laser at a wavelength of 808 nm, power of 2 W, and fluence of 214.2 J/cm<sup>2</sup> was applied on Au-H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> NTs -embedded LNCap cells, and the cells incubated at 37 °C for 24 h. Then, the cell viability assay using crystal violet was recorded, and the results are shown in Fig. <xref rid="Fig12" ref-type="fig">12</xref>a. Initial observations suggest that exposing a high-power NIR laser on Au-H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> NTs surrounded by LNCap cells has no significant effects on the cancer cells, as evidenced by the nearly unchanged cell viability percentages. This phenomenon can be explained by the high diffuse reflection (80.9%) and low light absorbance (0.07) of the NTs at 808 nm. However, taking a closer look and comparing the results with Fig. <xref rid="Fig9" ref-type="fig">9</xref>b reveals that the cell viability% of LNCap cells by irradiated Au-H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> NTs-embedded LNCap cells (95.8%, 93.1%, 89.4%, and 74.9% for NTs concentration of 62.5, 125, 250, and 500 µg/mL) is even higher than the viability% of Au-H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> NTs-embedded LNCap cells in the dark condition (76.1%, 69.3%, 67.5%, and 59.2% for NTs concentration of 62.5, 125, 250, and 500 µg/mL), where cancer cells are just incubated with Au-H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> NTs without applying any light. Therefore, in this process, two competing phenomena emerge, one leading to a decline in the viability of LNCap cells while the other contributes to its enhancement.</p><p id="Par56">For a better and more comprehensive understanding of the phenomena, a parallel experiment was conducted using the H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> nanotubes previously introduced in reference<sup><xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR2">2</xref></sup>, and the results are depicted in Fig. <xref rid="Fig12" ref-type="fig">12</xref>a. Cell viability% of LNCap cells in the vicinity of irradiated Au-H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> NTs-embedded (85.2%, 86.0%, 86.8%, and 82.9% for H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> NTs concentration of 62.5, 125, 250, and 500 µg/mL) is higher than its viability% in the vicinity H<sub>2</sub>Ti<sub>3</sub>O<sub>7</sub> NTs in dark condition in reference<sup><xref ref-type="bibr" rid="CR2">2</xref></sup>, too.
