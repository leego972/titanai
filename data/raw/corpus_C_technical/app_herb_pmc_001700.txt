The two-component PCA model explained 47.6% of the total X-variance (R<sup>2</sup>X [1] = 0.289; R<sup>2</sup>X [2] = 0.187; R<sup>2</sup>X(cum) = 0.476), indicating that nearly half of the metabolomics variation was captured by these principal components. The Q<sup>2</sup> (cum) was slightly negative (−0.124), suggesting limited predictive ability, likely due to the small sample size, with high variable features. However, as this is for unsupervised determination targeted for pattern recognition rather than prediction, the low value is acceptable (<xref rid="ref-14" ref-type="bibr">Debik et al., 2022</xref>). For the PLS biplot model that linked metabolite features to bioactivity data, it achieved a strong fit (R<sup>2</sup>Y(cum) ≈ 0.91) but low predictive ability (Q<sup>2</sup> cum = 0.0599, 7-fold CV). In addition, permutation testing (<italic toggle="yes">n</italic> = 200) yielded near-zero R<sup>2</sup>/Q<sup>2</sup> intercepts, suggesting that the observed fit is not due to chance. However, the modest balance accuracy and cross-validated confusion matrix were consistent with the low Q<sup>2</sup>. Thus, the PLS-DA model was interpreted as exploratory, using only VIP &gt; 1 to nominate candidates. Consequently, the biological conclusions were grounded on quantified/standard-confirmed metabolites and consistent bioactivity assay trends.</p></sec></sec><sec sec-type="discussion"><title>Discussion</title><p>The findings in this study are consistent with those reported by <xref rid="ref-31" ref-type="bibr">Nobossé, Fombang &amp; Mbofung (2018)</xref>, who observed significantly higher total flavonoid content (<italic toggle="yes">p</italic> &lt; 0.05) in day-60 <italic toggle="yes">M. oleifera</italic> leaves (1.82 ± 0.07 g CE/100 g DM) compared to day-30 leaves (1.40 ± 0.04 g CE/100 g DM). Similarly, the total phenolic content was lowest in day-30 leaves (3.32 ± 0.37 g CE/100 g DM) and increased significantly by day-60 (3.97 g CE/100 g DM). The variation in results between their study and our study may be attributed to differences in extraction methods in which they have used aqueous, ethanol, and methanol extractions, while this study used only ethanol.
