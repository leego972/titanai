Answer: <p>While wxPython is unavailable for the reasons listed by @Ali, you may want to take a look at <a href="http://wxnet.sourceforge.net/">wx.NET</a>. You could use IronPython to call these assemblies instead, and it should be cross-platform (I'm assuming that's what you're after, or you would just use WinForms).
