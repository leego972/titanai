Graphene, a two-dimensional (2D) layered material has attracted much attention 
from the scientific community due to its exceptional electrical, thermal, 
mechanical, biological and optical properties. Hence, numerous applications 
utilizing graphene-based materials could be conceived in next-generation 
electronics, chemical and biological sensing, energy conversion and storage, and 
beyond. The interaction between graphene surfaces with other materials plays a 
vital role in influencing its properties than other bulk materials. In this 
review, we outline the recent progress in the production of graphene and related 
2D materials, and their uses in energy conversion (solar cells, fuel cells), 
energy storage (batteries, supercapacitors) and biomedical applications.
