Title: From Grievances to Guarantees: Getting to Yes in the Iran Nuclear Talks

Abstract:
Iran insists on guarantees that what happened with Trump will never happen again-a subsequent Administration will not be able to break the deal, reimposing sanctions.  The challenge is that, under US law, even if a revived JCPOA were to be approved by the US Senate as a treaty (politically impossible under the current configuration in the Senate) it could not stop subsequent Congressional action inconsistent with the US commitments.
