# Municipal District of Ranchland No. 66

The Municipal District of Ranchland No. 66 is a municipal district (MD) in southwest Alberta, Canada. Containing no urban communities of any kind, its municipal office is located in Chain Lakes Provincial Park, approximately  southwest of the Town of Nanton. Ranchland's rugged foothill terrain is unsuited for the crop agriculture practiced elsewhere in Alberta, and ranching remains the single livelihood of its inhabitants. With a 2016 population of 92, it is the least populated municipal district in Alberta, and its population density of 29 square kilometres for every resident is almost exactly equal to that of the Northwest Territories.

History 
The MD was incorporated in 1995 from the Improvement District No. 6.

Geography 
Administration Building

The MD of Ranchland administration building is located at the junction of Highway 22 and Highway 533.

Communities 
There are no communities located within the MD of Ranchland No. 66.
