Answer: <p>Just updating an old question. DBSprockets has been replaced by <a href="http://sprox.org/" rel="nofollow">sprox</a> which learns a lot of lessons from it and is pretty cool.</p>

<p>It isn't quite the throwaway 'scaffolding' that Rails provides, it is more like an agile form generation tool that is highly extensible.</p>
