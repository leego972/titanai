Description:
Apraclonidine is an imidazoline that is 2-amino 4,5-dihydro-1H-imidazoline in which one of the exocyclic amino hydrogens has been replaced by a 4-amino-2,6-dichlorophenyl group. It has a role as a diagnostic agent, a beta-adrenergic agonist, an alpha-adrenergic agonist, an antiglaucoma drug and an ophthalmology drug. It is a dichlorobenzene, a member of guanidines and a member of imidazolines. It is a conjugate base of an apraclonidine(1+).
