An evidence-based systematic review of beta-glucan by the natural standard 
research collaboration.

Ulbricht C.

An evidence-based systematic review of beta-glucan by the Natural Standard 
Research Collaboration consolidates the safety and efficacy data available in 
the scientific literature using a validated, reproducible grading rationale. 
This article includes written and statistical analysis of clinical trials, plus 
a compilation of expert opinion, folkloric precedent, history, pharmacology, 
kinetics/dynamics, interactions, adverse effects, toxicology, and dosing.
