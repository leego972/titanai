Question: <p>What is the best python framework to create distributed applications? For example to build a P2P app.</p>

Answer: <p>If it's something where you're going to need tons of threads and need better concurrent performance, check out <a href="http://www.stackless.com/" rel="nofollow">Stackless Python</a>. Otherwise you could just use the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SOAP" rel="nofollow">SOAP</a> or <a href="http://www.xmlrpc.com/" rel="nofollow">XML-RPC</a> protocols. In response to Ben's post, if you don't want to look over the BitTorrent source, you could just look at the article on <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BitTorrent_(protocol)" rel="nofollow">the BitTorrent protocol</a>.</p>
