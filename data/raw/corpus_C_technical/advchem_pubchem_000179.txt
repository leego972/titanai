CID: 362
Source: ChEBI

Description:
1,2-dihydronaphthalene-1,2-diol is a member of the class of naphthalenediols that is 1,2-dihydronaphthalene substituted by hydroxy groups at positions 1 and 2 respectively. It has a role as a mouse metabolite and a bacterial xenobiotic metabolite. It is a member of naphthalenediols and a member of dihydronaphthalenes. It is functionally related to a naphthalene-1,2-diol. It derives from a hydride of a 1,2-dihydronaphthalene.
