Theater
Since 2011, the Vosto5 Theater in Prague has been presenting an action historical fiction called . The plot is placed in the real historical context of the period from the arrival of Reich Protector Reinhard Heydrich to Prague, until his assassination. In this production, Vosto5 combines elements of martial arts and extreme sports with a typical theater poetics and its verbal humor.

Videogames
In August 2023, a platform video game Pérák a legendy Prahy was released on Microsoft Windows.
