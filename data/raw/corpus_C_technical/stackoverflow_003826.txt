Question: <p>Can I run the python interpreter without generating the compiled .pyc files?</p>

Answer: <p>Super-dumb solution but I thought I'd post it anyway ;)</p>

<p>Add this your your ~.bash_rc:</p>

<pre><code>alias lv = 'rm *.pyc &amp;&amp; ls'
</code></pre>

<p>EDIT:</p>

<p>I also found this works.</p>

<p><code>#!/usr/bin/env python -B</code></p>
