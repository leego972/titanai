In addition, given the increasing number of OCTA systems, cross-platform agreements are needed for sublayer segmentation.<sup><xref rid="bibr19-2515841419840249" ref-type="bibr">19</xref></sup> Advances in software and hardware are necessary to better correct for motion and projection artifacts.
