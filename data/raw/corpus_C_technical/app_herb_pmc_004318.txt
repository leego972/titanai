# PMC: adaptogen stress response HPA axis — Overview

ref-type="bibr">184</xref>,<xref rid="B185-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">185</xref>] reported a somewhat unexpected finding in that there was a stimulatory effect in schizophrenic patients and chronic alcoholics. This was due to the ability of the <italic toggle="yes">S.</italic> <italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> extract to increase the reactivity to insulin, sulphadiazine and apomorphine. Thus, administration of a <italic toggle="yes">S.</italic> <italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> preparation, which was used in conjunction with apomorphine, frequently eliminated or decreased addiction to apomorphine. The authors noted development of a stable conditional emetic reflex in most of the chronic alcoholics at the end of the treatment [<xref rid="B184-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">184</xref>,<xref rid="B185-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">185</xref>,<xref rid="B186-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">186</xref>]. It was also found that <italic toggle="yes">S.</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> treatment increased associative processes in the subjects and improved the quality of associations, as demonstrated by the increased number of higher verbal responses and a decrease in the number of lower responses [<xref rid="B187-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">187</xref>].</p><p>It was also reported that <italic toggle="yes">Schisandra</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> extract had a positive effect in an open, uncontroled study on 386 patients with nervous and/or mental disorders of borderline exogenous-organic genesis (stress induced mild depression) [<xref rid="B188-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">188</xref>]. These positive therapeutic effects were the result of a stimulating action and a prevention, or reduction, of development of side effects associated with treatment with tranquilizers and anti-depressants. Furthermore, the combination of <italic toggle="yes">Schisandra</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> extract with nootropic preparations (e.g., nootropil, pyracetam) was found to be effective in patients with pronounced mild cognitive (mnestico-intellectual) disorders. Although, in patients with agitative dementia the <italic toggle="yes">Schisandra</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> therapy was less effective [<xref rid="B188-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">188</xref>]. Another observation, which could be of practical future importance, is that a combination of <italic toggle="yes">S.</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> therapy with tranquilizers or anti-depressants (e.g<italic toggle="yes">.</italic>, amitriptyline, relanium) may be very effective in the elimination of the undesirable effects of these drugs. For example, the development of side effects (e.g., headaches, dizziness, flaccidity, dryness in the mouth, and urination disorders) were observed in 23 out of 39 patients (53.5%) with neuro-mental disorders of exogenous-organic genesis, which when treated with increased doses of amitriptyline (from 50 to 75 mg). However, patients treated with <italic toggle="yes">S.</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> experienced a similar effect in only 4 out of 172 patients (1.9%). Thus, a combined administration of tranquilisers and adaptogens permitted the usage of optimal doses of these drugs in 96% of patients, whilst it was only possible in 16% of control patients (p &lt; 0.001). It has been concluded that:

<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> decrease fatigue, improve the general mood and appetite, and can be recommended as a tonic for healthy people in a state of fatigue. No negative effects were observed on the somatic state of patients.</p></list-item><list-item><label>(iii)</label><p>S. <italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> extract can be used in the treatment of psychoses as a stimulant without harmful side effects.</p></list-item><list-item><label>(iv)</label><p>the curative effect of <italic toggle="yes">S.</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> preparations is pronounced in cases of asthenic and depressive syndromes.</p></list-item><list-item><label>(v)</label><p>the combination of <italic toggle="yes">S.</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> therapy with tranquilisers or anti-depressants eliminates the side effects of these drugs and allows them to be employed at optimal doses [<xref rid="B188-pharmaceuticals-03-00188" ref-type="bibr">188</xref>].</p></list-item></list></p><p>We may suggest, based on the reported studies, that <italic toggle="yes">Schisandra</italic>
<italic toggle="yes">chinensis</italic> have a place in the treatment of schizophrenia in reduction of catatonia and negative symptoms, relief of fatigue, and amelioration of side effects from neurotropc and other sedating medications.
