Question: <p>Given:</p>

<pre><code>a = 1
b = 10
c = 100
</code></pre>

<p>I want to display a leading zero for all numbers with less than 2 digits, i.e.:</p>

<pre><code>01
10
100
</code></pre>

Answer: <pre><code>width = 5
num = 3
formatted = (width - len(str(num))) * "0" + str(num)
print formatted
</code></pre>
