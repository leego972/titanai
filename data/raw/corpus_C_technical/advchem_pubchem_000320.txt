Description:
Atenolol is an ethanolamine compound having a (4-carbamoylmethylphenoxy)methyl group at the 1-position and an N-isopropyl substituent. It has a role as a beta-adrenergic antagonist, an antihypertensive agent, a xenobiotic, an anti-arrhythmia drug, a sympatholytic agent and an environmental contaminant. It is a member of ethanolamines, a monocarboxylic acid amide and a propanolamine.
