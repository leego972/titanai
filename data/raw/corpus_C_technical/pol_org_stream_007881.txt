# Len Munsil

Republican primary election on September 12. Munsil received 51% of the vote, while Goldwater garnered 40%. Mike Harris earned 6% and Gary Tupper mustered 4%.

In the general election, just 8 weeks later, Munsil was defeated by incumbent governor Janet Napolitano by a 63% to 35% margin.
