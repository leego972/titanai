xlink:href="IJPharm-51-150.pdf"/><abstract><sec id="st1"><title>OBJECTIVES:</title><p>The objective was to study the adaptogenic effect of <italic toggle="yes">Chlorophytum borivilianum</italic> (CB) on physical performance when exposed to accustomed activity (AA) and unaccustomed activity (UA) and the effect of CB on heart rate (HR) and blood pressure (BP) in the presence of UA and the effect on muscle strength in comparison with placebo.</p></sec><sec id="st2"><title>MATERIALS AND METHODS:</title><p>A placebo-controlled, double-blind clinical study was initiated after ethics committee approval in healthy volunteers with fixed-dose regimen. Consented volunteers (<italic toggle="yes">n</italic> = 60) were assigned randomly into two groups of study drug (3 g/day) or placebo (3 g/day) for 2 months. They were evaluated at days 0, 30, and 60 with physical stressors (6-min walk test [6MWT] – distance, HR, and BP; 6-min exercise test [6ETC] – distance, maximum and average speed; and fixed workload test [FWT] – systolic BP, diastolic BP [DBP], and HR on cycle ergometer) and Jammar's dynamometer test for handgrip strength test on each visit.</p></sec><sec id="st3"><title>RESULTS:</title><p>In 6MWT in CB group, distance on day 30 (456 ± 42.1) and day 60 (468.3 ± 0.4) was significantly increased when compared with day 30 (422.6 ± 45.7) and day 60 (419.6 ± 45.1) of the placebo group. On day 60, distance in 6ETC in CB group was 2.92 ± 0.6 which was significantly more than that of placebo group 2.4 ± 0.6. On day 60, in FWT, DBP in the CB group was 75.8 ± 4.4 which was significantly low compared to the placebo group 82.4 ± 7.4 (<italic toggle="yes">P</italic> &lt; 0.05).</p></sec><sec id="st4"><title>CONCLUSION:</title><p>CB increased physical performance when exposed to AA even after one month and in case of UA increase in performance was seen when CB was administered for two months thus validating its adaptogenic (anti-stress) potential.</p></sec></abstract><kwd-group><kwd>Adaptogenic activity</kwd><kwd>cycle ergometer</kwd><kwd>safed
