Question: <p>What is the Python equivalent of Perl's <code>chomp</code> function, which removes the last character of a value?</p>

Answer: <p>If you are concerned about speed (say you have a looong list of strings) and you know the nature of the newline char, string slicing is actually faster than rstrip. A little test to illustrate this:</p>

<pre><code>import time

loops = 50000000

def method1(loops=loops):
    test_string = 'num\n'
    t0 = time.time()
    for num in xrange(loops):
        out_sting = test_string[:-1]
    t1 = time.time()
    print('Method 1: ' + str(t1 - t0))

def method2(loops=loops):
    test_string = 'num\n'
    t0 = time.time()
    for num in xrange(loops):
        out_sting = test_string.rstrip()
    t1 = time.time()
    print('Method 2: ' + str(t1 - t0))

method1()
method2()
</code></pre>

<p>Output:</p>

<pre><code>Method 1: 3.92700004578
Method 2: 6.73000001907
</code></pre>
