Antivirus activity</title><p>Salidroside provided protection against coxsackievirus B3, which causes viral myocarditis, in both <italic toggle="yes">in vitro</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">in vivo</italic> experiments [<xref rid="b99-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">99</xref>]. The IC<sub>50</sub> of salidroside for coxsackievirus B3 is 39.0 ± 1.2 mg/L. Salidroside can modulate the mRNA expression of interferon-g, IL-10, TNF-α, and IL-2 in coxsackievirus B3. Salidroside increased lactic dehydrogenase, aspartate transaminase, and creatine kinase activities in infected BALB/c mouse serum [<xref rid="b100-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">100</xref>]. Salidroside is a potential agent for treating viral myocarditis [<xref rid="b99-jfda-23-03-359" ref-type="bibr">99</xref>].</p></sec><sec sec-type="conclusions"><title>17. Conclusion</title><p><italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola</italic> plants are commonly used in traditional medicines in Asia and Europe. Studies have shown that the plants and their two major constituents, salidroside and tyrosol, exhibit adaptogenic, antifatigue, antidepressant, antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, antinoception, and anticancer bioactivities, modulate immune function, and prevent cardiovascular, neuronal, liver, and skin disorders.</p></sec></body><back><fn-group><fn id="fn1-jfda-23-03-359" fn-type="COI-statement"><p>
