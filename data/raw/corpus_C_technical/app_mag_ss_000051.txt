Title: Design of Low Speed and High Torque External Rotor Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor (PMSM) with Integer Slots

Abstract:
In this paper, the design of an external rotor permanent magnet synchronous motor (ERPMSM) with integer slots at low speed and high torque is studied. Low speed and high torque PMSMs with concentrated fractional slot (FSCW) are widely used for their high efficiency, high power density, and simple winding form. More research attention has been paid to the effects of different slot and pole combinations on torque pulsation, However, there is little research on the impact of integral slots ERPMSM on winding temperature rise and vibration noise. This paper takes a 720kw 76.5RPM low-speed external rotor drum for a coal-mine belt conveyor as an example. Firstly, the influence of different winding forms on motor performance was theoretically analyzed. Secondly, it was calculated and analyzed using finite element electromagnetic and temperature and Fluid fields. Finally, through prototype manufacturing and testing, it was verified that the low-speed high torque ERPMSM has lower winding temperature and vibration noise.
