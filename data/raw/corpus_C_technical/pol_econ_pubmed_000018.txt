Collaborative international research.

Callister LC(1), Getmanenko NI, Khalaf I, Garvrish N, Semenic S, 
Vehvilainen-Julkunen K, Turkina NV.

Author information:
(1)College of Nursing, Brigham Young University, Provo, Utah 84602, USA.

The current age of globalization poses challenges as to how to promote 
professional interaction to further international scholarly efforts in nursing. 
Models of global research are defined. Global health priorities are explicated, 
with recommendations for the conduct of outcomes-focused research leading to the 
generation of cost-effective interventions that contribute to the health and 
well-being of individuals, families, communities, and nations throughout the 
world. The challenges and opportunities in international collaborative nursing 
research are described based on the experiences of the authors during nearly two 
decades of collaborative work. International partnerships in globally relevant 
research, which respect and value equity between nurse researchers practicing in 
developed and developing countries, are essential.
