Guayusa (Ilex guayusa Loes.) Ancestral Plant of Ecuador: History, Traditional 
Uses, Chemistry, Biological Activity, and Potential Industrial Uses.

One of the medicinal plants used in Ecuador that has the best prospects for 
industrialization is guayusa (Ilex guayusa Loes.). This review shows the 
potential of the species, analyzing the ethnobotanical aspects, ancestral uses, 
secondary metabolites, and research. The plant has been consumed for thousands 
of years by the high Amazonian peoples of Ecuador and currently forms part of 
the gardens of ancestral peoples and mestizo settlers. The most relevant 
secondary metabolites that have been investigated are xanthine alkaloids, 
terpenes, and phenolic compounds, while from the pharmacological point of view, 
the following uses stand out: physical and mental stimulants, analgesic, 
antioxidant, antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, anti-diabetic, and phytohormonal. 
The goal of this review is to make known the benefits of guayusa, with the 
purpose of representing a resource that will provide benefits to the Amazonian 
inhabitants in the future.
