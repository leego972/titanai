# Jamila Abdallah Taha al-Shanti

Jamila Abdallah Taha al-Shanti (; 15 March 1955 – 19 October 2023) was a Palestinian politician who was a member of Islamist organisation Hamas and a previous member of the Palestinian Legislative Council. She was killed in an Israeli strike on Gaza on 19 October 2023, during the 2023 Israel–Hamas war.

Early life and education
Jamila al-Shanti was born on 15 March 1955. She held a PhD in English.

Career
Jamila al-Shanti was a member of Hamas and was the founder of the group's women's organisation. She was the most senior woman among Hamas deputies elected in 2006 since she was third on its list. She was the widow of the co-founder of Hamas, Abdel Aziz al-Rantissi, who was assassinated by Israel in April 2004. She worked as a faculty member at the Islamic University in Gaza.

Death
On 19 October 2023, Jamila al-Shanti was killed in an Israeli strike on Gaza during the 2023 Israel–Hamas war. She was the first woman in Hamas Politburo to be killed by Israel.
