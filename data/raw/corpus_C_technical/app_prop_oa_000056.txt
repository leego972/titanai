Title: Detonation pulse engine

Abstract:
Exploratory experiments with an air-breathing propulsion device which&#13;\nuses detonative combustion to achieve high cycle pressures, are reported. In&#13;\nthe first phase, detonation in a chamber containing ethylene and air was&#13;\ninitiated by a detonation produced in a small tube containing ethylene and&#13;\noxygen. An automobile spark system was used for ignition. In the second&#13;\nphase, a repetitive detonation process was sustained in a naturally-aspirated&#13;\nchamber at a frequency of 25Hz. Projections based on the present test results&#13;\nsuggest that frequencies of 150Hz and specific impulse in the range 1000-1400&#13;\nsec might be obtained in a practical engine.
