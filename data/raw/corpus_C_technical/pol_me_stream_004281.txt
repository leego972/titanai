Reception
The film was negatively received. Judge Patrick Naugle of DVD Verdict called it "a pale imitation of the two previous films" and said that the television movie lacked the creative wit and humor of the two feature films.

Home media
The Brady Bunch in the White House was first released on DVD in 2004, in 2011 as part of the A Brady Bunch of Movies collection and again in 2019 as part of The Brady-est Brady Bunch TV & Movie Collection.
