Question: <p>If I have this string:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>2+24*48/32</p>
</blockquote>

<p>what is the most efficient approach for creating this list:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>['2', '+', '24', '*', '48', '/', '32']</p>
</blockquote>

Answer: <blockquote>
  <blockquote>
    <blockquote>
      <p>s = "2+24*48/32"</p>
      
      <p>p = re.compile(r'(\W+)')</p>
      
      <p>p.split(s)</p>
    </blockquote>
  </blockquote>
</blockquote>
