Question: <p>Which is better to use for timing in Python? time.clock() or time.time()? Which one provides more accuracy?</p>

<p>for example:</p>

<pre><code>start = time.clock()
... do something
elapsed = (time.clock() - start)
</code></pre>

<p>vs.</p>

<pre><code>start = time.time()
... do something
elapsed = (time.time() - start)
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>The difference is very platform-specific.</p>

<p>clock() is very different on Windows than on Linux, for example.</p>

<p>For the sort of examples you describe, you probably want the "timeit" module instead.</p>
