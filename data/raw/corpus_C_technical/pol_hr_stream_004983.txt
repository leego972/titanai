# Han (name)

Han is used as a name in many cultures. The Western usage of the name is as a variant of Hans.

 Han (Chinese surname) (韓/韩/Hán), also romanized Hon
 Han (Korean surname) (韓/한)
 Han (given name), a Dutch given name
