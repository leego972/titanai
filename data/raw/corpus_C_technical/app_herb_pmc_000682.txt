# PMC: herbal medicine pharmacology mechanism review — Overview

Among these isolated components, some single flavonoid compounds such as quercitrin, isoquercitrin and quercetin-7-O-α-L-rhamnose are shown to have a variety of bioactivities <italic toggle="yes">in vivo</italic> or <italic toggle="yes">in vitro</italic>, and thereby are thought as the bioactive components of <italic toggle="yes">H. japonicum</italic>. Hence, quality control based on these bioactive components to ensure the effects of <italic toggle="yes">H. japonicum</italic> materials and its related products is urgent and necessary. However, the quality control of <italic toggle="yes">H. japonicum</italic> is still not listed in the Chinese Pharmacopoeia (2010 edition) and other official pharmacopoeias. A number of studies have thus attempted to develop accurate, sensitive and selective analytical methods for qualitative and quantitative evaluation of <italic toggle="yes">H. japonicum</italic>.</p><p>Aiming to provide beneficial information for modern uses and scientific studies of <italic toggle="yes">H. japonicum</italic>, this review summarizes and evaluates the available phytochemical and bioactive properties of <italic toggle="yes">H. japonicum</italic> reported by the literature. Besides, the research progress in the quality evaluation and pharmacokinetics of <italic toggle="yes">H. japonicum</italic> are also presented.</p></sec><sec><title>2.
