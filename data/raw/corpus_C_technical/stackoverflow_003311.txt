Question: <p>Given:</p>

<pre><code>a = 1
b = 10
c = 100
</code></pre>

<p>I want to display a leading zero for all numbers with less than 2 digits, i.e.:</p>

<pre><code>01
10
100
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>Here you are:</p>

<pre><code>print "%02d" % (1,)
</code></pre>

<p>Basically <strong>%</strong> is like <code>printf</code> or <code>sprint</code>.</p>
