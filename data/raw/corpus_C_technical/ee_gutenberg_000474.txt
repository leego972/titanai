CAPITOLIAN; CAPITOLINE Cap`i*to"li*an, Cap"i*to*line, a. Etym: [L. capitolinus: cf. F. capitolin.] Defn: Of or pertaining to the Capitol in Rome. "Capitolian Jove." Macaulay.
