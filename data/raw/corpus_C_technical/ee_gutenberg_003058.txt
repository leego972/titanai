WHOOP Whoop (, n. Etym: [See Hoopoe.] (Zoöl.) Defn: The hoopoe. WHOOP Whoop, v. i. [imp. & p. p. Whooped; p. pr. & vb. n. Whooping.] Etym: [OE. houpen. See Hoop, v. i.] 1.
