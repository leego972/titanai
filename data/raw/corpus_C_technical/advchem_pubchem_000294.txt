Description:
Amoxapine is a dibenzooxazepine compound having a chloro substituent at the 2-position and a piperazin-1-yl group at the 11-position. It has a role as a geroprotector, an antidepressant, an adrenergic uptake inhibitor, a dopaminergic antagonist and a serotonin uptake inhibitor.
