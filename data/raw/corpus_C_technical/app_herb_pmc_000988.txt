Its IR spectrum exhibited characteristic absorption bands for C–H stretching at 2914.8 cm<sup>− 1</sup> and 2848.4 cm<sup>− 1</sup>, a carbonyl group at 1733.8 cm<sup>− 1</sup>, and an olefinic C = C stretch at 1683.9 cm<sup>− 1</sup>. Olefinic carbons resonated at δ<sub>C</sub> 155.2, 143.6, 134.9, and 124.1. An oxygenated carbon appeared at δ<sub>C</sub> 64.1, while the remaining signals, ranging from δ<sub>C</sub> 39.3 to δ<sub>C</sub> 14.0, indicated the presence of a long alkyl chain. This was further supported by a series of multiplets in the upfield region of the <sup>1</sup>H NMR spectrum (δ<sub>H</sub> 2.34–0.88). An aldehydic proton was observed at δ<sub>H</sub> 9.36, and signals at δ<sub>H</sub> 6.44 and 5.12 were attributed to olefinic protons (Supplementary Information, Figure S13).</p></sec><sec id="Sec25"><title>Compound 6 (BL 2)</title><p id="Par67">6, a yellow gum, showed IR absorption bands at 2916.4 cm<sup>− 1</sup> and 2849 cm<sup>− 1</sup> (C–H stretching) and a carbonyl absorption at 1702.4 cm<sup>− 1</sup>.
