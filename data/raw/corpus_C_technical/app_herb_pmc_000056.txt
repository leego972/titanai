Noor <italic toggle="yes">et al.</italic>, investigated that <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> can increase sexual behavior of male rats and the sperm quality; which were found to be dose dependent [<xref rid="B122-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">122</xref>]. One study indicates that <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> exerts proandrogenic effects that enhance the testosterone level [<xref rid="B123-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">123</xref>].</p><p>The <italic toggle="yes">in vivo</italic> effect of aqueous extract of <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> was investigated on body and organ weight as well as functional sperm parameters in terms of safety and efficacy in the management of male infertility, in male rats. Testosterone concentration increased by 30.2%, total sperm concentration, progressive motility and vitality significantly increased, MMP improved markedly by 25.1%, with increased in muscle weight, non-significantly, so it appears that <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> use is safe for possible treatment of male infertility and ageing male problems [<xref rid="B124-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">124</xref>].</p><p>In human studies, Tambi <italic toggle="yes">et al.</italic>, treated a group of patients suffering from late-onset hypogonadism (LOH) with Tongkat ali extract, which showed significantly (<italic toggle="yes">p</italic> &lt; 0.0001) improved the Ageing Males’ Symptoms (AMS) score as well as the serum testosterone concentration. Thus, Tongkat ali extract appears to be useful as a supplement in overcoming the symptoms of LOH and for the management of hypogonadism [<xref rid="B125-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">125</xref>].</p><p>The testosterone deficiency syndrome (TDS), can be characterised by numerous symptoms, including low libido, fatigue, increased fat mass, osteoporosis or erectile dysfunction, and up-to 80% of men have experience some sort of ageing male symptoms. Conventionally, TDS is treated with testosterone replacement therapy (TRT). With the beneficial effects of this therapy, significant adverse effects have been indicated, including prostate cancer. <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> is the herbal alternative to TRT, which has been shown to successfully restore serum testosterone levels, and significantly improve the physical condition and sexual health of patients. Therefore, <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> might be considered a safe alternative to TRT [<xref rid="B126-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">126</xref>].</p><p>For the copulatory activity of sexually sluggish rats, with acute (500, and 1000 mg/kg) and also subacute treatments with <italic toggle="yes">E. longifolia</italic> root powder, significantly reduced ejaculation latencies, and increased the percentage of mounting and ejaculating animals; while the subacute administration reduced post-ejaculatory interval. In case of impotent rats, both treatments increased the percentage of mounting and ejaculating rats. Serum testosterone levels were increased in rats that were treated subacutely, in comparison with control [<xref rid="B127-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">127</xref>].</p><p>One experiment by Ang and Sim showed that <italic toggle="yes">E. Iongifolia</italic> Jack continued to enhance and also maintain a high level of both the total number of successful crossovers, mountings, intromissions and ejaculations during the 9–12th week observation period [<xref rid="B128-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">128</xref>].</p><p>In animal research, an herbal combination containing <italic toggle="yes">Panax quinquefolius</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Eurycoma longifolia</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Epimedium grandiflorum</italic>, <italic toggle="yes">Centella asiatica</italic>, and flower pollen extracts enhanced erectile function [<xref rid="B129-molecules-21-00331" ref-type="bibr">129</xref>].
