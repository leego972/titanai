Antibacterial polyphenol from Erodium glaucophyllum.

Gohar AA(1), Lahloub ME, Niwa M.

Author information:
(1)Pharmacognosy Department, Faculty of Pharmacy, Mansoura University, Mansoura 
35516, Egypt. gohar1952@yahoo.com

Geraniin and gallic acid were isolated from the alcohol extract of the aerial 
parts of Erodium glaucophyllum (Geraniaceae). The identity of the compounds was 
verified through different physical and spectrometric methods. The antibacterial 
and antifungal activity of geraniin is presented.
