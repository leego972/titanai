Question: <p>I want to open a TCP client socket in Python. Do I have to go through all the low-level BSD create-socket-handle / connect-socket stuff or is there a simpler one-line way?</p>

Answer: <p>Opening sockets in python is pretty simple. You really just need something like this:</p>

<pre><code>import socket
sock = socket.socket()
sock.connect((address, port))
</code></pre>

<p>and then you can <code>send()</code> and <code>recv()</code> like any other socket</p>
