# Improbable Cause

released on LaserDisc in Japan on October 2, 1998, in the half-season collection 3rd Season Vol. 2''.  The set included episodes from "Destiny" to "The Adversary" on double sided 12 inch optical discs; the box set had a total runtime of 552 minutes and included audio tracks in English and Japanese.

This episode was released on VHS paired with "Through the Looking Glass".
