Regional de Medicina de São Paulo and by Agência Nacional de Vigilância 
Sanitária (ANVISA). Specialists on behalf of Academia Brasileira de Neurologia 
prepared a critical statement about use of cannabidiol and other cannabis 
derivatives in neurological diseases.
