Question: <p>I've seen a number of postgresql modules for python like pygresql, pypgsql, psyco. Most of them are Python DB API 2.0 compliant, some are not being actively developed anymore.
Which module do you recommend? Why?</p>

Answer: <p>Psycopg1 is known for better performance in heavilyy threaded environments (like web applications) than Psycopg2, although not maintained. Both are well written and rock solid, I'd choose one of these two depending on use case.</p>
