Description:
3-pyridylacetic acid is a monocarboxylic acid that is acetic acid substituted by a (pyridin-3-yl) group. It is a metabolite of nicotine and other tobacco alkaloids. It has a role as a human xenobiotic metabolite. It is a monocarboxylic acid and a member of pyridines.
