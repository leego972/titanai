Question: <p>Assume I have the following list:</p>

<pre><code>foo = ['a', 'b', 'c', 'd', 'e']
</code></pre>

<p>What is the simplest way to retrieve an item at random from this list?</p>

Answer: <p>Use <a href="https://docs.python.org/2/library/random.html#random.choice"><code>random.choice</code></a>:</p>

<pre><code>import random

foo = ['a', 'b', 'c', 'd', 'e']
print(random.choice(foo))
</code></pre>
