Question: <p>I ported gchecky to the google app engine. you can <a href="http://web2py.appspot.com/plugin_checkout" rel="nofollow">try it here</a></p>

<p>It implements both level 1 (cart submission) and level 2 (notifications from google checkout).</p>

<p>Is there any other payment option that works on the google app engine (paypal for example) and supports level 2 (notifications)?</p>

Answer: <p>Paypal has a <a href="https://cms.paypal.com/us/cgi-bin/?cmd=_render-content&amp;content_ID=developer/e_howto_api_soap_PayPalSOAPAPIArchitecture" rel="nofollow">SOAP interface</a>. You can certainly access that from within GAE--though you might run into timeout issues while waiting for the response.</p>
