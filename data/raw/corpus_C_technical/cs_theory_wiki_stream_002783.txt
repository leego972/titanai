# List of television stations in the San Francisco Bay Area

The San Francisco Bay Area is currently defined by Nielsen Media Research as the sixth-largest television market in the United States, with all of the major U.S. television networks having affiliates serving the region. All but one of the major U.S. television networks are directly owned by the networks; the one exception is secondary MyNetworkTV affiliate KRON-TV (now The CW), which is owned by Nexstar Media Group.
