Question: <p>I'm running <code>Django 1.0</code> and I'm close to deploying my app.  As such, I'll be changing the DEBUG setting to False.</p>

<p>With that being said, I'd still like to include the stacktrace on my 500.html page when errors occur.  By doing so, users can copy-and-paste the errors and easily email them to the developers.</p>

<p>Any thoughts on how best to approach this issue?</p>

Answer: <p>You could call <code>sys.exc_info()</code> in a custom exception handler.  But I don't recommend that.  Django can send you emails for exceptions.</p>
