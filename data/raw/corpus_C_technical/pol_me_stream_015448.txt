# Aghvan Mkrtchyan

Aghvan Varazdati Mkrtchyan (, born 27 February 1981 in Yerevan, Soviet Union) is an Armenian former professional footballer who played as a defensive midfielder. He was a member of the Armenia national team, making in 44 appearances and scoring 1 goal after his debut in away friendly match against Andorra on 7 June 2002.
