# Kenny Adeleke

rkish Basketball First League appearing in 19 games. Averaging 14 points and 14 rebounds, the highest rebounding average that season in European Competition. In early March 2017, he signed with Lebanese club Hekmeh BC scoring 20 points and 17 rebounds against Lebanese champion Al Riyadi Club Beirut.

In 2017, he played 11 games with Fuerza Regia of the Mexican LNBP. In January 2018, he signed with CD Español de Talca of the Liga Nacional de Básquetbol de Chile.

Personal life
His name "Adeleke" means "the crown achieves happiness" in Yoruba.
