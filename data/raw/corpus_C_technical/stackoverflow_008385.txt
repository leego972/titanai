Question: <p>Is there a way to access a list(or tuple, or other iterable)'s next, or previous element while looping through with for loop?</p>

<pre><code>l=[1,2,3]
for item in l:
    if item==2:
        get_previous(l,item)
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>Not very pythonic, but gets it done and is simple:</p>

<pre><code>l=[1,2,3]
for index in range(len(l)):
    if l[index]==2:
        l[index-1]
</code></pre>
