Source: ChEBI

Description:
2-aminoacetaldehyde is an amino aldehyde that is acetaldehyde in which one of the hydrogens of the methyl group has been replaced by an amino group. It has a role as an Escherichia coli metabolite. It is an amino aldehyde and an omega-aminoaldehyde. It is a conjugate base of an ammonioacetaldehyde.
