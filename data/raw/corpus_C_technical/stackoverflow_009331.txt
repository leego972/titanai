<ul>
<li>Do you have a particular Vim plugin that you use and like?</li>

Answer: <p>Personally I can't convince myself to litter my code with the markers. I've become pretty used to (and efficient) at using indent-folding. Together with my mapping of space bar (see below) to open/close folds and the zR and zM commands, I'm right at home. Perfect for Python!</p>

<blockquote>
  <p><code>nnoremap &lt;space&gt; za</code></p>
  
  <p><code>vnoremap &lt;space&gt; zf</code></p>
</blockquote>
