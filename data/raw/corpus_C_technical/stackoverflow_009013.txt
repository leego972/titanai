Question: <p>I'm trying to format numbers. Examples:</p>

<pre><code>1     =&gt; 1
12    =&gt; 12
123   =&gt; 123
1234  =&gt; 1,234
12345 =&gt; 12,345
</code></pre>

<p>It strikes as a fairly common thing to do but I can't figure out which filter I'm supposed to use.</p>

<p>Edit: If you've a generic Python way to do this, I'm happy adding a formatted field in my model.</p>

Answer: <p>Regarding Ned Batchelder's solution, here it is with 2 decimal points and a dollar sign.</p>

<pre><code>from django.contrib.humanize.templatetags.humanize import intcomma

def currency(dollars):
    dollars = round(float(dollars), 2)
    return "$%s%s" % (intcomma(int(dollars)), ("%0.2f" % dollars)[-3:])
</code></pre>

<p>Then you can</p>

<pre><code>{{my_dollars | currency}}
</code></pre>
