# Hermes (footballer, born 1974)

Hermes Neves Soares, nicknamed Hermes (born 19 September 1974) is a Brazilian football manager and former player who played as a midfielder. He is currently the assistant manager of II liga club Kotwica Kołobrzeg.

Career

Hermes previously played for Bahia in the Campeonato Brasileiro.

National team

He was a member of Brazil national under-20 football team, which won the World Youth Cup in 1993.
