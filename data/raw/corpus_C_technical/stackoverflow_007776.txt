Question: <p>can someone give a scons config file which allows the following structure</p>

<pre><code>toplevel/
        /src - .cc files
        /include .h files
</code></pre>

<p>at top level I want the o and final exe.</p>

Answer: <pre><code>env=Environment(CPPPATH='/usr/include/glib-2.0/:/usr/lib/glib-2.0/include:include',
                CPPDEFINES=[],
                LIBS=['glib-2.0']) 

if ARGUMENTS.get('debug', 0):
    env.Append(CCFLAGS = ' -g')

env.Program('template', Glob('src/*.cc'))
</code></pre>

<p>Worked a treat. Thanks.</p>
