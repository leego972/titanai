Question: <p>What is the simplest way to determine the length (in seconds) of a given mp3 file, <strong>without using outside libraries</strong>? (python source highly appreciated)</p>

Answer: <p>Also take a look at audioread (some linux distros including ubuntu have packages), <a href="https://github.com/sampsyo/audioread" rel="nofollow">https://github.com/sampsyo/audioread</a></p>

<pre><code>audio = audioread.audio_open('/path/to/mp3')
print audio.channels, audio.samplerate, audio.duration
</code></pre>
