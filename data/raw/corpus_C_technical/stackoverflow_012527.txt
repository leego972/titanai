Question: <p>Is there a function in Python to split a string without ignoring the spaces in the resulting list? </p>

<p>E.g:</p>

<pre><code>s="This is the string I want to split".split()
</code></pre>

<p>gives me</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; s
['This', 'is', 'the', 'string', 'I', 'want', 'to', 'split']
</code></pre>

<p>I want something like</p>

<pre><code>['This',' ','is',' ', 'the',' ','string', ' ', .....]
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>Silly answer just for the heck of it:</p>

<pre><code>mystring.replace(" ","! !").split("!")
</code></pre>
