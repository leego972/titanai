Question: <p>I recently discovered the genshi.builder module. It reminds me of Divmod Nevow's Stan module. How would one use genshi.builder.tag to build an HTML document with a particular doctype? Or is this even a good thing to do? If not, what is the <em>right</em> way?</p>

Answer: <p>Genshi.builder is for "programmatically generating markup streams"[1]. I believe the purpose of it is as a backend for the templating language. You're probably looking for the templating language for generating a whole page.</p>

<p>You can, however do the following:</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; import genshi.output
&gt;&gt;&gt; genshi.output.DocType('html')
('html', '-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01//EN', 'http://www.w3.org/TR/html4/strict.dtd')
</code></pre>

<p>See other Doctypes here: <a href="http://genshi.edgewall.org/wiki/ApiDocs/genshi.output#genshi.output:DocType" rel="nofollow">http://genshi.edgewall.org/wiki/ApiDocs/genshi.output#genshi.output:DocType</a></p>

<pre><code>[1] genshi.builder.__doc__
</code></pre>
