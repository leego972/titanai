# Karel Piták

Karel Piták (born 28 January 1980) is a Czech association football manager and former player who manages Slavia Prague (women).

A midfielder, Piták was part of the Czech side which won the UEFA U-21 Championships in 2002. He joined SK Zápy in summer 2017.

Since July 2022 Piták is manager of Slavia Prague (women).
