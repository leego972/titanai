Question: <p>What's the difference between the list methods <code>append()</code> and <code>extend()</code>?</p>

Answer: <pre><code>list1 = [1,2,3,4,5]

list2 = ["a","b","c","d","e"]
</code></pre>

<p>append:</p>

<pre><code>print list.append(list2)
</code></pre>

<p>output : [1,2,3,4,5,["a","b","c","d","e"]]</p>

<p>extend :</p>

<pre><code>print list1.extend(list2)
</code></pre>

<p>output : [1,2,3,4,5,"a","b","c","d","e"]</p>
