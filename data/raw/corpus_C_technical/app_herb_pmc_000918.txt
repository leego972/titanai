Introduction</title><p>Dental caries (DC) and periodontal disease (PD) continue to be the most prevalent public health problems in the world. Although recent surveys show a decrease in the incidence and severity of carious lesions, PD remains the sixth most common disease worldwide, with 10% of the population affected by severe PD [<xref rid="B1-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>,<xref rid="B2-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B3-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>,<xref rid="B4-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">4</xref>,<xref rid="B5-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">5</xref>,<xref rid="B6-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">6</xref>,<xref rid="B7-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">7</xref>].</p><p>PD is a degenerative inflammatory disease of the periodontium caused by an altered oral microbiota associated with a reduced immune response of the body [<xref rid="B8-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">8</xref>,<xref rid="B9-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">9</xref>,<xref rid="B10-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">10</xref>].</p><p>It involves the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines, particularly IL-1β, PGE2 (linked to bone resorption and PD severity), and MMPs (especially MMP-8 and MMP-9), which degrade collagen and contribute to periodontal tissue destruction [<xref rid="B11-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">11</xref>,<xref rid="B12-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">12</xref>,<xref rid="B13-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">13</xref>,<xref rid="B14-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">14</xref>,<xref rid="B15-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">15</xref>,<xref rid="B16-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">16</xref>,<xref rid="B17-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">17</xref>]. Other metabolic, neurodegenerative, autoimmune, and oncological diseases are often associated with PD [<xref rid="B18-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">18</xref>,<xref rid="B19-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">19</xref>,<xref rid="B20-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">20</xref>]. The potential link between PD and systemic diseases has led to research into new treatment options and a greater emphasis on periodontal treatment and control, as well as early diagnosis [<xref rid="B21-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">21</xref>,<xref rid="B22-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">22</xref>].</p><p>PD is diagnosed by assessing the plaque index (PI), bleeding on probing (BOP), the depth of the periodontal pocket, and the extent of gingival recession, and by testing for biomarkers (MMP-8 and MMP-9, PGE2 and IL-1β) that are detectable in the crevicular fluid [<xref rid="B23-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">23</xref>,<xref rid="B24-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">24</xref>,<xref rid="B25-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">25</xref>,<xref rid="B26-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">26</xref>].</p><p>To assess the therapeutic efficacy of natural products in periodontal health, various clinical parameters and biomarkers are commonly evaluated.
