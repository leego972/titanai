# Filling factor — Overview

Filling factor,  is a quantity measuring the efficiency of absorption of pump in the core of a double-clad fiber. Definition

The large (close to unity) filling factor is important in double-clad amplifiers; it allows them to reduce the requirements for the brightness of the pump and to reduce the length of the fiber laser. Such a reduction is especially important for the power scaling of various nonlinear processes, and  contributions of stimulated scattering to the degradation of signal.
