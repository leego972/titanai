# Southern Shores Field Service Council

served the Wolverine council from 1946 until its merger in 1973.  The lodge totem was a 4-leaf clover due to the lodge's location in the area locally known as the Irish Hills.

Teetonkah Lodge (1941-1995)

Nacha-Mawat Lodge (1973-2012)

Wakazoo (1941-1973)

Mandoka (1945-1973)

Carcajou (1948-1973)
