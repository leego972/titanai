Question: <p>Is there a framework equivalent to Guice (<a href="http://code.google.com/p/google-guice">http://code.google.com/p/google-guice</a>) for Python?</p>

Answer: <p>If you just want to do dependency injection in Python, you don't need a framework. Have a look at <a href="http://code.activestate.com/recipes/413268-dependency-injection-the-python-way/" rel="nofollow">Dependency Injection the Python Way</a>. It's really quick and easy, and only c. 50 lines of code.</p>
