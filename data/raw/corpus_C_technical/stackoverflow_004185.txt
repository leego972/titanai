Question: <p>How can I find the public facing IP for my net work in Python?</p>

Answer: <p><a href="https://api.ipify.org/?format=json" rel="nofollow">https://api.ipify.org/?format=json</a> is pretty straight forward</p>

<p>can be parsed by just running <code>requests.get("https://api.ipify.org/?format=json").json()['ip']</code></p>
