Discussion</title><p>Stre<bold>ss</bold> alters the equilibrium of various hormones which have a significant impact on the immune response in general. The status of immune system immunosuppression versus immunopotentiation will depend upon the net effect of these changes. Stress has been shown to affect immune system functioning with both immunosuppression and immunopotentiation.</p><p>Plant adaptogens are smooth prostressor which reduces the reactivity of host defense system and decreases the damaging effect of various stressors due to increase of basal level mediators involved in the stress response [<xref rid="B18" ref-type="bibr">20</xref>].</p><p>The forced swimming is the most widely used method for assessing the antistress property of a compound. This paradigm is based on the observation that animal when forced to swim in water eventually assumed characteristics immobile posture, devoid of any activity. The appearance of immobility, therefore, reflects a state of tiredness, fatigue, and reduced stamina with the end point being the moment when the mice could not swim further and started drowning [<xref rid="B27" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>, <xref rid="B3" ref-type="bibr">18</xref>].</p><p>The increased swimming time has been observed in mice pretreated with<italic toggle="yes"> Pandanus odoratissimus </italic>with enhanced physical performances significantly longer than untreated (control) group and thus confirmed selected Indian medicinal plants having the adaptogenic nature.</p><p>During stress, blood glucose level increases which are found to be significantly reduced in<italic toggle="yes"> Pandanus odoratissimus</italic> treated mice. Lowering of stress induced hyperglycemia is an indication of Antistress, adaptogenic activity of plant.</p><p>In response to stress, ACTH is released, which acts on the adrenal cortex to stimulate the synthesis and release of cortisol [<xref rid="B16" ref-type="bibr">21</xref>]. Increased plasma cortisol influences the mobilization of stored fat and carbohydrate reserves which in turn increased blood glucose level. The increased cortisol levels are reversed by antistress agents [<xref rid="B16" ref-type="bibr">21</xref>]. LEPOsignificantly decreases the stress induced elevated levels of cortisol and blood glucose level. The reference drug in this study, WS, also produced similar result. Stressed animals spleen contracts and releases the more amounts of blood cells (RBC's and WBC's) into circulations. (this stage in acute stress phenomenon) [<xref rid="B20" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>, <xref rid="B25" ref-type="bibr">17</xref>]. The stress induced RBC and WBC count is decreased by the LEPO. Increased swim duration and decreased (this stage in chronic stress phenomenon) in RBC and WBC count in mice pretreated with<italic toggle="yes"> Pandanus odoratissimus</italic> are similar to the changes produced by reference drug<italic toggle="yes"> Withania somnifera</italic>.</p><p>Anorexia is a very sever stressor. All the body functions including cellular respiration depend on oxygen supply to them. Any lack of this vital element plays havoc on all body mechanisms. Increase in adaptation during this stress by any drug could be considered as its major antistress effect [<xref rid="B27" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>]. The result of the study demonstrates that the Pandanus odoratissimus extract significantly prolonged the meantime to convulsion, which therefore confirms its antistress property. Prolongation of meantime to convulsion could be a result of its powerful antioxidant and free radical scavenging activities [<xref rid="B27" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>, <xref rid="B3" ref-type="bibr">18</xref>], the mechanism by which stress rises which enhances the activity of hypothalamo-hypophyseal axis (HPA) resulting in liberation of catecholamine and corticosteroids.
