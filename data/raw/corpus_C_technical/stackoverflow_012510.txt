Question: <p>What's the difference between file and open in Python?  When should I use which one?  (Say I'm in 2.5)</p>

Answer: <p>Only ever use open() for opening files. file() is actually being removed in 3.0, and it's deprecated at the moment. They've had a sort of strange relationship, but file() is going now, so there's no need to worry anymore.</p>
