Question: <p>Is there any Database Abstraction Layer (DAL) or Object Relational Mapper (ORM) that works on Google App Engine (GAE), and on normal relational databases (RDBS), other  than <a href="http://www.web2py.com" rel="nofollow">web2py</a>'s?</p>

<p>If not, is anybody working on porting one of the existing DAL/ORM to GAE?</p>

Answer: <p>Web2Py has a DAL that is compatible to GAE. In fact, the whole framework can be deployed to GAE: <a href="http://mdp.cti.depaul.edu/examples/default/index" rel="nofollow">http://mdp.cti.depaul.edu/examples/default/index</a></p>
