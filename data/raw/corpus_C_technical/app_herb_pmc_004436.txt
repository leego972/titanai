These ingredients are repeatedly diluted, with shaking at each stage (Table <xref rid="med21743-tbl-0005" ref-type="table">5</xref>). Homeopaths consider that this technique prevents side effects, enhances the ability of preparations to amplify a response, and generates curative properties, even for ingredients that are chemically inactive or so significantly diluted that none of the original material remains.
