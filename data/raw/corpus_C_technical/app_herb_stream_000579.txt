# Calcio Montebelluna — Overview

Calcio Montebelluna is an Italian association football club located in Montebelluna, Veneto. It currently plays in Serie D. History 
The club was founded in 1919 as U.S. Montebellunese. Serie C2 
From the season 1981–82 to 1986–87 it has played in Serie C2. Serie D 
In the season 2003–04 it was promoted from Eccellenza Veneto to Serie D where currently plays. Colors and badge 
Its colors are white and blue. Players
