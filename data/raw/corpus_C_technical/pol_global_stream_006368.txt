# British Latin American

hat around 900 British settled in Peru, although many of them returned to Europe or emigrated to countries like Argentina and Chile. The regions from which most of the British immigrants originated were Southampton and London and Birmingham. There are an estimated 8,000 Peruvians of British descent.

The popular soft drink Inca Kola was invented by the British immigrant Joseph Robinson Lindley. The Lindley Corporation continues to manufacture the drink.
