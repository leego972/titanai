Question: <p>For example, the standard division symbol '/' rounds to zero:</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; 4 / 100
0
</code></pre>

<p>However, I want it to return 0.04. What do I use?</p>

Answer: <p>You need to tell Python to use floating point values, not integers. You can do that simply by using a decimal point yourself in the inputs:</p>

<pre><code>&gt;&gt;&gt; 4/100.0
0.040000000000000001
</code></pre>
