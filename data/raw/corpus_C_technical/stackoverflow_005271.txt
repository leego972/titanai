Question: <p>How do I convert a hex string to an int in Python? </p>

<p>I may have it as "<code>0xffff</code>" or just "<code>ffff</code>".</p>

Answer: <p>The formatter option '%x' % seems to work in assignment statements as well for me. (Assuming Python 3.0 and later)</p>

<p><strong>Example</strong> </p>

<pre><code>a = int('0x100', 16)
print(a)   #256
print('%x' % a) #100
b = a
print(b) #256
c = '%x' % a
print(c) #100
</code></pre>
