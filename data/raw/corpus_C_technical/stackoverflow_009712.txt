Question: <p>When is white space not important in Python?</p>

<p>It seems to be ignored inside a list, for example:</p>

<pre><code>for x in range(5):
    list += [x, 1
,2,3,

     4,5]
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>White space is only important for indentation of statements. You have a single statement across several lines, and only the indentation of the beginning of the statement on the first line is significant. See <em><a href="http://www.secnetix.de/~olli/Python/block_indentation.hawk" rel="nofollow">Python: Myths about Indentation</a></em> for more information.</p>
