Question: <p>When should you use generator expressions and when should you use list comprehensions in Python?</p>

<pre><code># Generator expression
(x*2 for x in range(256))

# List comprehension
[x*2 for x in range(256)]
</code></pre>

Answer: <p>Iterating over the <em>generator expression</em> or the <em>list comprehension</em> will do the same thing. However, the <em>list comprehension</em> will create the entire list in memory first while the <em>generator expression</em> will create the items on the fly, so you are able to use it for very large (and also infinite!) sequences.</p>
