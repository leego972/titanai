# Stewardship (theology)

l leaders and pastors in the stewardship (theology) arena like radio host Dave Ramsey, Pastor Dave Briggs (Central Arizona Church), Pastor Chris Goulard (Saddleback Church), Pastor David Thompson (Gateway Church), Pastor Andy Stanley (North Point Church), and Pastor Rick Warren (Saddleback Church)3.

Due to the popularity of the movement, organizations have grown to support Stewardship Theology understanding around the country. One particular ministry Christian Stewardship Network(CSN) stands out because its members are made up of practitioners. These stewardship pastors and leaders serve in local churches teaching and equipping their congregations with God's financial principles. Through relevant content and training, CSN focuses on helping churches launch their own stewardship and generosity ministries. The CSN membership  provides a church of any size with excellent resources and opportunities to engage in real-time with a community of stewardship leaders to help them build thriving stewardship and generosity ministries. 

Another organization started by David Thompson at Gateway Church is Stewardshippastors.com . An excellent resource for church leaders looking for stewardship-related content.
