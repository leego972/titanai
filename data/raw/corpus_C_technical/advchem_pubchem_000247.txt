Description:
Tyrphostin AG 1478 is a member of the class of quinazolines that is quinazoline substituted by methoxy groups at positions 6 and 7 and a (3-chlorophenyl)nitrilo group at position 4. It acts as an epidermal growth factor receptor antagonist. It has a role as a geroprotector, an antiviral agent, an antineoplastic agent and an epidermal growth factor receptor antagonist. It is an aromatic ether, a member of quinazolines and a member of monochlorobenzenes.
