Question: <p>It seems like there should be a simpler way than:</p>

<pre><code>import string
s = "string. With. Punctuation?" # Sample string 
out = s.translate(string.maketrans("",""), string.punctuation)
</code></pre>

<p>Is there?</p>

Answer: <p>Regular expressions are simple enough, if you know them. </p>

<pre><code>import re
s = "string. With. Punctuation?"
s = re.sub(r'[^\w\s]','',s)
</code></pre>
