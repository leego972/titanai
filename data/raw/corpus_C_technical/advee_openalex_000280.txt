Title: Design of 5g Mobile Millimeter Wave Antenna

Abstract:
This paper present the design and simulation of a single band microstrip patch antenna for 5G wireless application operating at 60GHz. The design is carried on Roger RT5880 with 2.2 dielectric, 0.0009 loss tangent and 0.003 mm thickness using inset feeding. The overall dimension of the patch antenna is 2.9 A— 3.5 mm 2 , two rectangular U-slot in conjunction with H-slot were loaded into the radiator to enhance the antenna bandwidth. The antenna is design and simulated and the analysis were carried out on CST studio. The antenna achieve a maximum reflection coefficient of about -41.648731 dB at 60 GHz with a very wide bandwidth of about 30 GHz and 8.82 dB realized gain.
