# PMC: adaptogen stress response HPA axis — Overview

rid="B62-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">62</xref>,<xref rid="B63-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">63</xref>,<xref rid="B64-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">64</xref>,<xref rid="B65-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">65</xref>,<xref rid="B72-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">72</xref>]. The stimulating effects of a single dose of adaptogens are primarily due to interactions within the central nervous system (CNS) and hypothalamic–pituitary–adrenal (HPA) axis but do not require prolonged processes such as gene and protein expression, neurogenesis, cell proliferation, and differentiation [<xref rid="B1-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>,<xref rid="B2-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B3-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>,<xref rid="B62-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">62</xref>,<xref rid="B63-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">63</xref>,<xref rid="B64-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">64</xref>,<xref rid="B65-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">65</xref>]. The tonic effect of adaptogens is due to their repeated administration at multiple doses [<xref rid="B3-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>,<xref rid="B15-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">15</xref>,<xref rid="B63-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">63</xref>].</p><p>Plant adaptogens stimulate the nervous system through mechanisms that are quite different from those of conventional stimulants. Depending on the mediators of the stress system involved in the adaptogen-induced stress response, an immediate (single-dose effect) or a long-term (following multiple administrations) stimulating effect may be observed [<xref rid="B65-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">65</xref>,<xref rid="B73-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">73</xref>].</p><p>There are essential differences between adaptogens and conventional stimulants of the CNS, such as caffeine, nicotine, amphetamines, and cocaine, which produce a sense of euphoria and can be used to increase alertness and the ability to concentrate on mental tasks, boosting endurance and productivity [<xref rid="B2-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B24-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">24</xref>,<xref rid="B65-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">65</xref>]. However, long-term stimulant abuse can impair cognitive function and lead to psychotic symptoms and drug dependence. In contrast, adaptogens which exhibit high rate of recovery process after exhaustive physical load, low energy depletion, increased performance in stress and survival in stress, good quality of arousal, lack of senescence, rare side effects, upregulation of expression DNA/RNA and proteins, increased Hsp70 synthesis, and activation of NPY production, which activate HPA -axis [<xref rid="B2-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B65-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">65</xref>,<xref rid="B74-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">74</xref>,<xref rid="B75-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">75</xref>].</p><p>Adaptogens exhibit stress-protective and tonic effects [<xref rid="B15-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">15</xref>,<xref rid="B16-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">16</xref>,<xref rid="B17-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">17</xref>,<xref rid="B18-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">18</xref>,<xref rid="B62-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">62</xref>,<xref rid="B65-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">65</xref>], as shown in <xref rid="pharmaceuticals-18-01346-f004" ref-type="fig">Figure 4</xref>a. After repeated dose administration, they trigger adaptive stress response signaling pathways and mediate gene expression in the HPA axis and other body tissues [<xref rid="B1-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>,<xref rid="B2-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B3-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>,<xref rid="B61-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">61</xref>,<xref rid="B65-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">65</xref>,<xref rid="B74-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">74</xref>,<xref rid="B75-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">75</xref>], as shown in <xref rid="pharmaceuticals-18-01346-f002" ref-type="fig">Figure 2</xref> and <xref rid="pharmaceuticals-18-01346-f003" ref-type="fig">Figure 3</xref>. The stress-protective effect achieved by multiple administrations of adaptogens is not the result of inhibiting the stress response of an organism but rather of adaptive changes in the organism in response to the repeated stress-agonistic effect of the adaptogens [<xref rid="B1-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">1</xref>,<xref rid="B2-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">2</xref>,<xref rid="B3-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">3</xref>,<xref rid="B62-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">62</xref>,<xref rid="B68-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">68</xref>,<xref rid="B76-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">76</xref>,<xref rid="B77-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">77</xref>,<xref rid="B78-pharmaceuticals-18-01346" ref-type="bibr">78</xref>].
