# Transitional porcelain

Kangxi reign marks on porcelain are few throughout the ceramic period, but a few can be identified with the pre1677 decades. Earlier Ming period marks can frequently be found. Their styles closely match the few Kangxi marks that are found and aid in delineating Kangxi transitional porcelain.
