Question: <p>I am creating a Python script where it does a bunch of tasks and one of those tasks is to launch and open an instance of Excel. What is the ideal way of accomplishing that in my script?</p>

Answer: <p>As others have stated, I would suggest os.system. In case anyone is looking for a Mac-compatible solution, here is an example:</p>

<pre><code>import os
os.system("open /Applications/Safari.app")
</code></pre>
