Question: <p>How can I output colored text to the terminal, in Python?
What is the best Unicode symbol to represent a solid block?</p>

Answer: <p>Use pyfancy it is a simple way to do color in the terminal!</p>

<p>Example:</p>

<p><code>
print(pyfancy.RED + "Hello Red" + pyfancy.END)
</code></p>
