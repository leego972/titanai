Lasers in dermatology. An overview of types and indications.

Alster TS(1), Lupton JR.

Author information:
(1)Washington Institute of Dermatologic Laser Surgery, Washington, DC, USA. 
info@skinlaser.com

Advances in laser technology have been so marked over the past two decades that 
successful eradication of many cutaneous pathologies and congenital defects, 
including vascular and pigmented lesions, tattoos, scars, and unwanted hair, can 
now be fully realized. Because of the relative ease with which many of these 
lesions can be removed, coupled with a low incidence of adverse postoperative 
sequelae, demand for laser surgery has increased substantially. In this review, 
the currently available laser systems with cutaneous application are outlined, 
with special reference to recent advancements and modifications in laser 
technology that have greatly expanded the laser surgeon's armamentarium and 
improved upon overall treatment efficacy.
