Question: <p>I am trying to sort a dict based on its key and return an iterator to the values from within an overridden iter method in a class. Is there a nicer and more efficient way of doing this than creating a new list, inserting into the list as I sort through the keys?</p>

Answer: <p>How about something like this:</p>

<pre><code>def itersorted(d):
    for key in sorted(d):
        yield d[key]
</code></pre>
