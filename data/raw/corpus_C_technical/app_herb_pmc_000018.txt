Lall et al. [<xref rid="B67-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">67</xref>] evaluated in vitro antimycobacterial activities of ethanolic shoot extracts of <italic toggle="yes">E. natalensis</italic> against <italic toggle="yes">Mycobacterium tuberculosis</italic> H37Rv using a 96-well microtitre to determine the minimum inhibitory concentration of the extract. The <italic toggle="yes">E. natalensis</italic> extracts were also evaluated for in vivo antimycobacterial activities in <italic toggle="yes">Mycobacterium tuberculosis</italic> H37Rv infected mice. The MIC value of the extract was found to be 125 µg/mL against <italic toggle="yes">Mycobacterium tuberculosis</italic> compared to MIC value of 0.25 µg/mL exhibited by the positive control isoniazid. The antimycobacterial activities of <italic toggle="yes">E. natalensis</italic> extracts evaluated on <italic toggle="yes">Mycobacterium tuberculosis</italic> (H37Rv) infected Balb/c mice showed substantial decrease in bacterial loads when comparing the infected control group to the treatment groups, showing a decrease in lung homogenate colony forming units from 1.5 × 10<sup>6</sup> (control) to 7.1 × 10<sup>3</sup> (drug control) [<xref rid="B67-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">67</xref>]. [<xref rid="B53-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">53</xref>] evaluated antifungal activities of the compounds betulin <bold>1</bold>, lupeol <bold>2</bold>, shinanolone <bold>13</bold>, 20(29)-lupene-3β-isoferulate <bold>14</bold>, octahydroeuclein <bold>15</bold> and β-sitosterol <bold>16</bold> isolated from the root bark of <italic toggle="yes">E. natalensis</italic> against <italic toggle="yes">Aspergillus flavus, Aspergillus niger, Cladosporium cladosporioides</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">Phytophthora</italic> spp. <italic toggle="yes">Aspergillus niger</italic> was significantly inhibited by shinanolone <bold>13</bold>, 20(29)-lupene-3β-isoferulate <bold>14</bold> and β-sitosterol <bold>16</bold> at 0.01 mg/mL. Of all the compounds tested, only octahydroeuclein <bold>15</bold> was found to be significantly effective against <italic toggle="yes">Phytophthora</italic> spp. at 0.1 mg/mL. The compounds octahydroeuclein <bold>15</bold> and β-sitosterol <bold>16</bold> significantly inhibited the growth of <italic toggle="yes">Cladosporium cladosporioides</italic> at 0.01 mg/mL [<xref rid="B53-molecules-22-02128" ref-type="bibr">53</xref>].
