# PMC: adaptogen stress response HPA axis — Overview

RRE Increases Neurite Outgrowth and BDNF Expression under Stress Condition</title><p>In a next step, we assessed the capacity of RRE in promoting neurite outgrowth in SH-SY5Y cells under stress condition and subsequent immunostaining and microscopy analysis (<xref rid="fig6" ref-type="fig">Figure 6</xref>). The most promising RRE concentration from the previous experiments (5 ng/ml) was used as curative treatment to test its effect on neurite outgrowth (<xref rid="fig6" ref-type="fig">Figure 6(a)</xref>). As for the ROS experiments, Dexa at 100 <italic toggle="yes">μ</italic>M was used to induce stress and caused a decrease of 52.4% in neurite count, a decrease of 41.1% in total neurite length, a decrease of 43.4% in the number of attachment points, and a decrease of 47.6% in the number of endpoints, when compared to the untreated control cells (Figures <xref rid="fig6" ref-type="fig">6(b)</xref>–<xref rid="fig6" ref-type="fig">6(e)</xref>). RRE 5 ng/ml was able to counteract the effects of Dexa by increasing the neurite count (<xref rid="fig6" ref-type="fig">Figure 6(b)</xref>), total neurite length (<xref rid="fig6" ref-type="fig">Figure 6(c)</xref>), number of attachment points (<xref rid="fig5" ref-type="fig">Figure 5(d)</xref>), and number of endpoints (<xref rid="fig6" ref-type="fig">Figure 6(e)</xref>) by 20.2%, 22.8%, 23.2%, and 21.2%, respectively, compared to the cells treated with Dexa alone.</p><p>Because RRE increases BDNF expression in basal condition, we next examined whether this effect is also observed in stress condition. Dexa induced a decrease of about 20% of BDNF mRNA level, while a curative treatment with RRE restored BDNF expression to the level of the untreated control cells (<xref rid="fig6" ref-type="fig">Figure 6(f)</xref>). Cellular actions of BDNF are mediated through two main receptors: tyrosine receptor kinase B (TrkB) and p75 neurotrophin receptor (p75NTR). Therefore, we next assessed whether RRE modulates TrkB and p75NTR expression under normal condition or under Dexa-induced stress. Although RRE or Dexa alone tended to increase TrkB, the difference with the control cells was not significant (<xref rid="fig6" ref-type="fig">Figure 6(b)</xref>). However, a 47.5% increase in TrkB expression was observed after RRE treatment under stress condition, when compared to the control condition. Strikingly, RRE strongly decreased p75NTR expression (-87% compared to untreated cells), while Dexa upregulated its expression (+72% compared to the control). A combined treatment of RRE and Dexa decreased of about 25% p75NTR expression compared to cells treated with Dexa alone (<xref rid="fig6" ref-type="fig">Figure 6(b)</xref>). These data demonstrate that RRE 5 ng/ml reduces the damages caused by Dexa, by promoting neurite growth and connectivity and modulating BDNF, TrkB, and p75NTR expression.</p></sec></sec><sec id="sec4"><title>4. Discussion</title><p>In this study, we hypothesized that WS®1375 extract of the adaptogenic plant <italic toggle="yes">Rhodiola rosea</italic> can reduce the detrimental effects of glucocorticoid-induced stress. The basal effect of RRE was examined at a concentration range of 1-100 ng/ml on two cell lines: the human neuroblastoma cells SH-SY5Y which is a known and well-characterized neuronal model and the murine hippocampal cell line HT22. The latter cell line was selected in order to confirm our results in cells generated from a brain structure that is at the forefront of the impact of glucocorticoid stress as the hippocampus highly expresses GRs [<xref rid="B43" ref-type="bibr">43</xref>]. Dexamethasone, a synthetic glucocorticoid mimicking the effect of the endogenous stress hormone cortisol, was used to simulate a glucocorticoid-induced stress. Two treatment conditions were tested on SH-SY5Y cells: a preventive treatment, in which cells were first treated with RRE for 24 h and then treated with Dexa for another 24 h, and a curative treatment, in which cells were first stressed with Dexa for 3 h and additionally treated with RRE for 24 h. The rational underlying this approach in everyday life is that RRE might be taken in prevision of a stressful event (e.g., examination session and upcoming conflict) to reduce the effect of stress, or during a stressful period to counteract the effect of stress.</p><p>Our key results were that (i) RRE increased ATP levels and cell metabolic activity in an inverted U-shaped dose response curve, in line with the adaptogenic nature of this plant extract; (ii) RRE pretreatment (preventive) and posttreatment (curative) reduced Dexa-induced increase of ROS and decrease of cell metabolic activity; and (iii) RRE increased neurite outgrowth and BDNF expression, both in healthy and stressed conditions. <xref rid="fig7" ref-type="fig"> Figure 7</xref> summarizes these key results.</p><p>Mitochondria are the main generators of ROS and are directly affected by their overproduction. At the same time, neurons are highly dependent on mitochondria for their function and survival as they demand high amounts of energy, while they are more vulnerable to oxidative stress [<xref rid="B44" ref-type="bibr">44</xref>–<xref rid="B46" ref-type="bibr">46</xref>].
