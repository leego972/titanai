# Talal Al-Bloushi

Talal Hassan Ali Al-Bloushi (; born May 22, 1986, in Kuwait) is a Qatari footballer and coach of Zakho. He was  a midfielder . and he played for Al Sadd and Al-Shabab and Al Arabi and  Al-Markhiya and the last team was  Mesaimeer and then quit football at the end of 2019.

Al-Bloushi is also a member of the Qatar national football team.
