In the current review, we provide a comprehensive overview of the existing knowledge and traditional uses of <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic>, including its botany, phytochemistry, pharmacodynamics and potential applications, toxicology and pharmacokinetics.<fig id="f0005" position="float" orientation="portrait"><label>Fig. 1</label><caption><p>Whole <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> plant (A); The roots of <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> (B); the roots of <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> Praeparata (C).</p></caption><graphic xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink" position="float" orientation="portrait" xlink:href="gr1_lrg.jpg"/></fig></p></sec><sec id="s0010"><label>2</label><title>Traditional usages</title><p id="p0010">With a wide spectrum of biological and pharmacological effects, <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> has been used as a traditional medicine for many centuries in China. The <italic toggle="yes">Compendium of Materia Medica (Ben cao Gang mu)</italic> reported that it exerts liver-tonic and hair-blacking effects. In <italic toggle="yes">Diannan Bencao,</italic> a famous monograph of traditional Chinese medicine, this plant was described to be useful for the treatment of sore scabies, ringworm and pruritus (<xref rid="bib59" ref-type="bibr">Lan, 1959</xref>). The toxicity of this plant was first described in <italic toggle="yes">Bencao Huiyan</italic> (another famous monograph of traditional Chinese medicine written in China during the Ming dynasty), which described this plant to be of minimal toxicity to humans. In other monographs of Materia Medica, such as <italic toggle="yes">Kaibao Bencao, Bencao Mengquan,</italic> and <italic toggle="yes">Xinbian Bencao</italic>, this plant was described to be used for the treatment of scrofula, carbuncles, and postpartum and morbid leucorrhea and to reinforce the kidney and promote anti-aging effects.</p><p id="p0015">The root of this plant is used as the effective agent after its common processing by steaming with black bean (<xref rid="bib225" ref-type="bibr">Zhou et al., 2010</xref>), rehmannia juice, or a wine- or ginger-black bean mixture (Committee on the Programming of Teaching Material for Higher <xref rid="bib29" ref-type="bibr">TCM Education, 1996</xref>).</p><p id="p0020">The black bean method (black beans are the dry and mature seeds of <italic toggle="yes">Glycine max</italic> (L.) Merr.) (<xref rid="bib182" ref-type="bibr">Yang et al., 2008</xref>) is the most commonly used processing method, as described in “Xian Shou Li Shang Xu Duan Mi Fang” (Secret Formulary for Traumatology and Fracture Taught by Immortal), which was written during the Tang Dynasty. This work first proposed that the plant be prepared as follows: “10 lb, cooked with half a catty of black bean”. The book “Sue Shen Liang fang” of the Song Dynasty states that “After a one-day-long immersion, cut the <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> into half-inch-thick sections and then mix well with the water of the black bean by placing fleece-flower root layer upon layer with the black bean. This should be followed by steaming until the beans become rotten, removal of the beans, and drying of the root in the shade”. The Qing Dynasty “Cheng Fang Qie Yong” proposed that the plant be “mixed seven times with black bean juice” (<xref rid="bib194" ref-type="bibr">Yu, 2014</xref>). In modern times, the 2010 edition of “Chinese Pharmacopoeia” described the following detailed rules for the <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> processing method: Mix the slices or pieces of <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> thoroughly with black bean juice (Preparation of black bean juice: Boil 10 kg of black bean in a sufficient quantity of water for 4 h and stew to get about 15 kg of juice. Boil the bean residue again in water for about 3 h and stew to get about 10 kg of juice. Combine to get about 25 kg of the black bean juice). Carry out the stewing method in a suitable non-ferrous container until the juice is exhausted or carry out the steaming method, steam it alone or steam it after being mixed with black bean juice to a brown color on all sides, dry in the sun to partial dryness, then cut into slice and dry. For each 100 kg of slice (piece) of <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> use 10 kg of black bean (<xref rid="bib35" ref-type="bibr">Editorial Committee of Chinese Pharmacopoeia., 2010</xref>).</p><p id="p0025">Steaming is another common preparation method for <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic>. In this method, slightly moist <italic toggle="yes">Polygonum multiflorum</italic> is enclosed in boiler and steamed until the drugs both inside and outside are brown, at which point it is removed and desiccated (<xref rid="bib225" ref-type="bibr">Zhou et al., 2010</xref>, <xref rid="bib144" ref-type="bibr">Tian et al., 2007</xref>, <xref rid="bib76" ref-type="bibr">Li et al., 2012g</xref>).
