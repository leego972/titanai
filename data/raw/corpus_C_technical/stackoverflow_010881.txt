Question: <p>That's it. If you want to document a function or a class, you put a string just after the definition. For instance:</p>

<pre><code>def foo():
    """This function does nothing."""
    pass
</code></pre>

<p>But what about a module? How can I document what a <em>file.py</em> does?</p>

Answer: <p>Add your docstring as the <a href="http://legacy.python.org/dev/peps/pep-0257/#what-is-a-docstring">first statement in the module</a>.</p>

<p>Since I like seeing an example:</p>

<pre><code>"""
Your module's verbose yet thorough docstring.
"""

import foo

# ...
</code></pre>
