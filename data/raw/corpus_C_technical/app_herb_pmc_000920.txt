These benefits are largely attributed to bioactive compounds such as polyphenols, flavonoids, tannins, and terpenoids, which exhibit anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. The active ingredients of phytotherapeutic products have the advantage that they have no side effects and are not harmful even in the medium and long term. In addition, all natural herbal mouthwashes do not contain sugar and/or alcohol, which are present in the vast majority of over-the-counter oral hygiene products, and can be a good substrate for the development of microorganisms [<xref rid="B54-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">54</xref>,<xref rid="B55-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">55</xref>,<xref rid="B56-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">56</xref>,<xref rid="B57-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">57</xref>]. Herbs from the <italic toggle="yes">Lythraceae</italic> family have high phytotherapeutic properties, including <italic toggle="yes">Punica granatum</italic> (pomegranate) and <italic toggle="yes">Lawsonia inermis</italic> (henna), whose extracts have shown remarkable anti-inflammatory properties [<xref rid="B58-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">58</xref>,<xref rid="B59-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">59</xref>,<xref rid="B60-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">60</xref>,<xref rid="B61-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">61</xref>]. Both extracts were used as mouthwashes, and the reduction in gingival and periodontal inflammatory status was correlated to the decrease in salivary enzyme activity of aspartate aminotransferase (AST), alanine aminotransferase (ALT), and lactate dehydrogenase (LDH), with a greater reduction with <italic toggle="yes">Punica granatum</italic> mouthwash [<xref rid="B34-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">34</xref>,<xref rid="B62-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">62</xref>,<xref rid="B63-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">63</xref>]. Pomegranate extract owes its beneficial effects to the presence of punicalin, phenols, flavonoids, and triterpenoids, which are mainly found in the rind. The bioactive components reduce inflammatory mediators, stimulate collagen production, and angiogenesis and reduce bacterial proliferation. <italic toggle="yes">Lawsonia inermis</italic> owes its antibacterial properties to the presence of 2-hydroxynaphthoquinone, saponins, and xanthones. <italic toggle="yes">Punica granatum</italic> extract, rich in punicalin, phenols, flavonoids, and triterpenoids, reduces inflammation, promotes collagen production, and inhibits bacterial growth. Similarly, other bioactive compounds such as tannins, terpenoids, and saponins found in medicinal plants contribute to oral health improvement by modulating inflammatory responses and reducing microbial adhesion. <italic toggle="yes">Lawsonia inermis</italic>’ antibacterial effects stem from 2-hydroxynaphthoquinone, saponins, and xanthones [<xref rid="B64-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">64</xref>,<xref rid="B65-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">65</xref>,<xref rid="B66-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">66</xref>,<xref rid="B67-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">67</xref>]. Polyherbal mouthwashes based on extracts of rosemary (<italic toggle="yes">Rosmarinus officinalis Lamiaceae</italic>), <italic toggle="yes">Zingiber officinale</italic>, and marigold (<italic toggle="yes">Calendula officinalis</italic>) have been produced which have shown efficacy in the treatment and prophylaxis of gingivitis comparable to that of CHX mouthwash without adverse effects [<xref rid="B68-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">68</xref>,<xref rid="B69-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">69</xref>,<xref rid="B70-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">70</xref>,<xref rid="B71-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">71</xref>,<xref rid="B72-nutrients-17-00762" ref-type="bibr">72</xref>]. Rosemary extract is a spice. It belongs to the <italic toggle="yes">Lamiaceae</italic> family and is also used in medicine for its many therapeutic properties and high antioxidant and antibacterial activity, thanks to the presence of active compounds: phenolic diterpenes (carnosic acid, but also carnosol, rosmanol and epi- and isorosmanol), flavonoids, and terpenoids.
