In popular culture 

The English rock band XTC mentioned British Steel in their 1979 song Making Plans for Nigel.

The iconic heavy metal band Judas Priest named their 1980 album British Steel after the (in)famous British Steel Corporation. Lead singer Rob Halford explained in an interview that the 'sounds of heavy metal' have been with him since childhood, due to the close proximity of the BSC plant where he grew up.
