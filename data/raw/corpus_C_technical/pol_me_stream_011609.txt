# Craig Pead

10–11 season.

International career 
Pead was capped by England at U18 and U20 level. He was a part of the U20 squad which finished fourth at the 2002 Toulon Tournament.

Coaching career 
On 14 August 2021, Pead joined Southern League Premier Division Central club Banbury United as first team coach.

Personal life 
Pead's father worked as a kit man at Coventry City. Whilst players at Brentford, Pead shared a flat in Feltham with Lee Thorpe. After his retirement from football in 2009, Pead returned to the West Midlands and opened Soccer Rockz, a youth football coaching school. He later worked as Business Development Manager for BPMpro, as a personal trainer and as an FA-registered intermediary.
