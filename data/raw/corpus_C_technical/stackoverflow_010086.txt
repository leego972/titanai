Question: <p>In many places, <code>(1,2,3)</code> and <code>[1,2,3]</code> can be used interchangeably.</p>

<p>When should I use one or the other, and why?</p>

Answer: <p><code>(1,2,3)</code> is a tuple while <code>[1,2,3]</code> is a list.  A tuple is an immutable object while a list is mutable.</p>
