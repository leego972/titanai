Description:
4-(trimethylammonio)butanoic acid is a quaternary ammonium ion that is the conjugate acid of 4-(trimethylammonio)butanoate. It has a role as a mouse metabolite, an Escherichia coli metabolite and a human metabolite. It is a conjugate acid of a 4-(trimethylammonio)butanoate.
