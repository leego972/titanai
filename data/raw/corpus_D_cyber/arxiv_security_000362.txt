Security Research Paper: Out-of-Domain Stress Test for Temporal Braid Group Privilege Escalation Detection

Abstract: In a companion paper, we prove that the Burau-Lyapunov exponent LE discriminates focused from dispersed privilege escalation ratchets in cloud IAM graphs, and that no abelian statistic can replicate this discrimination. To strengthen this claim beyond its synthetic validation corpus, we apply the identical pipeline, with zero parameter retuning, to solar coronal magnetic fields: a physical system with no connection to cloud identity and access management, whose binary eruptive/confined outcome is independently established by decades of astrophysical observation.
