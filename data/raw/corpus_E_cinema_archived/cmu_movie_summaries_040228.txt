Movie Plot Summary:
In 1910, in Arga, a small village from Galicia, the Mariño family are cursed by a gypsy woman as punishment for the excesses of the Marquise de Mariño. Upon reaching the age of ten years old, her son becomes a werewolf. A hundred years later, the last descendent of the Mariño family, Tomás , returns to the village. The villagers try to to kill him in a ritual ceremony in order to end the curse, but not everything goes according to their plan.Sinopsis en Filmaffinity
