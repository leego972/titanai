Movie Plot Summary:
The plot concerns the adventures of João Grilo  and Chicó , the most cowardly of men. Both struggle for daily bread and go through several episodes fooling all of the small town they live in, until they get through their confusion the wrath of the dreaded bandit Severino de Aracaju  and his gang. João is shot dead, and as Chico buries him, João is seem in the hereafter, matching wits the Devil , and asking the Virgin Mary  to intercede for him.
