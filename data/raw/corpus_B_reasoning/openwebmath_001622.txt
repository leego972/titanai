# LCM

Common multiple of three numbers is 3276. One number is in this number 63 times, second 7 times, third 9 times. What are the numbers?

Result

a =  52
b =  468
c =  364

#### Solution:

$a=3276/63=52$
$b=3276/7=468$
$c=3276/9=364$
