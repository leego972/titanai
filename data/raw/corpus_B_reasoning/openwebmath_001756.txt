# Write in terms of factorial:6 × 7 × 8 × 9 - Mathematics and Statistics

Sum

Write in terms of factorial:
6 × 7 × 8 × 9

#### Solution

6 × 7 × 8 × 9 = 9 × 8 × 7 × 6

Multiplying and dividing by 5!, we get

= (9xx8xx7xx6xx5!)/(5!)

= (9xx8xx7xx6xx5xx4xx3xx2xx1)/(5!)

= (9!)/(5!)
