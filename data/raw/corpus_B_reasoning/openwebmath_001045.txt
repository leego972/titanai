# Chapter 1 - Sections 1.1-1.8 - Exercises - Problems by Topic - Page 40: 89a

#### Answer

$$27.8 L = 2.78\times10^4 cm^{3}$$

#### Work Step by Step

To convert from L to cm$^{3}$, we can first convert liters to milliliters. 1 L = 1000 mL 27.8 L = 27800 mL Since, 1000 mL = 1000 cm$^{3}$ 27.8 L = 27800 cm$^{3}$ = $2.78\times10^4 cm^{3}$
