$$\frac{7}{2}$$ = $$\frac{21}{6}$$

$$\frac{21}{6}$$$$\frac{5}{6}$$=$$\frac{26}{6}$$$$\frac{13}{3}$$

Now we need to subtract 3/10

$$\frac{13}{3}$$-$$\frac{3}{10}$$$$\frac{130}{30} - \frac{9}{30}$$=$$\frac{121}{30}$$
