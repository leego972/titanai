# Minutes

Write as fraction in basic form which part of the week is 980 minutes.

Result

x = 7/72

#### Solution:

$x = \dfrac{ 980 }{ 7\cdot 24\cdot 60 } = \dfrac{ 980 }{ 10080 } = \dfrac{ 7 }{ 72 }$
