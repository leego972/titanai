 Question from Sarah, a student: what is pi + pi?

Hi Sarah,

$\pi + \pi$ is the same as $2 \times \pi.$ $\pi$ is approximately 3.1416 so $\pi + \pi$ is approximately 6.2832.

I hope this helps,
