## Examples of Prime Decompositions

The prime decompositions for the first few integers are as follows:

$n$ Prime Decomposition of $n$
$1$ $1$
$2$ $2$
$3$ $3$
$4$ $2^2$
$5$ $5$
$6$ $2 \times 3$
$7$ $7$
$8$ $2^3$
$9$ $3^2$
$10$ $2 \times 5$
$11$ $11$
$12$ $2^2 \times 3$
