2. Note that in the region $\displaystyle -1 \leq x \leq 2$,

$\displaystyle \frac{e^x}{2} \leq \frac{e^x}{x} \leq e^x$.

So $\displaystyle \int_1^2{\frac{e^x}{2}\,dx} \leq \int_1^2{\frac{e^x}{x}\,dx} \leq \int_1^2{e^x\,dx}$.
