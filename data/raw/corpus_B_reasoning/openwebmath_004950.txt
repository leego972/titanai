## Welcome!

My name is Sebastiaan Janssens and I work in mathematics under supervision of my PhD advisors O. Diekmann, S.A. van Gils and Yu.A. Kuznetsov at Utrecht University and the University of Twente in The Netherlands.

My interests are in dynamical systems: foundations, stability and bifurcation. In particular:

• various classes of delay equations,
• linear algebra and functional analysis,
• discrete dynamics on spaces of measures,
• computational aspects and computer algebra.

I consider myself competent in Maple, MATLAB, modern Fortran and C (but not C++). Also, I enjoy Lisp, but so far mostly just for editing text in Emacs.

Via the menu at the top you find some information on my work, as well as my blog.
