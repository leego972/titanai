Solve the following equation:

("a" + 5)/6 - ("a" + 1)/9 = ("a" + 3)/4

#### Solution

Since, L.C.M. of denominators 6,9 and 4 = 36

∴ ("a" + 5)/6 xx 36 - ("a" + 1)/9 xx 36 = ("a" + 3)/4 xx 36
...(Multiplying each term by 36)

⇒ 6(a + 5) - 4(a + 1) = 9(a + 3)

⇒ 6a + 30 - 4a - 4 = 9a + 27

⇒ 6a - 4a - 9a = 27 - 30 + 4

⇒ 6a - 13a = 1

⇒ -7a = 1

⇒ a = -1/7

Concept: Simple Linear Equations in One Variable
