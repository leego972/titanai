Question

# How many linear equations are satisfied by x = 2 and y = -3?

A

Only one

B

Two

C

Three

D

Infinitely many

Solution

## The correct option is A Infinitely many ∴ From a point, infinitely number of lines can pass. ∴ The solution x = 2, y = - 3 is the solution of infinitely many linear equations.
