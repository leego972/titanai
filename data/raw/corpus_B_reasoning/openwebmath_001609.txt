# NEN-EN 480-1

## Admixtures for concrete, mortar and grout - Test methods - Part 1: Reference concrete and reference mortar for testing

active, Most Current
 Organization: NEN Publication Date: 1 November 2014 Status: active Page Count: 15 ICS Code (Cement. Gypsum. Lime. Mortar): 91.100.10 ICS Code (Concrete and concrete products): 91.100.30
##### scope:

NEN-EN 480-1 specifies the constituent materials, the composition and the mixing method to produce reference concrete and reference mortar for testing the efficacy and the compatibility of admixtures in accordance with the series EN 934.
