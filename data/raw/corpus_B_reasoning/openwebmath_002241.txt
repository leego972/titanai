# Chapter 3 - Section 3.4 - Volume and Area - Exercise: 9

1,000,000

#### Work Step by Step

We know that there are 100 centimeters in 1 meter. Using this, we find how many cubic centimeters are in a cubic meter. $1m^3=(100cm)^3=1,000,000cm^3$
