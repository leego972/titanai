If 'A + B' means A is the mother of B, 'A - B' means A is the brother of B, 'A % B' means A is the father of B and 'A x B' means A is the sister of B, which of the following shows that P is the maternal uncle of Q? A)  $Q-N+M\times P$    B)  $P+S\times N-Q$ C)  $P-M+N\times Q$    D)  $Q-S\,%\,P$

P - M $\to$ P is the bother of M. M + N $\to$ M is the mother of N. N x Q $\to$ N is the maternal uncle of Q. Therefore, P is the maternal uncle of Q
