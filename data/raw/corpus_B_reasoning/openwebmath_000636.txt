$u^2 = 4-x^2$

$x^2 = 4-u^2$

$2x \, dx = -2u \, du$

$x \, dx = -u \, du$

$\int \frac{x^3}{\sqrt{4-x^2}} \, dx = \int \frac{x^2}{\sqrt{4-x^2}} \cdot x \, dx$

substitute ...

$\int \frac{4-u^2}{u} \cdot (-u) \, du$

$-\int 4 - u^2 \, du$

lower limit ... $x = 0$ , $u = 2$

upper limit ... $x = \sqrt{2}$ , $u = \sqrt{2}$

$-\int_2^{\sqrt{2}} 4 - u^2 \, du = \int_{\sqrt{2}}^2 4 - u^2 \, du$
