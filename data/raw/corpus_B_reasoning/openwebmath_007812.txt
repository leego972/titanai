# Algebrogram

Solve algebrogram for sum of three numbers:
BEK
KEMR
SOMR
________
HERCI

Result

a =  249
b =  9467
c =  5067
d =  14783

#### Solution:

$a = 249$
$b = 9467$
$c = 5067$
$d = a+b+c = 249+9467+5067 = 14783$
