# Straighten Camera in Blender

If I have a camera at 0/0/0 of the x/y/z and its heading is unknown.

How do I make it point in the direction of 1/0/0 quickly?

The fastest way if you haven't applied any transforms on the camera is to reset the rotation with AltR to 0/0/0 and then rotate it 90° on the X and -90° on the Z, RX 90, RZ -90.
