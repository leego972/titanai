# How many linear equations in x and y can be satisfied by x = 2, y = 3?

Question:

How many linear equations in x and y can be satisfied by x = 2, y = 3?
(a) Only one
(b) Only two
(c) Infinitely many
(d) None of these

Solution:

(c) Infinitely many

Infinite linear equations are satisfied by $x=2, y=3$.
