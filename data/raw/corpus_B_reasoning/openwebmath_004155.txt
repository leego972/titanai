## Understanding the formula

The formula is very easy to understand. It would help if you had the initial and final values for the calculation.

The formula is

$$percentage\;increase\;=\;100\;×\;\frac{(final\;value - initial\;value)}{(initial\;value)}$$

### Example

You have $500 in your savings account, and the Bank gave you$200 as your savings. How to calculate percentage increase in simple steps?

Solution

Initial value = 500

Increase value = 200

final value = 500 + 200 = 700

$$percentage\;increase\;=\;100\;×\;\frac{(final\;value - initial\;value)}{(initial\;value)}$$

%increase = 100 × (700 - 500) / 500

%increase = 100 × (200) / (500)

%increase = 100 × 0.4%

%increase = 40%
