Acoustic black hole is becoming an attractive topic in recent years, for it open-up new direction for experimental explorations of black holes in laboratories. In this work, the gravitational bending of acoustic Schwarzschild black hole is investigated. We resort to the approach developed by Gibbons and Werner, in which the gravitational bending is calculated using the Gauss-Bonnet theorem in geometrical topology.
