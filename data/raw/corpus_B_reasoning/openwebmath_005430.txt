Question

# What will be the slope of line having the equation as $$(y+2x)=5$$ ?

Solution

## $$y+2x=5$$ can be written as $$y=-2x+5$$ which is of form $$y=mx+c$$ Comparing we get, $$m=-2$$So slope is$$(-2)$$Physics
