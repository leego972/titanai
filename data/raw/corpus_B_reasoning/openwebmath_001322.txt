## Details about kilometre per second and inches per minute units:

Convert Kilometre per second to other unit:

### kilometre per second

Definition of kilometre per second unit: ≡ 1 km / 1 s. The speed with which the body moves 1 km in 1 second. The Earth's circulation speed is about 30 km/s.

Convert Inches per minute to other unit:

### inches per minute

Definition of inches per minute unit: ≡ 1 in / 60 s = 2.54 cm / 60 s. The speed with which the body moves 1 inch (or 2.54 centimetres) in 1 minute.
