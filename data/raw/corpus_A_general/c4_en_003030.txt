One August night, Harper Regan walks away from her home, her husband, daughter, and keeps walking. She tells no one of her plans, putting everything she has worked for at risk.
“The play tells about the lure of melancholy, lie, prejudice and coming to terms with painful truth; heroine’s obsessive curiosity is a good excuse for Stephens to reveal a great deal of original writing” – The Times.
