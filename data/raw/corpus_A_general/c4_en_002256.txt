For our first ever 'How to Wear' post, we wanted to begin with a Smart/Casual look, that is comfortable to wear, but with a glamorous flair.
Some slouchy boyfriend jeans, with a warm knitted turtleneck is the perfect way to achieve a cool, casual look this winter with a hint of glamour with cateye sunglasses, and a fun pair of heels to add some fun to your everyday wear. Stay practical with a large, and stylish, clutch bag - again sticking with the monochrome theme.
This outfit of monochrome tones is added with a pop of colour, achieved with pieces from our Equinox Collection. A perfect way to incorporate bright colours to your winter wardrobe, a necessity during these long and dark winter nights.
