When it comes to shopping for pants I usually play it very safe, especially for work. Skinny, high waisted, and dark. But this time around I wanted to step out of my comfort zone and really push my fashion boundaries.
I used to think that only Gwen Stefani during her No Doubt days could rock some checked pants. But once I tried these on, I felt Hella Good and told myself I would stop Running away from printed bottoms. Checkered pants are definitely a statement piece for your closet. From a rocker chick look to a professional outfit, they add fun and unique flair to any look while still keeping it classy.
