YouRCooK is the official sponsor of the YENA 5 a side football fundraiser tournament on Sunday 18th September in Filton, Bristol.
YouRCooK will specifically be sponsoring the YENA team kit as well as the tournament itself to help to build a larger pot of funding for YENA to help fund young startups through the YENA grant fund which officially kicks off at their Showcase event on the 28th September in Bristol.
Rachel Garwood, the CEO of YouRCooK said "We are delighted to support YENA’sFootball Fundraiser as it offers a great way to improve fitness levels,develop team building skills and cultivate a competitive edge and a champion’s mindset. At the same time this event raises funds for a fantastic organisation that is committed to supporting young start up businesses."
