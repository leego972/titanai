Come join us in celebrating the release of Greenville writer (and Presbyterian College professor) Terry Barr's first book of essays, Don't Date Baptists and Other Warnings from My Alabama Mother. A literary cousin of the great memoirs of fellow Alabama boy Rick Bragg, this collection explores what it was to come of age in the deep New South of the 1970s. Spread the word. Bring your friends, family, neighbors, Baptists, Methodists, Athiests... in other words come one and all!
