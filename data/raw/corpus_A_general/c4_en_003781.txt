230 new emojis are coming to your devices this year: With the arrival of 2019, Unicode has announced an interesting list of new emojis which will make their debut on devices this year.
This list includes 59 new emoji with 171 different for gender and skin tone, making the total count of 230.
A few of the new emojis will include mechanical arm, deaf person, drop of blood, waffle, ice cube, pinching hand, yawning face, and many more!
Creating the emoji set more diverse, new characters such as a man in a wheelchair or a woman in a wheelchair, a man with probing can, motorized wheelchair, people holding hands, and guide dog have also been added.
Later this year, the new emoji will start appearing on mobile devices in September or October.
