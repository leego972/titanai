ArtikPix is an app designed to help children improve their speech production at the word and sentence levels. This app is convenient for articulation practice and takes very little time on the part of the clinician to set up. This app also gives you the option of taking your own photos or importing pictures to create your own flashcards to use. There is a data collection feature and a recording option. Some downfalls of this app include the inability to remove unwanted words or pictures and you can only work on one target at a time.
