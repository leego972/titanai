Wild week at the Shore Conference as #1 St. John Vianney took on Rumson, Lenape and Holmdel and won all three contests. We saw two undefeated division teams get knocked off when Southern lost to Toms River North and Manchester lost to Point Boro which opened the race now for both divisions. Neptune, St. Rose, Middletown South and Vianney all moved one game closer to taking their division crowns. With just two weeks remaining before the Shore Conference Tournament selection, a total of 22 teams are above .500 and five more are looking to get to the .500 mark to qualify for the tournament.
Standings are based on stats and scores reported to Shore Sports Network by coaches/team representatives. Coaches may email corrections to brauntenj@gmail.com. Standings are based on games through Friday January 29th, 2016.
