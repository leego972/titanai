Turbike.org | Hugh Piggot inspired, pedal generated electricty!
The turbike combines my interest in cycling with off-grid, ecological living. This site documents my experience of making and using it to pedal my one-man office off the grid.
I hope this site encourages you to find ways to reduce your own grid-dependence. Or at least gives you pause for thought about where society is being led by those who control its resources.
