The Greater Pensacola Figure Skating Club is a community of families, athletes, and professionals united by a love of figure skating and a commitment to bringing excellence to every facet of the sport. Through the richness of our programs, the quality of our staff, and the energy of our members we ensure that all of our skaters have the opportunity to pursue their dreams while also giving back to the Greater Pensacola area community. From recreational skaters, beginners, to lifelong enthusiasts, The Greater Pensacola Figure Skating Club continues to add to its place in figure skating history in our area. We invite you to experience our program, coaches, and events while polishing skills, enriching lives, fostering teamwork and gaining friendships along the way.
