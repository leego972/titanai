InFocus entered the Indian market with the launch of InFocus M2 and now its time for InFocus M330.The phone has got a lot of positive responses from the people.They nailed the price of the phone with super-powered specifications.Now the company is preparing for the launch of another game changer in India.The InFocus M330 is listed in Snapdeal for the competitive price tag of Rupees 9,9999.But still the phone is not officially announced and there is no official launch date for the same.
The phone features the 5.5 HD display with 1280 X 720 pixels resolution and 267 PPI. The InFocus M330 is powered by 1.7 GHz Octa Core MediaTek MT6592 processor and 2GB of RAM.The internal storage of the device is 16 GB out of which 12GB id user available.It can be extended up to 64 GB through a Micro SD Card.The rear camera of InFocus M330 is 13MP and the front camera is 8MP.Other features of the camera are LED Flash, f2.2 aperture speed, and Sony Exmor R Sensor.
The InFocus M330 runs on the 4.4 version of Android KitKat.Some of the connectivity options are 3G, Bluetooth V2.1, WiFi, and GPS.It is the dual SIM version and both the SIM supports 3G as well as 2G.It has 3100 mAh battery to power the whole house.The phone could be a tight competitor to Micromax Yureka, Redmi Note 4G, Moto G(2nd Gen) and Huawei Honor 4X.Though the phone is listed in Snapdeal, the release date of InFocus M330 is unknown.
