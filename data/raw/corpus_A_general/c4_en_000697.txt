It Takes Teams to Tango (TM) is a totally unique and innovative facilitation tool for developing COMMUNICATION and COLLABORATION in organisations.
Based on principles of connecting with others in the Argentine tango, the tool is a fun team learning experience which is applicable to everybody who wants to work more productively with others.
Developed by Rachael Glaser, It Takes Teams to tango has strong foundations in her training and work as a communications therapist, policy consultant, and corporate communications facilitator. Based on the client's requirements Libertango collaborates with talented professional dancers and executive coaches to give maximum value to the event.
The tool is adaptable to any setting and is effective as a workshop, conference presentation or for corporate getaway learning.
