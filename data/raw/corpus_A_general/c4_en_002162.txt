Tomlith General Purpose Greases are comprised of three different grades in both NLGI 1 and NLGI 2 thicknesses. These lithium complex greases all stem from ISO 220 base oil with varying additives and treat rates to provide excellent film strength and extreme pressure performance.
Tomlith EP-220 NLGI 1 A versatile multi-purpose bearing grease fortified with a polymer enhanced lithium complex thickener. Tomlith EP 220 contains Tomlin's proprietary XFR+ EP and anti-wear technology designed for heavily loaded plain and moderate speed anti-friction bearings operating under normal to adverse conditions. It is formulated with high quality base fluids that have outstanding thermal and oxidative stability characteristics.
