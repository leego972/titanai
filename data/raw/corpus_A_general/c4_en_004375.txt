When this show My Village (ບ້ານຂອງຂ້ອຍ / ban kong koy) aired in 2013 it became likely the first national television show aimed at pre-school aged children with the goal of teaching early language skills in Laos. It even included 2 young hosts, claymation, voice over work and adorably the theme song was song by young Lao kids.
It was supported by UNICEF and produced by the Ministry of Education and Sports, Lao National TV, and Lao Star TV.
