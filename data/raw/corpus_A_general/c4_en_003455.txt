The movie that put me on the map, so to say, is finally out, and my verdict is in.
How did “King Arthur: Legend of the Sword” put me on the map? The reason is because this movie was in the planning stages way back before I was published, and its very presence in the pipeline communicated to my publisher that other media companies invest in King Arthur related books and movies.
And so “King Arthur: Legend of the Sword” helped to get me published with Zondervan, a subsidiary of HarperCollins, which therefore gave my novels worldwide distribution to every English speaking country, including Canada, the United Kingdom, most of Europe, South Africa, and Australia. Wow!
So in light of that you can read my oh so regretful of a review over at the Speculative Faith website.
