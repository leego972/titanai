Described by The Radio Times as having “a cheeky charm” the show is presented through this personality and is both draw-dropping and laugh-out-loud funny.
Nephew of the late Paul Daniels, James has been storming it online in the last twelve months whilst gaining a lot of traction in the national press. Gaining almost 200,000 social media followers of his own, his videos have average of around 400,000 views. Having toured as part of a troupe in 2016, James brings you his brand new own one-man show.
