Ingibjörg Gudmundsdottir - KAHLA.Porcelain for the senses.
Ingibjörg Gudmundsdottir was born in 1980 in Reykjavík, Iceland. After graduating from the Reykjavík School of Visual Arts she began working as an artist and teacher.
Ingibjörg is a collector. She collects objects, ideas, sensations, emotions and memories. Each of her works calls for a solution of its own. It is influenced by the material, the process and the subject of exploration.
