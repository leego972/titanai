How you always dreamed you would take care of your patients.
Downloaded by the patient, CoreyPatient™ enables your patients to access relevant information sent to them by their healthcare provider from the time a patient is admitted until they are discharged and recuperating. The patient is also able to send health status updates to their care team via text, voice-to-text, and images, as well as feedback regarding their care experience.
