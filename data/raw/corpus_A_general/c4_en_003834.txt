Deliveries to the US take on average 8 days to arrive, depending on the shipping method used and distance from South Central Arizona. Deliveries to other countries will take longer. To increase shipping security, a delivery receipt may be asked from you by the delivery company. If the product has still not been delivered after 30 days of purchase date, S&J Hardware will refund the customers money and pursue insurance claims with the carrier.
Drop shipping is a process where your order goes from our site directly to our supplier/vendor. They then fulfill the order and send it directly to you. This offers a number of advantages, but can add a day or two to processing time.
