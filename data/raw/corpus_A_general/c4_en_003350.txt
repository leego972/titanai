The finca is located just below the village of Cañar, itself about 7km from Orgiva. It is reached by a cement track, and consists of 8,758 m2 of terraced hillside land. 5,000m2 is irrigated agricultural land, and the rest scrubland.
The building was constructed in 2005 and is well built, of modern materials. It is in all 85 m2, with a large living area, three bedrooms and a large shower room. It is wired for mains electricity, which can be connected. The building is legally correct as a dwelling.
The land has irrigation water, which also provides water for domestic use. There are numerous olive trees and other fruit trees on the land, and there are spectacular views to the south, of the Sierra Lujar and the Guadalfeo valley. Cañar is a beautiful peaceful village a short distance from Orgiva, a market town of 5,000 inhabitants.
