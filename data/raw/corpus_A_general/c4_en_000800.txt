Drawing on a unique partnership of private enterprises and public entities, we’re able to give you hands-on guidance in setting up your business abroad. Want to know the cultural etiquette when entering a business meeting? Need to navigate a complex set of rules and regulations in a country very different from your own? We’re here to help!
When becoming a resident at Innovation House China-Denmark, you get so much more than just an office space. You get co-workers from both China and Denmark, all of whom are embarking on a journey similar to yours.
When you’re setting up a business far from home, that kind of kinship can be invaluable. And if you’re looking for a business partner in your new market, we’re happy to serve as matchmakers.
We offer Private Offices, Open Offices, and On-The-Fly workspaces.
Choose the solution that’s right for your business. You’re free to mix and match any way you please. All workspaces come equipped with ergonomic sit-stand desks, access to printers and high-speed internet as well as conference rooms and a kitchenette.
Please note: To ensure maximum flexibility, On-The-Fly workspaces may be used 2-3 times a week. All other workspaces are accessible 24/7.
