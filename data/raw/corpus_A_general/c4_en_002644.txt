A beautiful lot abutting the Smelt Brook with direct access off of Main Street within a mile of exit 9 to Route 3 expressway. This lot is in the 3ADD zoning district and allows residential or commercial development. This lot can be sold with all approvals from the Town of Kingston. The lot comes with a sewer hookup from the Sewer Commission with water, gas, and electricity in Main Street. The lot has views over the Smelt Brook and adjoining saltmarsh to offer an interesting setting for commercial or residential development.
