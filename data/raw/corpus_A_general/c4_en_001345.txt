The Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) officially announced on Friday, October 16 the complete list of passers for October 2015 Certified Public Accountant (CPA) board exam. Ralley Soriano Paragas of Colegio de Dagupan (for. Computronix College) topped the board with 92.86 percent grade.
Ian Kier Javate Valencia of Batangas State University (BatSU) placed second with 92.57 percent rating.
Polytechnic University of the Philippines (PUP) - Sta. Mesa, Holy Angel University (HAU), Ateneo de Davao (ADU) and University of Baguio (UB) graduates complete the upper 5 slots.
