Hotel Athina is located in the beautiful seaside village of Kinira, surrounded by tall green mountains and near a multitude of beaches along the coastline.
It is just 3 kilometres from one of the most beautiful and popular beaches of Thassos island, Paradise Beach.
The hotel offers 7 rooms with a fridge, private bathroom, and access to a communal kitchen as well as a barbecue outside in the garden. It's ideal for couples and families that desire to come to Thassos but have limited money to do so. These are some of the most economical rooms available on the island.
The rooms have a balcony with beautiful views and the ring road of Thassos passes right in front of the hotel, providing guests access to the beaches, archaeological sites, attractions, and villages around Thassos.
We hope to welcome you to our hotel and beautiful island!
