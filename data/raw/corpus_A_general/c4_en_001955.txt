After removing the 2 screws, pull out the battery.
The battery is connected to the motherboard on the left side.
Use your finger tips to evenly grip the battery on the left and right sides of the battery.
Pull straight up to lift the battery out of its place.
If you find yourself having trouble pulling out the battery, push the battery back in place and attempt to lift straight out again.
Do not use excessive force or attempt to pry the battery with any tools. Doing so will result in damage to the laptop.
