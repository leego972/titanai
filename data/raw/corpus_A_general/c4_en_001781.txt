The Davidson Playhouse is a perfectly located sanctuary for parents, grandparents, caregivers and their children. Our focus is sensory play in a safe, clean environment. We offer several levels of memberships, a diverse variety of classes, story time, and even summer camps! We cater to all children through age 7. There is always something exciting going on at The Davidson Playhouse and we want you to join in on the fun! If you're interested in becoming a member, please contact us today!
Anyone can come and play! You do not have to be a member to bring your little ones to the Playhouse.
We are open M-F from 9am-1pm for everyone to come and play.
The cost is $8/child per open play session.
