Concussion can have many symptoms—from cognitive difficulties, to headache, to fatigue, to feeling "slowed down," to sleep disturbances. As a key member of the multidisciplinary concussion care team, physical therapists conduct examinations and design treatment programs to help people safely return to activity and sport.
