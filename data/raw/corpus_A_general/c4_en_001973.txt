Friday, March 29th at 7:00 pm: Idaho Steelheads take on the Rapid City Rush!
Tickets are $15 and include a meal voucher (see below). $5 of each ticket purchased through the link above benefits IHS.
Enter your credit card information and your transaction is complete!
Orders must be placed 8 hours prior to the scheduled game date or subject to availability.
Pick up your meal vouchers at the Idaho Steelheads Information booth.
Electronic ticket: you will receive an electronic barcode that is scannable for entry.
Ticket counter: you can check in with the ticket counter and they will give you your tickets. You can also leave additional tickets for people in your party at the Will Call counter to enter at a later time.
You may want to bring a small blanket or extra jacket with you — the arena temperature typically hovers between 50-60 degrees.
Your ticket includes a Double RR Ranch hot dog, bag of Lay’s potato chips, and small Pepsi product.
You can print your meal voucher with your entry tickets at home.
If you go to the ticket counter, ask if they would print out your meal tickets as well as your entry tickets.
If you use the electronic barcode entry, you can redeem your meal ticket at the information counter inside the arena.
Your meal voucher lists the specific concession stands where you can redeem your meal. If the game is sold out, the Idaho Steelheads usually set up a table next to the Information Booth for quick service.
Pick up your meal quickly near the Information Booth if games are sold out.
Questions? Please contact Kendall at 208-387-2760. Thank you for supporting the Idaho Humane Society and enjoy the game!
