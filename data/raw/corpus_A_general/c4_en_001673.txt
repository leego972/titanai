HomePublishers: Taxes and legalitiesWhat is the license agreement between me and Steady?
Steady sells membership plans (including your predefined benefits) to your community. The important part of the license agreement is that you give Steady the right to distribute the content and benefits that you defined in your Steady page to your members. The license agreement sets out the compensation you will receive in return.
