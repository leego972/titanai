The Chocolate Factory’s Siro Carraro is taking part in The Open Art Competition at The Prince’s Foundation Gallery. This runs from 25th October to the 1st of November with a preview evening on October 24th from 6-9pm.
Prince’s Foundation Gallery, 19-22 Charlotte Road, Hoxton, London EC2A 3SG.
