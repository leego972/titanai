Andrew brings experience in both private and public companies to his position as chief financial officer at AgBiome. Andrew joined AgBiome in May 2015. From 2011 to 2015, he was previously chief financial officer at Advanced Animal Diagnostics. Prior to that he was chief financial officer and corporate secretary at Trimeris, Inc. (NASDAQ “TRMS”), having been promoted to that position from director of finance. From 2002 to 2004, Andrew served as chief accounting officer at Paradigm Genetics, Inc. (NASDAQ “PDGM”). From 1999 to 2002, he served as comptroller and later as director of finance at Paradigm. He is a certified public accountant who began his career as an auditor at Ernst & Young in Raleigh, NC. Andrew received his B.A. and M.A. degrees in accounting from North Carolina State University and his M.B.A. from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.
