John Cortland Timm graduated from Texas A&M University with a Bachelors of Science in Wildlife and Fisheries Sciences. Mr. Timm graduated from the University of Oklahoma College of Law where he worked at the Cleveland County District Attorney’s office as an intern. He has also interned in the Harris County District Attorney’s Office and Montgomery County District Attorney’s Offices in Texas.
Prior to joining Dunham & Jones, Mr. Timm was an Assistant District Attorney for the Hays County District Attorney’s Office prosecuting misdemeanor crimes such as DWI, Assault, and Criminal Trespass. Mr. Timm currently works exclusively on criminal cases for the firm.
Outside of work, John enjoys going to concerts, playing guitar/bass guitar, hiking with his dog, and watching Texas A&M football and San Antonio Spurs games.
