Montessori School of Excellence (“MSE”) is a private school, enrolling children ages 3 years to 12 years (Pre Kindergarten – 6th grade). Montessori School of Excellence is unique among Islamic schools in Dallas as all of our teachers are Montessori-certified and MSE is a member of the American Montessori Society (AMS). The school is open in Collin County in McKinney, close to the cities of Plano, Frisco, and Allen in North Dallas.
