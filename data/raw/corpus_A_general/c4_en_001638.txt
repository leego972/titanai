There is no Sun without Shadow is a personal response to the death of my Father. After making a solitary journey to the Alps; to find meaning, to be alone. I came back with no answers. Only the chaos of life and the grandeur of nature embedded in my mind.
These photographs are a contemplation on cyclical life, the absurd and my own experience.
Alex Catt lives and works in New Forest, England.
