The Falmouth University community is spread over two campuses - Penryn and Falmouth, which are connected by frequent bus services and a train line. We offer a variety of accommodation in Penryn and Falmouth.
Please see the Halls of Residence page for full information on our halls of residence and information about costs and contract lengths.
Applications for accommodation open on Wednesday 10th April 2019 at 12pm and are made via our online booking system - Room Service.
To be able to apply for accommodation you must have firmly accepted a course offer with Falmouth University as your first choice.
Applications for accommodation are processed in date order and allocated on a first come first served basis.
Please read the Application and Allocation Policy here.
