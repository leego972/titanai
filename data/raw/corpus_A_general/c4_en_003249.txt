Skitely has a 'wow' feature to add multiple users to a group without any limit.
To bring a rapid evolution in communication within the organizations, the client wanted to build an iOS app and integrate some changes in the existing Android App. Since thousands of users would log-in to the same app in real-time, the major challenge was to give them a seamless user experience to all the users. Through the app, users can exchange different forms of content including messages, audio, video, and documents thus the client wanted to include a data syncing feature which offers convenience in real-time interaction.
Another requirement of the client is to build a secure and multi-lingual app (French and English) to cater masses at the enterprise level.
Clavax successfully developed the app for iOS and Android which effectively lets the users communicate with their teams in real-time. To make it convenient and time-saving, the app allows the admin to share all the important data with the entire team, with a few finger taps. The admin has complete control over the activities thus gives them the ability to alter/delete messages or delete the group permanently at any point in time.
Instead of asking for contact details of all the team members, Skitely uses a 'Dynamic Code Generation Feature' through which users can join the group immediately.
Focused on excellence, our developers have used the latest technologies and frameworks to build the Skitely app.
