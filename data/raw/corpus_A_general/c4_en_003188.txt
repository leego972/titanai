Welcome to Chateau la Tulipe, the winecastle of Ilja Gort.
Chateau la Tulipe, situated in the lush green hills of Bordeaux, is the estate of winemaker/author Ilja Gort and his son Klaas, known for the LaTulipe and Slurp wines.
On this site you can read all about the chateau, the folks who work there, father and son Gort and what he does, and of course about our wines and how they are made. Keep up to date with all the winemaker’s hot-news.
