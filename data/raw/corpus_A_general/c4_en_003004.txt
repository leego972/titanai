Each mouthguard is individually moulded to fit the mouth of the person for whom it is made. It is made from heavy duty vinyl, and is designed to reduce impact and lessen the chance of chipped or broken teeth, particularly when playing sport. We recommend mouthguards for sports men and women of all ages (particularly children), and suggest that they should be worn at all times while playing and training.
