The e 906 is a professional super-cardioid, dynamic instrument microphone, specifically designed for use on guitar amplifiers to deliver the exact guitar sound you desire. It has a switchable presence filter for different user requirements with three adjustable sound characteristics; one to boost the presence range, one for "normal" presence and one to attenuate the presence range. It can also be used for percussion and brass instruments.
A more direct sound - more directly from the amp.
