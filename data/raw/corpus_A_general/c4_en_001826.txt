GiveBIG to Northwest Folklife – Help Us Meet Our Challenge!
Visit our Seattle Foundation Profile Page on May 5, 2015 and click ‘Donate Now’, to make a donation to Northwest Folklife!
Or, just visit the ‘Donate Now‘ page directly. Thank You!
Every donation made to Northwest Folklife via our Seattle Foundation Profile Page on May 5 will be stretched further thanks to the Seattle Foundation and GiveBIG sponsors, who will match a share of every contribution!
And, there’s more! Just for Northwest Folklife, an anonymous donor will also match gifts dollar for dollar up to $30,000! Don’t miss this opportunity to increase the impact of your donation to Northwest Folklife. Help us reach our goal of $30,000!
Any gift you make during GiveBIG will count toward your 2015 Friend of Folklife Benefits, to be redeemed at the Festival!
Thank you for your support of Northwest Folklife! Your gift creates opportunities for all to celebrate, share, and participate in the evolving cultural traditions of the Pacific Northwest.
