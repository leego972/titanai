The Mayne Tigers would like to thank Hart Sport for their generous sponsorship of the QFA Division 1 competition for 2018 continuing their long association with AFL in Queensland.
As part of their competition sponsorship Hart Sport also provide a very valuable in kind sponsorship to all the QFA Division 1 clubs with a $1,000 voucher for equipment of their choice. The Tigers coaching staff recently enjoyed some quality shopping at the Zillmere Hart Sport outlet to gear up for the pre-season and the year ahead.
It was already being put to good use last night at the Tigers training session at the Den.
