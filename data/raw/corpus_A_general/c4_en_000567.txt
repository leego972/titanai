Jeremy Stoller (Resident Dramaturg at the Jewish Plays Project) is a freelance dramaturg and producer. He also serves as resident dramaturg for terraNOVA Collective's Groundbreakers writers group and literary consultant for Luna Stage. Founder, The Dramaturgy Open Office Hour Project. Past: general manager; THE GOLEM OF HAVANA workshop production @ La MaMa; literary manager, Two River Theater (2010-2013); dramaturgy/literary work with The Flea, Disney Theatrical Group, PlayPenn, 52nd Street Project, Target Margin, First Person Arts Festival, George Street Playhouse. He received his BA from Drew University.
At Art Kibbutz Jeremy participated as a member of the Jewish Plays Project team rehearsing to present excerpts from And You Shall Be A Blessing, a play with music about Debbie Friedman. The play took place on June 11th at the 14th Street Y at 10 pm as part of the Tikkun Leil Shavuot event - with Shira Kline, Naomi Less, Sarah Sokolic, Michael Bradley Cohen, Deborah Yarchun, and Max Jared.
