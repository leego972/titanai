Magaluf is Majorca’s liveliest resort with a perfect mixture of sun and fun. It has developed a reputation as the party capital of the island, as well as a mecca for sandy beaches and warm Spanish sunshine.
The beach is Maguluf’s main attraction, with an array of watersports, including scuba-diving, snorkelling, parascending and jet skis. The bustling promenade, behind the beach, has souvenir shops and lively restaurants, offering cuisine from all over the world, as well as delicious local dishes.
Activities, like bungee jumping, mountain biking, go-karting, tennis and bowling, are all found on holidays in Magaluf. Children will also enjoy the Wild West theme park and the popular giant water park Aqualand Magaluf.
A weekly market takes place in nearby Calvia and historical Palma is only a short trip away, for those looking for a bit of culture.
With a notorious party atmosphere, the resort is home to around 100 bars and clubs that carry on until the early hours. The animated resort is packed with pubs, karaoke bars and dance clubs featuring international DJ’s - perfect for those craving some fun after relaxing days in the sun.
