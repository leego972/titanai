HUGO BOSS is one of the world's best known fashion and luxury brands and are synonymous with a number of sporting and cultural events including sailing, golf and Formula 1. HUGO BOSS targets different, clearly differentiated consumer groups. The brands cover an extensive product range consisting of classic-modern business wear, elegant evening and relaxed casual fashion, shoes and leather accessories, as well as licensed fragrances, eyewear, watches, children’s fashion and motorcycle helmets.
