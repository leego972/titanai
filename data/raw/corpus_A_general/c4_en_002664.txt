Snail Bob 3 is a cool logic game in which our friend the snail will need to try to collect all the stars for a nice rating at the end of each level. You will need to clear the way for him and this way Bob will be really happy once he reaches the end. Make your way through all the many and entertaining levels that this game has to offer so you will then be able to check out another instance of the game. Keep your eyes on the prize and this way you will make sure to keep Bob as a friend for life because he always has to go through some intricate roads that have traps.
Use the mouse to help Snail Bob.
