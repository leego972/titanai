SCBA mask and helmet dryers should be part of every first responder’s daily routine. The thermal stability of your gear protects you on the job, but caring for your gear between alarms can play just as big of a role in your personal health. PPE and turnout gear hold carcinogens when they are not cleaned properly. These toxins are then absorbed through the skin. Regular gear maintenance is not simply a matter of regulation; it’s a matter of life and death. Williams Direct Dryers is committed to helping first responders maintain their gear for years to come, saving your department money and keeping your team safe every step of the way.
Our PPE dryers make it easy to keep your gear in ready-to-use condition.
