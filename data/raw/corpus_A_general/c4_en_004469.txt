Located in the heart of Amman's business center, Nahas Travel and Tourism - Jordan commenced operation in 1972.
In addition to being the General Sales Agent of two international airlines, Japan Airlines and Iran Air, NTT - Jordan is already a leading travel agency whose name has been associated with superior quality services as evidenced by the numerous prizes and recognition certificates.
NTT - Jordan is a member of Japan Association of Travel Agents (JATA) and American Society of Travel Agents (ASTA).
