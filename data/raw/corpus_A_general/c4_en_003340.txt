Little Rock Scripture study begins with The Acts of the Apostles; telling the story of the spread of the Gospel and the growth of the early church from its beginnings at Pentecost, following the Ascension of Jesus.
It meets in the Conference Room every other Thursday from 9:30 to 11:00. Parishioners Tony Zadel and Donna Lenahan will share the leadership. Materials will be available at the first session. Newcomers are welcome.
