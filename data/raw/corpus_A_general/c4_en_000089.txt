Hulen R. Loyd, Sept. 27, 1884 - Aug. 30, 1959. Margaret F. Loyd, Aug., 24, 1878 - Mar. 14, 1934. Hulen R. Loyd was a son of James S. Loyd & Nancy or Martha Wilkerson. He married Margaret Finley McIntosh on Sept. 5, 1902 in Callaway Co., Missouri. She was a daughter of William McIntosh and Emma Finley.
