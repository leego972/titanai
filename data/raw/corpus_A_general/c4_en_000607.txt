Safely secure your dog in the car with the Click adjustable dog car seat belt attachment from EzyDog. Universal car seat belt clip and compatible with any dog harness that has a back D-ring. Simply clunk, clip and go.
Easily attaches straight from your dog's harness into the existing seat belt clip of your car. Keeps your dog safe in case of sudden braking and stops them roaming around too much in the car as you drive. Safe and sensible.
The universal seatbelt clip works in most modern car seat belt sockets *. The heavy duty snap hook on the other end easily attaches to your dog's existing harness.
The adjustable length means it can be used for small or large dogs or to make sure your dog is in your preferred position. For example you might like your dog to have room to move about on the back seat and lay down or relax but be restrained from scrambling their way to the front seats or out of an open car window.
* Unless your car has particularly larger or smaller than normal seatbelt ports then it should fit. Does NOT work in the Volvo range of cars. If you have an issue with the socket sizes, take a look at the alternative Dog Seat Belt Loop Restraint.
