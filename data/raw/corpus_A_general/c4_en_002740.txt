Colour : Dark brown, with white horizontal lines.
Habit : Butterfly of open areas, found through out the year, often noticed basking on leaf about 3-4 mt above ground, flight is graceful.
Larval Host Plants : Erythrina indica, Xylia dolabriformis, Pongamia glabra, Thespesia populeana, Hibiscus rosa-chinensis, Grewia spp. etc.
