Size without frame: 29.0" x 39.0"
Size including frame: 36.0" x 47.0"
A truly superb painting full of action and conjuring up hope and despair and human endeavour. Roe painted many subjects around the Yorkshire coast, and elsewhere, and having compared this painting with quite a number of others we can confidently assert that this ranks among his finest works. A commanding canvas both in execution and size.
