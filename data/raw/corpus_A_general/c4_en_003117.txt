All Queer Bodies is a body positive and trauma-informed community-based portrait illustration project with one goal in mind: to capture an authentic representation of the diversity of our global queer community. The project was launched in December of 2017 by artist, Alyssa Andrews, and is rooted in consent and the equity and inclusion of marginalized people. To achieve this, there is no jury or selection process (all bodies submitted are drawn), and there is no monetary cost for participants or consumers (the project is funded entirely on community support and donation).
Visibility is important and we deserve to not only be seen, but seen as we are. This project seeks to honor our community. Our lives. Our stories.
