Velvet Underground- Prominent Men LP. Wow this LP contains some amazing Velvet Underground pre recording demos from the "Andy Warhol" and "White Light White Heat" albums. The power of these versions can not be overstated. An absolute must have for any fan of the Velvet Underground. Pressing limited to 500 copies and the tracks include Venus In Furs, Prominent Men, Heroin, I'm Waiting for the Man, Wrap Your Troubles In Dreams, All Tomorrow's Parties, There Is No Reason, Sheltered Life, Its All Right, Im Not Too Sorry and Here She Comes Now.
