Love Heals All Wounds is an evening-length theatrical show by MAI (Movement Art Is) that addresses social issues such as police brutality and violence in America while seeking to promote diversity, inclusion, and empathy as a uniting force. MAI, co-founded by Jon Boogz and Lil Buck, uses movement artistry to inspire and change the world while elevating the artistic educational and social impact of dance. Through movement art films, workshops, performances and exhibitions, MAI is resetting the spectrum that defines dance.
In 2018, Jason joined the show’s creative development team as the composer, sound designer, and musical director. Together, they completed two creation residencies and a VIP Soft Premiere at California State University, Fullerton (CSUF).
Love Heals All Wounds is currently touring around the US, with the official World Premiere coming in May 2019 in Los Angeles.
