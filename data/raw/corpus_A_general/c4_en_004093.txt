There has always been an organic relationship between skateboarding and art as the two communities often share the same space; this time they share White Lady Art. The exhibition entitled “Board Rigid” opens this weekend with a unique collection of work from artists like DMC, Morgan, Friz and many more.
The Royal Hibernian Academy’s Annual Exhibition brings some of the best artists, sculptures and photographers together. With 567 works by 354 artists, it is well worth a visit and you will definitely find a number of pieces that blow you away. Some of our favourite emerging artists are alongside familiar names that have directed the Irish scene for a number of years.
