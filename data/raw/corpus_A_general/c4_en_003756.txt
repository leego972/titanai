Rose Collection Exclusive Designs has come a long way from its modest beginnings designing prom dresses and children’s wear. Having solidified her presence and brand within the bridal industry (including a designer label debut in 2006), designer Sandra Horn now focuses her craft primarily on bridal couture.
Horn’s gowns are all custom designed originals or official Rose Collection creations. Yet their exclusivity does not result in extravagant prices. Horn works well within a client’s budget, even offering affordable payment plans to ensure that each bride’s wedding dress dream is attainable.
With high praise coming from past customers and amazing styles to choose from, it’s no wonder Rose Collection Exclusive Design’s “Ultimate Bridal Gown” was recently showcased in the February/March 2017 issue of Bride’s Magazine.
