"Ugly are the best new band in London," say Sports Team.
Published: 10:50 pm, November 01, 2018Words: Sam Taylor.
Sports Team have signed their first band to their new record label, Holm Front.
Ugly - who recently supported the band at their Scala headliner - will release ‘The Last Supper At The Regal Wetherspoon’ and ‘Redemption, On the Road To Damascus’ on 7” from 10th December.
