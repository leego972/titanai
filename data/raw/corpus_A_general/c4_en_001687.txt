How does the TennisTampaBay Tourney Work?
The TennisTampaBay staff will seed 25% of the participants based veteran status (Most number of matches played in their career) with the program. If we have 8 players enrolled we'll seed the top 2 players, 16 players the top 4 players. The rest of the players will be randomly entered into the draw.
We will run a consolation bracket for the players who lost their first match. Seedings for the consolation bracket will be randomly selected by the TennisTampaBay staff. The winner of the consolation bracket will receive at least a $25 Tennis Warehouse Gift Card. Matches need to be played to crown a consolation bracket winner.
