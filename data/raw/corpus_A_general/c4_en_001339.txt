Hostal El Pino is a beautiful residence that is in an excellent place for travelers who want to know Cienfuegos. Our hostel is located just 200 meters from the cienfueguero boardwalk. From various points of the house you will have full access to the hostel's WIFI so that you can communicate with your family and work. The most important thing is that you as a guest feel at ease, with privacy and always well attended by our family with whom you will have direct contact.
