One of the more advanced tools I use in emotional healing work is age regression. This is a way of taking people back to trigger events in their lives and discovering how these events have affected them. You then help them process the previously unresolved emotional trauma and release the trigger. This technique requires the use of muscle response testing, so, I will explain the basics of how to do muscle response testing in the webinar.
