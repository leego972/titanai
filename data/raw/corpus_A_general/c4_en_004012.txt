AOL Q2 Results are out!
Earning reports for second quarter of the year are out for AOL. Company is ahead of the expectations of the analysts experts of Wall Street.
AOL is able to make up revenue of 541 Million U.S. Dollars. Company received a bump of 2 per cent as compared to previous quarter while the diluted earnings per share are of $0.35. Company received a net income of $28.5 million which falls in the same category of previous Quarter where the company was able to make $25.9 million dollars. The number of unique visitors to AOL-owned properties also grew by 3 per cent domestically year-over-year, and visitors to the AOL ad network grew 1 per cent.
AOL continued to get leaner during Q2 while growing consumer traffic, growing all advertising revenue lines, and improving our subscription trends.
AOL previously known as America Online is a global advertising-supported Web founded in the early 1980’s as Control Video Corp. in U.S. Company does get worldwide audience and it owns various famous Tech Sites including Tech Crunch. The company’s business spans digital distribution of content, products, and services. AOL provides local content, platforms and services covering geographic levels ranging from neighborhoods to major metropolitan areas.
As for the next Quarter Armstrong said to get Patch profitable by next quarter they will be removing under-performing parts by finding local partners in markets that are under performing in either traffic or revenue. Though these measures will reduce the traffic and revenue in return it will improve the profitability. All they have to do is find and upload high quality content which will engage more and more traffic.
