Hot Tub Specialists repairs all brands of hot tubs and spas on Long Island and in the five boroughs of New York City.
If parts are needed, they of course would be extra. If a second visit is required to complete the repair of your hot tub as long as you are within your initial hour there would be no extra charge. If the repair runs into a second hour, Hot Tub Specialists charges by the half hour.
Hot Tub Specialists is committed to providing excellent service. Quality work and customer satisfaction are our goals.
