Leisure activities in the renewed Leisure Center of Tervis Medical Spa Hotel!
The renewed sports hall offers better opportunities for various sports – come and play badminton, basketball, volleyball and floorball (indoor hockey). There is a high quality sound system in the hall, allowing to organize large-scale events.
The table tennis hall now has several additional functions – along with table tennis proper you can also play snooker or koroona. This room is also great for holding various events!
The fitness gym with the total area of 130 m2 offers you 13 different Gym80 weight machines and 8 Matrix cardio machines. Here you will also find a secluded room for stretching exercise. From 16:00 daily gym users have free access to the swimming pool and sauna! The atmosphere of the gym and the quality of the equipment will surely please every sports enthusiast.
Our therapy pool has also changed. Now it has multi-level bottom – all our customers will be safe and comfortable regardless of their height.
