(1/9/02) Dave’s Travel Corner (DTC) today announced the release of a new online service – ePostcards. These postcards can be sent electronically via email to friends & family.
Each postcard is created with a photograph taken by Dave from some of his trips. Each photo is accompanied by a brief description and a link to a related travel article. The ePostcards are entirely customizable. For example you can change the background color, change the size & color of the text, choose from variety of background images, and choose to notify yourself when the recipient of the postcard receives his/her postcard.
These postcards are now available on Dave’s Travel Corner under “photography” as an option next to every photograph. Send a postcard today!
