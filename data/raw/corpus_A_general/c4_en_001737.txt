You can't say it's not thoroughly deserved. Two impressive achievements by Claudio Marchisio rightly called for a standing ovation from the Juventus Stadium faithful before the match against Cagliari. And Il Principino duly collected two awards for his efforts following the pre-match warm-up.
First there was a commemorative plaque for his 300th Bianconeri appearance – which came against Fiorentina last week – handed to him by president Andrea Agnelli.
Then it was time for J-Member Martina Divis to hand him the MVP of the Month Powered By Hanwha award for April on behalf of the Juventus Membership community.
A memorable night for Marchisio and an unforgettable one for Martina, who got to meet one of her heroes while soaking up the pre-match atmosphere on the hallowed J-Stadium turf.
