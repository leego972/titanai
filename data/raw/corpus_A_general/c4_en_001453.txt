PostdocTraining specialises in both face-to-face and web-based professional development support for researchers. It offers workshops tailored specifically to the needs of individual institutions or groups of postdocs and PhD students, designed case-by-case to tackle any of the issues that typically affect researchers’ career progress and performance.
PostdocTraining’s workshops aim to give researchers essential skills and tools that they can apply immediately in their work, to quickly build confidence, increase output and improve career prospects.
PostdocTraining also runs ‘open’ sessions for participants outside their institutes, subject to demand.
