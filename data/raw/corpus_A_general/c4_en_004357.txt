Clyde & Co received the most accolades at the Legal Week Middle East Legal Awards 2018, winning Arbitration Team of the Year, Litigation Team of the Year and CSR Initiative of the Year.
These awards further reinforce the firm's leading position in the Middle East and confirms the breadth and depth of Clyde & Co's offering and sector specialisms in the region. In the past 12 months, the firm bolstered its practice with 6 key partner hires and also recently expanded into Oman with the opening of an associate office.
The Legal Week awards highlighted the international legal industry's commitment to the Middle East, with a number of large firms recognised for their exceptional achievement and best practice in the region. The awards were judged by an independent panel comprised of the most senior and innovative individuals in the sector, chaired by PepsiCo's Vice President & General Counsel for Middle East & Africa, Dean Sheehan.
Clyde & Co has the largest presence and is one of the most experienced international law firms in the Middle East with over 40 partners and 400 staff, based full time in Abu Dhabi and Dubai in the UAE, Doha in Qatar, Muscat in Oman and Riyadh in Saudi Arabia, providing a full-service commercial offering of local specialists.
