# Titan World History & Historical Reasoning v1

Status: in development — not for marketplace sale until validation and packaging complete.

## Goal
Create a globally balanced history dataset that teaches chronology, causation, continuity/change, comparison, source interpretation and uncertainty rather than isolated date memorisation.

## Coverage
- ancient civilisations
- classical societies
- medieval societies
- early modern transformations
- industrialisation
- imperialism and decolonisation
- world wars
- cold war
- political and economic transformations
- scientific, technological and cultural history
- regional histories of Africa, Asia, Europe, the Middle East, Oceania and the Americas

## Quality standard
Records must distinguish established historical facts from interpretation. Materially disputed claims require perspective labels or qualified language. Coverage must avoid treating one region as the default world history narrative.

Record types include:
- chronology reconstruction
- causal-chain reasoning
- continuity vs change
- compare/contrast
- primary/secondary-source interpretation
- consequence analysis
- misconception correction
- evidence-strength assessment

## Initial milestone
First milestone: 500 reviewed seed records with regional and period quotas. Scale-up only after factual verification, perspective balance, duplicate checks and leakage controls pass.
