# VCE Tutor — All Subjects Roadmap

## Goal
Build the highest-quality no-paid-source VCE tutoring corpus we can, covering the full VCE study catalogue progressively. Every subject remains IN DEVELOPMENT until it independently passes the Titan Dataset Competency & Release Standard.

The roadmap follows the current VCAA study catalogue as the curriculum map, but does not copy protected assessment content. Original tutoring examples, practice questions, worked solutions, feedback and evaluation items are authored independently from free/open sources and structured synthetic data.

## Quality standard per subject
A subject may not be advertised as competent until it has:
- a current curriculum/outcome map
- a free/open source registry with licence review
- >=3,000 accepted training examples for a broad subject, and more where evaluation requires
- >=500 accepted examples per major area of study/competency cluster
- multiple independent scenario families per competency
- misconception and hard-negative coverage
- at least 20% hard/expert examples for reasoning-heavy subjects
- family-isolated train/validation/test partitions
- >=150 held-out items per major competency cluster where practical
- transfer and adversarial evaluation
- deterministic answers code-verified where applicable
- factual/provenance audit
- stratified human review
- no material weakness hidden by aggregate scoring

Broad subjects are expected to exceed the minimum substantially; 8,000-20,000+ accepted records per individual VCE subject may be appropriate depending on breadth. English/language/writing-heavy subjects may require substantially more diverse examples and qualitative evaluation.

## Free-source policy
No paid textbook, commercial revision provider, subscription question bank or paid dataset is required.

Use, after licence review:
- VCAA public pages for curriculum mapping, accreditation and assessment structure
- Australian Government and Victorian Government open data
- public scientific agencies
- OpenStax and other permissively licensed OER
- Wikidata CC0
- Project Gutenberg/public-domain literature for applicable English tasks
- public-domain historical archives
- World Bank/UN open datasets where compatible
- original structured synthetic data with code-computed ground truth
- original diagrams/tables/data representations produced from open facts/data

Do not treat public accessibility as permission to reproduce. Copyrighted VCAA examinations, commercial textbooks and copyrighted teacher resources are not to be copied into the commercial corpus.
