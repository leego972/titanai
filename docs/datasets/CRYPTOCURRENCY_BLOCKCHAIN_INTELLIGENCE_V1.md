# Titan Cryptocurrency & Blockchain Intelligence v1

Status: IN DEVELOPMENT — NOT SELLABLE

Goal: produce a competency-grade dataset for cryptocurrency, blockchain systems, digital-asset markets, on-chain analysis, protocol mechanics, risk, custody, compliance awareness, scams/fraud detection, and quantitative reasoning.

## Release target

- Target accepted corpus: 18,000–30,000+ records, expandable until competency gates pass.
- Minimum 1,500 accepted examples per major competency.
- Minimum 300 hard/expert transfer examples per major competency.
- Minimum 150 adversarial/edge-case examples per major competency.
- Separate train/validation/test scenario families with no family leakage.

## Core competencies

1. Blockchain architecture and consensus fundamentals
2. Wallets, custody, keys, signing, and operational security
3. Tokenomics and supply mechanics
4. Exchange, market microstructure, liquidity, and slippage
5. DeFi mechanics: AMMs, lending, collateral, liquidations, staking
6. On-chain data interpretation and transaction-flow analysis
7. Smart-contract and protocol risk awareness
8. Stablecoins, bridges, oracles, and systemic risk
9. Crypto portfolio/risk management and position sizing
10. Fraud, scam, rug-pull, phishing, and manipulation detection
11. Regulatory/compliance awareness without pretending jurisdiction-specific legal advice is static
12. Quantitative crypto analysis: returns, volatility, drawdown, correlations, funding, basis, yield

## Record requirements

Every accepted record must include structured metadata for competency, subcompetency, difficulty, scenario family, provenance/source family, verification mode, and split family.

Answers must distinguish fact, calculation, inference, and uncertainty; avoid guaranteed-return claims; identify assumptions; and prefer quantitative verification where possible.

## Competency gate

A dataset cannot be marked complete until held-out evaluation demonstrates strong performance across every core competency, transfer to unfamiliar scenarios, correct quantitative calculations, calibrated uncertainty, and no systematic promotion of fraudulent or deceptive conduct.

## Quality controls

- deterministic validation for mathematical examples
- duplicate and near-duplicate rejection
- scenario-family leakage prevention
- balanced bull/bear/sideways and high/low-liquidity contexts
- no dependence on transient token prices as timeless facts
- provenance review for protocol/mechanism claims
- adversarial scam/manipulation cases
- train/validation/test isolation
- independent spot review

Likely premium specialist dataset. Final pricing should be set only after record count, benchmark performance, and package quality are known. Hard floor remains USD $5.
