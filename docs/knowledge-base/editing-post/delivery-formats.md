# Delivery Formats

## Purpose
Delivery formats are the specific technical specifications required by distributors, streaming platforms, and festivals for the final version of the film.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- Every distributor and platform has different delivery specifications — get them early
- Common delivery specs: DCP (cinema), ProRes/DNxHR (broadcast), H.264/H.265 (streaming), IMF package (Netflix/Amazon)
- A DCP is required for theatrical release — it must be created by a specialist facility
- Netflix and Amazon have specific IMF requirements that must be met exactly

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Get delivery specifications from distributors, platforms, and festivals before post begins
- Brief the post team on delivery requirements early — some affect the edit and grade workflow
- Budget for delivery mastering — DCP creation, IMF packaging, and platform deliveries all have costs

## Scene-level checks
- Are delivery format requirements confirmed for this platform or festival?
- Are there format requirements that affect the edit or grade workflow?

## Project-level checks
- Are delivery requirements researched and documented for all planned releases?
- Is the budget sufficient to create all required delivery formats?

## Warning signs
- Delivery specs not researched until the week of submission — too late
- Different versions managed without clear version control

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Get delivery specifications from all planned distributors and festivals before production ends
- Build delivery creation and QC time into the post schedule
