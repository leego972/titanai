# AI for Pre-Production

## Purpose
AI tools accelerate and enhance pre-production work — from script analysis and coverage planning to concept visualization and production design reference generation.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- AI accelerates but doesn't replace human creative judgment in pre-production
- Script analysis AI can identify structural issues, character inconsistencies, and pacing problems
- Concept art AI generates visual references faster than traditional illustration
- AI scheduling tools can optimize shooting schedules for efficiency

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Evaluate AI pre-production tools for their specific value in the production workflow
- Check that AI tools are augmenting human creative work, not replacing essential human judgment
- Test AI tools before integrating them into the production workflow

## Scene-level checks
- Is this AI pre-production tool producing genuinely useful output for the specific task?
- Is the AI output being reviewed and validated by a human with appropriate expertise?

## Project-level checks
- Are AI pre-production tools evaluated for their specific value before adoption?
- Is AI pre-production output reviewed and validated by human experts?

## Warning signs
- AI pre-production tools adopted without evaluation
- AI output used without human expert validation — errors propagate
- Pre-production AI tools that slow down rather than accelerate

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Evaluate each AI pre-production tool against a specific use case before adopting it
- Create a pre-production AI workflow: which tools, for which tasks, and how output is validated

## Example assistant output
> For your pre-production AI integration: 1) Script analysis: use AI to identify structural issues as a second opinion. 2) Concept art: use AI image generation for location concepts and atmosphere references. 3) Shot list development: use AI to suggest coverage options for complex scenes, then evaluate with your DP. Each uses AI as a thinking partner, not a decision maker.
