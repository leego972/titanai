# AI Storyboarding

## Purpose
AI storyboarding uses image generation tools to create visual representations of planned shots quickly and at scale.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- AI storyboarding can produce visual shot references at a fraction of the time and cost of traditional storyboarding
- It's most effective for: concept alignment between director and DP, pre-visualization of complex sequences
- Current limitations: precise character consistency across frames, specific actor likeness
- AI storyboards are reference tools, not finished documents

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Evaluate AI storyboarding for the specific production need before choosing the approach
- Check that AI storyboard output is specific enough to communicate the intended shot
- Brief the DP and department heads: AI storyboards are intention references, not precise technical documents

## Scene-level checks
- Does this AI storyboard accurately communicate the intended shot composition and camera angle?
- Is the AI storyboard specific enough to use as a communication tool with crew?

## Project-level checks
- Is AI storyboarding integrated into the pre-production workflow appropriately?
- Are AI storyboards validated by the DP before being used as crew reference?

## Warning signs
- AI storyboards used as precise technical documents when they're intention references
- Crew taking AI storyboards as firm commitments rather than starting points

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Use AI storyboarding for complex sequences first — that's where the speed advantage is greatest
- Add hand annotations to AI storyboards to clarify elements the AI couldn't represent accurately

## Example assistant output
> For your Act 3 action sequence, use AI storyboarding to explore three different visual approaches before committing to your shot list. Generate 8-10 panels for each approach. Then sit with your DP and pick the best elements from each approach. That creative exploration would take three days of traditional storyboarding. With AI generation, you can explore all three approaches in half a day.
