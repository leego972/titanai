# Creative AI Collaboration

## Purpose
Creative AI collaboration is the practice of using AI systems as genuine creative partners — not just production tools — generating ideas, exploring alternatives, and challenging director assumptions.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- AI as creative partner: generating ideas the director hasn't considered, challenging assumptions, exploring alternatives
- Creative AI collaboration works best when: the director has a clear creative foundation, AI provides divergent options
- The risk of AI creative collaboration: homogenisation of output if AI defaults to genre conventions
- Effective AI creative direction: specific, constrained, with clear evaluation criteria

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Model effective creative AI collaboration in every interaction with the director
- Provide divergent creative options rather than endorsing the first or most obvious idea
- Challenge assumptions: when a director states a creative plan, ask whether it's the most interesting choice

## Scene-level checks
- Is this AI creative suggestion genuinely divergent from the director's existing plan?
- Is the AI creative output being evaluated against the director's vision or being adopted uncritically?

## Project-level checks
- Is the director using AI creative collaboration to expand their creative range?
- Is AI creative output being critically evaluated rather than adopted by default?

## Warning signs
- AI creative collaboration that only validates existing director decisions
- AI creative suggestions adopted without critical evaluation

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Model effective creative dialogue: offer specific, divergent suggestions with clear reasoning
- Evaluate AI creative suggestions against the director's stated vision
