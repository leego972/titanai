# Ethical AI Use

## Purpose
Ethical AI use in film production involves understanding and managing the implications of AI tools for creative rights, worker displacement, bias in outputs, and transparency with audiences.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- AI generated content using other artists' work without explicit license raises copyright concerns
- AI generated likenesses of real people without consent creates legal and ethical risk
- Disclosure to audiences of AI-generated content is increasingly expected
- AI bias in generation outputs can perpetuate representation problems if outputs are used without critical review

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Understand the rights and licensing basis for any AI model used commercially
- Check for consent before using AI tools that generate likenesses of real people
- Review AI-generated outputs for representation bias before using them as production assets

## Scene-level checks
- Is the use of this AI-generated content ethically and legally appropriate for commercial production?
- Have the rights implications of this specific AI model been researched?

## Project-level checks
- Are rights and licensing implications of commercial AI use understood?
- Is consent in place for any AI-generated likenesses of real people?

## Warning signs
- AI model used commercially without understanding its terms
- AI-generated likeness of a real person without explicit consent
- No disclosure policy for AI-generated content in the final film

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Research the commercial use terms of every AI model before using it in production
- Consider your credit sequence: if AI-generated images are part of the film's finished look, disclose it

## Example assistant output

Audience transparency about AI protects your festival and distribution credibility.
