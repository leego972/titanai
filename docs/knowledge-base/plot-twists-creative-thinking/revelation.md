# Revelation

## Purpose
A revelation is any moment where the audience learns something new — about a character, the world, or the plot — that changes their understanding. Revelations are the primary dramatic mechanism for advancing complex stories.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- Revelations operate on three levels: information (we now know X), implication (knowing X changes how we see Y), and emotional (knowing X makes us feel differently about the story)
- The strongest revelations work on all three levels simultaneously
- Revelations must be earned through prior investment — an unrevealing scene followed by a revelation lands harder
- The timing of a revelation determines its impact — too early and it's just information; too late and it's a plot hole
- Every revelation should change something in the story

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Evaluate revelations for all three levels: information, implication, and emotional
- Check that revelations are timed correctly — earned by what precedes them
- Brief the director: revelations are the film's key dramatic beats, plan their placement carefully
- Ensure revelations are cinematically designed — not just delivered in dialogue

## Scene-level checks
- Does this revelation work on all three levels: information, implication, and emotional?
- Is this revelation timed correctly — earned by prior investment?

## Project-level checks
- Are major revelations planned at structurally appropriate moments?
- Does each revelation change something in the story?
- Are revelations staged cinematically, not just delivered as dialogue?

## Warning signs
- Revelations that only deliver information — no implication or emotional resonance
- Revelations delivered too early — no investment built
- Revelations that change nothing — information without consequence
- Revelations delivered entirely through dialogue — missed visual opportunity

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Plan revelations as visual moments — what does the camera do when the revelation lands?
- Test each revelation: what changes as a result of it? If nothing, it's information, not a revelation
