# Ensemble Films

## Purpose
Ensemble films follow multiple protagonists of approximately equal narrative weight. They require specific structural and character management discipline. The 1B Brain must help directors balance ensemble character arcs without letting the film become unfocused.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- Ensemble films give multiple characters protagonist-level arcs
- The ensemble must share a central thematic question — different characters embody different answers
- All major ensemble characters should have: a want, a need, a flaw, and an arc
- The ensemble's individual arcs should interconnect — choices that affect one character should affect others
- Ensemble films fail when one character becomes dominant and the ensemble isn't actually an ensemble

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Check that all ensemble characters have equivalent arc development
- Evaluate whether ensemble arcs interconnect — isolated arcs create a collection of short films
- The central thematic question must tie all ensemble characters together
- Ensemble films need a structural event that affects all characters simultaneously — usually the climax

## Scene-level checks
- Does this scene develop multiple ensemble characters or just one?
- Is this ensemble character's arc integrated with other characters' arcs?

## Project-level checks
- Do all ensemble characters have equivalent arc development?
- Are ensemble arcs interconnected — do character choices affect each other?
- Is there a central thematic question that all ensemble characters are answering?

## Warning signs
- Ensembles where one character dominates and others become supporting
- Ensemble arcs that are entirely parallel — characters don't affect each other
- Ensemble without a shared thematic question — the film lacks a centre

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Map all ensemble arcs on a single chart — verify equivalent development
- Design climactic scenes that affect all ensemble characters simultaneously

## Example assistant output
> Your ensemble film has four characters but Marcus gets 60% of the screen time and is the only character with a complete arc. Elena, Dr. Vance, and Rodriguez are all supporting characters for his story. Either commit to a single protagonist (Marcus) or genuinely distribute the story — which means reducing Marcus's scenes and expanding the others' arcs. Pseudo-ensembles where one character dominates are structurally dishonest.
