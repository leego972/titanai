# Audience Building

## Purpose
Audience building is the ongoing process of identifying, connecting with, and growing the community of people who want to see a filmmaker's work.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- Audience building starts before the film is complete — during production and post-production
- Core audiences: genre fans, subject matter specialists, the director's existing following
- Social platforms allow direct audience connection without distribution intermediaries
- Email lists are the most valuable audience asset — owned, not rented from a platform

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Help directors identify their core audience before production ends
- Brief directors: audience building is a long-term activity, not a pre-release rush
- Email list building should begin before the film is complete

## Scene-level checks
- Who is the core audience for this film, and where do they live online?
- What audience building activities are possible during production and post-production?

## Project-level checks
- Is the core audience identified and understood?
- Is audience building activity happening during production and post-production?

## Warning signs
- Audience building left until the release date — too late
- No email list — entirely dependent on platform algorithms for audience reach
- No understanding of who the core audience is

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Identify the core audience before production ends — be specific
- Build an email list from day one of public-facing activity

## Example assistant output
> Your film is a psychological thriller set in the art world. Your core audience: thriller fans, art world enthusiasts, psychological drama viewers. Where they live: Letterboxd (film enthusiasts), Instagram (art world), Reddit (genre fans). Start now, during post-production.
