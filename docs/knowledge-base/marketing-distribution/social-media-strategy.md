# Social Media Strategy

## Purpose
Social media strategy for film distribution is the planned, consistent use of social platforms to build audience anticipation, connect with communities, and drive distribution outcomes.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- Social media strategy for film: identify platform, content plan, cadence, and goal
- Platform selection should be based on where the core audience is
- Behind-the-scenes content during production builds anticipation authentically
- Social media consistency matters more than peak effort

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Help directors identify the right platforms for their specific audience
- Check that social media content plan is sustainable
- Brief directors: social media is a long game — start during production, not at release

## Scene-level checks
- Is this social media activity reaching the film's core audience on the right platform?
- Is the content plan sustainable for the duration of the release campaign?

## Project-level checks
- Is social media strategy planned specifically for the core audience and distribution goal?
- Is social media activity beginning during production, not at release?

## Warning signs
- Social media across all platforms with no focus — effort spread too thin
- Starting social media activity only at release
- Content plan that requires more resources than the team has

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Choose 2 platforms maximum and commit to them consistently
- Create a content calendar for the entire release campaign

## Example assistant output
> Your social media plan has you active on Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, Facebook, Letterboxd, and YouTube. Six platforms for a two-person team is impossible to do well. Choose two: Letterboxd (your core film audience) and Instagram (visual storytelling). Do those two well — 4 posts per week on Instagram, filmmaker diary on Letterboxd.
