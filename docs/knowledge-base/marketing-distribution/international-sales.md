# International Sales

## Purpose
International sales is the process of licensing a film's distribution rights to distributors in different countries and territories.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- International sales are typically handled by a sales agent — they license rights to distributors in each territory
- Sales agents assess commercial potential: genre, cast, budget, and subject matter all affect international sales potential
- The international sales market operates through key markets: Cannes Marché, EFM, AFM
- A film needs a professional delivery package to complete any international sales

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Help directors understand what makes a film attractive for international sales
- Check that the film's delivery package will meet international sales requirements
- Brief directors on the difference between sales agents and distributors

## Scene-level checks
- Does this film have the commercial characteristics needed for international sales?
- Is the delivery package meeting international sales requirements?

## Project-level checks
- Is international sales potential assessed honestly for this film?
- Is a delivery package planned that meets international sales requirements?

## Warning signs
- Unrealistic international sales expectations for a film without commercial characteristics
- No delivery package planning — can't complete any international sales

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Assess international sales potential honestly before investing in a sales agent relationship
- Research which sales markets are appropriate for this film's type and stage
