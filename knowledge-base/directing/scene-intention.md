# Scene Intention

## Purpose
Scene intention is the specific emotional and dramatic effect the director wants the audience to experience during and after each scene.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- Scene intention answers: how should the audience feel during this scene, and what should they think/feel when it ends
- Intention is separate from content — a scene about breakfast can have the intention of dread
- Every creative decision in a scene (camera, performance, sound, light) should serve the intention
- When a scene isn't working, the first question is: what was the intention?
- Unclear intention produces confused scenes where crew execute technically but not emotionally

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Before advising on any scene, establish the director's intention
- Check that every creative choice in the scene serves the stated intention
- If a scene feels wrong in edit, trace back to whether the intention was clear

## Scene-level checks
- What is the specific emotional effect this scene should produce?
- Is every creative choice in this scene serving the same intention?

## Project-level checks
- Has intention been communicated to all key crew for each scene?
- Are there scenes where intention conflicts with execution?
- Is intention consistent with the overall film's emotional arc?

## Warning signs
- Scenes where the director knows the content but not the intention
- Crew executing technically correctly but emotionally incorrectly
- Intention that contradicts the scene's role in the larger structure

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Write a one-line intention for every scene in the script before production
- In editing, evaluate takes based on whether they achieved the intention, not just technical quality

## Example assistant output
> Scene 24 isn't working in the edit because the DP shot it for beauty and the actor played it for grief and you cut it for tension. Three different intentions. What is the scene for? If it's the moment Elena understands she's alone, everything must serve isolation. Decide the intention and recut it with only that.
