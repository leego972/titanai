# Marketing Materials

## Purpose
Marketing materials are the creative assets used to present the film to audiences, press, and industry — poster, key art, trailer, website, and social content.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- The poster/key art is the film's single most important visual marketing asset
- Effective key art: immediately communicates genre, mood, and subject — creates desire in one image
- The film's website is a hub for all distribution activity
- Consistency across all marketing materials is essential — one visual identity, one tone

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Evaluate key art for genre communication, visual quality, and marketing effectiveness
- Check that all marketing materials are consistent in visual identity and tone
- Assess whether key art would attract the film's core audience in a crowded marketplace

## Scene-level checks
- Does this marketing material accurately communicate the film's genre, mood, and appeal?
- Would this marketing material attract the film's core audience?

## Project-level checks
- Are all required marketing materials prepared before any public film activity?
- Is there a consistent visual identity across all marketing materials?

## Warning signs
- Marketing materials prepared after festivals and press begin
- Key art that doesn't communicate the film's genre or appeal clearly
- Inconsistent visual identity across marketing materials

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Commission key art before festival submissions
- Test key art with members of your target audience before finalizing
