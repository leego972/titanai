# Distribution Strategy

## Purpose
Distribution strategy is the plan for how a film reaches its audience — through which channels, in which territories, at what times, and at what price points.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- Distribution channels: theatrical, streaming (SVOD, AVOD, TVOD), VOD, broadcast, festival, educational
- Theatrical distribution is available to a small fraction of films — strategy must be realistic
- The distribution strategy should be considered before production begins — it affects creative decisions
- Sales agents and distributors have different roles

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Help directors evaluate distribution options realistically against their film's characteristics
- Check that distribution strategy is considered before production, not as an afterthought
- Brief directors on the difference between sales agents, distributors, and self-distribution

## Scene-level checks
- Is the distribution strategy for this film realistic given its genre, budget, and subject matter?
- Is distribution considered as a strategic factor in production decisions?

## Project-level checks
- Is a distribution strategy developed before or during production?
- Are the film's distribution characteristics honestly assessed?

## Warning signs
- Distribution strategy developed only after the film is finished — too late
- Unrealistic distribution expectations — every director expects theatrical, most films don't get it
- Self-distribution chosen by default rather than by strategic decision

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Develop a distribution strategy in pre-production — it may influence creative decisions
- Consider distribution platform requirements before finalizing the film's format

## Example assistant output
> Your film is a 2-hour art house drama with no stars and a $500K budget. Honest distribution assessment: theatrical is extremely unlikely. Your realistic path is: festival strategy to build critical credibility, then streaming, with international sales possible if festival response is strong. Plan for this reality before you finish the film.
