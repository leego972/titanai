# Relationship Dynamics

## Purpose
Relationship dynamics are the power, emotional, and informational connections between characters — who needs whom, who holds power, and how those relationships evolve across the film.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- Relationships have three dimensions: power (who controls whom), emotional (who feels what about whom), and information (who knows what about whom)
- Relationship dynamics should change across the film as the story changes the power and information balance
- The most interesting relationships have characters who need each other and can't admit it
- Relationship changes should be caused by story events — not arbitrary
- Secondary relationships (between supporting characters) should reflect or contrast the primary relationship

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Map the power, emotional, and information dimensions of every major relationship
- Check that relationship dynamics change in response to story events
- Evaluate whether secondary relationships are reflecting or contrasting the primary relationship
- Brief the director: relationship dynamics are visual — they should be expressed in blocking, not just dialogue

## Scene-level checks
- How has this relationship's power and emotional dynamic changed since the last time these characters appeared together?
- Is the relationship change in this scene caused by a specific story event?

## Project-level checks
- Are major relationship dynamics mapped across the film's timeline?
- Do relationship changes arise from story events?
- Are secondary relationships reflecting or contrasting the primary relationship?

## Warning signs
- Static relationships — power and emotional dynamics don't change
- Relationship changes that happen without causal story events
- All relationships having the same dynamic — no variety in how characters relate

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Map all major relationships on a power/emotion chart and track how they change at each act transition
- Design scenes that specifically shift relationship power dynamics

## Example assistant output
> The relationship between Marcus and Elena in Act 1 has Marcus clearly holding power — he knows more, he controls the situation. By Act 3, she should hold power — she's made the critical choice. Map the power shift: when does each information revelation shift the balance? The relationship dynamic is your film's emotional through-line.
