# AI Workflow Integration

## Purpose
AI workflow integration is the systematic incorporation of AI tools into the production pipeline — ensuring AI generation fits smoothly into existing production processes.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- AI workflow integration requires: tool selection, pipeline compatibility, team training, quality review processes, and documentation
- AI tools must produce outputs compatible with the next stage of the production pipeline
- Team members who interact with AI tools need training — both on the tools and on quality evaluation
- AI-integrated workflows must have human review checkpoints

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Plan AI workflow integration before production begins
- Check pipeline compatibility: can AI outputs be used directly in the next production stage?
- Brief all team members on AI tools they'll interact with and quality evaluation criteria

## Scene-level checks
- Is this AI workflow integration producing output compatible with the next stage of the production pipeline?
- Is there a human review checkpoint at each AI workflow stage?

## Project-level checks
- Is AI workflow integration planned before production begins?
- Are all team members trained on the AI tools and quality evaluation criteria?

## Warning signs
- AI tools integrated without pipeline compatibility checks
- Team members using AI tools without training
- No human review checkpoints — AI errors propagate through the pipeline

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Plan AI workflow integration before production — identify tools, pipeline connections, and team training requirements
- Test end-to-end AI workflow integration before production begins

## Example assistant output
> Your AI image generation workflow produces PNG files but your production design pipeline uses layered PSD files. Before you generate 500 location concept images, verify whether your design team can work from PNGs or needs PSDs. Test the complete workflow end-to-end — from generation to final use — before you begin production generation work.
