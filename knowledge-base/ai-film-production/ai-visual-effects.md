# AI Visual Effects

## Purpose
AI visual effects tools automate or assist with VFX tasks that previously required specialized artists and expensive software.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- AI VFX tools provide value in: background removal and replacement, object removal and clean-up, sky replacement, de-aging
- Production-quality AI VFX requires review and refinement by VFX artists — fully automated output is rarely delivery-ready
- AI VFX tools range dramatically in quality: some produce broadcast-ready output, others produce obvious artifacts
- AI VFX can reduce costs significantly for straightforward effects, but complex effects still require traditional VFX pipelines

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Evaluate AI VFX tools specifically for the type of effect required
- Check that AI VFX output is reviewed and refined by a VFX professional before delivery
- Plan AI VFX integration from pre-production

## Scene-level checks
- Is this AI VFX tool's output quality sufficient for the specific effect required?
- Has this AI VFX output been reviewed and refined by a VFX professional?

## Project-level checks
- Are AI VFX tools evaluated for each specific effect type before adoption?
- Is AI VFX output reviewed by VFX professionals before delivery?

## Warning signs
- AI VFX output used at delivery without professional review — quality issues in final cut
- Assuming AI VFX is equivalent to traditionally produced VFX

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Test AI VFX tools on sample frames before committing to them for an effect
- Always have a VFX professional review AI VFX output

## Example assistant output
> Your film needs 35 background extension shots and 12 object removal shots. Test AI VFX on both: background extensions are currently strong in AI. Object removals on static shots are also strong. On moving camera shots, object removal requires more VFX artist work. Budget accordingly.
