# Red Herrings

## Purpose
A red herring is a deliberately misleading clue or narrative element that directs the audience toward a false conclusion. Used well, red herrings make twists feel earned; used poorly, they feel like cheating.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- A red herring must be plausible — it must genuinely earn the audience's suspicion
- Red herrings should have their own narrative function beyond misdirection — they're structural elements
- When the red herring is revealed as false, it should reveal something true about the story
- The best red herrings are emotionally satisfying even after they're discarded
- Red herrings that serve no purpose beyond misdirection feel like a writer's trick

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Evaluate red herrings for plausibility and narrative function beyond misdirection
- Check that red herrings are resolved — a planted suspicion must be addressed
- A red herring character should still serve a narrative function when cleared
- The resolution of a red herring should illuminate the real answer

## Scene-level checks
- Is this red herring plausible enough to genuinely mislead?
- Does this red herring serve a narrative function beyond misdirection?

## Project-level checks
- Are all red herrings resolved — even if as false leads?
- Do red herrings contribute to the story beyond their misdirection function?

## Warning signs
- Red herrings that are implausible — the audience never suspects them
- Red herrings introduced then forgotten — unresolved false leads feel like abandoned subplots
- Red herrings that require too much screen time to establish relative to their misdirection value

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Design red herrings to have their own narrative function — they should work even if the audience doesn't fall for them
- Resolve every red herring explicitly — even briefly

## Example assistant output
> Your character Dr. Nkosi serves as a red herring villain for 40 minutes of screen time. When he's cleared, he disappears from the film. 40 minutes of investment in a character who contributes nothing after his reveal is a structural waste. Give Dr. Nkosi a function after his clearing: he becomes an ally, or his clearing reveals information that points toward the real answer.
