# Natural Light

## Purpose
Natural light is one of the most powerful and most unpredictable elements in filmmaking.

## What the 1B Brain must understand
- Natural light changes constantly — color temperature, intensity, and direction vary through the day
- The golden hour provides the warmest, most directional natural light
- Overcast light is the most consistent natural light — soft, diffuse, ideal for matched coverage
- Direct sunlight is harsh and creates hard shadows — must be managed or embraced deliberately

## Director-level reasoning rules
- Plan shoots around natural light conditions — not the other way around
- For exterior scenes, schedule golden hour coverage during the golden hour
- Brief the DP: chasing natural light means faster setups — plan accordingly

## Scene-level checks
- Is this scene using natural light as a creative asset?
- Is the natural light consistent within the scene?

## Project-level checks
- Is natural light planned for and maximised in the shooting schedule?
- Are scenes requiring consistent light conditions scheduled accordingly?

## Warning signs
- Shooting natural light scenes over multiple hours without consistent light
- Missing the golden hour because of poor scheduling

## Strong recommendation patterns
- Schedule all exterior scenes requiring specific light quality during that light quality's window
- Have diffusion and reflectors on hand to shape natural light without contradicting it

## Example assistant output
